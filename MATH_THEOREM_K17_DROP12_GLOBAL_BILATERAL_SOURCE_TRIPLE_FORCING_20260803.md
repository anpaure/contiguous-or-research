## 5. Global triple theorem

### Theorem 5.1 (global bilateral-source forcing)

\[

## 6. Scope boundary

The proof is special to three modes.  In higher arity, a support-one source
prefix can require two or more additional modes to repair its helper and
other roles, so the one-third-mode zero (4.3) does not close the branch.
Nothing here proves a higher-arity bilateral source, an extensive bank, an
all-dimensional analogue of H/S, or regeneration.

The remaining finite triple gates are:

* complete helper menus and occurrence replay on the unique H crossed-G1
  source child;
* the no-H S bilateral B/G1/G2 joins;
* simultaneous child materialization and a fresh supplier matching for every
  occurrence positive.

Chronology, address/history/reset/residence, upper/compiler transport and a
K17 word remain outside this theorem.  No all-\(k\) or \(B(k)+O(1)\)
consequence is inferred.
