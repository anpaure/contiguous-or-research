# The aligned upper-safe folded-C8 face has two exact internal host no-gos

Date: 2026-08-01  
Lane: H2, independent quotient-fold host audit  
Status: exact finite replay for `2<=d<=12` plus symbolic rank/stutter
certificates.  Exterior or owner-rethreaded host planting remains open.

## 0. Verdict

The folded quotient has more common-cut faces than the first-witness summary
suggests.  In addition to the minimum-signature, fixed-anchor-aligned, and
minimum upper-safe rows, there is an **aligned and upper-safe** family.  Its
exact census is

\[
 \#\mathcal A=4d+8,\qquad
 \#\mathcal A_{\rm safe}=4d+4.                         \tag{0.1}
\]

For the canonical minimum-`L1`, forward-oriented member of
`mathcal A_safe`, the two common phase-base addresses are

\[
       p=4d+10,\qquad q=5d+10,\qquad q-p=d,             \tag{0.2}
\]

and its graded source-counter distance is

\[
                            22d+44.                     \tag{0.3}
\]

The opened path has `8d+24` distinct owners, `8d+23` distinct lower
colours, all sixteen upper support values, and no depth-`d` residence defect.
It necessarily loses the one lower colour of the cut edge.  “Upper safe”
means support-surjective: the upper load histogram is

\[
                         1^8(d+1)^1(d+2)^7,             \tag{0.4}
\]

not an upper-rainbow partition.

The canonical cut exchanges `f_0,f_1`; its lost lower colour and retained
upper colour are respectively

\[
 C_{\rm cut}=K+za_2+F[2,d+1],\qquad
 U_{\rm cut}=K+za_2+F[0,d+1].                           \tag{0.5}
\]

Thus a path realization needs one independently protected occurrence of
`C_cut`; upper safety says only that another edge still supplies `U_cut`.

Neither natural internal use of the aligned bases is a strict flat factor
move.

1. **Whole-host mode.**  Retaining the union host and exposing the opposite
   ray base creates exactly two rank-`r+1` owner windows, at expanded owner
   starts

   \[
                          3d+10,\qquad5d+12.             \tag{0.6}
   \]

   Every other owner has the expected rank `r`.
2. **Base-pair mode.**  Putting the two phase bases in opposite orders keeps
   every owner at rank `r` and passes residence, but creates exactly one
   triple owner

   \[
               T_p=T_{p+1}=T_{p+2},                    \tag{0.7}
   \]

   hence two consecutive stutter edges.  Their lower intersections have
   rank `r`, so this is not a Johnson path and not a strict lower-`q1`
   chronology.  The phase owner counters differ.  For `d>=3` the lower
   counters differ as well; at `d=2` they accidentally agree, but (0.7)
   remains fatal.  The two source-support decks retain directed residue

   \[
      2\quad(d=2),\qquad 8d-16\quad(d\ge3)              \tag{0.8}
   \]

   in each direction.

Thus an aligned upper-safe host exists algebraically, but its two canonical
internal plantings do not produce a strict physical fold.

## 1. Exact mode definitions

At the two positions write the phase bases as

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 P_0=K+za_3f_1,&P_1=K+za_1f_1,\\
 S_0=K+za_1f_d,&S_1=K+za_3f_d,
\end{array}                                               \tag{1.1}
\]

and put `H_P=P_0 union P_1`, `H_S=S_0 union S_1`.

For phase `epsilon`, whole-host mode replaces the two old positions by

\[
                 (H_P,P_{1-\epsilon}),\qquad
                 (S_{1-\epsilon},H_S).                  \tag{1.2}
\]

Direct dilation of the resulting source gives (0.6).  This is also forced
by maximal erosion: the old source letters at `p,q` are the three-active-
label bases in (1.1), whereas each union host contains the opposite private
active label.  The maximal inverse is already the largest source antecedent
of the unchanged owner path.

Base-pair mode instead uses

\[
          (P_\epsilon,P_{1-\epsilon}),\qquad
          (S_{1-\epsilon},S_\epsilon).                  \tag{1.3}
\]

The separation `q-p=d` makes all natural `(d+1)`-letter owner windows have
rank `r`; the two insertions cancel one another's scalar valley.  But the
three windows in (0.7) all equal

\[
                 K+za_1a_3+F[1,d],                     \tag{1.4}
\]

which proves the stutter without a solver.  No interpretation of (1.3) as
a strict Johnson factor is therefore possible.

## 2. Regenerative and common-cap scope

Both modes have a positive pointwise containment cap and use two extra
source positions.  That proves only a **formal structural charge** of two.
It does not prove a legal serial state with `chi<=2`, because neither mode
belongs to the strict flat owner/lower state space.

There is a valid conditional reset statement.  If an exterior or rethreaded
construction supplies the two actual typed union hosts, preserves the owner,
lower/upper, residence and deadline rows, assigns the cut lower colour to one
protected cell, and has one simultaneous contraction/phase-change Hall
matching for the complete protected bank, then the two boundaries may be
contracted and re-expanded in the opposite orientation.  The old side-ray
tasks become native in the opposite folded phase and the reverse side cells
supply the newly missing rays.  Under exactly those hypotheses,

\[
                              \chi\le2.                  \tag{2.1}
\]

Without them, only `chi'<=chi+2` is justified.  In particular the source
history is not reset by support equality.  On the canonical aligned-safe
face, the folded graded distance is `22d+44`; an oppositely oriented isolated
two-host ray word cancels only `4d-4`, leaving signed-disjoint-union distance
`18d+48`.  A phase-sensitive address/collar transport is still required for
a graded regenerative move.

The one-common-host construction remains a separate cost-one nonflat option:
it has one inherited deadline jump and cannot be inserted as one internal
flat depth-`d` split.
