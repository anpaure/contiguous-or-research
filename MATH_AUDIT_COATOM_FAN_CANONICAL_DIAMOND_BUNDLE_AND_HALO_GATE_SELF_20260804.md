## 1. Rotor inverse

Put `n=d+2`.  The owner window starting at `i` sees residue labels

\[
                         i+1,\ldots,i+n-1\pmod n,
\]

which are all residues except `i`.  Its union is therefore `Z-f_i`.
This proves every row of `D^dA=O` at once, including the two endpoint rows.
The source has `(d+2)+d=2d+2` positions, so the scalar inverse length is
exact.

## 2. Native diamond

The port interval has `n-2` successive residues and omits exactly
`i,i+1`.  The owner intervals have `n-1` residues and omit `i` or `i+1`.
The q1 interval has `n` residues and contains every filler.  The address and
value ranks are consequently

\[
 d:r-1,qquad d+1:r,qquad d+2:r+1,
\]

with exact Hasse containments.  This replays the local interval diamond.

## 3. Antidiagonal addresses

The right source substring beginning at `d+2` is
`f_1,f_2,...`; hence `[d+2,d+j+1]` has value
`C+{f_1,...,f_j}`.  The left substring `[j,d-1]` is
`f_(j+1),...,f_d`.  Their intersection and union are exactly `C` and
`C+{f_1,...,f_d}`.

All prefix addresses lie in the right position range, all suffix addresses
in the left, and each family has strictly changing endpoint.  Their lengths
are at most `d-1`, separating them from ports, owners, and q1 cells of
lengths `d,d+1,d+2`.

## 4. Bundle capacity

For `j=1,...,d-1`, route index `i=j-1` gives injective port, owner, and q1
addresses.  Section 3 gives injective upstream addresses.  Cross-family
lengths prevent equality between any upstream and native coordinate.
Therefore the complete records are pairwise occurrence-disjoint.

The conclusion remains conditional on the state-aware polarized type:
literal occurrence supply and capacity disjointness do not themselves make
an external cap accept the code.

## 5. Phase and halo scope

A fixed active block is pointwise common in the two tensor phases, so its
isolated rotor inverse and all relative addresses are phase-common.  This
does not imply that the source positions already used by neighbouring
owners in the full packet equal the rotor source letters.  Exactly `d`
boundary source positions on each side participate in exterior owner
windows, so the two halo systems must be solved jointly with the ambient
chronology.

The source correctly stops before asserting that splice, background
coexistence, or recursive regeneration.  Within the isolated block, the
algebra, counts, and occurrence capacities are exact.
