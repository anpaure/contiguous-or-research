## 3. Fixed-child split-G1 dependency orbits

The next statements are abstract and unconditional.  They do not assert
that the K17 crossed joins are nonempty or that analogous all-dimensional
tickets exist.

Let \(V\) be a finite set of modes in one fully materialized child.  Suppose
each \(x\in V\) has one selected split-G1 two-phase option whose unique
installed helper host in phase \(\phi\) is
\(h_{\sigma_\phi(x)}\), with \(\sigma_\phi(x)\in V\).  Assume same-phase
capacity makes each \(\sigma_\phi\) injective.  Since its domain and codomain
are both \(V\), it is a permutation.

Form the coloured dependency digraph

\[
       x\longrightarrow\sigma_0(x),\qquad
       x\longrightarrow\sigma_1(x).                  \tag{3.1}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 (physical closure equals permutation-group closure)

A subset \(W\subseteq V\) contains every installed helper mode on which its
selected source tickets depend if and only if

\[
                 \sigma_0(W)=W=\sigma_1(W).           \tag{3.2}
\]

The inclusion-minimal nonempty helper-closed blocks are exactly the orbits of
the permutation group

\[
                         \Gamma=\langle\sigma_0,\sigma_1\rangle. \tag{3.3}
\]

Consequently a uniform bound on local ticket depth or arity, or separate
perfect matchings in the two phases, gives no bound on physical module size.
A decomposition into uniformly bounded helper-closed modules needs a uniform
bound on the relevant \(\Gamma\)-orbit sizes, plus closure of every
non-helper dependency.  This is not logically necessary for a size-free
strict-gammoid contraction if a large orbit is instead fused into one
protected zero-sidecar rotor and all losses and casualties are certified
directly.

#### Proof

Helper closure is the pair of forward inclusions
\(\sigma_\phi(W)\subseteq W\).  A permutation preserves cardinality, so
each inclusion is equality.  Hence \(W\) is invariant under both generators
and their inverses.  The minimal invariant sets are precisely the group
orbits. \(\square\)

Split bilaterality gives

\[
       \sigma_0(x)\ne x,\qquad \sigma_1(x)\ne x,
       \qquad \sigma_0(x)\ne\sigma_1(x),              \tag{3.4}
\]

but (3.4) does not bound an orbit.  On \(V=\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\),

\[
             \sigma_0(i)=i+1,qquad \sigma_1(i)=i-1  \tag{3.5}
\]

satisfies (3.4) for every \(n\ge3\), while \(\Gamma\) is transitive and the
only nonempty helper-closed block is all of \(V\).  Thus the opposed-cycle
pattern is a bounded three-mode actuator only when it is planted in bounded
closed blocks; its local form alone is not regenerative.

If every \(\Gamma\)-orbit has size at most \(D\), every non-helper
dependency of its selected tickets--including base providers, donors,
endpoints, addresses, histories, reset, residence, upper, common-cap,
supplier and compiler incidences--is internal or private to that orbit, and
all flags and same-phase rows are compatible, then each orbit is a
terminally closed module of at most \(D\) modes.  The extra clauses are
essential: Theorem 3.1 classifies only the installed-helper dependency.

## 4. Separate phase Hall is not joint crossed-G1 Hall

Take two source tasks \(x,y\), phase-0 helper hosts \(a,b\), and phase-1
helper hosts \(c,d\).  Let the complete crossed choices be

\[
 {\cal A}_x=\{(a,c),(b,d)\},\qquad
 {\cal A}_y=\{(a,d),(b,c)\}.                          \tag{4.1}
\]

Each phase projection is the complete two-by-two bipartite graph and has a
perfect matching.  Nevertheless no choice of one ordered pair from each
part is coordinate-disjoint: each choice for \(x\) meets both choices for
\(y\), once in phase 0 and once in phase 1.

### Proposition 4.1 (projection-Hall obstruction)

Separate phasewise Hall conditions do not imply a crossed-G1 bank, even
before base-provider, donor, flag, endpoint or supplier conflicts are added.

Thus a valid selection theorem must act on complete crossed records.  Let
each source task \(x\) have a family \({\cal A}_x\) of internally valid,
fully enriched module candidates.  Build the conflict graph on the disjoint
union of these parts, omitting within-part edges.  If every candidate has at
most \(R\) counted noncommon conflict tokens, every interpart
incompatibility is witnessed by such a token, and through each token a
candidate has at most \(D-1\) rivals, then its conflict degree is at most
\(R(D-1)\).  Haxell's independent-transversal theorem gives a full selection
whenever

\[
                 \boxed{|{\cal A}_x|\ge
                    \max\{1,2R(D-1)\}\quad
                         \text{for every }x.}          \tag{4.2}
\]

Equality is allowed.  This is applicable only after a crossed source record
has been enriched with complete helper tickets, terminal closure, literal
state and corridor resources.  Applying (4.2) to bare source options would
not prove a module bank.

## 5. Exact Regenerating Bilateral Bank interface

The finite K17 oracle facts end with Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.2.  An
all-dimensional split-G1 proof would have to establish the following new
rows uniformly over a quantified parent class.

1. **Extensive exposure.**  At least

   \[
                    |V_0|\ge\alpha\Phi-b_0,
                    \qquad\alpha>0,                  \tag{5.1}
   \]

   source tasks have authenticated crossed split-G1 candidate families.
   The K17 H/S source cover gives only at least one labelled source in a
   target child and does not imply (5.1).
2. **Joint module enrichment and selection.**  Crossed source records extend
   to complete bounded terminal modules in one common child.  Either an exact
   joint set-packing theorem or a sufficient condition such as (4.2) selects
   \(g\ge|V_0|-C_H\) modules with \(C_H=O(1)\).  In a phase-permutation
   construction, either the physical group orbits of Theorem 3.1 have
   uniformly bounded size or a direct protected integral-rotor theorem
   fuses the selected larger orbits with no accumulating sidecar.
3. **Literal closure.**  All helper tickets, donor casualties, endpoints,
   addresses, histories, reset, residence, upper, common-cap and compiler
   incidences are replayed simultaneously.  Shared state is retained as an
   exact capacity gadget; uncounted shared unit resources are forbidden.
4. **Service expansion, casualties and accounting.**  In the one fixed
   child claim gammoid, let \(q\) be the number of compensation claims and
   \(p\) the rank of the combined compensation/gain claim set.  The selected
   bank has a compensation anchor with defects
   \(\beta,\gamma=O(1)\), anchored gain expansion \(\eta\), and the exact
   same-child inequalities

   \[
   \begin{aligned}
      p&\ge q-\beta+\eta g-\gamma,\\
      c_{\rm ns}&\le\chi g+b_{\rm ns},\qquad \eta>\chi,\\
      \Phi'&\le\Phi+q-p+c_{\rm ns}.
   \end{aligned}                                      \tag{5.2}
   \]
5. **Regeneration.**  The fully replayed child, together with only an
   \(O(1)\) boundary sidecar, belongs to the same authenticated parent class
   and exposes the same uniform constants on one compatible spine.

### Theorem 5.1 (conditional split-G1 regeneration implication)

If rows 1--5 hold, then one transition satisfies

\[
 \Phi'\le
 [1-\alpha(\eta-\chi)]\Phi
 +(\eta-\chi)(b_0+C_H)+\beta+\gamma+b_{\rm ns}.       \tag{5.3}
\]

With regenerated steps \(i=0,\ldots,N-1\), put

\[
 \lambda_i=\alpha_i(\eta_i-\chi_i),\qquad
 K_i=(\eta_i-\chi_i)(b_{0,i}+C_{H,i})
       +\beta_i+\gamma_i+b_{{\rm ns},i}.              \tag{5.4}
\]

If \(0<\lambda_i\le1\), then

\[
 \Phi_N\le
 \Phi_0\prod_{i=0}^{N-1}(1-\lambda_i)
 +\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}K_j
       \prod_{i=j+1}^{N-1}(1-\lambda_i).              \tag{5.5}
\]

#### Proof

The joint selection row supplies the fixed coherent terminal bank required
by the private-claim strict-gammoid theorem.  The anchored expansion gives
\(p\ge q-\beta+\eta g-\gamma\).  Substitution with (5.1), (5.2), and the
complete potential row gives (5.3); iteration gives (5.5). \(\square\)

Theorem 5.1 is an implication, not an all-dimensional instance.  The new G2
zero improves none of \(\alpha,C_H,\eta,\chi,\beta,\gamma\) unconditionally
and may reduce the available candidate density.  No live crossed-G1 K17
module, extensive private bank, supplier corridor system or regenerated
parent is known.
