## 1. Verdict and the required wording correction

Let

```text
q=sum_(ell=1)^(r-1) alpha_ell v_ell,
A_ell=W alpha_ell,
A_0=W-sum_(ell>=1)A_ell.
```

The equations in
`MATH_THEOREM_AGGREGATE_MONOTONE_ROTOR_SEMIGROUP_AND_BUFFER_ROUNDING_20260801.md`
correctly prove that the canonical integral vector `A` is, under the stated
buffer hypothesis, in the semigroup generated by

```text
e_a                                      (0<=a<=d),
g_(a,b)=q_(a,b)e_a+p_(a,b)e_b            (0<=a<d<b<r),
q_(a,b)=C(b-a-1,d-a),
p_(a,b)=C(b-a-1,d-a-1).
```

The identity

```text
(d-a)q_(a,b)=(b-d)p_(a,b)
```

and the two-buffer proofs are exact.  The output is an integer union of
uniform **age-type** rotor packages with exactly

```text
sum_ell A_ell=W
```

**type-state occurrences** and with the exact required number of abstract
marked slots at every rank.

The conductor theorem also uses the standing hypothesis that `A` comes
from `W M_(r,d)`, in particular `L>=H` (and the displayed nonnegative
coordinates with total mass `W`).  Its short statement should not be read
as applying to an arbitrary nonnegative vector satisfying only the four
buffer lower bounds; the proof explicitly starts with `E=L-H>=0` inherited
from the monotone-polytope context.

However, the phrase “inside the original `W` owner rows” is not justified.
The exact safe wording is:

> the decomposition has total integral **age-type/signature mass** `W`.

It is not yet a literal labelled multicycle of that mass, much less a
one-copy owner factor.  The biregular labelled-lift lemma is fractional.
Clearing its denominators realizes an integer multiple of a package, and
therefore an integer multiple of `A`; it does not show that one copy of the
semigroup generator has a labelled lift.

This is not merely a logical possibility.  Take

```text
r=4, d=2, a=1, b=3.
```

The mixed rotor has the two type states

```text
c=(1,2,1),       c'=(2,1,1),
```

and generator `g_(1,3)=e_1+e_3`.  Suppose these two type occurrences had a
two-state literal same-owner lift, with labelled cells `C_i,C'_i`.  From
`c->c'`, cardinality forces

```text
C'_1=C_0.
```

From `c'->c`, cardinality forces

```text
C_2=C'_1.
```

Hence `C_2=C_0`, contradicting that the nonempty cells of one labelled
state are disjoint.  Thus this primitive mixed generator has no one-copy
labelled cycle.  Repetition can remove the obstruction, exactly as the
fractional biregular lift predicts.

There is a second useful scope warning.  The coefficients `q_(a,b)` and
`p_(a,b)` are aggregate coefficients in the suffix-vector basis.  They do
not partition the states of a mixed rotor into owners whose individual mark
sets are literally `v_a` and `v_b`.  A state carries its actual offered
cut ranks; only the sum over the whole uniform rotor equals

```text
q_(a,b)v_a+p_(a,b)v_b.
```

Consequently the next problem must retain the occurrence-labelled flag
slots of each package.  Colouring the basis coordinates `A_ell` alone loses
the relevant correlations.

## 2. Exact aggregate gate that is closed

For one semigroup decomposition, form an age-signature template

```text
R=(U,c,J).
```

Here `U` is the set of rotor-state occurrences, `|U|=W`; `c(u)` is the age
type at `u`; and `J(u)` is the set of marked proper-prefix positions at that
occurrence.  The package decomposition supplies a formal type-level
successor certificate, but this successor is not frozen in the physical
problem: fixed points and primitive necklaces need not have one-copy
labelled lifts.  Put

```text
s_j(c)=c_0+...+c_(j-1).
```

For the Ferrers demand `n_s=C(k,s)-b_s`, the aggregate theorem gives exactly

```text
|{(u,j):j in J(u), s_j(c(u))=s}|=n_s       (1<=s<r).    (2.1)
```

Thus the following gate is closed:

> **Integral aggregate type-rotor gate.**  The canonical rank-count vector
> has an integer stationary age-type rotor template of total type-occurrence
> mass `W`, with zero residual in every rank count (2.1).

This is stronger than the earlier rational theorem only at the quotient
count level.  It does not supply a one-copy labelled lift, name owners or
targets, or turn the type necklaces into one owner-simple factor.

The theorem file proves the buffered criterion for all sufficiently large
`k`.  Its O3 audit reports that the sufficient condition first passes at
`k=23`, changes truth value through `k=30`, and passes for every tested
`31<=k<=1000`.  That finite scan alone does not prove the universal sharp
threshold “every `k>=31`”; such a statement requires a separate analytic
monotonicity/bound proof beyond `1000` (or an independently proved exact
threshold theorem).

## 3. The weakest literal named-colouring theorem still missing

The correct successor is a factor-level theorem; Hamiltonicity may remain
separate.  The following formulation keeps exactly the information erased
by the aggregate quotient.

### Named rotor flag-colouring theorem (NRFC; open)

For the canonical Ferrers data, there exists a semigroup type template
`R=(U,c,J)` satisfying (2.1), a successor permutation `tau` of `U`, together
with nonempty source letters `B_u subseteq [k]` and age cells

```text
C_(u,0),...,C_(u,d)
```

such that:

1. the cells are pairwise disjoint, `|C_(u,i)|=c_i(u)`, and

   ```text
   T_u=dotunion_(i=0)^d C_(u,i)
   ```

   has rank `r`;
2. `u -> T_u` is a bijection from `U` to `C([k],r)`;
3. `B_u=C_(u,0)` and the literal shift recurrence holds on every selected
   transition:

   ```text
   C_(tau(u),i+1)=C_(u,i)-B_(tau(u))           (0<=i<d). (3.1)
   ```

   Equivalently,

   ```text
   T_(tau(u))=B_(tau(u)) union (T_u-C_(u,d)).           (3.2)
   ```

   In particular `c_(i+1)(tau(u))<=c_i(u)`, but the new `tau` may fuse or
   rethread different primitive packages; it need not equal their formal
   type-level rotations;

4. every marked occurrence carries its literal nested target

   ```text
   P_(u,j)=C_(u,0) union ... union C_(u,j-1),            (3.3)
   ```

   and, for each rank `s`, the sets (3.3) with
   `s_j(c(u))=s` are pairwise distinct.  In the prescribed-boundary form
   they must equal the declared residual target family

   ```text
   T_s^res=C([k],s)-B_s^bdry,       |B_s^bdry|=b_s.     (3.4)
   ```

Conditions (3.1)--(3.2), rather than the same-owner type inequalities
alone, are essential.  For one fixed owner, the inclusions
`C'_(i+1) subseteq C_i` are sufficient because the common ground set forces
all leftovers into the refreshed class.  When owners change, literal word
semantics requires the exact deletion/refresh recurrence (3.1).  An
arbitrary owner-colouring of compatible type edges need not satisfy it.

NRFC gives a literal owner-simple union of cyclic words and the exact lower
named trace deck.  Requiring `tau` to be one cycle is a strictly stronger
fusion/Hamilton version and is not part of the weakest gate.  Likewise,
upper palettes, source-letter residence, deeper upper witnesses, and the
common compiler cap are extra rows.

If only an open protected incidence bank is produced, its completion to a
spanning two-factor remains governed by the independent Ore--Ryser cuts

```text
sum_(x in S)(2-d_P(x))
 <=sum_y min{2-d_P(y),d_(G-P)(y,S)}
```

for every subset `S` on one shore.  The aggregate semigroup has no
occurrence-incidence information and cannot imply these cuts.  Separate
rankwise Hall matchings also do not imply NRFC: targets at different ranks
belong to the same nested flag (3.3), and all flags are coupled by the
literal transition recurrence (3.1).
