## 1. Frozen input and the first correction

Thread D's adopted ledger has exactly 3,664 rows.  Equality is equality of
the complete binary occurrence-labelled q1 delta key, not equality of a
displayed fingerprint.  The authenticated output is

\[
  3664\text{ rows},\qquad 3664\text{ exact delta signatures},\qquad
  \max\text{ group size}=1.                              \tag{1.1}
\]

The rebased local-clean partition is

\[
  169426=165713+49+3664,                                  \tag{1.1a}
\]

where the first term is rejected by the combined authenticated semantic-core
filters, the 49 dirty-central banks already have checked exact q1
refutations, and the final term is the clean-anchor exact-build bucket used
here.  Thus 3,664, not the earlier 5,054, is the current unsolved q1 bucket
under this proof library.

Each key records the anchor number, the old and new serialized native state
records of all four changed oriented states, every selected-colour toggle,
and the exact removed and added atom sets.  Across the ledger the recorded
per-case ranges are

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 &|\Delta\mathrm{out}|&|\Delta\mathrm{in}|&
 |\Delta\mathrm{colour}|&|\Delta^-A|&|\Delta^+A|\\ \hline
 \min&14&14&3&6&4\\
 \max&89&89&19&168&180.
\end{array}                                               \tag{1.2}
\]

This proves that **literal exact-delta reuse gives no compression**.  It
does not prove that there are 3,664 boundary behaviours.  The anchor id is
itself a key field, and the tail/head state indices live in the corresponding
anchor context.  Interior-only differences may disappear after projection
to a common exterior interface.  At the opposite extreme, the projected
languages could all still be different.  Neither conclusion follows from
(1.1).

Lane A's boundary-language theorem supplies exact gluing and a complete
functional-Hall cylinder alternative once a proof-grade final-provider
atlas and an interface have been fixed.  It explicitly does not supply a
small interface or a small cylinder cover.  Those are the two rows audited
below.

## 2. Proof-grade common occurrence universe

Let \({\cal D}\) be the 3,664 cases and let \(B_\delta\) be the native q1
system of case \(\delta\).  Before intersections of exteriors are meaningful,
transport every case to one immutable occurrence universe.  A sufficient
state key contains

\[
 (\text{base},\text{side},\text{orientation},\text{cut data},
   \text{complete ordered owner trace}).                 \tag{2.1}
\]

The transported delta must be the **whole** anchor-plus-partner delta
relative to round02.  An anchor-relative partner delta alone is not a
cross-anchor common-exterior object.

Put in \(W_0\) every state, atom, row, clause or incidence which changes in
at least one transported case.  If literal invariance of an object is not
certified, put it in \(W_0\).  Let \(W\supseteq W_0\) be any chosen variable
closure and retain the complement as the literal common exterior.

Define \(\Gamma(W)\) to consist of every typed object meeting both sides:

1. tail, head and selected-colour capacity rows;
2. orientation pairs;
3. every genuine protected q1 unit-capacity resource; and
4. every protected Boolean state occurring in a cross-boundary clause.

A boundary signature records the residual capacities, chosen orientations,
immutable endpoint occurrences and protected Boolean values on
\(\Gamma(W)\).  Fixed selected seam atoms may first be contracted.  A collar,
upper witness, topology socket or compiler state may be contracted only if
it is literally fixed and all of its cross effects are also fixed.

### Theorem 2.1 (exact semantic boundary factorization)

Let \(\sigma_I\) and \(\sigma_E\) be the proposed signature maps on the
side-legal interior and exterior selections.  Restriction and gluing through
these signatures are sound and complete if and only if the truth value of
the **conjunction** of all crossing constraints is constant on every product
of signature fibres

\[
 \sigma_I^{-1}(i)\times\sigma_E^{-1}(e),                 \tag{2.2}
\]

and the accepted fibre pairs are exactly the declared complementary pairs.

#### Proof

If the crossing conjunction is constant on (2.2), it defines a relation on
signature pairs.  By the second hypothesis this relation is complementarity,
so complementary side-legal selections glue and no noncomplementary pair
does.  Conversely, if restriction and gluing are exact, two pairs in the
same product of fibres cannot have different legality: the signature rule
would give both the same verdict.  The accepted fibre pairs are therefore
exactly the complementary ones. \(\square\)

### Corollary 2.2 (typed incidence separator, sufficient form)

If \(\Gamma(W)\) records every shared capacity, orientation and protected
state through which a crossing factor is incident, then deleting those
recorded factor incidences separates the two sides of the native factor
graph, and Theorem 2.1 applies.

This structural separator is sufficient, not necessary.  A crossing clause
may be entailed by one side, or an omitted incidence may be determined by
the retained coordinates.  A claimed lower bound from an omitted coordinate
therefore needs an **irredundancy witness**: two side-legal selections with
the same retained signature but different compatibility with a legal
selection on the other side.

Theorem 2.1 is the precise semantic common-exterior condition; Corollary 2.2
is its proof-safe syntactic implementation.  Provider counts, owner masks
and equality of the five numbers in (1.2) certify neither one.

## 3. Exact response languages and the three quantifiers

Let \(\Sigma_\Gamma\) be the finite signature space.  Let
\(E_P\subseteq\Sigma_\Gamma\) be the signatures realized by common-exterior
selections satisfying all exterior-only constraints of the protected q1
system.  For case
\(\delta\), let \(I_\delta\) be its realizable interior signatures and put

\[
 R_\delta=\{e\in\Sigma_\Gamma:
       e\text{ is complementary to some }i\in I_\delta\}. \tag{3.1}
\]

Theorem 2.1 and Lane A's gluing theorem give

\[
       B_\delta\text{ is boundary-feasible}
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad E_P\cap R_\delta\ne\varnothing.
                                                               \tag{3.2}
\]

There are three different statements:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \text{some case works}
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 E_P\cap\bigcup_{\delta\in{\cal D}}R_\delta\ne\varnothing,\\
 \text{every case works, with its exterior allowed to vary}
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 E_P\cap R_\delta\ne\varnothing\quad(\delta\in{\cal D}),\\
 \text{one selected exterior works for every case}
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 E_P\cap\bigcap_{\delta\in{\cal D}}R_\delta\ne\varnothing.
                                                               \tag{3.3}
\end{aligned}
\]

The last line is strictly stronger.  It is not required for the existential
same-parity host: one oriented complete child and its reflected certificate
suffice.  Nor is it required merely because the **region** called the
exterior is common.  A common selected exterior is relevant only if a proof
or construction explicitly demands one simultaneous continuation.  In the
last line, an element \(e\in E_P\) is first a common boundary signature.
Choose any exterior selection realizing \(e\); Theorem 2.1 makes its crossing
compatibility depend only on \(e\), so that one actual selection works for
all the displayed interiors.

## 4. The exact continuation quotient

The coarsest quotient relevant to the fixed exterior is

\[
 \delta\equiv_E\delta'
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 E_P\cap R_\delta=E_P\cap R_{\delta'}.                   \tag{4.1}
\]

If (4.1) fails, a signature in the symmetric difference distinguishes the
two cases.  If it holds, no selection of the retained exterior can
distinguish them for the boundary-feasibility question.  This is the exact
continuation, or Myhill--Nerode, quotient.

Computing (4.1) may be as hard as the remaining decision problem.  A
proof-safe finer quotient is obtained by canonically serializing the full
boundary transition relation and the occurrence-labelled residual
provider/master incidence, modulo typed isomorphisms fixing every boundary
and protected resource pointwise.  Equality of these structural signatures
permits reuse of every exterior blocker, guarded minor and Hall cylinder.
Displayed hashes are only indices; equality must be checked on the complete
canonical serialization.

The 3,664 distinct delta keys give no positive lower bound on the size of
this quotient.  For scale only:

* if all cases induce the same response language, their additional abstract
  interface cost can be zero;
* if they induce 3,664 distinct single binary boundary states, at least
  \(\lceil\log_2 3664\rceil=12\) abstract bits are needed; and
* if only 3,664 distinct subsets of \(\{0,1\}^b\) are asserted, counting
  gives merely \(b\ge4\), since
  \(2^{2^3}<3664\le2^{2^4}\).

None of these three premises is established by (1.1).  Moreover, an
arbitrary binary recoding need not be available.  For \(e,e'\in E_P\), write
\(e\sim e'\) when, for every case \(\delta\) and every realizable
\(i\in I_\delta\), \(e\) is complementary to \(i\) if and only if \(e'\) is.
For native coordinates \(J\subseteq\Gamma(W)\), exactness is

\[
 e,e'\in E_P,quad e|_J=e'|_J
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad e\sim e'.                     \tag{4.2}
\]

The minimum \(|J|\) satisfying (4.2) can exceed the logarithm of the abstract
quotient size.

## 5. What “bounded interface” must mean

At fixed \(k=17\), every interface and every cover is finite.  Taking
\(W\) to be the whole formula even gives an empty exterior interface.  This
is vacuous.  A nontrivial protected-host statement must be uniform in the
ambient dimension and must control the work hidden inside \(W\).

Call the family **\((s,b,r)\)-localized** if there is a proof-grade closure
\(W\) such that, in a declared immutable physical-support norm,

\[
 |W|\le s,\qquad |\Gamma(W)|\le b                          \tag{5.1}
\]

and, after fixing the boundary, the interior orientation/head--colour master
has at most \(r\) finite-domain coordinates in one common typed atlas.
Coordinates inactive in one case receive a distinguished inactive symbol,
and a full boundary/master word determines that case's residual predecessor
graph.  The logical cylinder count below depends on \(b+r\); the support
bound \(s\) is separately load-bearing for a bounded protected owner/module
charge.

### Theorem 5.1 (bounded interface implies bounded cylinder count)

Suppose the common atlas has coordinate domains
\({\cal A}_1,\ldots,{\cal A}_n\), with \(n\le b+r\) and
\(|{\cal A}_j|\le q\).  If every member of an \((s,b,r)\)-localized family is
q1-UNSAT under the complete boundary-conditioned functional-Hall reduction,
then it has a common Hall-cylinder cover with

\[
              K\le\prod_{j=1}^n|{\cal A}_j|
                \le q^{b+r}.                             \tag{5.2}
\]

#### Proof

Use one full-word cylinder for every word in the common atlas.  In each fixed
case a nonempty cylinder determines one residual graph.  Lane A's theorem
gives that graph a deficient shore, because the case is UNSAT under the
complete reduction.  Empty cylinders need no shore.  The displayed count is
the number of full words. \(\square\)

The shores in this proof may depend on both the case and the full word.
Thus bounded \(K\) does not bound the total proof payload, which may contain
\(|{\cal D}|K\) shore descriptions.  Genuine reusable compression needs
typed shared-shore templates/transports.  Nor may bounded master dimension
be replaced by a bounded list of shore names: a shore's deficiency locus can
require exponentially many cylinders.  A valid alternative hypothesis is a
uniformly bounded family of **cylinder--shore templates** whose deficiency
loci already cover all traces.

The converse warning is essential: bounded per-case delta support does not
bound either the union interface or the residual master dimension.

There is also a direct interface obstruction.  Suppose cases expose atoms
incident with pairwise distinct exterior capacities \(q_1,\ldots,q_t\).
For every \(j\), require an irredundancy witness: two side-legal exterior
selections with every other retained boundary datum fixed respectively leave
and consume \(q_j\), and one side-legal interior selection glues to the first
but not the second.
Every exact native interface records all \(q_j\).  Otherwise two exterior
selections differing only at an omitted \(q_j\) have the same signature,
while the corresponding atom glues to only one of them.  Hence

\[
                         |\Gamma(W)|\ge t.                \tag{5.3}
\]

This proves that a union of constant-size local deltas can have a linearly
growing common interface.  Even without independent exterior capacities,
\(t\) private changed occurrences force \(|W|\ge t\).  No corresponding
independent-capacity or unavoidable-support family has yet been extracted
from the present 3,664 ledger.

## 6. Common Hall cylinders

Lane A Section 4 defines the exterior language.  Its Section 8 cylinders
cover integral master spaces, not the exterior by itself.  The distinction
must be kept literal.

For case \(\delta\), let \(\Omega_\delta\) consist of compatible pairs
\((e,p)\), where \(e\in E_P\) and \(p\) is an interior
orientation/head--colour master.  Let \(G_{\delta,e,p}\) be the remaining
predecessor graph and write \(G_\omega\) for it when
\(\omega=(e,p)\).  Fix a finite complete common coordinate atlas
\({\cal A}=\prod_h{\cal A}_h\), padding coordinates inactive in a case by a
distinguished symbol, and maps

\[
             \pi_\delta:\Omega_\delta\longrightarrow{\cal A} \tag{6.1}
\]

such that a full word determines the selected shore universe and residual
graph of that case.  A reusable cylinder is the inverse image of a partial
assignment \(\alpha\) under these maps.  It is Hall-closed for case
\(\delta\) if some occurrence-labelled shore \(X_{\delta,\alpha}\) is
selected throughout the cylinder and

\[
 |N_{G_{\delta,e,p}}(X_{\delta,\alpha})|
       <|X_{\delta,\alpha}|                               \tag{6.2}
\]

for every \((e,p)\in C_\delta(\alpha)\).  The shore may depend on
the case; using one shore across cases requires an explicit typed transport.

### Theorem 6.1 (complete finite common-cover alternative)

Assume that the boundary-conditioned functional-Hall reduction is complete
and that (6.1) is a complete finite atlas.  Then every case is q1-UNSAT if
and only if there are partial assignments
\(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_K\) such that, for every case \(\delta\),

\[
 \Omega_\delta\subseteq\bigcup_{j=1}^K C_\delta(\alpha_j) \tag{6.3}
\]

and the exact shore quantifiers are

\[
 \forall\delta\ \forall j\quad
 C_\delta(\alpha_j)\ne\varnothing\Longrightarrow
 \exists X_{\delta j}\ \forall\omega\in C_\delta(\alpha_j):
 \quad X_{\delta j}\text{ is selected and deficient in }G_\omega.
                                                               \tag{6.4}
\]

The least such \(K\), denoted \(\tau_{\rm common}\), is the exact common
cylinder-cover number for this atlas.

#### Proof

If (6.3)--(6.4) hold, every integral trace of every case violates Hall, so
completeness of the reduction makes every case q1-UNSAT.  Conversely, take
all full words of \({\cal A}\) as cylinders.  In each fixed case a nonempty
full-word cylinder determines one residual graph.  Since that case is
q1-UNSAT, the complete reduction and Hall's theorem give a deficient shore
for the graph.  This supplies (6.4) and a finite common cover. \(\square\)

Condition (6.4) permits a case-dependent shore.  Requiring one transported
shore per cylinder has the strictly stronger quantifiers

\[
 \forall j\ \exists X_j\ \forall\delta\
 \ \forall\omega\in C_\delta(\alpha_j),                 \tag{6.5}
\]

and needs an explicit typed identification of the shore resources across
cases.

Thus Lane A's theorem proves completeness of a finite certificate system,
not that \(\tau_{\rm common}=O(1)\).

### Proposition 6.2 (sharp failure of common compression)

There are twelve abstract functional-Hall banks on one common set of 3,664
masters in which every bank has a two-cylinder width-one cover but

\[
                         \tau_{\rm common}=3664.           \tag{6.6}
\]

#### Proof

Choose a set \(P\) of any 3,664 distinct masters from \(\{0,1\}^{12}\), and
put \(\Omega_i=P\) for \(i=1,\ldots,12\).  For coordinate \(i\), define bank
\(B_i\) whose three-by-three predecessor graph is, when
\(p_i=0\),

\[
 N(a)=N(b)=\{1\},\qquad N(c)=\{2,3\},                    \tag{6.7}
\]

and, when \(p_i=1\),

\[
 N(a)=\{1,2\},\qquad N(b)=N(c)=\{3\}.                    \tag{6.8}
\]

In (6.7) the only deficient nontrivial shore is \(\{a,b\}\); in (6.8) it
is \(\{b,c\}\).  No shore is deficient in both modes.  Each bank therefore
has the two width-one cylinders \(p_i=0\) and \(p_i=1\).

A cylinder reusable for all coordinate banks cannot contain two different
masters.  Such masters differ in some coordinate \(i\); on that bank the
cylinder contains both modes, for which no one Hall shore is uniformly
deficient.  Every common cylinder contains at most one of the selected 3,664
masters.  Conversely the 3,664 full-word singleton cylinders give a common
cover.  This proves (6.6). \(\square\)

This is a sharp obstruction for the general functional-Hall theorem, not a
claim that the current Boolean ledger realizes this example.  It shows why
small bank-specific covers cannot be combined without an additional Boolean
separator or shared-shore theorem.

## 7. Protected-host scope

The present D key and Lane A theorem concern the native relaxed q1 formula.
They contain no complete signature for

* rank-ten or deeper upper witnesses;
* exact cyclic residence and boundary ages;
* all-width exterior interval-OR windows;
* component topology or the terminal opening; or
* the lower/root/head/graphic terminal compiler.

Consequently, even a bounded q1 interface would not yet be a protected
upper-host interface.  For the protected host, every such cross-boundary
state must be added to \(\Gamma(W)\), and Theorem 2.1 must be rechecked.  The
global-reversal quotient removes the need for a phase-common compiler and
phase-common selected exterior; it does not remove these one-oriented
boundary rows.

## 8. Exact verdict and D--H3 handoff

The current evidence gives the following sharp answer.

\[
 \boxed{\text{distinct exact deltas; bounded exterior quotient remains open}}
                                                               \tag{8.1}
\]

The 3,664 cases have no repeated complete anchor-relative q1 delta.  A
bounded common exterior and a bounded Hall-cylinder cover are neither proved
nor refuted.  Exact-delta uniqueness is strictly finer than boundary-language
equivalence, while Lane A's completeness theorem has no size bound.

A proof-grade next ledger should, without duplicating a q1 solve:

1. transport every anchor-plus-partner case to the immutable round02
   occurrence universe;
2. export the union variable closure \(W\) and every typed coordinate of
   \(\Gamma(W)\);
3. canonically serialize each projected boundary transition relation and
   residual provider/master incidence;
4. report the structural quotient sizes and, for every nonempty
   case--cylinder intersection, its uniform occurrence-labelled Hall shore;
   if reuse is claimed, also export the typed transport or shared template;
   and
5. either prove a dimension-uniform bound on \(|\Gamma(W)|\) together with
   bounded absorbed support and master dimension, or give a uniformly
   bounded family of typed cylinder--shore templates; otherwise export an
   independent-capacity or Hall-fooling family certifying growth.

For the existential H3 host, a positive branch needs only one
\(E_P\cap R_\delta\ne\varnothing\), followed by the protected upper,
residence, topology and compiler checks.  A common cylinder cover is instead
a compressed way to reject all cases.  Conflating those two objectives would
impose an unnecessary universal-exterior requirement on the host.
