# Orientation-free short-run clauses and localization of the voltage pump

Date: 2026-08-02

Status: exact local theorems; exact one-program `k=17` catalogue census;
global factor existence is not claimed.

## 1. Literal setting

Let `F` be a selected undirected degree-two subgraph of a Johnson graph.
For a coordinate `z`, write `chi_z(v)=1[z in v]`.  A positive `z`-run of
length `ell` is a maximal cyclic segment

\[
 v_0v_1\cdots v_{\ell+1},\qquad
 (\chi_z(v_0),\ldots,\chi_z(v_{\ell+1}))
       =(0,1^\ell,0).                                  \tag{1.1}
\]

Put `e_i=v_i v_(i+1)`.  In the quotient master, `p(e)` denotes the primary
variable selecting the full orbit of a nonfixed physical edge; a protected
fixed edge is treated as the constant one.

## 2. Exact forced-segment characterization

### Theorem 2.1 (orientation-free primary blocker)

For `1<=ell<=3`, the literal segment (1.1) occurs in a selected factor if
and only if all of

\[
                         e_0,e_1,\ldots,e_\ell          \tag{2.1}
\]

are selected.  Consequently the clause

\[
       \bigvee_{x\in Q(S)}\neg x,
       \qquad
       Q(S)=\{p(e_i):e_i\text{ is nonfixed}\},          \tag{2.2}
\]

after duplicate variables are removed, is sound and has size at most
`ell+1<=4`.  If `Q(S)` is empty, the protected base itself is impossible.

The clause is independent of the orientation of the component, of the
orientation chosen for every other component, and of the position of the
quotient cut.

#### Proof

Necessity is immediate from (1.1).  Conversely, selection of the path edges
in (2.1) saturates every internal vertex `v_1,...,v_ell` to degree two.
They are therefore consecutive in the selected component, and the fixed
literal memberships in (1.1) make them one maximal short positive run.

Reversal reads the same owner sequence backwards and leaves the edge set and
run length unchanged.  Other components are disjoint.  In a voltage
quotient, traversing a selected edge orbit in either direction fixes the
relative physical phase.  Thus a physical segment projects to the same set
of primary edge-orbit variables in both orientations.  Selecting those
variables develops the offending physical segment again, proving (2.2).
\(\square\)

### Corollary 2.2 (completeness)

Every positive-history violation at depth three falsifies one clause of
size at most four of the form (2.2).  This remains true for a run which
crosses the chosen quotient cut or lies in a zero-voltage component: enumerate
physical cyclic segments before projecting their edge orbits.

There is therefore no phase or voltage counterexample to the local clause
claim.

### Proposition 2.3 (the singleton row is already excluded)

On the exact lower-`q1` face, a positive run of length one is impossible.

#### Proof

If `u-v-w` has statuses `0,1,0`, both Johnson edges have the same lower
facet `v-{z}`.  Selecting both contradicts the exact-one row for that facet.
\(\square\)

Hence the new primary history rows are only:

* a three-literal clause for every `0,1,1,0` segment; and
* a four-literal clause for every `0,1,1,1,0` segment.

These supports are geometrically minimal.  Omitting a boundary edge permits
the positive block to continue outside the displayed segment, while omitting
an internal edge no longer forces the displayed owners to be consecutive.
They need not be prime implicates after resolving against a particular
global base formula: a separate global resource cut can make a proper subset
impossible.  The exact safe term is **minimal primary segment clause**.

## 3. Polynomial does not mean manageable

Let the quotient catalogue have `n` owner orbits, `M` edge orbits and maximum
multidegree `Delta`.  All primary segment clauses can be generated by
enumerating voltage-labelled nonbacktracking walks of lengths two, three and
four and testing the developed membership pattern.  For fixed residence
depth three this takes

\[
                         O(kM\Delta^3)                  \tag{3.1}
\]

arithmetic time after development (or the corresponding
`O(n Delta^4)` direct walk bound).  Thus the family is polynomial in the
catalogue size.

Its output size is nevertheless prohibitive.  An exact `O3` census of the
authenticated marker-58 catalogue used the complete 35,945 quotient rows,
discarded the eight killed quotient loops, developed all 610,929 physical
edges, and fixed coordinate zero.  The free `Z_17` action makes this a unique
representative of every simultaneous coordinate/segment orbit.  It found:

```text

physical catalogue edges                 610,929
coordinate-0 crossing edges               71,874
coordinate-0 present--present edges      287,496
raw length-1 segments                     230,714
raw length-2 segments                  11,959,067
raw length-3 segments                 603,342,034
```

To get a rigorous nonredundant lower calibration, the audit retained only
segments whose primary variables are all nonzero and distinct, whose owner
orbits are all distinct, and whose lower-facet orbits are all distinct.
For such a segment the selected edge set is a simple path in the quotient
multigraph, so its set of variables determines the path up to reversal.
The entry coordinate fixes the unique developed phase.  Distinct retained
segments therefore give distinct clauses, and none is an immediate owner or
facet resource contradiction.  The counts are

```text
distinct resource-compatible 3-clauses    11,694,216
distinct resource-compatible 4-clauses   520,993,962
```

So an explicit primary-only completion would require more than 532 million
clauses and more than 2.1 billion literals before DIMACS overhead.  It is not
a practical `v3` master.

The correct use of Theorem 2.1 is one of:

1. lazy separation: replay an incumbent, extract each bad segment, and add
   its sound three- or four-primary clause;
2. the existing polynomial extended formulation with directed darts and
   three-step history states; or
3. a smaller reachability/automaton extended formulation which is proved
   propagation-complete.

The theorem proves completeness of lazy primary separation; it does not make
the full primary clause list compact.

## 4. Voltage can be localized to one pump

Let `(G,delta)` be a voltage graph over an abelian group `A`.  For an
oriented walk `P`, let `omega(P)` be the signed sum of its edge voltages.

### Theorem 4.1 (zero-current rethread preserves global voltage)

Suppose an oriented quotient cycle is cut into retained oriented paths

\[
                         P_0,P_1,\ldots,P_{t-1}.         \tag{4.1}
\]

Assume its old seams and a proposed new seam system satisfy:

1. the new seams reconnect the same oriented paths into one quotient cycle;
2. no retained path is reversed; and
3. the total signed seam current is unchanged:

\[
       \sum_i\omega(s_i')=\sum_i\omega(s_i).            \tag{4.2}
\]

Then the old and new cycles have the same total voltage.

#### Proof

In either cycle every retained path occurs once with the same orientation.
Since `A` is abelian, their order is irrelevant.  Hence

\[
 \omega(C')-\omega(C)
 =\sum_i(\omega(s_i')-\omega(s_i))=0.                  \tag{4.3}
\]

\(\square\)

### Corollary 4.2 (fixed-`z`, `h=0` heptagons are voltage-neutral)

Orient the old and new fixed-`z` seams in the retained-path direction as

\[
                   y_i\longrightarrow x_i,\qquad
                   y_i\longrightarrow x_{i+1}.         \tag{4.4}
\]

In the phasewise fixed-`z` representatives these seams have zero intrinsic
shift.  After any consistent endpoint gauge change `g`, their directed seam
sums are

\[
 \sum_i\bigl(g(x_i)-g(y_i)\bigr),\qquad
 \sum_i\bigl(g(x_{i+1})-g(y_i)\bigr).                 \tag{4.5}
\]

They agree because `i mapsto i+1` permutes the seven moving endpoints.
Equivalently, in the usual local gauge the new seam increments sum to the
moving holonomy `h`, so the fixed-`z`, `h=0` circuit has zero directed seam
displacement.  If the retained paths satisfy the odd step-two one-cycle
planting criterion, Theorem 4.1 applies and the central heptagon preserves
the quotient-cycle voltage exactly.  The label `z` is needed for the cap
rotor, not for the telescoping identity (4.5).

Consequently, once a connected quotient factor has coprime total voltage,
any sequence of such topology-safe zero-current repairs preserves coprime
voltage.  Only the initial construction, or one exceptional pump, has to use
a twisted nonzero-holonomy ticket.  The large twisted-anchor load is not
needed for every later residence or upper repair.

For `Z_17`, preserving any nonzero total voltage preserves a connected
physical lift.

### Exact qualification

Protecting a single edge of nonzero voltage is not enough: other cycle edges
can cancel it.  What must be protected is the **total cycle voltage after the
pump**, together with the invariant that every subsequent exchange satisfies
Theorem 4.1.

Nor does the scalar statement `h=0` alone guarantee safety.  If a rethread
reverses a retained path, or reconnects the paths into several quotient
components, its voltage change includes respectively

\[
 -2\omega(P_i)
\]

or ceases to be one global cycle voltage.  This is the precise topology/order
failure.  The fixed-`z` atlas becomes a voltage-local repair family only on
the authenticated step-two host face (or another face explicitly checking
the same-orientation one-cycle condition).  Moreover, (4.5) audits only the
central heptagon.  Every cap/history/aperture/join sidecar seam belongs in
the complete-ticket displacement ledger; a completed ticket is neutral only
when that total displacement is zero.

## 5. Consequence for the next master

Two simplifications are proof-safe:

1. Residence CEGAR cuts may be emitted directly on the at most four primary
   edge orbits of the literal bad segment; they exclude both component
   orientations at once.
2. The nonzero-voltage machinery may be isolated to one protected pump.
   Subsequent fixed-`z`, cap-exact `h=0` repairs need only carry their
   residence, upper, source/compiler and topology histories together with
   zero **total** sidecar displacement; no new central voltage choice is
   required.

Neither statement removes the global host-spread problem.  In particular,
the second statement is conditional on every local switch preserving one
quotient cycle with the retained paths in the same orientations.
