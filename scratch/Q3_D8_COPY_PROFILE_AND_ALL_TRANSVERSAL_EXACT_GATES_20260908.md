# Copied partners retain global fractional room, but fixed translations do not

2026-09-08. Two successive bounded h100 LPs, each with matching
unscaled exact rational primal and dual certificates. No integer
search ran. These results distinguish arbitrary copied partners
from copies coupled by one fixed F_2^3 translation structure.

## 1. The global S8 profile family has exact optimum 2364

Two full shore paths may have the same four-coordinate Dyck
histogram profile, with independent endpoint modes 0..8, 1..8,
0..7, or 1..7. There are fourteen profiles and ten unordered pairs
of endpoint modes, hence 140 columns. The target orbits under S8
are the 45 coordinate histograms, with their exact multinomial
demands.

The fourteen profiles were generated by Dyck recursion and checked
independently against actual four-coordinate words. Every incidence
column was obtained by summing the literal shore histograms at all
retained rank pairs. No target histogram was omitted.

The LP optimum is exactly

    K_S8 = 2364.                                     (1)

The exact primal has thirteen nonzero entries; the exact dual has
nine. Both rational scaling factors are one. The margin to the
improvement gate is

    76545/32 - 2364 = 897/32.                        (2)

This optimum also applies to the smaller family in which the two
full paths are literal copies under a coordinate bijection. Every
same-profile column is realized by choosing a copied pair, and
uniform S8 development makes its histogram incidence into an
actual fractional cover. Conversely every copied pair has the same
profile on both shores. Thus no fractional barrier here forces
copied partners themselves to be abandoned.

The fractional result supplies no integral cover. A bank with
different shore bijections in different rows need not respect any
single F_2^3 translation structure.

## 2. Pure lower bound explaining the six-unit profile penalty

Use the universal balanced projection dual

    y(x)=(1/4)*#{a : sum_(i != a) x_i=7},

of total weight 2358. Every endpoint-mode row retains ranks 1..7
on both shores and is tight for that dual.

Only the profile that opens all four coordinates before closing
any can contain a seven-one rank-seven target. In the same-profile
family, every row of this profile contains exactly two such targets
and also the central all-ones target. All other profile rows contain
neither. Hence

    y'(x)=y(x)+1_{x has seven ones and one zero}
               -2*1_{x is all ones}                 (3)

is nonnegative and has the same row load as y on every permitted
row. Its total weight is 2358+8-2=2364. This gives a pure lower
bound matching (1). Equivalently, the eight seven-one targets force
at least four total rows of the all-opening profile and therefore
at least four occurrences of the all-ones target, whose projection
weight is two.

The saved LP primal, indexed by profile in opening-first Dyck DFS
order and then lexicographic unordered mode pair, is

    6:4, 19:24, 27:1, 28:10, 39:8, 44:1, 49:12,
    59:47, 76:6, 79:11/2, 99:8, 113:8, 139:63/2.

These are weights of averaged row families, not selected physical
rectangles.

## 3. All translation transversals give exact optimum 7504/3

Now fix E=F_2^3 and require a row's full shores to be C and C+v,
where its four-point shore A satisfies A+v=E minus A. Both endpoint
modes remain independent. Unlike the earlier affine-only family,
allow every such four-point shore.

There are exactly two affine-group types of shores:

* Fourteen affine planes. Each has four complementing translations.
  Normalize to A={0,1,2,3}, v=4.
* Fifty-six non-affine four-sets. Each is an affine-independent
  tetrahedron and has the unique complementing translation
  v=xor(A). Normalize to A={0,1,2,4}, v=7.

The full AGL(3,2) group was generated explicitly. Its images of the
two normalized shores were checked to partition all seventy
four-subsets. Every complementing translation was checked. The
non-affine shore stabilizer has size 24 and induces all S4
permutations of its vertices; the subgroup fixing its first axis
has size six. Thus it has the same 105 canonical word types and
ten unordered endpoint-mode pairs as the affine case.

Adding these 1050 non-affine columns to the 1050 affine columns
gives the complete 2100-column family. Targets are the same sixty
actual AGL orbits, rather than the coarser 45 histograms. The exact
LP optimum is

    K_translation = 7504/3.                         (4)

The exact primal has eighteen nonzero entries and the dual eight.
All 2100 column inequalities and all sixty target-demand
inequalities pass exactly, with no scaling and no primal-dual gap.
The deficit to the gate is

    7504/3 - 76545/32 = 10493/96.                    (5)

Consequently every integral endpoint-mode cover whose partner
maps are translations of this fixed F_2^3 structure has charge at
least 7504/3. Non-affine shores do not rescue that restriction.
This does not contradict (1), which permits arbitrary coordinate
bijections between shores in different rows.
