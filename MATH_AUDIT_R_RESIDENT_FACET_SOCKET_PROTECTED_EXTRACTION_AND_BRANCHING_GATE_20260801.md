## 2. Decisive correction: complete residence guards

The original draft encoded only the staircase inequalities.  That was
false as an exact guard criterion.  Already for `h=1`, an order can satisfy
the staircase SDR while an incoming one-step `v_0` run is terminated by the
first socket zero.

Let
\[
C_L=\{x:\ell(x)\in\{0\}\cup[h+1,\infty)\},\qquad
C_R=\{x:\rho(x)\in\{0\}\cup[h+1,\infty)\}.
\]
One must first require `X\setminus U\subset C_L\cap C_R`.  The exact omitted-label
lists are then
\[
\begin{aligned}
B_0&=U\cap R_1\cap C_L,\\
B_h&=U\cap L_1\cap C_R,\\
B_j&=U\cap L_{h+1-j}\cap R_{j+1}\quad(1\le j<h).
\end{aligned}
\]
Assuming the two exterior pieces are themselves internally resident, under
the outside-`U` cleanliness condition the compact socket is resident across
both boundaries if and only if these lists have distinct representatives.
This follows coordinate by coordinate from the corrected local
resident-socket theorem.

## 3. Scope that remains open

The exact thirty-macro `m=9` bank shows that a correlated exterior
selection can close its immediate child cascade.  It is a distinct
three-facet/two-seam `L=3` macro family, not an instance of the present
four-facet/three-seam `h=3` compact socket.  Its surviving deeper upper
holes show that even this nearby finite success is not an all-depth
regenerative theorem.  A general positive result must still supply, after
every reachable history:

- exact colour-by-colour casualty restoration;
- occurrence-clean guards and bounded-load owner/lower menus;
- a contracting weighted child matching;
- graphic chronology and every deeper-shadow witness; and
- one common-`Q` source/cap matching for the literal compiler.

Thus the corrected report isolates a genuine correlated coloured-absorber
gate.  No conclusion `nu(k)=B(k)+O(1)` follows without that gate.
