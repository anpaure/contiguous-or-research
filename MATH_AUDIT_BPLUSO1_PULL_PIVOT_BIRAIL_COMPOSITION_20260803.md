# Independent audit of the pull-clock--pivot--birail route to `B+O(1)`

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** `NO-GO` for the unconditional composition; `GO` for the six
ingredients only in the scoped forms stated below.  The earliest live missing
implication is the passage from the stationary fractional pull clock to one
fixed-host, one-copy, occurrence-labelled connected chronology.  A stale
pivot theorem also contains a later, explicit false inference about upper
colours; its corrected successor removes that inference.

No finite search or finite-model evidence is used in this audit.

## 1. Executive verdict

The proposed chain has the form

\[
 \text{stationary pull law}
 \Longrightarrow \text{pivot-rich literal host}
 \Longrightarrow \text{aligned birail sequence}
 \Longrightarrow \text{physical zero-block compiler}
 \Longrightarrow \text{bounded terminal repair}
 \Longrightarrow B(k)+O(1).
\]

The first arrow is not proved.  The stationary theorem supplies a rational
circulation in a convex hull of literal trace atoms, after averaging over
owners, private sets, orders, omitted cores, and payload tables.  It does not
supply a point of one fixed physical candidate matrix, an integral choice of
one atom per owner and target, a connected Euler support, or prescribed
canonical pull occurrences.  In particular,

\[
 \mu\in\operatorname{conv}\!\left(\bigcup_H P_H\right)
 \quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
 \exists H:\ \mu_H\in P_H\cap\mathbb Z^E,
\]

and the later local theorems do not change this quantifier order.

The exact missing interface has already been isolated as three nonformal
arrows:

\[
 \text{stationary trace law}
 \xrightarrow{\text{one-host literal pushforward}}
 \text{fractional ticket point}
 \xrightarrow{\text{integralization}}
 \text{integral private bank}
 \xrightarrow{\text{Euler fusion}}
 \text{integral chronology}.
\]

Therefore none of the downstream lemmas can be invoked on one common witness
until this interface is constructed.

## 2. Ingredient-by-ingredient audit

### 2.1 Stationary pull clock: valid fractional theorem, not a host theorem

The corrected pull-clock theorem proves a rational stationary circulation on
literal depth-`d` traces with the required rank marginals.  It explicitly
states that integral one-copy/Euler rounding is open.  The pull blocks are
literal atoms, but clearing denominators repeats the owner inventory; it does
not produce a length-`W` one-copy period.

There are two rigorous obstructions to the naive inference.

1. At `k=10`, `W=252` and the canonical block density satisfies

   \[
   x_{1,1}=\frac{605}{7308},\qquad
   W x_{1,1}=\frac{605}{29}\notin\mathbb Z.
   \]

   Hence the displayed canonical histogram cannot be assigned to exactly
   `W` one-copy positions.  This does not rule out a different integral
   architecture; it rules out denominator-clearing followed by ownerwise
   assignment.

2. In the general depth-one rank-two pull-clock family on even `k`, a
   one-copy choice orients every edge of `K_k`.  Balance would require equal
   indegree and outdegree at each vertex, impossible because every degree is
   odd.  A circuit needs at least `k/2` repair edges and an open trail at
   least `k/2-1`.  This is not an asymptotic counterexample on the central
   optimal-depth Boolean slice, but it proves that no bounded-defect rounding
   theorem follows from the stationary-clock axioms alone.

The earliest missing theorem is therefore a role-, resource-, history-, and
balance-preserving map from the clock atoms into one fixed physical host,
followed by integral one-copy rounding and connected fusion.

### 2.2 Pivot aperture and pivot-rich source: valid local block, missing host

The sharp aperture inequality and the displayed geodesic block are correct.
The block preserves old interval-OR values and creates the singleton and two
nested lower rays.  Its compiler conclusion is conditional on released
occurrence-labelled ray targets, a reference matching, and one common
cap/guard state.  Its protected-factor corollary embeds the lifted path in
some owner/lower-`q1` two-factor, but that factor is not a literal source word
containing the insertion block and need not provide upper surjectivity,
bounded component count, exterior residence, or a common cap.

Thus the local statement

\[
 \forall p\ \exists\text{ a pivot block }B_p
\]

and a separate factor-extension statement do not imply the existence of one
physical host containing all prescribed blocks in compatible phases.  The
same-host existential must precede the pivot, factor, cap, and compiler
clauses.

#### Stale false upper-colour inference

`MATH_THEOREM_THREAD_D_PIVOT_RICH_CATALAN_SEED_AND_SAFE_CUT_GATE_20260801.md`
claims that resource-disjoint copies automatically have distinct upper
colours across packets.  In the authoritative sharp theorem,
resource-disjointness separates owner vertices and lower-colour vertices,
not upper unions.

Let `U` have rank `m+1` and take distinct `a,b,c,d in U`.  The two Johnson
edges

\[
 (U-a,U-b),\qquad (U-c,U-d)
\]

have four distinct owners and distinct lower intersections
`U-{a,b}` and `U-{c,d}`, but both have upper colour `U`.  Hence the stale
global upper-independence claim is false for multiple packets.  The corrected
successor theorem states only packetwise upper injectivity and makes global
upper independence an additional hypothesis.

### 2.3 Aligned birail telescoping: valid value identity, not occurrence Hall

The birail identity is a direct telescope in the free abelian group on
literal target values, provided every packet uses the same literal
`B,P_s,S_s` data and the serial old slots are prepared prospectively.  Equal
profile cardinalities, isomorphic flags, or separately relabelled bases do
not suffice.  The quadratic atlas does not itself produce the aligned chain.

Even on an aligned chain, cancellation of signed target multiplicities does
not identify physical cells, preserve a cap, or imply a target-to-cell
matching.  Occurrences of the same value may move to colliding or illegal
addresses.  Therefore

\[
 \sum_i\Delta_s(x_i,x_{i+1})=0
 \quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
 \lambda_d=0\text{ or }O(1).
\]

The proof-safe serial inequality remains

\[
 \nu(k)\le B(k)+u(T_0)+
 \min_{T\in\operatorname{Comp}(T_0)}
       \bigl(\chi(T)+\lambda_d(T)\bigr).
\]

The theorem is useful only after a literal aligned safe component and a
terminal occurrence-Hall certificate are supplied.

### 2.4 Zero-block collapse: valid abstract threshold theorem, missing lift

For integer simple-positive threshold marginals, the abstract identity

\[
 \delta(\pi)=t(\pi)
\]

is correct, and zero defect is equivalent to perfect matchings in the two
cross graphs.  This removes a positive-threshold sorting problem.

It does not construct the cross graphs physically.  A folded cross edge is
a paired ticket using two distinct interval cells, normally in opposite
phase words; it is not one compiler cell.  A physical use requires two
simultaneous addressed cell banks, compatible caps/endpoints, disjointness
from the transported background, and an owner-legal, upper-safe, resident,
length-recycled host.  Full-block background transport is exact only after
an admissible contraction-exact host has already been planted.

Consequently zero-block collapse cannot be inferred from birail target-value
identities or from the existence of two abstract pivot rays.

### 2.5 Bounded eviction: valid deletion lemma, missing bounded damage

Given one final word/cap, a literal task partial matching, and a **complete**
damage set `D` containing every reference-matching cell invalidated by all
changes, the eviction lemma is correct: delete invalid or occupied reference
edges and insert the task edges.

For a proof-safe statement, the task edges must form a matching on both
shores.  If a task target is also an old target, its old reference edge must
be released; the task edge then re-covers it.  Equivalently, with a literal
partial matching `F`, restrict `M_0` away from both `C(F)` and `T(F)`, not
only away from task cells.

The lemma does not prove that the complete damage set is bounded.  Marginal
cell legality does not control remote cap shrinkage, and a sidecar invariant
must bound the total output debt, not add a fresh constant at every step.
Terminal phase decoupling is valid only after an independently legal minus
successor and a complete plus-state terminal certificate exist.

### 2.6 Prescribed-pull extension: valid canonical forest theorem, missing
packet identification

For an already identified labelled set `A` of literal canonical GMN pulls,
`A` extends to a canonical labelled spanning tree if and only if `A` is a
graphic forest (including the exclusion of loops and parallel two-cycles).
This is an edge-level theorem.  It does not protect shared vertices or any
owner, witness, residence, history, cap, or compiler resource.

A pivot/coatom/collar packet is not automatically one of these pulls.
Protected use additionally requires a literal occurrence expansion,
GMN/MMM provenance, compatible complete interfaces, a hereditarily
admissible completion bank, and connectivity after contraction.  A raw
portal forest may map to a loop or parallel pair in the actual auxiliary
graph, or its purported edge may be absent from the selected factor.

Thus prescribed-pull extension is a final topology lemma for already
prepared canonical occurrences; it neither creates the host nor repairs the
first pull-clock integrality gap.

## 3. Earliest invalid/missing implication

The earliest live failure is

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{fractional stationary trace circulation}
 \not\Longrightarrow
 \text{one fixed-host integral connected chronology with prescribed sites}.}
\]

This failure precedes pivot planting, birail alignment, zero-block lifting,
eviction, and canonical pull extension.  It is a genuine missing theorem,
not a matter of accounting.  The `k=10` denominator obstruction disproves
the most direct proposed realization, and the parity family disproves any
generic bounded-defect consequence of the clock axioms.

After that gap is granted, the next missing implications are, in order:

1. local pivot block `=>` one common protected host/cap/phase;
2. signed birail telescope `=>` addressed occurrence matching;
3. abstract zero-block cross pairs `=>` two physical cross-cell banks;
4. marginal task cells `=>` bounded **complete** final damage;
5. raw packet/portal labels `=>` literal canonical GMN pull expansions; and
6. bounded terminal casualties `=>` a nonaccumulating next sidecar on one
   compatible parity spine.

The existing six theorems prove none of these arrows.

## 4. Corrected theorem

### Theorem 4.1 (same-host regenerative composition; conditional)

Fix absolute constants `H` and `C`.  Suppose that for every sufficiently
large dimension on one compatible parity spine (and for each required even
terminal child) there is **one single tuple**

\[
 \mathcal Z_k=(T_k^-,T_k^+,H_k,\pi_k,X_k,A_k,
               \mathcal P_k,M_k^0,F_k,D_k)
\]

for which all of the following hold simultaneously.

1. **One-host integral pull clock.**  `pi_k` is a role- and
   resource-preserving pushforward of the stationary trace atoms into one
   fixed host `H_k`, including every capacity, balance, target, address,
   flag, and carried-history row.  It admits an integral one-copy selection
   `X_k`; after the prescribed topology joins, its support is one chronology.
2. **Bounded pivot bank.**  At most `H` selected sites are literal sharp
   pivot blocks in `T_k^+`.  Their released targets, cap/guard state,
   exterior residence continuations, upper witnesses, and full physical
   footprints are simultaneously legal.
3. **Canonical pull topology.**  The packet labels `A_k` are literal
   canonical GMN pulls forming a graphic forest.  Each carries a valid
   occurrence expansion; complete protected interfaces are jointly
   compatible; and a hereditarily admissible bank has connected contraction.
4. **Literal birail/zero-block realization.**  Every serial telescope is
   aligned on the same literal bases and profiles.  Its abstract cross
   tickets expand to a physical partial matching `F_k`, with both cells of
   every paired ticket present, all left and right endpoints distinct, one
   compatible terminal cap, and disjointness from the transported
   background.  The true uncontracted position charge is `chi_k`.
5. **Complete terminal damage.**  `M_k^0` is a reference matching for the
   old lower bank in the final plus state, and `D_k` contains every cell of
   `M_k^0` whose edge is invalid after all host, packet, cap, guard, and
   compiler changes.  Put

   \[
   M_k'=M_k^0\upharpoonright
     \{e:C(e)\notin D_k\cup C(F_k),\ T(e)\notin T(F_k)\}
   \]

   and let `K_k` be the old lower targets not covered by `M_k' union F_k`.
   Let `U_k` be the complete terminal upper-target casualty set.
6. **Regeneration.**  The minus state `T_k^-` exports exactly the declared
   bounded signature used to construct the next odd state, independently of
   the terminal plus matching.  The output sidecar contains every inherited
   and new casualty and remains in the same bounded state class.
7. **Uniform charge.**  The complete terminal charge obeys

   \[
                 \chi_k+|K_k|+|U_k|\le C.
   \]

Then

\[
                         \nu(k)\le B(k)+C
\]

in every covered dimension.  In particular, uniform `C` gives
`B(k)+O(1)`.

#### Proof

Clause 1 supplies the common literal host and one-copy factor missing from
the fractional clock.  Clause 3 and the prescribed-pull extension connect
the factor without changing the declared complete interfaces.  Clauses 2
and 4 make the pivot and birail tasks literal on that same host; the
zero-block theorem certifies their abstract threshold defect, while the
physical matching `F_k` supplies the occurrence-level realization which the
abstract theorem lacks.

By completeness of `D_k`, every edge of `M_k'` remains legal in the final
plus state.  Its cell and target shores are disjoint from `F_k` by
construction, so `M_k' union F_k` is a matching and misses exactly `K_k` on
the old lower shore.  Append the masks in `K_k union U_k` as literal letters.
Appending destroys no existing witness, so the final length is at most

\[
 B(k)+\chi_k+|K_k|+|U_k|\le B(k)+C.
\]

Clause 6 prevents this terminal repair from accumulating along the spine and
permits the plus and minus compiler matchings/caps to differ.  This proves the
claim.  \(\square\)

### Scope of the corrected theorem

The theorem is deliberately conditional.  The six audited ingredients
discharge only internal parts of clauses 2--5:

* the stationary clock does not discharge clause 1;
* the pivot theorem does not plant the common host in clause 2;
* the telescope does not construct `F_k`;
* zero-block collapse does not lift its cross edges to cells;
* eviction does not bound `D_k`, `K_k`, or regenerate the sidecar; and
* prescribed-pull extension does not construct the literal packet
  expansions or admissible bank in clause 3.

Bounded task birth, upper-decorated bounded-component completion, complete
damage control, and sidecar regeneration remain additional open rows.  No
unconditional `B(k)+O(1)`, `B(k)+1`, or exact new value of `nu(k)` follows.
