# Rooted pentagons: conveyor obstruction and exact PCap capacity

## 0. Outcome

The five-row packet is a genuine new exact atom.  Its old and new target
multisets differ by

\[
                    \Delta_{\rm pent}=2e_3+e_5-e_4-2e_2,
\]

so one packet has three units of negative target mass and three units of
positive target mass.  It crosses the former leaf-rotation partition and
admits arbitrary common one-hole contexts.

Three separate conclusions must nevertheless be distinguished.

1. **No full Catalan conveyor.**  The full common-context graph is still
   disconnected.  There are explicit Dyck words of every semilength
   \(r\ge4\) containing no size-three Dyck factor, hence admitting no
   pentagon move at all.
2. **Dense same-hole packing exists.**  Fringe copies alone partition
   \(1-o(1)\) of \(D_r\) into disjoint five-row packets.  Their ideal raw
   intrinsic capacity is \((3/5+o(1))\operatorname{Cat}_r\), which exceeds
   the numerical plateau demand.
3. **That raw capacity is at the wrong interface.**  A port-preserving
   packet installed strictly inside the size-\(r\) hole fixes the two
   boundary states and cannot split that hole's certified full-window
   plateau.  The directly certified parent-boundary lift has only

   \[
      H_{m,r+1}\operatorname{Cat}_{r-2}
      =\left(\frac1{64}+o(1)\right)
         H_{m,r}\operatorname{Cat}_r
   \]

   packets.  At a fixed protected depth each packet has four nontrivial
   boundary profiles, each of negative mass three.  Even granting all
   twelve units perfect routing, its supply is only
   \((3/16+o(1))H_{m,r}\operatorname{Cat}_r\), whereas the hereditary
   plateau demands between \(1/4\) and \(7/16\) of the same normalization.

Thus the pentagon repairs the local-generator defect but does not by
itself repair Lane G.  The live gate is a dense **parent-aligned** packing,
not further same-hole copies.

## 1. Exact packet ledger

For reference, the old paths are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
1&123&136&146&456\\
2&124&126&156&356\\
3&125&145&345&346\\
4&135&235&245&246\\
5&134&234&236&256,
\end{array}
\]

and the new paths are

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
1&123&126&156&456\\
2&124&234&345&356\\
3&125&235&236&346\\
4&135&136&146&246\\
5&134&145&245&256.
\end{array}
\]

Both tables use every three-subset of \([6]\) once.  Their adjacent-union
tables use every four-subset once, so this is an exact five-for-five
middle-factor trade with every initial port and complementary endpoint
fixed row by row.

Intersecting the three noninitial states in each row gives

\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 &1&2&3&4&5\\ \hline
 \text{old}&6&6&4&2&2\\
 \text{new}&6&3&3&6&5.
 \end{array}
\]

Hence

\[
 (2e_6+2e_3+e_5)-(2e_6+e_4+2e_2)
 =2e_3+e_5-e_4-2e_2.                                  \tag{1.1}
\]

The old tuple is \((6,6,4,2,2)\), not \((6,6,4,5,2)\).  The latter
arithmetic slip would lose one of the two distinguished negative
\(e_2\)-arms.

## 2. The full common-context graph is disconnected

Write the five size-three Dyck words as

\[
 T_1=111000,\quad T_2=110100,\quad T_3=110010,
 \quad T_4=101010,\quad T_5=101100.                     \tag{2.1}
\]

The rooted context functor is generated by

\[
                       x\mapsto Ax,\qquad x\mapsto xB,
                       \qquad x\mapsto1x0,               \tag{2.2}
\]

with fixed Dyck words \(A,B\).  In every case the six-letter local word
\(x\) remains a contiguous Dyck factor.  Consequently a Dyck word which
contains no contiguous size-three Dyck factor is an isolated vertex of the
full common-context move graph.

Such words exist at every semilength at least four.  For \(k\ge2\), put

\[
 U_{2k}=(1100)^k,
 \qquad
 U_{2k+1}=1(1100)^k0.                                   \tag{2.3}
\]

Both are Dyck words.  In the four-periodic interior \((1100)^\infty\), a
six-letter block beginning in the four possible residues is respectively
of type

\[
 110011,\qquad100110,\qquad001100,\qquad011001.          \tag{2.4}
\]

The first has four up-steps, the second falls below its starting height at
its third step, and the last two begin with a down-step.  None is a Dyck
word.  For \(U_{2k+1}\), the unique block meeting the new first symbol is
\(111001\), which is unbalanced, while the unique block meeting the new
last symbol begins with zero.  Thus neither word in (2.3) contains any of
the five factors (2.1).

Therefore:

### Theorem 2.1 (constant-core pentagons are not a full conveyor)

For every \(r\ge4\), the arbitrary common-context pentagon graph on
\(D_r\) is disconnected; in fact it has the isolated vertex \(U_r\) from
(2.3).  Hence its phase cycles cannot generate
\(\operatorname{Sym}(D_r)\) and cannot realize an unrestricted Catalan
conveyor.

This obstruction is weaker than a positive-density obstruction: one
isolated owner does not prevent an asymptotically complete packet packing.
It does, however, settle the literal full-conveyor question.

There is also a correction to the tempting collapsed-three-skeleton
argument.  That skeleton is invariant for **fringe-subtree** copies, but
not for every context in (2.2).  For example Theorem 4.1 directly permits

\[
                            T_1\,10\longleftrightarrow T_4\,10. \tag{2.5}
\]

In first-return form the left subtree has size two on the left and size
zero on the right.  Thus the two words have different root split and
different collapsed fringe skeleton.  Any no-go which treats (2.5) as a
fringe substitution is false.

## 3. A near-perfect same-hole packet partition

Although the full graph is disconnected, the packet has abundant
same-hole supply.  Let \(a_r\) be the number of size-\(r\) ordered binary
trees containing no **fringe subtree** of size three, and put

\[
                         A(z)=\sum_{r\ge0}a_rz^r.
\]

A tree is empty or is obtained from two avoiding children, except that all
five trees of total size three must be deleted.  Therefore

\[
                         A(z)=1+zA(z)^2-5z^3,             \tag{3.1}
\]

and

\[
 A(z)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4z+20z^4}}{2z}.                  \tag{3.2}
\]

The discriminant is decreasing and positive on
\([0,13/50]\): at the right endpoint it equals

\[
 1-\frac{52}{50}+20\left(\frac{13}{50}\right)^4>0,
\]

and its derivative there is still negative.  Since the coefficients of
\(A\) are nonnegative, Pringsheim's theorem gives radius of convergence
strictly greater than \(13/50\).  Consequently

\[
 \frac{a_r}{\operatorname{Cat}_r}
 =O\!\left(r^{3/2}\left(\frac{25}{26}\right)^r\right)
 =o(1).                                                   \tag{3.3}
\]

For every tree not counted by \(a_r\), choose its first size-three fringe
subtree in preorder.  Equal-size fringe subtrees cannot be nested, and
replacing the chosen one by any of the five shapes neither creates nor
destroys another size-three fringe root.  Hence the choice is stable under
all five replacements.  The resulting equivalence classes have exactly
five members and partition \(D_r\setminus\mathcal A_r\).

Thus there are exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 P_r^{\rm same}=\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_r-a_r}{5}
                 =\left(\frac15+o(1)\right)
                    \operatorname{Cat}_r}                \tag{3.4}
\]

row-disjoint pentagon packets.  They may be installed simultaneously,
because each packet preserves its complete \(X\)- and \(Y\)-ledgers and
the packet row sets are disjoint.

If all three negative units in (1.1) were useful, their raw intrinsic
capacity would be

\[
                    3P_r^{\rm same}
                    =\left(\frac35+o(1)\right)
                       \operatorname{Cat}_r.             \tag{3.5}
\]

Numerically this is larger than the fatal plateau demand per context,

\[
 \operatorname{Cat}_r\left(\frac12-\frac1\theta\right),
 \qquad4\le\theta<16,                                   \tag{3.6}
\]

whose coefficient lies between \(1/4\) and \(7/16\).

This favorable comparison is real but does not yet concern the certified
plateau target.

## 4. The parent-interface gate

The plateau at scale \(r\) is a full child-window intersection in a
size-\(r+1\) parent.  A \(D_r\)-port-preserving replacement performed
strictly inside the same size-\(r\) hole fixes both complementary boundary
states.  Every local coordinate is missing from one boundary or the other,
so the full-hole intersection remains the old exterior target.  This is
the exact same-hole no-go.

Therefore the packets counted in (3.4) have zero certified drain on the
size-\(r\) plateau whose PCap cost was computed in Lane G.  Their intrinsic
delta (1.1) belongs to a shorter one-sided window.  It may be useful for
shallower shadow dispersion, but it cannot simply be credited against
(3.6).

To service the fatal fibre, a pentagon must be installed in the parent and
must change the entrance or exit state of the nested child window.  The
rooted context theorem proves exact completion of such candidates only
after a concrete parent alignment has been specified; it does not turn
the dense same-hole partition into a parent partition automatically.

## 5. The directly certified parent lift is too sparse

There is one immediate parent-aligned lift.  For every
\(R\in D_{r-2}\), right-concatenate the five local ports by \(R\):

\[
                            T_iR,\qquad1\le i\le5.        \tag{5.1}
\]

These have total semilength \(r+1\).  The two ports

\[
 T_4R=10(1010R),\qquad T_5R=10(1100R)                  \tag{5.2}
\]

belong to the canonical nested size-\(r\) child fibre \(10D_r\).  By the
exact target table, they move from the common old target \(2\) to targets
\(6\) and \(5\), respectively.  Thus each packet retargets exactly two
marked child occurrences.  Its third negative unit, at target \(4\), is
an auxiliary arm.

Distinct \(R\)'s use disjoint sets of five parent ports, so all these
packets may be applied simultaneously.  Across all aligned parent
contexts their number is

\[
                  P_{m,r}^{\rm par}
                  =H_{m,r+1}\operatorname{Cat}_{r-2}.   \tag{5.3}
\]

Put \(d=\operatorname{Cat}_r\) and \(k=m-r\).  The exact normalized count
is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \pi_{m,r}
 &:=\frac{P_{m,r}^{\rm par}}{H_{m,r}d}\\
 &=\frac{k}{2(2k-1)}
   \frac{r(r+1)}{4(2r-3)(2r-1)}\\
 &=\boxed{
 \frac{k r(r+1)}
 {8(2k-1)(2r-3)(2r-1)}}
 =\frac1{64}+o(1).                                      \tag{5.4}
\end{aligned}
\]

The matched intrinsic child-fibre profile has marked supply at most
\(2P_{m,r}^{\rm par}\).  Even if its auxiliary negative arm is also
perfectly routed, that one profile decreases cap tail by at most

\[
 3P_{m,r}^{\rm par}
 =\left(\frac3{64}+o(1)\right)H_{m,r}d.                 \tag{5.5}
\]

For hereditary PCap one must include every intersection window whose
boundary cuts the packet slab.  There are exactly four nontrivial local
profiles.  Writing \(e_A\) for unit mass at target \(A\), direct
intersection of the two tables gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_{01}
   &=e_{24}+e_{25}+e_{14}-e_{15}-e_{35}-e_{34},\\
 \Delta_{012}
   &=2e_4+e_2-2e_5-e_3,\\
 \Delta_{23}
   &=e_{35}+e_{36}+e_{25}-e_{34}-e_{24}-e_{26},\\
 \Delta_{123}
   &=2e_3+e_5-e_4-2e_2.                                \tag{5.6}
\end{aligned}
\]

Every vector in (5.6) has negative mass exactly three.  Single-phase
profiles have zero aggregate change because each internal phase is merely
permuted, while a window containing the whole slab contains both fixed
complementary endpoints and is unchanged.  Thus a long protected window
can change only at the four cuts in (5.6).

Intersecting with an arbitrary common exterior core is a push-forward of
these target measures and cannot increase total negative mass.  The same
remains true dynamically in the presence of other phase-disjoint packets.
Consequently one pentagon can lower cap tail by at most twelve at any
fixed protected depth:

\[
                         \boxed{\text{perfect drain}\le12.} \tag{5.7}
\]

The certified one-depth demand is

\[
 D_{m,r}(\theta)
 =H_{m,r}d\left(\frac12-\frac1\theta\right),
 \qquad \theta=\frac d p.                               \tag{5.8}
\]

Therefore the ideal all-profile supply fraction is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{12P_{m,r}^{\rm par}}{D_{m,r}(\theta)}
 =\frac{3\theta}{8(\theta-2)}+o(1).}                    \tag{5.9}
\]

It is at most \(3/4+o(1)\), attained at \(\theta=4\), and tends to
\(3/7\) as \(\theta\) approaches sixteen.  Thus this parent lift is
insufficient even when every negative unit in all four profiles is routed
perfectly.

The surviving one-depth deficit is

\[
 \boxed{
 D_{m,r}(\theta)-12P_{m,r}^{\rm par}
 =\left(\frac{5\theta-16}{64\theta\sqrt\pi}+o(1)\right)
   \frac W{r^{3/2}}.}                                   \tag{5.10}
\]

It is positive throughout \(4\le\theta<16\).  Summing over
\(r\le q\le p^{1/4}/4\) multiplies its leading term by
\((1/4+o(1))p^{1/4}\), while the ambient \(W-N_q\) correction remains
negligible.  Hence the hereditary deficit is much larger than \(W\).

## 6. Exact density required from a new parent packing

Suppose a future theorem produces \(P\) parent-aligned, effectively
disjoint pentagon packets per normalization \(H_{m,r}d\).

If all four boundary profiles are perfectly useful, the necessary packet
density is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{P}{H_{m,r}d}
 \ge\frac1{12}\left(\frac12-\frac1\theta\right).}       \tag{6.1}
\]

The right side ranges from \(1/48\) at \(\theta=4\) to \(7/192\) near
\(\theta=16\).  The direct parent lift (5.4) supplies only \(1/64\), so it
misses even this maximally optimistic threshold.

If only the three negative units of the matched intrinsic profile are
useful, the necessary density is

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{P}{H_{m,r}d}
 \ge\frac13\left(\frac12-\frac1\theta\right).}          \tag{6.2}
\]

The right side ranges from \(1/12\) at \(\theta=4\) to \(7/48\) near
\(\theta=16\).  These are the stronger requirements when only the matched
intrinsic profile is controlled.

If only the two distinguished \(-2e_2\) arms are known to drain the
canonical plateau, the necessary density becomes

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{P}{H_{m,r}d}
 \ge\frac12\left(\frac12-\frac1\theta\right),}          \tag{6.3}
\]

ranging from \(1/8\) to \(7/32\).  A row-disjoint five-packet factor has
the absolute density ceiling \(1/5\).  Hence marked-arm routing alone is
numerically incapable of handling the overshoot range \(\theta>10\), even
with a perfect parent near-factor.  For the full overshoot interval, the
third negative arm or additional packet orientations are genuinely
necessary.

These are the sharp constant gates for the pentagon lane.

## 7. Decision

The rooted pentagon is not a full Catalan conveyor: the factor-free words
(2.3) are exact isolated owners.  It does provide an asymptotically dense
same-hole packet partition, so lack of local atoms is no longer the issue.

For hereditary PCap, however, same-hole density cannot be credited to the
fatal plateau.  The presently certified parent-boundary deployment has
normalized all-profile capacity \(3/16\), strictly below the minimum demand
\(1/4\).  Thus the known arbitrary-context construction does not yet meet
PCap demand, even with perfect routing of its direct parent lift.

The viable next theorem is precise: construct parent-aligned pentagon
packets at density at least (6.1) and prove simultaneous control of all
four boundary profiles.  If only the intrinsic profile can be aligned,
the stronger gates (6.2)--(6.3) apply.  Without such a parent packing, the
pentagon remains a powerful shallow-shadow disperser rather than a
coefficient-one seed.
