# Exact audit of the triangular pin/factorability repair problem

## Verdict

The strict triangular spanning problem has the new certified exact value

\[
\boxed{\rho(5)=4}.
\]

Here \(\rho(R)=g_\triangle(R)-|T_R|\), where a word is made of coherent
triangular cells \((s,y)\), every cell of

\[
T_R=\{(0,0)\}\cup\{(s,y):1\le s\le R,\ 0\le y<s\}
\]

must occur, and every target \((u,r,x)\) must be the bounding rectangle of
one contiguous interval:

\[
(\min s,\max s,\min y,\max y)=(u,r,0,x).
\]

The alphabet has 16 cells at radius 5.  Independently checked DRAT
certificates prove that lengths 17, 18, and 19 are impossible.  The existing
length-20 word passes a direct exhaustive verifier.  Therefore its excess
four is minimal.

The exact small-radius sequence is now

\[
\rho(1),\ldots,\rho(5)=0,0,1,2,4.
\]

This is a genuine factorability/pin result, not an abstract endpoint count.
It also quantifies the gap exposed by the linear-excess endpoint braid: the
endpoint system by itself is almost-once, whereas coherent triangular cells
and surviving extrema require four repetitions already at radius 5.

At radius 6, lengths 23, 24, and 25 (one through three repeated cells) are
also certified impossible, so

\[
4\le \rho(6)\le5.
\]

The upper bound is the previously verified length-27 construction.  Further
radius-6 lengths are being tested separately and are not needed for the
radius-5 theorem.

## 1. Why insertion-only repair of the proposed endpoint braid is impossible

In the linear-excess endpoint braid, the intervals assigned to all targets
with height \(x=0\) form a connected cover of the full physical line.  Every
such target omits the first positive height atom \(h_1\).  Hence its legal
set is empty:

\[
Z_{h_1}=[n]\setminus\bigcup_{x=0}I_{u,r,0}=\varnothing.
\]

Order-preserving insertion of new positions does not help.  Positions
inserted inside the old hull remain covered by the connected negative
interval union, while positions outside the hull belong to no unchanged
positive witness.  A valid repair must reroute/select different endpoints,
duplicate witness roles, or reorder coherent cells.  Thus “minimum inserted
separators for the fixed endpoint assignment” is infinite and is not the
right finite optimization problem.

The canonical finite optimization is exactly \(\rho(R)\) above.

## 2. Exact SAT encoding

The generator is

```text
scratch/triangular_pin_repair_sat.cpp
```

with SHA-256

```text
ae4a32335a756ed949ef4806216453324478e164e498bee8c712752a30164ced
```

For a proposed length \(n\), the formula contains:

1. one-hot variables selecting one coherent cell of \(T_R\) at every
   physical position;
2. one occurrence clause for every cell, enforcing spanning;
3. for each target and each non-singleton physical interval, a witness flag;
4. under a selected witness flag, an allowed-box clause at every contained
   position and four provider clauses forcing \(s=u\), \(s=r\), \(y=x\),
   and \(y=0\);
5. one support clause requiring at least one witness per target;
6. a safe reversal-symmetry breaker.

The witness variables are intentionally one-way.  This is exact: every SAT
model selects a genuine interval for every target; conversely, for a real
word one may set one genuine witness flag per target.

Two propagation strengthenings are redundant and sound:

- the target \([u,u+1]\times\{0\}\) contains only peaks \(P_u,P_{u+1}\),
  so some physical adjacency of these two values is mandatory;
- because every alphabet cell occurs and
  \(n=|T_R|+q\), the exact multiplicity identity is

  \[
  \sum_{c\in T_R}\sum_{j\ge2}[\operatorname{mult}(c)\ge j]=q.
  \]

  The encoding exposes this identity with truncated exact unary counters.

The strengthened formula reproduces the previously known small cases:
radius 3, length 7 is UNSAT and length 8 is SAT; radius 4, length 12 is
UNSAT and length 13 is SAT.

## 5. Mathematical interpretation

The endpoint theorem proves that ordered left/right chain decompositions,
orthogonality, triangular placement, and short witness intervals need only
linear excess.  The exact \(\rho(5)=4\) result proves that coherent-cell
factorability is already a real additional obstruction at small radius.

It does **not** determine the asymptotic order of \(\rho(R)\).  The first five
values happen to agree with \(\lfloor(R-1)^2/4\rfloor\), but the known
radius-6 construction has excess 5, below the value 6 predicted by that
formula.  Small-radius data therefore neither proves quadratic growth nor
supports a claimed subquadratic recurrence.

The correct remaining constructive target is still a protected-backbone or
equivalent global superposition theorem that reroutes negative height-zero
witnesses while preserving positive-height extrema.  Scalar endpoint
repacking and insertion-only padding cannot supply it.
