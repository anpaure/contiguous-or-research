# Lane Y audit: transfer from short-permutation heat to the original transposition heat

Date: 2026-07-25

Method: pure mathematics only. No web search, finite search, solver, or
computational experiment was used.

## 0. Verdict

Let

\[
n=2m+1,\qquad W=\binom nm,\qquad
B=\operatorname{Cat}_m=\frac Wn,
\]

and let \(\mathfrak F_m\) be the finite set of literal squarefree exact
middle wreath factors. Let \(K_{\rm tr}\) be the original fair
transposition-component heat kernel and let \(K_{\rm sh}\) be the intrinsic
short-permutation heat kernel formed from transpositions, 3-cycles, and
double transpositions as in
`MATH_ATTACK_Y_SHORT_PERMUTATION_HEAT_CHAIN_20260725.md`.

There is an exact class-transfer theorem:

\[
\boxed{
\text{every }K_{\rm sh}\text{-stationary law is }K_{\rm tr}\text{-stationary}.}
\tag{T0.1}
\]

More precisely, every short communicating class \(\mathscr D\) is a
disjoint union of original transposition classes \(\mathscr C\), and

\[
\boxed{
\pi_{\mathscr D}
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}\,\pi_{\mathscr C}.}
\tag{T0.2}
\]

Consequently, for the full floor energy \(\mathcal Q_H\),

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr D}}\mathcal Q_H
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H.}
\tag{T0.3}
\]

Thus a short-stationary law of expected excess at most
\(R\) immediately selects an original communicating class of uniform mean
at most \(R\). All states involved are literal integral exact factors; no
fractional projection or labelled common-owner synchronization is used.

The short-permutation report, however, does **not** produce the premise.
Its restitution identities are exact but circular at stationarity. If

\[
\Theta_{\rm tr}(H)
=\min_{\mathscr C}
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H,
\qquad
\Theta_{\rm sh}(H)
=\min_{\mathscr D}
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr D}}\mathcal Q_H,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{\Theta_{\rm tr}(H)\le \Theta_{\rm sh}(H).}
\tag{T0.4}
\]

Therefore proving a Catalan-scale short-stationary mean is a valid, stronger
sufficient route than the requested original stationary selection. The
converse implication is not proved, and equality in (T0.4) is possible.
Class containment alone supplies no numerical upper bound on either side.

The automatic transfer proved here uses the fact that the augmented heat
actually includes all original transposition projections with positive
weight. A different chain could conceivably have the same stationary
fixed space for another reason, but this does not follow from coordinate
group generation. In particular, the theorem does not automatically apply
to the standalone even-only short chain or the standalone long-involution
bundle chain.

## 1. The two exact heat chains

For each coordinate transposition \(\tau\), let \(K_\tau\) be uniform
averaging on the intrinsic transposition-component cell. The audited cell
theorem gives

\[
K_\tau^2=K_\tau=K_\tau^*.
\tag{T1.1}
\]

The original kernel is

\[
K_{\rm tr}=\binom n2^{-1}\sum_\tau K_\tau.
\tag{T1.2}
\]

Let \(\mathscr G_{\rm sh}\) consist of all cyclic subgroups generated by
a transposition, a 3-cycle, or a double transposition. For each
\(\Gamma\in\mathscr G_{\rm sh}\), intrinsic owner-group cells give an
orthogonal partition projection \(K_\Gamma\). Put

\[
K_{\rm sh}
=\sum_{\Gamma\in\mathscr G_{\rm sh}}a_\Gamma K_\Gamma,
\qquad a_\Gamma>0,\qquad \sum_\Gamma a_\Gamma=1.
\tag{T1.3}
\]

The report uses equal weights, but no argument below needs equal weights.
It only matters that every transposition subgroup has positive weight.

Write \(\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}\) and \(\mathfrak C_{\rm sh}\) for the two
sets of communicating classes.

## 2. Fixed-point and class-transfer theorem

### Theorem 2.1 (stationary inclusion)

Every stationary probability law for \(K_{\rm sh}\) is stationary for
every \(K_\Gamma\) separately. In particular it is stationary for every
\(K_\tau\) and for \(K_{\rm tr}\).

#### Proof

Use the unnormalized counting inner product on functions on
\(\mathfrak F_m\). Since every \(K_\Gamma\) is an orthogonal projection,

\[
\langle g,(I-K_{\rm sh})g\rangle
=\sum_\Gamma a_\Gamma
\|(I-K_\Gamma)g\|_2^2.
\tag{T2.1}
\]

Let \(\nu\) be stationary. Symmetry of \(K_{\rm sh}\) identifies its
density \(g\) relative to counting measure with a fixed vector:
\(K_{\rm sh}g=g\). The left side of (T2.1) is zero. Every summand on the
right is nonnegative and every weight is positive, so

\[
K_\Gamma g=g
\qquad(\Gamma\in\mathscr G_{\rm sh}).
\]

In particular \(K_\tau g=g\) for every transposition, and averaging gives
\(K_{\rm tr}g=g\). By symmetry this is the required stationarity of
\(\nu\). \(\square\)

### Theorem 2.2 (exact disintegration over old classes)

Every \(\mathscr D\in\mathfrak C_{\rm sh}\) is a disjoint union of
classes \(\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}\). Its uniform law satisfies
(T0.2). More generally, if

\[
\nu=\sum_{\mathscr D\in\mathfrak C_{\rm sh}}
b_{\mathscr D}\pi_{\mathscr D}
\tag{T2.2}
\]

is any short-stationary law, then

\[
\nu
=\sum_{\mathscr D}
\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
b_{\mathscr D}\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\pi_{\mathscr C}.
\tag{T2.3}
\]

#### Proof

Every original transposition-cell edge is an edge of the short graph.
Hence every original path remains a short path, so every old class lies in
one short class.

If \(F\) is uniform on \(\mathscr D\), then

\[
\Pr(F\in\mathscr C)=\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\]

and, conditional on \(F\in\mathscr C\), every member of \(\mathscr C\)
still has probability \(1/|\mathscr C|\). This proves (T0.2). The general
formula follows by mixing over \(\mathscr D\). \(\square\)

Theorem 2.1 is an analytic proof and Theorem 2.2 is an independent graph
and counting proof of the same stationary inclusion.

## 3. Conditional variance and covariance do not acquire a between-class term

For \(1\le q\le H\), put

\[
N_q=\binom n{m-q},\qquad
\lambda_q=\frac W{N_q}=c_q+\theta_q,
\qquad 0\le\theta_q<1.
\]

Fix a rank-\((m-q)\) target \(S\), and write

\[
Z_q(F)=\mu_q^F(S).
\]

Every original class is \(S_n\)-stable: in a transposition cell, switching
all ownership components sends \(F\) to \(\tau F\), and transpositions
generate \(S_n\). Thus the entire distribution of \(\mu_q^F(S)\), not only
its mean, is independent of the target \(S\). Target transitivity and the deterministic total
\(\sum_S\mu_q^F(S)=W\) therefore give

\[
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}Z_q=\lambda_q
\qquad
(\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}).
\tag{T3.1}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 (exact variance disintegration)

For every short class \(\mathscr D\), every depth \(q\), and every target
\(S\),

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_{\mathscr D}}Z_q
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}Z_q.}
\tag{T3.2}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr D}}Q_q
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}Q_q,}
\tag{T3.3}
\]

and summing with weights \(1/c_q\) proves (T0.3).

#### Proof

Apply conditional variance to the random old class \(\mathscr C(F)\):

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\mathscr D}Z_q
=\mathbb E_{\mathscr D}
\operatorname{Var}(Z_q\mid\mathscr C(F))
+\operatorname{Var}_{\mathscr D}
\mathbb E(Z_q\mid\mathscr C(F)).
\]

The last term is zero by (T3.1), and the first term is the right side of
(T3.2). For an integer-valued variable of mean
\(c_q+\theta_q\),

\[
\mathbb E Q_q
=N_q\bigl(\operatorname{Var}Z_q-\theta_q(1-\theta_q)\bigr).
\]

Substitute (T3.2) to obtain (T3.3). \(\square\)

There is an equivalent exact covariance statement. Let \(M=(n-1)!/2\)
be the number of unoriented wreaths, let \(x_F\in\{0,1\}^M\) be the row
indicator, and put

\[
p=\frac BM,\qquad y_F=x_F-p\mathbf1,
\qquad
\Sigma_{\mathscr A}=\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr A}}y_Fy_F^{\mathsf T}.
\]

Every original class has \(\mathbb E x_F=p\mathbf1\), again by
\(S_n\)-stability and row transitivity. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{\mathscr D}
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}\Sigma_{\mathscr C}.}
\tag{T3.4}
\]

There is no covariance of conditional means. If

\[
 D_qx_F=\mu_q^F,
\qquad
T_H=\sum_{q\le H}\frac{D_q^{\mathsf T}D_q}{c_q},
\qquad
\mathfrak B_H=\sum_{q\le H}\frac{\beta_q}{c_q},
\]

then

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(T_H\Sigma_{\mathscr D})-\mathfrak B_H
=\sum_{\mathscr C\subseteq\mathscr D}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathscr D|}
\left(
\operatorname{Tr}(T_H\Sigma_{\mathscr C})-\mathfrak B_H
\right).
\tag{T3.5}
\]

Thus the short heat does not dilute old-class excess variance by creating a
favourable between-class cancellation. It takes an ordinary convex average
of the original class covariances.

## 4. The exact stationary-selection corollary

### Corollary 4.1 (Catalan-scale transfer)

Let \(H=H_A=\lceil A\sqrt m\rceil\), with fixed \(A>0\) and sufficiently
large \(m\) that \(H\le m-1\). Suppose there is a \(K_{\rm sh}\)-stationary
law \(\nu\) such that

\[
\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal Q_H\le C_AHB.
\tag{T4.1}
\]

Then there is an original transposition communicating class
\(\mathscr C\) satisfying

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H\le C_AHB.}
\tag{T4.2}
\]

Consequently some literal exact factor \(F\in\mathscr C\) satisfies

\[
\mathcal Q_H(F)\le C_AHB.
\tag{T4.3}
\]

#### Proof

Expand \(\nu\) by (T2.3). Its energy expectation is a convex combination
of the original class means. At least one class mean is no larger than the
combination. Averaging once more inside that class gives (T4.3). \(\square\)

This is the exact composition into the frozen factor route: the selected
\(F\) is already an integral squarefree exact factor, and the known
floor-energy-to-overload implication applies inside that one factor.

## 5. Quantitative restitution gates that would now suffice

The transfer theorem makes the unproved estimates in the short heat report
directly composable. It does not prove them.

For the 3-cycle owner-group heat, let \(\overline V_3\) and
\(\mathcal S_3\ge0\) be as in that report. Every short-stationary law
\(\nu\) obeys

\[
\mathbb E_\nu\overline V_3
=\frac4n
\left(\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal Q_H+\mathfrak B_H\right)
+\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal S_3.
\tag{T5.1}
\]

Therefore, for any \(R\ge0\),

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_\nu\overline V_3
\le\frac4n(\mathfrak B_H+R)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}:
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H\le R.}
\tag{T5.2}
\]

For double transpositions, and in the audited spectral range (n\ge9), put

\[
\alpha_{22}=\frac{4(n-4)}{n(n-3)}.
\]

The corresponding exact implication is

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_\nu\overline V_{22}
\le\alpha_{22}(\mathfrak B_H+R)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}:
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H\le R.}
\tag{T5.3}
\]

Taking \(R=C_AHB\) gives the required scale with the constants exactly as
displayed.

There is an analogous statement for the long involutions of cycle type
\(1\,2^m\). Let

\[
V_{\rm inv}(F,\sigma)
=\sum_{\mathcal O}\|\delta_{\mathcal O}\|_H^2,
\]

with component orbits bundled as in Theorem 9.1 of
`MATH_ATTACK_Y4_CLASS_CONNECTIVITY_20260725.md`. Fair signing has drift

\[
K_\sigma\mathcal Q_H(F)-\mathcal Q_H(F)
=\frac14\bigl(V_{\rm inv}(F,\sigma)-A_\sigma(F)\bigr).
\tag{T5.4}
\]

If a reversible projection chain includes both every original
transposition cell and these involution cells, then every stationary law
\(\nu\) is separately invariant under every cell projection. Hence

\[
\mathbb E_{\nu,\sigma}V_{\rm inv}
=\mathbb E_{\nu,\sigma}A_\sigma
\ge2\left(1-\frac1n\right)
\left(\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal Q_H+\mathfrak B_H\right).
\tag{T5.5}
\]

It follows that

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_{\nu,\sigma}V_{\rm inv}
\le2\left(1-\frac1n\right)(\mathfrak B_H+R)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}:
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H\le R.}
\tag{T5.6}
\]

Equations (T5.2), (T5.3), and (T5.6) are proved conditional implications.
No required restitution upper bound is proved in the source reports.

## 6. Why stationarity and class containment alone do not supply the premise

The exact variational comparison is

\[
\Theta_{\rm tr}(H)
=\inf_{\nu K_{\rm tr}=\nu}\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal Q_H,
\qquad
\Theta_{\rm sh}(H)
=\inf_{\nu K_{\rm sh}=\nu}\mathbb E_\nu\mathcal Q_H.
\tag{T6.1}
\]

Theorem 2.1 gives inclusion of the stationary simplices, and hence
(T0.4). Equivalently, (T0.3) says that every candidate in the short
minimum is a size-weighted average of candidates for the original minimum.
Thus augmentation cannot lower the stationary variational optimum. It can
only make a separately proved estimate transfer to the original chain.
In particular, even a proof that the short graph is connected would give
only

\[
\mathbb E_{\rm unif(\mathfrak F_m)}\mathcal Q_H
=\sum_{\mathscr C\in\mathfrak C_{\rm tr}}
\frac{|\mathscr C|}{|\mathfrak F_m|}
\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H.
\tag{T6.2}
\]

Connectivity supplies no upper bound on this global average and can average
a low original class together with high ones.

At short stationarity, (T5.1) is an equality in which restitution tracks
the already selected energy. Nonnegativity of restitution gives the wrong
direction: it lower-bounds restitution in terms of energy. An upper bound
at relative Catalan accuracy is new mathematical content, precisely the
unproved premise of (T5.2). The same observation applies to (T5.5).

Running a low-energy state under the short heat also does not solve this
problem. Pointwise,

\[
K_\Gamma\mathcal Q_H(F)-\mathcal Q_H(F)
=V_\Gamma(F)-A_\Gamma(F),
\tag{T6.3}
\]

and the sign is not controlled. If one owner packet is active, restitution
can equal coherent displacement exactly, even when the latter is large.
Thus convergence to a short stationary class average is not known to
preserve a low starting energy.

## 7. Projected short-Haar no-go model

The following extension of the audited first-depth model proves that the
short-Haar projection algebra, coherent spectra, integer first-slice
margins, and stationarity do not by themselves imply Catalan-scale energy.
It is not a cross-depth chronology model and is not an exact-factor
counterexample.

Take infinitely many \(m\) for which \(n=2m+1\) is prime, put
\(r=m-1\), and let \(\rho\) be an \(n\)-cycle. Every \(\rho\)-orbit on
the rank-\(r\) slice has size \(n\) and contains each coordinate exactly
\(r\) times. The number of such orbits is

\[
\frac{N_1}{n}=B\frac{m}{m+2}\ge\left\lceil\frac B2\right\rceil
\]

for all sufficiently large \(m\). Choose \(\lfloor B/2\rfloor\) such
orbits and give their
members load \(2\). If \(B\) is odd, choose one further orbit and give its
members load \(1\). Give all other rank-\(r\) sets load \(0\). Denote the
resulting profile by \(z\). Then

\[
z(S)\in\{0,1,2\},\qquad
\sum_Sz(S)=W,
\qquad
\sum_{S\ni x}z(S)=rB
\quad(x\in[n]).
\tag{T7.1}
\]

Since \(c_1=1\), only the zero cells contribute to \(Q_1\), and the
audited count gives

\[
Q_1(z)
=2\left(N_1-n\left\lceil\frac B2\right\rceil\right)
\ge W\frac{m-2}{m+2}-n
\ge\frac W2
\tag{T7.2}
\]

for all sufficiently large members of this prime subsequence.

Use the coordinate orbit \(\mathscr Z=S_nz\) as a formal state space. For
every cyclic subgroup \(\Gamma\) occurring in the short heat, define
\(P_\Gamma\) to be uniform averaging over the group orbit \(\Gamma v\).
This is an orthogonal partition projection. No transposition fixes any
state: if a transposition fixed \(z\), its conjugates by powers of \(\rho\)
would generate \(S_n\), forcing \(z\) to be constant on the slice. Hence
the transposition edges connect the entire coordinate orbit. The averaged
short chain has one communicating class and its stationary law is uniform.

The energy \(Q_1\) is coordinate-invariant, so

\[
\mathbb E_{\mathscr Z}Q_1=Q_1(z)\ge\frac W2,
\qquad
\frac{\mathbb E_{\mathscr Z}Q_1}{H_AB}
\ge\frac n{2H_A}\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{T7.3}
\]

The same formal construction admits all long-involution orbit-Haar
projections. If the whole profile is viewed as one formal packet, then

\[
V_\Gamma=A_\Gamma
\tag{T7.4}
\]

pointwise for every group cell. Thus even the constant-order coherent gap
of the long-involution conjugacy average is exactly restored by packet
restitution.

This first-depth profile also has a signed integral exact-middle lift, by
the audited rank-isolated integral selector theorem. What it lacks is decisive: its
states are not nonnegative squarefree exact wreath factors and its group
orbit cells are not literal ownership-packet cells. Therefore (T7.3) is a
rigorous no-go for proofs using only the listed stationary/Haar/slice
axioms, not a counterexample to the true exact-factor theorem. A successful
proof must use additional Boolean common-owner geometry to bound
restitution.

## 8. Standalone even and involution chains: exact scope warning

Let \(K_{\rm ev}\) average only 3-cycle and double-transposition owner
cells, or let \(K_{\rm inv}\) average only the long-involution bundle
cells. A stationary density for either kernel need not be fixed by any
\(K_\tau\). The fact that the underlying coordinate permutations generate
\(A_n\), or that products of such permutations may generate a larger
group, does not imply a factorization of their **heat projections** into
the original transposition projections.

Accordingly:

* a low class mean for the full augmented chain
  \(aK_{\rm tr}+(1-a)K_{\rm ev}\) or
  \(aK_{\rm tr}+(1-a)K_{\rm inv}\), with \(a>0\), transfers by Theorem
  2.1;

* a low class mean for the standalone even or involution chain does not
  automatically transfer to a uniform original-class mean;

* subsequently running original heat is not a repair, because no
  inequality shows that original class averaging decreases
  \(\mathcal Q_H\).

This is the same distinction as the audited one between a group
commutator and an operator commutator.

## 9. Final theorem status

The proved, composable conclusion is:

> Any literal short-augmented stationary law with expected floor excess
> \(O_A(H_A\operatorname{Cat}_m)\) already is an original stationary law,
> and conditioning on a suitably selected original communicating class
> preserves that bound. Equivalently, a low short class selects a low
> original class by the exact size-weighted formulas (T0.2)--(T0.3).

This fully settles the class-containment and conditional-expectation part
of the proposed route. It does not prove the required low stationary law.
The remaining unproved mathematical statement is an exact-owner
restitution upper bound such as (T5.2), (T5.3), or (T5.6), or a different
Boolean exact-factor pair-correlation theorem. Short permutation spectra,
long-involution coherent contraction, reversibility, and communicating
class enlargement do not supply that bound.
