# Full proof audit of the thirty constant-one attack threads

## Scope and decision rule

This ledger is the primary-agent audit required before any of the thirty
top-level attack threads is archived.  A lane is retained only if its latest
theorem package is (i) mathematically sound after checking the proof body,
(ii) not subsumed by a stronger retained lane, and (iii) still supplies a
live route, a sharp reduction, or a reusable no-go theorem.  Conditional
implications are classified as conditional; they are never promoted to a
proof of the constant-one theorem.

## Cross-lane consistency checks

1. **I versus J (resolved):** Lane I's marked-gap theorem is valid after
   supplying the missing contextual insertion--erasure induction.  It gives
   \(M_1(F_m^{\rm MSW})\ge(1/16-o(1))W\), so J's earlier statement that no
   such bound was known is obsolete.  Together with AB7 it closes J's
   proposed canonical degree-two continuous cube.
2. **F/U/X/T/H:** endpoint-dual lower bounds must be checked for the same
   ambient-word and cross-parent hypotheses before treating them as one
   global no-go family.
3. **C/M/N/AB/J/L:** all heat and switching conclusions must use the same
   floor-corrected energy, exact-factor fibre, component definition, and
   quantifier order.  A no-go for a fixed transposition or prescribed cell
   does not refute an adaptive or multistep statement.
4. **A/G/O/AD/W:** all positive reductions must preserve one common exact
   factor/chronology across depths; marginal or fractional balance alone is
   not counted as progress on the final gate.
5. **W/P/A:** the (O(m^{-2})) codegree belongs only to the projected
   (P_v)-trace hypergraph.  Mandatory (Q_v) and depth-one incidences are
   already (2/m)-scale.  W's factor-first survival-packet LP is an exact
   formulation of what would still have to be bounded; it is not supplied
   by the projected nibble heuristic.
6. **Y/Z/AC:** allowing all comparator circuits or all lifted Graver moves
   does connect the exact fibre and removes nonglobal local minima, but the
   resulting minimum is exactly the original unknown one-factor optimum.
   The live content is instead Y5's literal completion-count estimate and
   Z5's ownership-restitution estimate.
7. **AA versus genuine wreath geometry:** AA's (W/4) holonomy gap and
   packet braid preserve exact abstract ownership, rank labels, and point
   margins, but not cyclic-row realizability.  They invalidate generic
   discrepancy/rounding black boxes only; they cannot be cited as a lower
   bound for actual wreath factors or rotors.
