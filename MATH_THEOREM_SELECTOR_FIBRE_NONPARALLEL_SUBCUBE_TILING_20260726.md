# Selector-fibre nonparallel subcube tilings

Date: 2026-07-26

## 0. Result

The rank-twisted macroblock construction produces large physical status
cubes (Q_S), typically with (S=\Theta(m)).  A trivial split into
(Q_r)'s uses one active (r)-set throughout the whole cell.  This note
records an exact way to vary the active set and compiler option while
preserving a literal owner partition.

### Theorem 0.1 (selector-fibre tiling)

Let (B\subseteq[S]) have size (t), and for every selector word
(z\in\{0,1\}^B) choose arbitrarily

\[
 A_z\subseteq[S]\setminus B,qquad |A_z|=r.         \tag{0.1}
\]

Then (Q_S) has an exact partition into physical (Q_r)'s such that the
packets in the selector fibre (x|_B=z) have active coordinate set
(A_z).  Independently for every individual packet (P_{z,y}), one may
install any legal cell-factor-blind compiler conjugate.

Thus any list of (2^t) active-set/compiler labels can be realized by one
integral owner tiling.  No matching, absorption, or seam is involved.

If (S=\Theta(m)), (r=m^{\alpha+o(1)}) with (0<\alpha<1), and

\[
 t\ge\left\lceil\log_2\binom{S-t}r\right\rceil
       +\left\lceil\log_2(2^r r!)\right\rceil,       \tag{0.2}
\]

then (t=O(r\log m)=o(m)).  The selector labels can therefore enumerate
all active (r)-sets and all cube-affine compiler conjugates, up to
floor/ceiling repetition, while leaving (S-t=\Theta(m)) payload axes.

This is an exact owner-side derandomization resource.  It does not by
itself prove target coverage: uniform enumeration reproduces a symmetric
fractional point, while the constant-one theorem needs one highly
correlated integral labeling with (o(W)) aggregate holes.

A subsequent exact target audit sharpens this caveat.  The symmetric
fractional point is uniform only on the payload axes
\(V=[S]\setminus B\).  Every literal target retains the complete frozen
selector word \(z\), so different selector fibres have disjoint target
supports.  Consequently an orthogonal array or fixed-slice coupling among
the visible \(z\)'s has zero off-diagonal target-overlap covariance.  See
`MATH_THEOREM_SELECTOR_FIBRE_AFFINE_OA_AND_VISIBLE_TRACE_FLOOR_OBSTRUCTION_20260726.md`.

## 1. Proof of the tiling

For (z\in\{0,1\}^B) and

\[
 y\in\{0,1\}^{[S]\setminus(B\cup A_z)},             \tag{1.1}
\]

define

\[
 P_{z,y}=\{x\in\{0,1\}^S:
                 x|_B=z,
                 x|_{[S]\setminus(B\cup A_z)}=y\}. \tag{1.2}
\]

The coordinates in (A_z) are free and every other coordinate is fixed,
so (P_{z,y}\cong Q_r).  For fixed (z), the sets (1.2), over all (y),
partition the selector fibre (x|_B=z).  Different selector fibres are
disjoint.  Their union is (Q_S), proving Theorem 0.1.

The compiler is factor-blind: after identifying its abstract directions
with (A_z), any axis permutation and endpoint reversal remains legal.
The compiler choice may therefore depend on the full frozen tag ((z,y))
without changing the owner partition.

The selector/OA proposal studied below is the more restrictive specialization
in which one conjugate \(g_z\) is used for every \(y\) in a fixed selector
fibre.  Allowing \(g_{z,y}\) removes the parallel-cylinder repetition
constraint, but it does not remove the visible word \(z\) or permit an
active set \(A_z\) to meet \(B\).

## 2. Label capacity

Put (V=S-t).  There are

\[
                         \binom Vr                  \tag{2.1}
\]

active (r)-sets in the payload.  The cube-affine group generated by
axis permutations and endpoint reversals has size

\[
                         2^r r!.                    \tag{2.2}
\]

Hence (0.2) supplies at least one selector word for every pair of labels.
Stirling's estimate gives

\[
 \log_2\binom Vr+\log_2(2^rr!)
 =O\bigl(r\log(V/r)+r\log r\bigr)
 =O(r\log m).                                      \tag{2.3}
\]

For every fixed (\alpha<1), this is (o(m)).  Choosing (t) by a
fixed-point iteration changes (V=S-t) only by (o(S)), so (0.2) is
consistent for all large (m).

## 3. Balanced incidence lists

The selector list may be chosen as a nearly uniform repetition of all
(r)-subsets of the (V) payload axes.  Consequently, for every fixed
(q\le r), the number of labels containing a prescribed (q)-set differs
from

\[
 {2^t\binom{V-q}{r-q}\over\binom Vr}               \tag{3.1}
\]

by at most the contribution of one incomplete repetition, namely

\[
                         \binom{V-q}{r-q}.          \tag{3.2}
\]

If (2^t/\binom Vr\to\infty), the relative error is (o(1)), uniformly
for each prescribed (q)-set.  The same enumeration can be refined by
compiler conjugates.

Equations (3.1)--(3.2) prove quantified near-balance of axis incidence in
the selector catalogue (and exact balance when the incomplete repetition
is absent).  They do not prove literal target balance after the packets are
placed in (inom{[2m]}m): target identities also depend on the frozen
payload word (y), the physical rank-twisted frames, and one common
choice across all depths.

For the full affine label menu the exact common all-depth payload marginal
is

\[
 \Pr((J_1,\ldots,J_q)=(j_1,\ldots,j_q))={1\over(V)_q},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E Z_{q,T}={2^q\over\binom Vq}.             \tag{3.3}
\]

This does not average literal targets across selector words: a target
emitted in fibre \(z\) displays one endpoint on every selector pair and
therefore determines \(z\).  Thus the literal target cylinders of two
different fibres are disjoint.

## 4. Exact remaining selector theorem

Apply Theorem 0.1 independently inside every good product status cell of
`MATH_THEOREM_RANK_TWISTED_MACROBLOCK_PACKET_TILING_20260726.md`.
Ownership remains exact and its exponentially small leave is unchanged.
The remaining statement is:

> Choose the lists (z\mapsto(A_z,g_z)), where (g_z) is a compiler
> conjugate, so that the union of the resulting literal lower and upper
> targets has aggregate hole count (o(W)) through a height
> (H/\sqrt m\to\infty), (H=o(r)).

The symmetric list in Section 3 proves the correct fractional marginals
only after restricting to payload flags (or projecting away the selector
trace).  It cannot itself create the required correlated integral
labeling: all cross-\(z\) derivative Grams are exactly zero.  Moreover its
support law on deletion \(q\)-sets has total-variation distance

\[
 1-{\binom{S-t}q\over\binom Sq}                     \tag{4.1}
\]

from the uniform all-\(S\)-axis flag law.  At the label-capacity scale
\(t\sim r\log S\), with \(S=\Theta(m)\), \(q=H=\Theta(\sqrt m)\), and
\(H=o(r)\), this distance tends to one.  A proof must therefore hide or
consume the selector trace, or correlate labels across genuinely
transverse status cells whose literal target cylinders overlap.

## 5. Audited boundary

Proved:

* arbitrary selector-dependent active sets and compiler conjugates can be
  installed in one exact owner tiling;
* the selector budget is (o(m)) for every polynomially sublinear (r);
* complete active-set incidence can be made asymptotically uniform on the
  payload axes; and
* the complete affine menu has exact common all-depth payload marginals.

Open:

* the literal target-capacity theorem for the rank-twisted physical cells;
* a construction which erases the visible selector trace or supplies
  cross-cell negative target covariance;
* an integral selector labeling beating the Poisson hole scale; and
* `CPM`.

The value of the selector theorem is that the remaining integrality no
longer comes from owner overlap: all option entropy is available inside an
already exact owner partition.
