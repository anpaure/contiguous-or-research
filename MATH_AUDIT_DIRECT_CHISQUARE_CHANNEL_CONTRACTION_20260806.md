## 1. Channel contraction

The column identity in (2.1) makes the denominator in the Cauchy--Schwarz
step exactly \(c'_y\).  The row inequality then gives

\[
 \|J^*f\|_{c'^{-1}}^2
 \le\sum_{x,y}J_{xy}f_x^2
 \le\sigma\sum_xc_xf_x^2.
\]

No reversibility, stochastic independence, or equal cardinality of the
two state spaces is used.  The assertion remains valid for a
transition-dependent channel and with killed mass omitted.

## 2. Random coefficient and exact constants

For one outcome,

\[
 \Psi'\le\sigma\Psi+2\sqrt{\sigma\Psi H}+H.
\]

Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
 \mathbb E\sqrt{\sigma H}
 \le\sqrt{(\mathbb E\sigma)(\mathbb EH)}.
\]

Consequently only the mean inequality \(\mathbb E\sigma\le a\) is
required.  Young with parameter
\(\varepsilon(1-a)/a\) gives the displayed coefficient

\[
 1+{a\over\varepsilon(1-a)}.
\]

For \(\varepsilon=1/2\), this is
\((1+a)/(1-a)\le2/(1-a)\).  Hence (3.2)--(3.3) have the stated constants.

## 3. Stopping and `GDIR`

Summing

\[
 \mathbb E_i\Psi_{i+1}-\Psi_i
 \le-{h_i\over2}\Psi_i+{2H_i\over h_i}

 \sum h_i\Psi_i\le2\Psi_0+4\sum H_i/h_i.

The conversion to `GDIR` uses only
\(X_ih_i\ge\lambda_->0\).  These steps pass.

## 4. Interface coefficient

The complete-row ledger carries

\[
 {\beta_Td\over X_i}\mathcal E_i^{\rm row}.
\]

The chi-square recurrence divides mismatch by \(h_i\).  Therefore its
literal local precursor is exactly

\[
 h_i{\beta_Td\over X_i}\mathcal E_i^{\rm row},
\]

which is the coefficient in `(CHA)`.  This verifies the normalization:
there is no missing \(X_i\), \(h_i\), \(d\), or \(\beta_T\) factor.

The operator factorization (6.4)--(6.6) is a sufficient condition.  With
\(\theta=1\), the elementary two-vector inequality doubles both the row

## 5. Scope of complete-row conjugation

The complete-row theorem controls the quadratic form in the current row
scores \(g_A-g_{\tau A}\).  It does not identify the next-state response
map \(R_i^\omega\).  Equation (6.5) is therefore an additional physical
statement, not a consequence smuggled into the contraction proof.

This distinction is necessary.  In the two-point example, \(f=0\), so
every current row score vanishes, while the next centered incidence
measure is nonzero.  Its chi-square value is exactly
\(2\epsilon^2/a\).  The example validates the claim that current
conjugation alone cannot price newly created numerator mismatch.

## 6. Relation to the old normalization gate

The old auxiliary switch operator admitted arbitrary rescaling, so its
orbit scalar could not be compared with \(\beta_T\).  The new criterion
contains no such object.  Its matrix \(R_i^\omega\), denominator \(c'\),
and diagonal weight \(W_i\) are all fixed by the literal transition and
future coefficient.  Thus the former scaling counterexample does not
apply.

This does not make the new criterion automatic.  A physical response may
concentrate on one next resource, or may contain an incidence innovation
not generated by the current row-score vector.  Those possibilities are
precisely (6.5) and (6.6).
