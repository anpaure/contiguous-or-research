
For the resulting 763 fragments, require a path which uses every fragment
once, in either orientation, and whose physical word satisfies:

1. every seam is a Johnson edge;
2. every seam intersection is a rank-eight label unused by the 4,242 fixed
   internal edges, and all 762 seam labels are distinct;
3. every missing rank-10, rank-11 and rank-12 upper target has a contiguous-OR
   witness; and
4. no coordinate trace contains `010` or `0110`.

Then no such path exists for any of the 29 supplied recuts.

This is a source-relative finite theorem.  It is not an unrestricted K17
no-go, and it says nothing about a different occurrence selection, a larger
PBBS recut, or another carrier source.

## 2. Lean exact formulation

For an oriented fragment state \((i,o)\), introduce a seam variable
\(a_{i,o,j,p}\) only when the endpoint masks differ in two bits, their
intersection is absent from the fixed internal palette, and concatenating
the two oriented fragments creates no already-local `010` or `0110`.

A CP-SAT model may use one mandatory dummy node and `AddCircuit` on the
physical fragments; deleting the dummy gives one Hamilton path.  A
proof-producing PB/CNF model instead uses one start and end, the usual degree
equations, and gated nonmodular positions

\[
 a_{i,o,j,p}=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p_j=p_i+1.
\]

For each unused rank-eight label \(c\), impose

\[
 \sum_{a:\,\operatorname{intersection}(a)=c}a\le 1.
\]

Local residence is exactly recognized by the four live prefix states
\(\epsilon,0,01,011\) of the forbidden language generated by `010` and
`0110`.  Equivalently, because the forbidden words have length at most four,
one may prefilter one-seam violations and add every incompatible two-seam
arc pair and three-seam arc triple.  The three-seam case can occur only
through two singleton fragments.

Upper coverage has a lossless finite ladder normalization.  If rank-nine
masks \(W_1,\ldots,W_m\subseteq T\) form an endpoint-minimal interval with
union \(T\), put \(C_i=T\setminus W_i\) and \(d=|T|-9\).  For \(m>2\),

\[
 A=\bigcap_{i<m}C_i,\qquad B=\bigcap_{i>1}C_i
\]

are nonempty and disjoint, and every interior \(C_i\) contains
\(A\cup B\).  Since the masks are distinct,

\[
 m\le 2+\binom{9+d-|A\cup B|}{d-|A\cup B|}.
\]

Thus every realized rank-10, rank-11 or rank-12 target has an
endpoint-minimal witness of at most 2, 3 or 12 cells respectively.  It is
therefore exact to enumerate these bounded oriented ladders and require at
least one ladder per internal hole.

The complete encoding semantics are frozen in
`scratch/K17_PBBS_U_LEAN_ENCODING_SEMANTICS_20260731.md`.

## 3. Static cascade

The deterministic audit reconstructs all 29 atlases from the source rather
than trusting saved survivor files.  Every atlas has 5,005 distinct rank-nine
masks, 4,242 Johnson internal edges with distinct rank-eight intersection
labels, and no sealed `010` or `0110`.

The exact cascade is:

1. **Twenty-six rows fail at rank 10.**  Some missing rank-ten target has no
   admissible directed seam.  This is conclusive: an interval for a missing
   rank-ten target must cross a seam, and any two distinct rank-nine submasks
   of that target have union equal to the target.
2. **Rows 10 and 20 fail at rank 11.**  Both have missing target `0x23ff`,
   but the complete three-cell ladder catalogue is empty.  The three-cell
   normalization above makes this lossless.
3. **Row 24 has a forced provider conflict.**  It is the recut
   \((x,y,z)=(\mathtt{20ff},\mathtt{21f7},\mathtt{61f6})\).  Its only
   `0x23ff` ladders are

   ```text
   03fd,01ff,20ff    requiring F0 forward -> F762 forward,
   20ff,01ff,03fd    requiring F762 reverse -> F0 reverse.
   ```

   Its only `0x60ff` providers are

   ```text
   607f,20ff         requiring F0 reverse -> F762 forward,
   20ff,607f         requiring F762 reverse -> F0 forward.
   ```

   In either physical order of fragments 0 and 762, the two targets force
   opposite orientations of fragment 0.  Hence no oriented path can cover
   both targets.  The audit records the arc sets `{0,9529}` and `{1,9528}`
   and checks all four provider pairings explicitly.

Consequently the Hamilton, global rank-eight-label, and full residence
solver stages have empty input.  Running CP-SAT or SAT on these 29 rows would
be theorem-redundant.
