# K17 greedy296: guarded Rado cut, exact cost-to-go, and uniform contraction

Date: 2026-07-31  
Lane: R, flow theorem / regenerative compiler  
Status: an unconditional, explicitly scoped K17 obstruction for the complete
pure residual-\(U\) fibre; an exact Rado/min-cost theorem for a declared
serializable closed-packet atlas; and a conditional dimension-uniform
criterion for \(B(k)+O(1)\).  No K17 word and no unconditional all-\(k\)
upper bound are claimed.

## 0. Result

The occurrence-greedy296 state cannot be finished by residual \(C_6\)
circuits, even if circuits have unbounded support and are composed in an
arbitrarily long sequence.  On its fixed occurrence base there are

* 296 occurrence-labelled component-interior run obstructions; and
* 177 rank-ten targets with zero support even after relaxing the residual
  \(U\)-owners to every locally admissible pairing.

Every pure residual-\(U\) circuit leaves the first family fixed and cannot
provide the second.  Thus, for the complete pure-fibre packet ground set,

\[
 A(X_{296})=\varnothing,\qquad
 r_{M_{296}}(A(X_{296}))=0<473=|X_{296}|.                 \tag{0.1}
\]

The pair \((X_{296},\varnothing)\) is therefore a literal guarded-Rado
rank cut: the empty set is already a minimum resource separator.  This is
not a bounded-radius or greedy-descent statement.  It proves that any
successful repair of this state must change the occurrence/macro base (or
use a genuinely nonflat compiler which makes these obligations irrelevant).

The much broader marked-exchange catalogue does remove singleton rank-ten
isolation: all 1,908 current rank-ten holes have 10--45 raw providers, and
the marginal donor max-flow has value 1,908.  But its selected donor set has
owner imbalance on 4,195 owners, \(L_1=4536\), and maximum absolute
imbalance 3.  Its 545,721 entries are raw exchange columns, not closed
factor packets.  Conversely, the frozen 5,433 active incidence hexes were
generated on the old carrier, not on greedy296.  Neither object can be
silently combined with (0.1).

For a correctly rebased closed compound atlas, the exact finite test is a
hard/soft Rado test.  If \(H\) is the hard replay/host/topology bank, \(S\)
the integer-weighted appendable target bank, \(M\) the joint guard matroid
on closed compounds, and \(A_o\) the compounds serving obligation \(o\),
then the induced Rado matroid \(N\) has

\[
 r_N(Q)=\min_{Y\subseteq Q}
 \left(|Q\setminus Y|+r_M\left(\bigcup_{o\in Y}A_o\right)\right). \tag{0.2}
\]

Hard feasibility is exactly \(r_N(H)=|H|\).  Conditional on it, put
\(S_{\ge t}=\{o\in S:w(o)\ge t\}\).  The maximum declared soft weight
simultaneously served is

\[
 R=\sum_{t\ge1}
 \bigl(r_N(H\cup S_{\ge t})-|H|\bigr),                  \tag{0.3}
\]

and the minimum declared leave is \(\sum_{o\in S}w(o)-R\).  Every minimizing
set in (0.2), together with a gammoid separator when \(M\) is a strict
gammoid, is an exact checkable cut.  Formula (0.3) is exact for the declared
additive token weights.  If literal casualty sets overlap, it remains a
conservative potential unless those overlaps are unitized explicitly.

Finally, suppose a regenerative Pascal step amplifies carried debt by at
most \(\lambda\), the exact guarded ranks service a fraction \(\eta\), and
new carried casualties have slope at most \(\theta<1\) per serviced unit.
Then the carried potential satisfies

\[
 \Phi'\le \rho\Phi+B_0,qquad
 \rho=\lambda\bigl(1-(1-\theta)\eta\bigr).              \tag{0.4}
\]

The sharp uniform quantitative gate is

\[
             \boxed{\lambda\bigl(1-(1-\theta)\eta\bigr)<1}. \tag{0.5}
\]

With exact hard replay, protected deeper witnesses, a bounded exported
interface, and uniformly bounded physical and terminal literal charge,
(0.5) gives \(\nu(k)\le B(k)+O(1)\).  Packet support may grow with \(k\);
no bounded-radius descent is assumed.

## 2. Occurrence bases and residual fibres do not commute

Write a selected two-bank factor as \((M,F,R)\), where \(M\) is the chosen
macro incidence set, \(F\) is the frozen marked structure, and \(R\) is the
residual incidence lift.  At every port vertex,

\[
                         d_M+d_F+d_R=2\mathbf 1.          \tag{2.1}
\]

### Lemma 2.1 (base--fibre equation)

If an occurrence substitution changes \(M\), then a closed factor move
must also change the residual lift by

\[
                         \Delta d_R=-\Delta d_M.          \tag{2.2}
\]

A residual incidence circuit has \(\Delta d_R=0\) and is therefore a move
inside one fixed base fibre.  In particular, the 196 occurrence
substitutions are not 196 independent Rado elements which can be mixed
with circuits from a different base.

#### Proof

Subtract (2.1) before and after the move.  The marked term is frozen, hence
\(\Delta d_M+\Delta d_R=0\).  A residual alternating circuit removes and
adds one residual incidence at each visited port, so its residual degree
derivative is zero.  This proves both assertions. \(\square\)

The exact audit makes the size of this coupling visible.  Passing to
\(b_{296}\) deletes and inserts 765 macro endpoint pairs, changes macro
degree at 710 of 6,435 ports with \(L_1=744\), and changes 4,674 of the
4,872 residual-owner pair choices.  The new residual lift cancels the
boundary coordinatewise.  Reusing the old residual lift fails at exactly
the 710 changed ports.

Thus the correct order is:

1. choose one global occurrence base;
2. construct and authenticate a fresh residual lift; and only then
3. apply a Hall/Rado theorem to closed fibre compounds on that base.

## 3. Exact K17 guarded rank cut

Let \(\mathscr E^{U}_{296}\) be the complete set of finite closed compounds
formed solely from residual-\(U\) incidence circuits on the fixed base
\(b_{296}\).  There is no bound on circuit length, compound support, or
number of constituent circuits.

Let \(I_{296}\) be the 296 occurrence-labelled component-interior short-run
signatures in (1.1).  Let \(Z_{177}\) be the 177 rank-ten targets in the
zero-support payload

\[
 b383958488c70ea67ecf5678f84677c68e666cbf5eb4a1f98b7ecd48bee3f778.
                                                               \tag{3.1}
\]

This payload was computed after allowing every residual old-\(U\) owner to
use every locally incident positive-demand port.  Simultaneous degree
balance and connectivity were relaxed.  Thus it is a genuine support
superset of every pure residual circuit state.  All 177 masks remain
literal holes in \(b_{296}\); their tag profile is 90 X-only and 87 Y-only.

### Lemma 3.1 (two fibre invariants)

For every \(p\in\mathscr E^{U}_{296}\):

1. every signature in \(I_{296}\) remains; and
2. no target in \(Z_{177}\) is created.

#### Proof

A pure residual circuit changes only the residual owner--port incidences.
It does not alter the ordered interior of any fixed macro component.
Therefore every zero-bracketed short run wholly inside such an interior is
unchanged.  This proves (1), including for a compound of any length.

Every incidence used by a pure residual circuit is contained in the
arbitrary locally admissible residual-\(U\) relaxation used to define
\(Z_{177}\).  A target having no provider in that relaxation has no provider
in any of its degree-balanced, connected subfamilies.  This proves (2).
\(\square\)

### Theorem 3.2 (rank-zero cut)

Put

\[
                         X_{296}=I_{296}\sqcup Z_{177}.
\]

For every unit-debt serialization of \(\mathscr E^{U}_{296}\), every
candidate set \(A_o\) with \(o\in X_{296}\) is empty.  Hence for every
guard matroid \(M_{296}\) on the serialized compounds,

\[
 r_{M_{296}}\left(\bigcup_{o\in X_{296}}A_o\right)=0,
 \qquad
 |X_{296}|=296+177=473.                                  \tag{3.2}
\]

The pure residual fibre has Rado deficiency at least 473 and contains no
K17 accepting state satisfying residence and all rank-ten targets.

#### Proof

Lemma 3.1 says that no compound in the declared ground set serves any
obligation in \(X_{296}\).  The union of the candidate sets is empty, and
every matroid has rank zero on the empty set.  Rado's necessary inequality
for \(X_{296}\) is therefore \(0\ge473\), which fails. \(\square\)

In a strict-gammoid realization, the empty resource set separates the
empty source bank \(A(X_{296})\) from the absorber bank, so
\((X_{296},\varnothing)\) is already a sharp rank certificate.

The 296 run packets have formal replay weight 478 in the frozen audit; if
the 177 target rows are given unit weight, the declared hard-defect weight
of this cut is 655.  This is **not** automatically a lower bound of 655
appendable literal masks.  Replay/run obligations are hard physical
constraints for this compiler, and a run weight becomes a terminal literal
charge only after an explicit casualty-set theorem.

## 5. Exact static guarded-Rado theorem

Fix one authenticated base/lift \(b\).  Partition the live obligations into

* \(H\): hard replay, host, topology, protected-palette, or exported-state
  obligations which cannot be paid by appending a target mask; and
* \(S\): soft target obligations, each with an integer additive weight
  \(w(o)\ge0\).

Let \(E_b\) be a set of **closed** ordered compounds.  Each compound must
have zero final owner/lower-colour boundary, exact marked and topology
semantics, literal run/shadow replay, explicit provider-withdrawal and
deeper-witness tickets, and a terminal common-cap state or guarded cap
interface.  Intermediate switches may be neutral or uphill.  At the
declared obligation level each representative is unit-debt: it annihilates
its assigned token and leaves every other live token unchanged.  A
correlated compound which necessarily repairs or creates several tokens is
first made one grouped obligation with its entire endpoint derivative; it
may not be cloned as several independent representatives.

For each obligation \(o\), let \(A_o\subseteq E_b\) be its serving
compounds.  Suppose simultaneous physical serializability is represented
by a matroid \(M_b\) on \(E_b\).  A strict gammoid is the main useful case:
resource vertices encode incidence capacities, occurrence tickets,
providers, collars, protected witnesses, topology sockets, and cap guards.
The directed network must be splice-closed: concatenating a represented
prefix and suffix at a shared resource must still decode to an admitted
compound.  Moreover, every \(M_b\)-independent representative family must
decode to a common ordering whose endpoint derivative is the disjoint sum
of its grouped unit-debt derivatives.  Otherwise the alleged gammoid may
contain fictitious packets or omit a nonlinear interaction.

### Theorem 5.1 (Rado rank and hard cut)

Let \(N_b\) be the independence system on obligation indices in which
\(Q\) is independent exactly when its obligations have distinct
representatives \(e_o\in A_o\) and \(\{e_o:o\in Q\}\) is independent in
\(M_b\).  Then \(N_b\) is a matroid and

\[
 r_{N_b}(Q)=\min_{Y\subseteq Q}
 \left(|Q\setminus Y|+
 r_{M_b}\left(\bigcup_{o\in Y}A_o\right)\right).        \tag{5.1}
\]

In particular, all hard obligations are simultaneously repairable iff

\[
 r_{M_b}\left(\bigcup_{o\in Y}A_o\right)\ge |Y|
                         \quad\text{for every }Y\subseteq H. \tag{5.2}
\]

When (5.2) fails, a minimizing \(Y\) is an exact guarded rank cut.  If
\(M_b\) is a strict gammoid and a resource separator \(C\) of size
\(r_{M_b}(A(Y))\) is supplied together with that many disjoint routes,
then \((Y,C)\) is a sharp min-max certificate.

#### Proof

This is Rado's independent-transversal theorem and its rank formula.  For
completeness, any independently representable subset of \(Q\) has at most
\(|Q\setminus Y|\) members outside \(Y\) and at most \(r_{M_b}(A(Y))\)
inside \(Y\), giving the upper bound in (5.1).  Rado's theorem attains the
minimum simultaneously for all \(Y\).  Taking \(Q=H\), equality
\(r_{N_b}(H)=|H|\) is equivalent to (5.2).  The gammoid certificate follows
from the vertex-disjoint path/min-separator theorem. \(\square\)

### Theorem 5.2 (hard-contracted weighted leave)

Assume \(r_{N_b}(H)=|H|\), and contract \(H\) in \(N_b\).  For

\[
                         S_{\ge t}=\{o\in S:w(o)\ge t\},
\]

the maximum additive soft weight served together with all hard obligations
is

\[
 R_b=\sum_{t\ge1}r_{N_b/H}(S_{\ge t})
    =\sum_{t\ge1}\bigl(r_{N_b}(H\cup S_{\ge t})-|H|\bigr). \tag{5.3}
\]

The minimum declared leave is

\[
 L_b^*=\sum_{o\in S}w(o)-R_b.                            \tag{5.4}
\]

#### Proof

An independent set of \(N_b/H\) is exactly a soft obligation set which is
jointly representable with every hard obligation.  For an integer-weighted
matroid, the greedy theorem and the layer-cake identity give

\[
 \max_{I\text{ independent}}\sum_{o\in I}w(o)
       =\sum_{t\ge1}r(S_{\ge t}).
\]

Apply this to \(N_b/H\).  The contraction rank identity gives the second
expression in (5.3), and subtracting from total weight gives (5.4).
\(\square\)

The word "exact" in Theorem 5.2 refers to the declared additive token
model.  For literal physical cost, either the casualty sets represented by
the tokens must be disjoint, or overlapping masks must themselves be
unitized as common tokens.  Merely summing upper bounds remains safe but
need not be sharp.

### Corollary 5.3 (min-cost and cost obstruction)

If the serialization is an integral unit-capacity network, packet and
physical costs are handled by integral min-cost flow after adding a drop
arc of cost \(w(o)\) for each soft token and no drop arc for hard tokens.
For a general guard matroid, use the pair ground set \((o,e)), the
partition constraint selecting at most one representative per obligation,
and the lifted guard matroid; weighted matroid intersection gives the
minimum representative cost.  Its standard dual, or a min-cost-flow
potential/capacity dual in the network case, is a checkable proof that the
required cost exceeds the available collar/cap reserve.

This corollary is valid only for a complete unit-debt serialization.  A
compound which repairs several ungrouped obligations, or branches into
several live casualties, cannot be cloned into independent Rado elements.
It must be made one grouped obligation/compound, or retained in the full
state graph.

## 6. Global cost-to-go when unitization fails

Let \(\Gamma_b\) be the complete guarded factor-state graph on one base or
on explicitly labelled base transitions.  A vertex records the selected
incidences, occurrence base, physical order, run and target-labelled shadow
state, protected tickets, topology, and cap linkage state.  Its arcs are
literal legal switches or compiled compounds.  Let \(\mathcal A\) be the
accepting states and let \(c\ge0\) charge physical cells, collars, exported
debt, and other declared resources.  A nonadditive terminal common-cap
charge is evaluated at the terminal vertex.

Define

\[
 J(x)=\inf_{x=x_0\to\cdots\to x_s\in\mathcal A}
 \left(\sum_{i<s}c(x_i,x_{i+1})+c_{\rm term}(x_s)\right). \tag{6.1}
\]

Then

\[
 J(x)=\min_{x\to y}\bigl(c(x,y)+J(y)\bigr),             \tag{6.2}
\]

with the terminal boundary condition.  Along a minimum-cost route, \(J\)
is nonincreasing; tie it by the minimum remaining hop count to obtain a
strict lexicographic descent.  If \(J(x)=\infty\), the set reachable from
\(x\) without leaving any chosen finite sublevel is a literal directed cut
from \(x\) to \(\mathcal A\).

This is the unconditional exact fallback.  It permits globally long,
correlated compound exchanges and makes no local-radius assumption.  The
Rado and min-cost-flow theorems are tractable quotients of (6.1) only when
their unit-debt and serializability hypotheses have actually been proved.

For the current K17 state, a valid broad state must condition on one
occurrence base, rebuild the hex/long-circuit bank on that base, install a
fresh residual lift after every base move, and replay the resulting literal
word.  Combining the greedy296 base with the old 5,433-hex signatures does
not define a path in \(\Gamma_b\).

## 7. Dimension-uniform guarded contraction

We now state the exact quantitative hypothesis which would turn these
finite certificates into \(B(k)+O(1)\).

Let \(\Phi_m\) be the integer additive weight of **carried** debt in a
declared regenerative state at dimension \(m\).  Exported central,
palette, occurrence, or provider debt needed by the next Pascal step belongs
in \(\Phi_m\).  Terminal middle masks which are appended once do not.

After the Pascal lift and one authenticated occurrence/base
preconditioning, fix constants
\(\lambda>0\), \(0\le\eta\le1\), \(0\le\theta<1\), and
\(b_0,\delta,b_1\ge0\), and suppose

\[
                         \widehat\Phi_m\le\lambda\Phi_m+b_0. \tag{7.1}
\]

At that prestate let \(H_m,S_m,N_m\) be the hard bank, soft bank, and exact
Rado matroid of Theorem 5.1.  Assume uniformly:

1. **Hard acceptance:** \(r_{N_m}(H_m)=|H_m|\).
2. **Weighted rank expansion:** with
   \(S_{m,\ge t}=\{o:w(o)\ge t\}\),
   \[
   R_m:=\sum_{t\ge1}
   \bigl(r_{N_m}(H_m\cup S_{m,\ge t})-|H_m|\bigr)
       \ge\eta\widehat\Phi_m-\delta.                    \tag{7.2}
   \]
3. **Controlled new casualties:** literal replay assigns every new carried
   casualty to the selected batch and proves
   \[
                           C_m\le\theta R_m+b_1,
                           \qquad 0\le\theta<1.           \tag{7.3}
   \]
4. **Regeneration:** the output exports the same bounded interface type;
   every retired deeper target has a protected physical witness; topology,
   lower palettes, and hard host rows are exact.
5. **Physical accounting:** after the tight baseline is removed, the
   representative family attaining (7.2) has min-cost physical/cap charge
   within the declared reserve, and the physical overhead of the final
   dimension is uniformly bounded by \(c_0+c_1\Phi_m\), rather than adding
   a fresh positive constant at every recursive level.

### Theorem 7.1 (uniform contraction)

Under (7.1)--(7.3),

\[
 \Phi_{m+1}
 \le \widehat\Phi_m-R_m+C_m
 \le \alpha\widehat\Phi_m+(1-\theta)\delta+b_1,          \tag{7.4}
\]

where

\[
                         \alpha=1-(1-\theta)\eta.
\]

Consequently

\[
 \Phi_{m+1}\le\rho\Phi_m+B_0,qquad
 \rho=\alpha\lambda,qquad
 B_0=\alpha b_0+(1-\theta)\delta+b_1.                   \tag{7.5}
\]

If \(\rho<1\), then

\[
 E=\max\left\{\Phi_{m_0},\frac{B_0}{1-\rho}\right\}    \tag{7.6}
\]

is a forward-invariant bound (replace the quotient by its ceiling for
integer debt), and \(\Phi_m\) strictly decreases whenever
\(\Phi_m>B_0/(1-\rho)\).

#### Proof

The selected batch removes service weight \(R_m\) and creates at most
\(C_m\) new carried weight.  This gives the first inequality in (7.4).
Substitute (7.2) and (7.3):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_{m+1}
 &\le \widehat\Phi_m-(1-\theta)R_m+b_1\\
 &\le \bigl(1-(1-\theta)\eta\bigr)\widehat\Phi_m
       +(1-\theta)\delta+b_1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Now use (7.1) to obtain (7.5).  Iterating an affine recurrence with
\(\rho<1\) proves (7.6) and the strict-descent assertion. \(\square\)

Equivalently, when \(\lambda>0\), the quantitative expansion threshold is

\[
                    (1-\theta)\eta>1-\frac1\lambda.      \tag{7.7}
\]

In the no-proportional-casualty case \(\theta=0\), this reduces to
\((1-\eta)\lambda<1\).

### Corollary 7.2 (bounded additive physicalization)

Assume Theorem 7.1 and, after hard replay/residence/deep-shadow acceptance,
suppose the final word has terminal appendable leave and exact common-cap
defect at most \(a\Phi_m+b\).  Then

\[
             \nu(k)\le B(k)+c_0+(c_1+a)E+b= B(k)+O(1).   \tag{7.8}
\]

Here the common-cap term is the exact terminal quantity
\(V_*(Z)=\min_Q(|T|-\nu(H_Q))\).  If every child is freshly compiled,
\(V_*\) is paid once and is not a carried scalar.  If a cap is exported,
the carried state must contain the bounded guard/linkage relation, not only
the scalar \(V_*\).

#### Proof

By (7.6), \(\Phi_m\le E\).  The physical overhead is at most
\(c_0+c_1E\), and appending each remaining literal target costs at most
\(aE+b\).  All nonappendable obligations are hard and have already passed
the rank/replay conditions.  This gives (7.8). \(\square\)

A weaker invariant-region theorem is often enough: if every regenerative
prestate with \(\Phi\le E\) has full hard rank and admits a batch with
service minus new casualty at least \(\widehat\Phi-E\), then the region
\(\Phi\le E\) is invariant.  Global proportional contraction is sufficient,
not logically necessary, for an additive constant.

## 8. Precise open gate

The finite and uniform boundaries are now sharp.

* The complete pure residual-\(U\) fibre of greedy296 is excluded by the
  rank-zero cut (3.2).
* The broad rank-ten donor relaxation passes marginal Hall but is
  owner-unbalanced; balance closure is the first missing row.
* The 5,433 active balanced hexes are tied to the old base and must be
  regenerated on greedy296.
* A valid broad K17 certificate requires closed occurrence/hex compounds,
  a fresh residual lift, exact \(\mathcal R_4\times\mathcal U_{13}\) replay,
  protected ranks14--17, topology, and terminal common-cap acceptance.
* If those compounds admit a complete splice-closed unit-debt gammoid,
  (5.1)--(5.4) are the exact accepting/cost cuts.  Otherwise the exact
  object is the full state graph (6.1).
* An all-\(k\) result additionally requires the uniform weighted rank and
  casualty inequalities (7.2)--(7.3), with \(\rho<1\), on every state in a
  regenerative class.  One K17 certificate would not prove regeneration.

Thus the theorem gives a genuine K17 violated guarded cut and the requested
dimension-uniform contraction condition, while leaving no ambiguity about
the atlas to which either conclusion applies.
