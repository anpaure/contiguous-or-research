## 1. Scope

This note characterizes a large explicit face of the graph generated by
repeatedly re-enumerated unary and support-two palette-neutral circuits.  It
also proves that the displayed (k=17) factor has a root-perfect bank of 715
pairwise support-disjoint support-two circuits.

It does **not** prove that the complete fixed-resource factor fibre is
connected.  It also does not assert that an arbitrary palette-neutral circuit
merges two chronology components.  Upper rows, owner-state attachment,
residence beyond the encoded three-age flag, voltage, opening, and the lower
compiler are outside the statement.

## 2. The factor and its resource signature

There are (N=1430) rank-eight necklace roots.  A literal option at root
(Q) is a partition

\[
Q=C_0\mathbin{\dot\cup}C_1\mathbin{\dot\cup}C_2
\]

of one of the nine allowed types.  Its exact static resource signature is

\[
\sigma_Q(C_0,C_1,C_2)=
\left(
  \text{type},
  [C_0]\text{ if }|C_0|\ge2,
  [C_0\cup C_1]
\right),
\]

where brackets denote the cyclic necklace orbit.  A factor chooses one option
at every root and has the prescribed nine type masses and multiplicity one on
every rank-2 through rank-7 target orbit.

A unary circuit changes one option without changing its signature.  A
support-two circuit changes two roots and has opposite nonzero resource
deltas at them.

## 3. Exact unary product-face theorem

For a fixed exact factor (F), let

\[
\Omega_Q(F)=\{x:\ x\text{ is a literal option on }Q,
                  \ \sigma_Q(x)=\sigma_Q(F_Q)\}.
\]

### Theorem 3.1

The connected component of (F) under unary circuits alone is exactly

\[
\mathcal U(F)=\prod_Q\Omega_Q(F).
\]

Its move graph is the Cartesian product

\[
\square_Q K_{|\Omega_Q(F)|}.
\]

### Proof

Two options in the same local set \(\Omega_Q(F)\) have identical resource
signature, so their difference is a literal unary circuit.  Thus the local
graph is complete.  Unary moves on different roots have disjoint supports and
commute, so every point of the product is reachable.  Conversely, a unary
move preserves the signature at its only changed root, and therefore cannot
leave the product.  This proves both assertions.  ∎

This is a genuine connectivity theorem on an explicit fixed-resource face;
it is not a relaxation through independently incompatible menus.

## 5. The support-two tangent graph

Let (G_2(F)) be the graph on the 1430 roots in which (Q,Q') are adjacent
when at least one literal support-two circuit at the current factor changes
exactly those roots.

For the authenticated seed, complete enumeration gives

* 214,803 literal support-two circuits;
* 26,935 distinct root-pair edges;
* one connected root component;
* minimum/maximum root degree (1/370);
* six bridges and six articulation roots.

The connectedness of (G_2(F)) rules out a conserved partition of roots at
this incumbent.  It does **not** imply that the complete exact-factor state
graph is connected: after a support-two move the available root-pair graph is
re-enumerated and can change.

### Theorem 5.1 (root-perfect circuit bank)

The graph (G_2(F)) has a perfect matching of size 715.  For every matched
root pair there is an explicit pair of new literal options with opposite
nonzero resource deltas.  Applying all 715 circuits simultaneously changes
all 1430 roots and preserves every type and rank-2 through rank-7 palette row
exactly.

### Proof

The O3 audit enumerates every literal option on every root, groups nonzero
resource deltas, and creates a root edge precisely when opposite delta groups
occur on distinct roots.  Edmonds augmentation returns 715 disjoint root
pairs, which is maximum because no matching on 1430 vertices can exceed 715.

For each matched pair the certificate stores one literal option from each
opposite delta bucket.  The independent replay verifies:

1. every root occurs exactly once;
2. both new options partition the indicated root and have the stated type;
3. both deltas are nonzero and are exact negatives;
4. the decoded terminal 1430-row table has the original type masses;
5. every rank-2 through rank-7 target orbit still has multiplicity one.

The root supports are disjoint, so the circuits compose without a hidden
option collision.  ∎

## 6. Cross-SCD-chain interpretation

A rooted flag (C_0\subset C_0\cup C_1\subset Q) is a three-age local chain
fragment.

* A unary circuit slides/rethreads the cut inside one rooted chain fragment
  while retaining its named outer colours.
* A support-two circuit cuts two rooted fragments and replaces them by a
  cross-paired pair with the same combined named palette.  It is therefore an
  exact two-chain splice at the static resource level.

The perfect matching is a bank of 715 pairwise root-disjoint two-chain
splices.  Under the free \(\mathbb Z_{17}\) lift, every quotient circuit has
17 physical translates.  Thus the bank represents

\[
715\cdot17=12155
\]

physical two-chain exchanges and changes all

\[
1430\cdot17=24310
\]

physical owner flags.  Consequently the unary/support-two family has genuine
\(\Theta(W)\) **raw splice capacity** at (k=17).  The fragmentation wall is
not caused here by too few palette-neutral circuits.

## 7. What the result does and does not do to the fragmentation wall

The result answers the cardinality question positively at (k=17): a linear
number of resource-exact cross-chain splices exists in one simultaneous bank.

It does not yet answer the signed topological question.  A static
palette-neutral exchange can merge two chronology components, leave their
number unchanged, or split one, depending on its literal successor ports.
The perfect matching above was selected in the uncoloured root-pair graph and
does not certify that its 715 edges reduce fragmentation.

The exact remaining lemma is therefore:

> **Topologically signed support-two bank lemma.**  Colour each currently
> available support-two circuit by its literal effect on the SCD/owner
> successor components.  Prove that the component-reducing circuits contain
> a matching or graphic forest of rank \(\Theta(N)\), and that their physical
> translates preserve the required chronology guards.

An all-dimensional version must additionally prove a uniform positive-density
bank, not merely the finite (k=17) census.

Thus the present theorem supplies the number of splices demanded by the wall,
but not yet the guarantee that those splices all have the useful sign.
