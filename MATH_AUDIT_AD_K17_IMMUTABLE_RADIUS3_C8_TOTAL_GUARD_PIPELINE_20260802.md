and one-out at every path make the selected seam relation a permutation.

guard receives the frozen original factor, while only path geometry uses the
packet-derived factor.  Reversing these arguments would erase atomic-`C8`
casualties and is unsound.

rows or report diagnostics.  They are not used to reject a packet having a
