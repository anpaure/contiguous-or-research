## Why the short pin is exhaustive

For a word `A` with `D^3 A = T`, every physical interval of length at least
four is the union of a consecutive interval of carrier rows.  Since `0xa9fe`
is absent from the complete carrier interval-OR audit, any delivery of that
mask must therefore use a physical interval of length one, two, or three.

The named-pin event DAG enumerates every monotone three-hole P/Q schedule and
every legal placement of such a length-one-to-three pin.  Inside the pin, each
maximal envelope is capped by `0xa9fe`; every carrier row is replayed exactly.
States with equal active-row accumulators, hole counts, pin phase, and pin OR
have identical futures, so retaining the maximum selected area is exact for
this scalar-capacity question.
