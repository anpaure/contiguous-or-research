# A crossed rerouter closes the residual service Hall, but not the physical packet gate

Date: 2026-07-31  
Status: exact service theorem; complete `ML(11)` residual census; canonical
`ML(13)` comparison; scoped physical-circuit no-go; no all-size packet theorem

## 0. Verdict

The nonnested pair left by the best standard `ML(11)` nine-turn block
packing is not a new four-coordinate obstruction.  It can be absorbed by
**crossing it through one already selected nine-turn block**.

At service level, the residual pair plus that block form a four-by-four
containment graph with a perfect matching.  Every one of the six full
`ML(11)` gluing-tree endpoints which admits seven disjoint nine-turn blocks
has such a rerouter.  The same relational rerouter also absorbs the
nonnested isolated pair in the canonical `ML(13)` endpoint.

This is meaningful progress toward a bounded regenerative state:

> For the audited residuals, the service geometry is generated by the
> nine-turn three-partition block and one crossed Hall move.

It is not yet a physical second packet.  The exact coordinate patterns at
`ML(11)` and `ML(13)` are not padded copies of one another, and the frozen
root-alternating `C10` catalogue cannot realize all four required services
independently.  A physical compound still has to satisfy joint augmented
linkage, topology, negative-turn reserve, residence, deeper-provider, and
compiler-Hall guards.

## 1. The crossed Hall lemma

Let `A_0,A_1,A_2` be the three lower colours of a nine-turn block.  Its
upper colours are

\[
 B_{01}=A_0\cup A_1,
 \qquad B_{02}=A_0\cup A_2,
 \qquad B_{12}=A_1\cup A_2.                         \tag{1.1}
\]

Let `(ell,u)` be one further lower/upper defect pair, not necessarily
nested.

### Lemma 1.1 (two-crossing rerouter)

The four lower colours

\[
                 \{\ell,A_0,A_1,A_2\}
\]

and four upper colours

\[
                 \{u,B_{01},B_{02},B_{12}\}
\]

admit a containment perfect matching whenever

\[
  \ell\subseteq B_{ab}\quad\hbox{for some }a<b,
  \qquad A_c\subseteq u\quad\hbox{for some }c.       \tag{1.2}
\]

#### Proof

Match `ell` to `B_ab` and `A_c` to `u`.  Delete those two rows and columns.
The remaining graph is a two-by-two subgraph of the crown containment graph
between the `A_i` and the pairwise unions in (1.1).  If `c` is the index
omitted by `B_ab`, each remaining `A` has its evident distinct union.  If
`c` is one of `a,b`, the other member of `{a,b}` has a unique remaining
union and the omitted-index lower colour fits both remaining unions.
Either way the last two rows have a perfect matching.  Together with the
two crossed assignments this gives the required four-matching. \(\square\)

### Corollary 1.2 (minimality relative to nested unit macros)

If `ell` is not contained in `u`, one padded unit macro cannot service the
residual pair, by Proposition 5.3 of the translated-packet theorem.  Lemma
1.1 shows that two crossed assignments through one existing nine-turn block
already remove this obstruction.  Thus this is the smallest service-level
extension of the nested-unit basis for these residuals.

This is a statement about assignment geometry, not a statement that the
four matched services have compatible alternating physical realizations.

### Theorem 2.1 (`ML(11)` service closure)

For every labelled MMM gluing-tree endpoint on `ML(11)` which has a
seven-block packing, its complete 22-by-22 defect bank admits a containment
perfect matching obtained from six untouched blocks, one crossed block, and
the residual pair.

#### Proof

The unique seven-block packing matches each untouched block by its crown
containments.  Apply Lemma 1.1 to the residual pair and one of the blocks
listed above.  The eight pieces are disjoint as defect colours, so their
matchings unite to a perfect matching of the complete bank. \(\square\)

This removes the **outer service-Hall** obstruction found in the previous
audit.  It does not construct the corresponding factor switches.

## 3. The canonical `ML(13)` comparison

For the canonical `ML(13)` endpoint, the isolated pair

```text
713 -> 4843
```

is nested and therefore has a direct padded unit macro.  The other isolated
pair

```text
713,5483
```

is nonnested.  Exactly three nine-turn blocks support the crossed rerouter;
their block indices in the canonical catalogue are `59,160,161`.  Two
representative assignments are

```text
(713,1123,2249,4689) -> (6873,5483,3307,5747),
(713,1315,1609,3217) -> (3801,5483,1899,3507).
```

Thus the **same relational template** survives the next dimension.

It is not one literal padded coordinate orbit.  Canonicalize a four-service
configuration by allowing arbitrary permutation of its four pairs and
recording the multiset of coordinate columns.  The nine `ML(11)` rows give
one orbit.  After deleting one all-zero and one all-one padding coordinate,
the three `ML(13)` rows give three orbits, all disjoint from the `ML(11)`
orbit.  Therefore a universal second atom cannot be defined as only the
coordinate-padding orbit of the `ML(11)` fixture.

This does not refute a finite relational packet family or a construction
whose internal coordinates vary with the carried block.

### 4.2 The canonical `ML(11)` `C10` catalogue

For the canonical seven-block endpoint with gluing selection
`(7,8,9,11,12)`, there are 8,345 alternating `C10` circuits.  Exactly 305

Therefore the desired compound is not the union of four independently
chosen palette-monotone root `C10` units.  Longer circuits, nonmonotone
intermediate states, a coupled compound, or a different physical factor are
not excluded.

## 5. Exact second-packet gate

The service theorem identifies the right finite interface.  A physical
`q*` rerouter must realize four matched services simultaneously.  If it is
built from four `C10` units, its raw ledger is at most

```text
20 replaced middle-level factor seams,
12 negative turn-occurrence units on each shore.
```

Those scalar bounds are not sufficient.  A valid packet must additionally
export and pass:

1. a joint floor-tight augmented linkage (or exact subset-state matching
   table), rather than four individually improving units;
2. legal ordered/subset topology for the physical factor;
3. declared reserve for all negative turn units;
4. the residence boundary automaton;
5. the negative-part provider inequalities at every protected deeper rank;
6. the common-cap lower compiler Hall inequalities.

The finite general-construction target can now be stated sharply:

> Construct a bounded guarded compound implementing Lemma 1.1 for every
> admissible residual/block incidence type, or prove that the stateful MMM
> recursion emits only a finite subfamily of such types and reserve one
> guarded compound for each.

The audit proves that the relational crossed template handles all residual
geometries presently exposed at `ML(11)` and the canonical `ML(13)` endpoint.
It also proves that a single literal padded orbit and the simplest
independent-`C10` implementation do not suffice.
