## 3. Seven rank-five witnesses cannot be covered

Only old p and q bundles, old t bundles, old XXX self triples,
new V bundles, and new U bundles can cover a W_L. The full orbit
and every other permitted type cover none. Their combined capacity
for distinct witness orbits is therefore at most

    p+q+2t+XXX+v+u
      =17-r-z-u
      <=5.                                         (5)

The equality uses (1) and (4). Nonnegativity gives r+z+u>=11+v+r,
and (4a) makes this an even integer, hence at least twelve. This
sharpens the earlier bound of six witnesses to five. Seven distinct
W_L orbits are required, contradicting (5). Thus no bank in Section
1's enlarged family has charge 2368.

The argument excludes the old one-full-four-row design even after
adding every admissible affine diamond bundle. It does not exclude
different endpoint budgets, non-affine copied bundles, a different
full-orbit family, or banks outside the prescribed translation and
complement closure.
