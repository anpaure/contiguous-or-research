## Verdict

The facet theorem has a large and reproducible effect on branching: by
5,000 conflicts it reduces decisions by 84–89 percent in both seeds.  It
also shifts later work into propagation, so the CPU gain is much smaller
and not seedwise monotone.  The two-seed mean is mildly favorable at both
budgets, while memory is effectively neutral.

It is therefore reasonable to retain the facet-enabled Type-I job, but this
small bounded sample does not establish a robust end-to-end solution-time
speedup.  A facet-off control remains useful.
