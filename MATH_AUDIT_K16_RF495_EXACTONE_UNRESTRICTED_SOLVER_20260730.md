middle-row or `D^3` condition.  The frozen DIMACS instance is

## Independent semantic audit

`scratch/audit_rf495_exactone_semantic_decoder_20260730.py` (SHA-256
`1a4ed2d097c2cd6206b8ad43f2a699789b960985428d92ee6d3078a6b55ebf37`)
does not import the builder or decoder.  It independently:

1. reconstructs the two hash-bound parent bodies and the exact 18-cell free
   partition of the 12,873 positions;
2. replays all fixed-only intervals, obtaining 65,465 covered masks and
   exactly 70 residual targets;
3. enumerates every physical free-touching interval compatible with a
   residual target, obtaining 6,089 physical rows and 6,081 semantic
   `(target,fixed_or,Q)` classes;
4. partitions those classes into exactly 4,900 `(target,Q)` fibres and proves
   that every fibre has a unique greatest fixed OR;
5. checks that those 4,900 greatest providers are exactly the selector table;
6. checks the needed/forbidden-bit semantics and the decoder's exact
   body/free layout.

The resulting audit is

`scratch/k16_rf495_exactone_maximal_cnf_20260730/rf495_exactone_4900.semantic_decoder.audit.json`

with SHA-256

`f41750b98917486c2975fb73500a07e290ce518349061eb3b6c9e9bffaabe7fd`

and payload SHA-256

`8a9bcf710ffeeee44f91e2d99f0c0e29f5cfabfe6096cacab5b32cabfec8952b`.

Thus the formula is equisatisfiable with this frozen collar fibre.  On the
forward side, a selected provider makes its physical interval OR exactly its
target.  On the reverse side, any literal delivery can be replaced by the
unique greatest provider in the same `(target,Q)` fibre, which only weakens
needed-bit demands.

For a SAT event, a second decoder is already frozen at
`scratch/decode_verify_k16_rf495_exactone_independent_20260730.py` (SHA-256
`1e93844148c6ed952aef1027eadc293a1dd6f1164e2c0a3f010d8f69d5b93b0c`).
It deliberately ignores the selector map: it reads only variables 1..288,
reconstructs the two bodies from the pinned seed-5 hash, and performs its own
literal ending-OR replay.  Its emitted word must agree byte-for-byte with the
primary decoder.

## Acceptance

SAT is not accepted from a solver line alone.  It must be decoded and replayed
against every mask `1..65535` at literal length 12,873.  The only accepting
status is `PASS_LITERAL_65535_OF_65535`; no middle-row gate is allowed.

UNSAT is not accepted without an independently replayed DRAT proof.  Timeout,
resource failure, or an incomplete proof is `UNKNOWN`.

## Exact fallback faces

Six exact lead-centered subfaces were generated by leaving either one or two
of the three collar chains free.  The independent prefix/unit auditor
`scratch/audit_k16_rf495_chain_repair_faces_independent_20260730.py`
(SHA-256 `7cd0c1884e4f71242da1f7acb227816c2df87fd42084b841af444fa3ea3524e5`)
checks all six DIMACS headers, the unchanged 175,462-clause base prefix, and
every pinned lead-cell unit.  Its audit SHA-256 is
`a9d0145ff3f84b306ea29d0d4b4c1413b99ba77387d3e2da5b22ae1a37dec2a3`.

The smallest face, in which only the left four cells may move, is already
closed without SAT.  With the other two chains fixed, exactly seventeen
residual masks remain.  The cellwise closure normalization

`cl_R(v) = intersection { T in R : v subset T }`

preserves every delivery of a target in `R`.  Its domain has thirty values,
so all `30^4 = 810,000` normalized left-chain assignments were enumerated.
None covers all seventeen targets, even using all thirty-eight raw physical
provider types.  The result
`scratch/k16_rf495_chain_repair_branches_20260730/left4.closure_census.result.txt`
has SHA-256
`1dc342e495ee08bac5e0497d671627b470da5b23e7c8275ae41aa37d48f91dcf`.
An independent Python implementation also checked all 810,000 states and
returned `UNSAT`.  Therefore any RF495 completion must change at least one
cell outside the left chain; this is a solver-free face theorem, not a global
RF495 no-go.
