# Passive terminal types can be erased from the final OR-word proof

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Method:** pure mathematics; no computation, search, or solver  
**Status:** unconditional semantic reduction and sharp boundary theorem.
For the deterministic aligned `Ibc/Ica` bundle bank, terminal acceptance of
the polarity code is not an additional physical condition in a final
universal-OR-word proof **once the bundle has no remaining non-target
service**.  It can then be installed by a conservative state extension, or
bypassed altogether by a direct literal-terminal argument.
The remaining condition is one-state physical coexistence of the exact
bundle footprints and the residual compiler/background.  Prescribed sink
identity, a physical type-dependent guard, and a type-dependent future
continuation cannot be erased.

## 0. Result

The definition of a universal OR word refers only to a word, interval
addresses, and their Boolean OR values.  It contains no terminal type.
Terminal types enter the current common-cap/Rado architecture for two
reasons:

1. to prevent an unlabelled flow from permuting noninterchangeable sinks;
2. to remember the two atoms of a folded-C8 Boolean diamond after its exact
   ray witnesses have been attached to a native nested socket.

Both reasons disappear from the **final literal target certificate** for a
bank with one deterministic complete physical bundle per logical ticket.
There is no representative or sink permutation left for that certificate to
choose, and the exact ray values remain certified at their upstream
occurrences.  The cut label and prefix/suffix role determine the polarity
bit.  This does not prove that a pre-existing typed router accepts the
deterministic bundle: if the socket is still used to carry a gain,
compensation, topology, or continuation service, its type may remain active.

Consequently, fix one phase and one literal cap/guard state.  Suppose that

* every aligned ray ticket has its two exact upstream occurrences;
* its displayed native port--owner--q1 route exists physically;
* the complete bundle bank is mutually capacity-compatible after identical
  occurrence facts have been coalesced; and
* the complementary compiler/background is feasible in that same literal
  state.

Then the aligned ticket bank is complete without a further axiom saying
that the cap "accepts" the product type.  The polarity code may be adjoined
as passive metadata, and doing so changes neither the word nor any literal
constraint.

This removes only the **local deterministic ray-terminal acceptance** row.
It does not construct the common literal state, the complementary matching,
or a regenerative continuation.

## 1. Physical records and passive terminal data

Fix a literal word `A` and a complete physical state `c`.  The state fixes
the phase, interval addresses, cap letters, guards, endpoints, and every
unit-capacity occurrence resource.

A complete physical bundle is a record

\[
             b=(i,c,E_b,\Phi_b),                     \tag{1.1}
\]

where `i` is its logical ticket, `E_b` is its finite set of occurrence and
route capacities, and `Phi_b` is the conjunction of all literal facts and
physical constraints asserted on those capacities.  In particular, a
target occurrence contributes a fact

\[
                    (e,\operatorname{OR}_A(e),c).    \tag{1.2}
\]

Let `pi(b)` denote this type-free physical record.

### Definition 1.1 (passive terminal signature)

A terminal signature `tau(b)` is **passive** on a family of complete
bundles when

1. it is a deterministic function of `pi(b)` and the already retained
   logical ticket label;
2. adjoining it adds no membership, absence, endpoint, guard, chronology,
   or capacity condition to `Phi_b`;
3. no terminal resource is selected by optimizing over that signature; and
4. every final or downstream legality predicate factors through the
   type-free record: for each such predicate `P` there is a predicate
   `bar P` with

   \[
                 P(b,\tau(b))=\overline P(\pi(b)).     \tag{1.3}
   \]

   In particular, there is no independently prescribed allowed-type set
   which may reject `tau(b)` while accepting the same physical record with
   another label.

The signature may contain a cut label, a phase label, an ordered ray role,
and a polarity bit.  Condition 4 is automatic at the end of a final
OR-word proof.  It is a substantive premise at an inductive interface.

### Theorem 1.2 (conservative state extension)

Let `F` be any family of mutually feasible complete physical bundle banks,
where feasibility depends only on the type-free records `pi(b)`.  Extend a
state `c` to

\[
       \widetilde c=(c,\{\tau(b):b\text{ selected}\})                \tag{1.4}
\]

using passive signatures, and record exactly the deterministic signature of
every selected bundle in a newly adjoined bookkeeping field.  Projection

\[
                         \widetilde c\longmapsto c                    \tag{1.5}
\]

is a bijection between feasible extended banks and feasible physical
banks.  It preserves the word, every interval OR, the maximal common cap,
every guard, and every unit-capacity load.

#### Proof

Existence and uniqueness of the lift follow from Definition 1.1(1).  By
items 2 and 3, the added signature introduces neither a literal constraint
nor a new choice or capacity.  Thus a bank is feasible before the lift if
and only if its unique labelled lift is feasible afterward.  Item 4 makes
the same statement true for the complete terminal construction rather than
only for its current layer.  All named physical objects are components of
`c` or of `pi(b)` and are therefore fixed by projection. \(\square\)

This is not an assertion that arbitrary terminal types may be relabelled.
It says that a deterministic label which has no physical consumer may be
recorded without creating a new existence gate.

## 2. Direct terminal elimination

The conservative-extension theorem still mentions an accepted code.  At a
final OR word even that bookkeeping can be erased.

Let `L` be the strict-lower target family and `H subset L times C(A)` the
sound target/occurrence graph in the fixed state.  Let `B={b_i:i in I}` be
a deterministic complete bundle bank.  Inside the bundles, let

\[
 E_* = \{(Z_a,e_a):a\in A_*\}\subseteq H                \tag{2.1}
\]

be pairwise target- and occurrence-disjoint exact facts.  Let `C_*` be
their occurrence set, `L_*` their target set, and let `B_*` be every other
bundle capacity unavailable to an ordinary compiler assignment after all
identical occurrence facts have been coalesced.

### Theorem 2.1 (direct literal-terminal theorem)

Assume:

1. every target identity used by a bundle is certified at an exact literal
   occurrence retained in that same bundle;
2. the type-free physical records `pi(b_i)` are mutually feasible in one
   state `c`;
3. the ticket-to-bundle relation is deterministic;
4. all ordinary roles sharing a bundle occurrence assert the identical
   occurrence fact and are admitted in one compound record; and
5. the residual graph

   \[
                         H-(L_*\cup C_*\cup B_*)        \tag{2.2}
   \]

   has a matching saturating `L minus L_*`.

Then the union of the deterministic bundle bank, its compound facts, and
the residual matching is a literal compiler certificate for every target
in `L`.  No terminal-type acceptance predicate is needed.

If all other owner, residence, upper-witness, topology, and opening
conditions of the OR-word construction hold in `A`, the same `A` is a
valid final universal OR word regardless of whether terminal signatures
are retained or erased.

#### Proof

Let `M_0` be the matching in (2.2).  Then

\[
                         M=M_0\cup E_*                  \tag{2.3}
\]

is a matching saturating `L`.  The forced facts and their bundle roles
coinstantiate by literal occurrence-fact coalescence; every remaining
bundle capacity has already been charged in the mutually feasible
type-free bank, and `M_0` avoids `B_*`.  Thus all lower targets have exact
literal witnesses in one common cap state.

The other physical hypotheses are properties of `A` and its interval
occurrences.  A terminal signature is absent from the definition of a
universal OR word, so erasing it changes none of them. \(\square\)

### Why the Rado type condition disappears

In the terminal two-Rado theorem, a type is necessary because a gammoid
selects representatives and may connect a source to the wrong sink.  In
Theorem 2.1 there is one preselected complete bundle per ticket and no
representative selection.  Thus neither an unlabelled sink permutation nor
a product-of-marginals error is possible.  The deterministic full bundle,
not an untyped aggregate flow, carries the ticket correlation.

Likewise, the type-homogeneity premise in the regular factor-router theorem
remains necessary for its max-flow proof: integrality may choose any
incident port.  Theorem 2.1 does not erase types from that nondeterministic
router.  It bypasses that router only for the already deterministic bank.

## 3. Specialization to the aligned `Ibc/Ica` bank

Fix a phase `epsilon` and the exact antecedent `A^epsilon` from the aligned
ambient theorem.  For `1<=j<d`, that theorem supplies the exact upstream
occurrences

\[
 X_j^\epsilon=J\cup\{x_\epsilon\}\cup P_j,
 \qquad
 Y_j^\epsilon=J\cup\{y_\epsilon\}\cup S_j,          \tag{3.1}
\]

and the deterministic native route

\[
 p_{j-1}(s),\quad o_{j-1}(s),\quad q_{j-1}(s).       \tag{3.2}
\]

The complete records are pairwise occurrence-disjoint over `j`.  Their
native facts are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 R_j^\epsilon&=\operatorname{OR}(p_{j-1}),\\
 T_j^\epsilon&=\operatorname{OR}(o_{j-1}),\\
 U^\epsilon&=\operatorname{OR}(q_{j-1}).
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{3.3}
\]

The cut `j`, phase `epsilon`, block side, and ordered prefix/suffix ray role
determine the polarized-chain code.  Therefore that code is a deterministic
signature of the complete record.  The exact values (3.1), rather than the
native chain values, retain literal target identity.

### Corollary 3.1 (aligned typed-acceptance elimination in the direct
literal branch)

For one fixed phase, replace the former premise

> the terminal state accepts the polarized native code

by the following purely physical premise:

> one literal cap/guard state contains the exact aligned ray occurrences,
> the deterministic native bundle bank, and a complementary compiler/
> background satisfying the forced-edge contraction of Theorem 2.1.

Then all `d-1` aligned tickets are simultaneously complete as final literal
compiler records.  Their polarity fields may be reconstructed uniquely or
erased.  No independent typed terminal-acceptance theorem remains **in this
direct-literal presentation**.

#### Proof

The aligned ambient theorem gives hypotheses 1--3 of Theorem 2.1 and the
pairwise physical compatibility internal to the displayed bank.  Literal
fact coalescence gives hypothesis 4.  The stated complementary compiler is
hypothesis 5.  Apply Theorem 2.1.  If a later exposition wants the
polarized-chain code, attach it by Theorem 1.2. \(\square\)

This corollary does **not** identify phase `P` and phase `Q`.  Their equal
address templates generally have different Boolean values, and their
pointwise union is only a containment cap.  If a proof demands both phases
inside one state, their literal common-state coexistence remains a physical
premise.  What disappears is only an additional acceptance test on the
already materialized deterministic code.

### Corollary 3.2 (direct-ray compiler bypass)

For a final fixed phase, the native socket bank is not needed merely to
certify the aligned ray targets.  The `2(d-1)` forced edges

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{\rm ray}^\epsilon=
 \{&(X_j^\epsilon,X_j^\epsilon\text{-address}),
      (Y_j^\epsilon,Y_j^\epsilon\text{-address}):
      1\le j<d\}
 \end{aligned}                                       \tag{3.4}
\]

form a literal matching already realized by `A^epsilon`.  Hence a final
compiler may contract `E_ray^epsilon` directly and solve only the
complementary target/cell problem.  Neither a native terminal socket nor a
terminal type is required for this direct target-coverage row.

#### Proof

Within the prefix family, the strict inclusions

\[
                         P_1\subset\cdots\subset P_{d-1}
\]

make the target values distinct.  The suffix family is distinct in the
opposite order, and a prefix target contains `x_epsilon` whereas a suffix
target contains the different label `y_epsilon`.  Thus all target rows in
(3.4) are distinct.  The aligned theorem proves that their physical
addresses are pairwise distinct and that `A^epsilon` realizes every stated
OR equality simultaneously.  Therefore (3.4) is a matching of sound
literal edges.  Forced-edge contraction proves the claim. \(\square\)

The native bank may still be useful for an independent compensation,
topology, or routing interface.  If it is retained for such a purpose,
Corollary 3.1 removes its passive terminal-acceptance row but keeps its
whole physical footprint charged.  Corollary 3.2 does not discard a socket
that another physical theorem genuinely uses.

In the present global common-cap architecture the native ports and suffixes
do have such a non-target role: they are the two occurrence coordinates of
the gain/compensation linkage used to *prove* the complementary compiler.
Accordingly, Corollary 3.2 is not by itself a replacement for the terminal
two-Rado or regular-factor router.  It becomes a replacement only after the
complementary literal matching in Theorem 2.1(5) has been established by a
different argument.

## 4. Sharp active-type obstruction

Passive terminal data can be erased; active terminal data cannot.

### Proposition 4.1 (wrong-sink no-go)

There are two tickets with a full untyped linkage but no typed linkage.

#### Proof

Take starts `s_1,s_2` and terminal occurrences `t_1,t_2`.  The only two
routes are

\[
                         s_1\longrightarrow t_2,
 \qquad                  s_2\longrightarrow t_1.      \tag{4.1}
\]

They are vertex-disjoint, so the untyped linkage has value two.  Give
`t_i` the externally prescribed type `i`, and require ticket `i` to end at
type `i`.  Neither route is accepted.  Relabelling the terminal occurrences
would change the prescribed physical specification, not extend it
conservatively. \(\square\)

The same obstruction occurs when a type selects a different phase, guard,
endpoint class, literal value, or future transition.  A logical bit cannot
create a missing physical route or change an interval OR.

### Proposition 4.2 (continuation boundary)

Suppose two extended terminal records have the same type-free physical
projection, but exactly one admits a required next-stage transition.  Then
terminal type is not passive and no projection-only terminal theorem can
preserve extendability.

#### Proof

The two records are indistinguishable after projection but have different
truth values for the existence of a legal continuation.  Any theorem based
only on the projection gives the same verdict for both and is wrong on one
of them. \(\square\)

This is the contextual-equivalence boundary of the folded-C8 theorem in
its minimal form.

## 5. The weakest remaining premise

For a **final** OR-word proof, the aligned bank needs no terminal-type
premise.  The weakest proof-safe condition is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{one fixed phase and one literal cap/guard state;}\\
\text{exact upstream target occurrences;}\\
\text{one deterministic, mutually compatible complete bundle per ticket;}\\
\text{fact coalescence followed by a residual compiler/background matching.}
\end{gathered}}
\tag{5.1}
\]

For a **regenerative** induction, add exactly one condition:

\[
\boxed{
 \text{every required child continuation factors through the type-free
 physical record, or its active type is carried and proved compatible.}}
\tag{5.2}
\]

Thus the local aligned terminal gate is eliminated on the direct-literal
final branch.  The live correlated gate is physical common-state/background
coexistence.  On the existing gain/compensation-router branch, typed socket
legality remains active until that router has actually produced the
complementary matching; (5.2) is additionally required when the socket state
is exported to a later lift.
