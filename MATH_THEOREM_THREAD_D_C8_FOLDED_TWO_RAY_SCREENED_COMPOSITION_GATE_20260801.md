# The folded C8 two-ray provider does not close by literal boundary wrapping

Date: 2026-08-01  
Lane: Thread D, quotient-folded C8 physicalization  
Status: exact source/common-cap composition theorem and a sharp scoped
no-go.  The abstract terminal birail Hall problem is feasible with zero
defect; owner-legal host planting and matching-closed regeneration remain
open.

## 0. Verdict

Let `A^- , A^+` be an aligned quotient-folded Hamilton-path source pair at
depth `d`.  Its directed support differences are the two full rays

\[
\begin{aligned}
 P^-&=\{\{z,a_3\}\cup F[1,j]:1\le j<d\},&
 S^-&=\{\{z,a_1\}\cup F[t,d]:1<t\le d\},\\
 P^+&=\{\{z,a_1\}\cup F[1,j]:1\le j<d\},&
 S^+&=\{\{z,a_3\}\cup F[t,d]:1<t\le d\}.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{0.1}
\]

These are exactly the `P` and `G_d` rows of the seven-host menu.  The
isolated two-host gadget therefore has the correct **provider** signature.
It is not, by itself, a composition with `A^\pm`.

Take the literal opposite near cells and screen them on both sides by their
common unions.  This gives a pointwise-common prefix signature, a
pointwise-common suffix signature, and an exact pointwise common cap, but
for every `d>=3` it still leaves

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat D^-\setminus\widehat D^+
   ={}&\{\{z,a_3\}\cup F[1,j]:2\le j<d\}\\
     &\dot\cup\{\{z,a_1\}\cup F[t,d]:2\le t<d\},\\
 \widehat D^+\setminus\widehat D^-
   ={}&\text{the same two families with }a_1,a_3\text{ swapped}.
                                                               \tag{0.2}
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the exact directed residue is `2(d-2)` on each side.  It vanishes for
`d=2` and is nonzero for every `d>=3`.  The natural screened wrapper is not
a full source reset.

The positive news is that (0.2) is already on the zero-defect birail Hall
boundary.  Its marginal threshold multisets have the form

\[
        \{0^{d-2},1,\ldots,d-2\}
\]

on both shores.  Hence the antitone-pairing theorem gives abstract terminal
deficiency zero.  What remains is purely physical: realize those rows in an
owner-legal, matching-closed common-cap state, repair the cut colours, and
recycle the host state rather than paying a fresh constant for every serial
comparator.

## 1. Literal screened composition

Suppress the fixed core and write

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 L^- =\{z,a_3,f_1\},&L^+=\{z,a_1,f_1\},\\
 R^- =\{z,a_1,f_d\},&R^+=\{z,a_3,f_d\},
\end{array}                                                \tag{1.1}
\]

with phase-common caps

\[
                  X_L=L^-\cup L^+,
              \qquad X_R=R^-\cup R^+.                    \tag{1.2}
\]

Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat A^-&=X_L,L^+,X_L\mid A^-\mid X_R,R^+,X_R,\\
 \widehat A^+&=X_L,L^-,X_L\mid A^+\mid X_R,R^-,X_R.
                                                               \tag{1.3}
\end{aligned}
\]

The first `X_L` makes the complete prefix signatures pointwise equal; the
last `X_R` does the same for suffixes.  The inner screens separate the
opposite near cells from the inherited source.  At every inherited address
use the cap `A^-_p union A^+_p`; at the six new addresses use `X_L` or
`X_R`.  This proves literal common-cap containment.

### Theorem 1.1 (exact residual)

The two support decks of (1.3) satisfy exactly (0.2).

#### Proof

Intervals lying wholly inside `A^\pm` contribute (0.1).  The opposite near
cells supply the two endpoint members `j=1` and `t=d`.  Any interval which
continues past a near cell into the inherited word also contains the inner
common screen, so it contains both `a_1,a_3` and cannot equal a member of
either one-label ray.  The outer common screens make all intervals meeting
the external boundary phase common and remove the three exterior composite
values generated by the unscreened order.  Therefore precisely the members
with `2<=j,t<d` remain.  These families are disjoint by their active label
and filler endpoint, proving (0.2).  The replay checks the complete interval
deck, not only this subtraction argument.  \(\square\)

If the phase-specific cells are moved outside their common screens, the
same internal residue remains but the prefix and suffix signatures cease to
be pointwise equal.  Thus simply reversing the two host cells trades an
external-signature failure for no improvement in the internal residue.

## 2. Why the isolated host theorem does not compose automatically

The isolated gadget has the form

\[
 X_L,L^\epsilon,f_2,\ldots,f_{d-1},R^\epsilon,X_R.       \tag{2.1}
\]

Inside (2.1), the displayed filler rail joins the two bases directly, so its
only phase differences are the desired two rays.  In (1.3), the folded
source already contains its own oriented rail.  Appending a second provider
without auditing the seam introduces

\[
          \operatorname{Suf}(A^\epsilon)
          \vee\operatorname{Pref}(H^{1-\epsilon}),       \tag{2.2}
\]

and the analogous exterior rows.  Internal deck cancellation says nothing
about (2.2).  Exact composition requires pointwise screened prefix/suffix
rows at every join.  Formula (0.2) is the residue after imposing the
strongest literal `P/G_d` screens in (1.3).

Consequently a positive zero/constant-length construction needs one of two
new objects:

1. an opposite-signed **interior** corridor which reuses an existing filler
   rail and satisfies all pointwise seam screens; or
2. a guarded physical matching which treats (0.2) as terminal obligations
   rather than demanding literal deck equality.

The first is a source/owner construction.  The second is precisely the
birail compiler route of Section 3.

## 3. Terminal Hall is not the obstruction

Put `h=d-2`.  The two residual chains in (0.2) give marginal threshold
multisets

\[
                  \{0^h,1,\ldots,h\}                    \tag{3.1}
\]

on both sides, with `N=2h`.  Thus

\[
                  E_L=E_R=h,
             \qquad E_L+E_R-N=0.                        \tag{3.2}
\]

By the antitone-birail theorem, some pairing has zero corner deficiency, and
concordance-removing `2x2` switches monotonically reach it.  Equations
(3.1)--(3.2) are an abstract integral statement.  They do not create the
physical cells, make the six wrapper positions owner legal, or show that one
common matching survives both phases.

The exact remaining physical hypothesis is therefore:

> **Matching-closed folded comparator.**  Expose the two occurrence-labelled
> ray banks (full (0.1), or truncated (0.2)) in one common-Q state; realize
> every antitone swap by an owner-, palette-, upper- and residence-safe
> physical move; assign the one lower cut casualty and any upper cut casualty
> to fixed boundary cells; and return the same host/cap state after the move.

If that hypothesis is proved, the terminal two-ray compiler has no Hall
defect.  A fresh `+O(1)` wrapper per comparator is not enough: the state must
be recycled, because the number of concordance-removing switches is not
bounded.

## 4. Scope

This theorem closes only the literal `P/G_d` boundary-wrapper composition.
It does **not** rule out:

* a new opposite-signed corridor supported inside the folded source;
* a nonflat variable-deadline host;
* an occurrence-permuted common-cap matching; or
* terminal rather than literal source-deck completion.

It also does not use the erroneous implication “an isolated provider is a
contextual closure.”  The owner, lower/upper palette, topology and residence
claims remain those of the independently audited quotient-folded carrier.
