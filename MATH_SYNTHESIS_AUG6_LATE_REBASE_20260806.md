## 1. Retractions which must remain visible

Two attractive shortcuts are false.

1. The old private-switch/FSW argument applied a resource-layer resolvent
   to a scalar FIFO covariance. The expressions were not type-correct, and
   the symmetric \(K_3\) example shows that the claimed switch domination
   is false even under regularity.
2. In the odd terminal relay, the row \(11\) is selected nonquiet:
   \(02\leftrightarrow11\). Priority therefore does not pass from \(p_1\)
   to a prepared \(p_2\), and the advertised three-coordinate path leaves
   the actual matching contraction at its first forced edge.

The authoritative replacements are respectively the future-service
small-cap programme and the literal full-matching beta paths below.

## 2. Bottom analytic gate

### 2.1 What is now closed

The affine insertion-position obstruction is real: every bounded-local
order generator loses \(\Theta(d^2)\) on the affine mode. It is nevertheless
paid exactly by future service. On the stopped small-cap interval,

\[
 \frac{\beta_i}{X_i}
 \le \frac{C\varepsilon_P}{d^2}(1-\rho_i^2),
\]

so the missing \(d^2\) is recovered by service drift rather than a path
Poincaré inequality.

For the moving adapted mark, the exact square expansion produces:

- favorable future service;
- a quadratic innovation;
- a killed correlation \(K_1\);
- a signed one-root mean-hazard term.

The quadratic innovation is bounded by

\[
 C\bigl(d^2\mathfrak G+(1+d\beta)V_{\rm root}\bigr).
\]

After the \(\varepsilon_P/d^2\) occupation coefficient this is a small
multiple of GDIR plus root carré-du-champ. Ordinary Young,

\[
 -K_1\le D_{\rm kill}+Q_{\rm kill},
\]

closes \(K_1\): \(D_{\rm kill}\) costs only
\(O(\varepsilon_P/d)\) of service and \(Q_{\rm kill}\) is the same
quadratic innovation. No speculative FE3 routing is needed for \(K_1\).

The potential-cap bootstrap is also noncircular. If

\[
 D+H\le C_0A+C_1I,\qquad
 I\le C_2\varepsilon_P(A+D+H),
\]

then choosing \(\varepsilon_P\) first absorbs \(D,H,I\) simultaneously.
The first-violation argument gives a constant-probability PCAP-survival
event after the separator constant is chosen. The annealed
quarantine--Haxell theorem may retain that terminal event at constant cost.

Finally, HDIR is no longer an independent analytic row. The exact
Bonferroni decomposition, the PCAP coefficient, and FE3 give, on the
root-omitted base family,

\[
 \operatorname{HDIR}
 \le C\theta\,\operatorname{GDIR}
   +C\frac{\varepsilon_P}{\theta}\operatorname{ROOT}
   +CA+o(A).
\]

The proof removes distinguished roots before identifying coefficients,
retains the deterministic product remainder, separates slot pair classes,
and uses FE3's \(1/d\) spare to pay exactly the factor \(d\) in
\(\mathcal B^2\le Cd\mathcal B\).

### 2.2 Exact remaining bottom statement

The live analytic row is GDIR, equivalently the adaptive actual-vector
Johnson resolvent perturbation \(JRES/JSW\), together with finitely many
marked occurrence conventions. A proof must control the predictable
compensator and weighted bracket of the current-versus-pristine operator.
It may not use the false uniform sector norm; affine \(E_1\) and even
\(E_2\) modes refute that norm by \(\Theta(d)\).

A promising stronger formulation is the normalized chi-square potential

\[
 \Psi_T=\frac{\mathfrak G_T}{\beta_T}
 =\sum_x\frac{(g_x^\circ-\beta_TY_x)^2}{\beta_TY_x}.
\]

Its future-service rate is exactly the GDIR normalization \(1/X\). If the
adapted numerator/denominator drift has only the already closed HDIR,
root, and \(\varepsilon_P\)-small innovation terms, GDIR self-absorbs and
the resolvent perturbation can be bypassed. That identity is being checked;
it is not asserted here.

## 3. PBBS native payload gate

### 3.1 Ranks already closed

On an untouched height-\(d+1\) hook component the exact root recurrence and
mandatory core are

\[
 q_{i+1}=q_i-(2z_i+1),\qquad
 F_i=\{q_i,q_{i+1}+1\}.
\]

This gives:

1. every singleton on one transitive hook component;
2. every pair, using a zero-terminal envelope for cyclic distance at least
   three, a \(z=1\) cell for distance two, and two consecutive \(z=0\)
   cells for adjacent pairs;
3. every cyclic-consecutive target of ranks \(1,\ldots,d\).

The angle bank supplies superpolynomially many components per fixed small
target, so the singleton and pair atlases admit distinct-component
selection without changing any owner or immediate-palette edge.

### 3.2 The single-hook interval route is false

For a path of length \(\ell\le d\) and cost \(\sum z_i\le b=m-d-1\), the
mandatory emitted set is

\[
 M=\{q_0,\ldots,q_{\ell-1}\}
   \cup\{q_1+1,\ldots,q_\ell+1\}.
\]

A target \(S\) would be carried whenever

\[
 M\subseteq S\subseteq\bigcup_iP_i,
\]

where \(P_i\) are the hook survivor envelopes. The full envelope algebra is
now known:

\[
 P_{i+1}=P_i-\{y_i\}+\{y_i-1\}.
\]

Hence \(\bigcup_iP_i=P_0\cup\{q_1,\ldots,q_{\ell-1}\}\); successive
envelopes do not supply independent fresh banks.

This exposes a sharp parity obstruction. Every maximal dimer
\(\{a,a-1\}\) forces the slide \(a\to a-1\). In a pure-dimer target, an
odd outgoing distance makes that dimer terminal. Therefore

\[
 S=\{0,-1,-4,-5,-8,-9\}
\]

has no native single-hook interval ticket for odd \(n\ge13\): its first
two outgoing dimer distances are both three, so two different dimers would
have to be terminal. The proposed component Hall theorem is false even
before multiplicity.

The corrected PBBS target is a parity-changing extension—a second action
partition, protected two-hook seam, or equivalent packet—which can change
orientation between forced dimer slides while preserving the owner/q1
factor and depth-\(d\) antecedent. The largest-gap calculation proves that
cost is favorable whenever the parity condition holds; parity, not budget,
is the remaining obstruction.

## 4. Odd finite interface

### 4.1 Collar scheduling

The protected first connector is paid by the zipper split: setup changes

### 4.2 Local beta dynamics

The false relay path has a literal replacement in the actual matching
contraction. With local physical order \(u|v|b|g\), head \(uv=02\), and
\(p_1=(g,b)\), the nontrivial endpoints are

\[
 0201\leadsto0021,\qquad
 0221\leadsto2201.
\]

The first path has four physical/matching macrosteps. At its unique
quiet-\(p_1\) checkpoint, the returned head itself is the adjacent scan
pair \(p_2=(v,u)=01\), whose forced edge \(01\to10\) performs the missing
macrostep. The second path has four macrosteps and follows \(p_1\)
throughout. The neutral path is the first two macrosteps of the second.
Every forced row is one of

\[
 01\leftrightarrow10,\quad
 02\leftrightarrow11,\quad
 12\leftrightarrow21.
\]

An independent audit verifies these local routes when the returned head is
literally the next scan pair \(p_2=(v,u)=20\).

More strongly, the scan/connector lattice mismatch can be avoided
altogether. Put a connector-lattice double head \(C_2|C_3=02|02\)
immediately after the mixed first connector \(C_1\), keep \(C_3\) fixed,
and work on five physical coordinates. Exact audited paths give the
uniform endpoint

\[
                    C_1|C_2|C_3
       =C^*(a_1)|C(a_1)|H.
\]

For \(a_1=0,2\), the copied block \(C(a_1)=C^*(2-a_1)\) is exactly the
target of an opposite source digit guaranteed by global charge. For
\(a_1=1\), the first connector is already final and the copy is optional.
Every physical endpoint keeps \(p_1\) nonquiet, so no later clock ever
acts. This simultaneously solves the local beta dynamics, the lattice
alignment, and preservation of \(a_1\).

### 4.3 Exact remaining odd statement

At the fully tagged checkpoint, stationary-corridor train transport returns
the changed train \(E|H\) to its berth. Endpoint parity is exact: mark all
corridor blocks when their number is even; when it is odd, leave the
berth-adjacent block unmarked and use the fixed-berth swap on that one
block. Thus an even number of marks is created and erased.

The remaining bounded rows are:

- retain the ordered collar source independently; the copied
  \(C(a_1)\) records \(a_1\), not the order of the two collar digits;
- route \(C(a_1)|H\) from the local connector berth to the named

- remove or pack the bounded non-\(\mathcal G\) midpoint/interface blocks

The odd obstruction is therefore bounded preparation, not local matching
dynamics, long transport, or endpoint parity.

## 5. Current implication chain

An unconditional \(B(k)+O(1)\) theorem follows once the following three
statements are proved in one regenerative same-parity construction:

1. **GDIR closure:** the stopped normalized-gradient transfer, including
   finite marked occurrence conventions;
2. **PBBS parity repair:** extend the native hook language by a protected
   parity-changing seam and prove the resulting rank-\(3,\ldots,d\)
   component injection;
3. **Odd return:** the audited local beta path plus one
   occurrence-labelled tagged-checkpoint return schedule.

After these, the already proved annealed quarantine--Haxell composition,
upper/deep-payload backups, and bounded terminal literal repair give a
dimension-independent additive constant.

This chain is structurally short but not yet complete. In particular it
does not prove \(k=17\), does not identify the best constant, and does not
justify setting that constant to zero.
