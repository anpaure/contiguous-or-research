## 3. A lower-rainbow directed factor canonically supplies `M_0`

`J(2m-1,m)`.  Suppose every rank-`m-1` lower colour occurs on exactly one

\[

\]

### Proposition 3.1 (the predecessor matching is forced)

owner shore, and every directed edge of `F` obeys

\[
                         M_0(T\cap V)=T.                  \tag{3.2}
\]

#### Proof

Every owner is the tail of exactly one oriented factor edge.  Exact lower
rainbow makes the domain values in (3.1) distinct, so (3.1) is a bijection.
Containment `L subset T` is literal, and (3.2) is its definition.  \(\square\)

Thus an exact lower-rainbow factor is already on the fixed-`M_0`
functional face.  If its rank-`m+1` upper colours are all represented, the
lower/root--head correlation is solved for *that factor*, not merely in its
two-endpoint projection.

For comparison with the path min--max, let `C_(M_0)(P)` be the family of
`M_0`-legal oriented path-cycle factors containing the protected arcs and

\[
 \Delta^{\rm cyc}_{M_0}(P)
 =W+U-\max_{F\in C_{M_0}(P)}

## 5. Exact fixed-interior seven-component splice criterion

Let the seven directed cycles be `C_1,...,C_7`.  Choose one directed cut
edge `e_i=a_i b_i` from every cycle, choose an order and orientation of the
resulting paths, and add six Johnson join edges `f_1,...,f_6` between
successive path endpoints.  Let `Gamma` be the resulting owner path.

Two finite summaries make the criterion genuinely compositional.  For an
open owner block `B`, let `R_d(B)` record, for every coordinate, its initial
and final positive-run lengths capped at `d+1`, whether it is one on all of
`B`, and whether `B` contains a short positive run meeting neither end.
These data have the evident associative binary-word concatenation law.

Let

\[
             U(B)=(I(B),P(B),S(B),t(B))                  \tag{5.0}
\]

be its occurrence-labelled interval-OR multiset, prefix-OR list,
suffix-OR list, and total OR.  The exact associative product is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 I(BC)&=I(B)\uplus I(C)\uplus
       \{s\cup p:s\in S(B),\ p\in P(C)\},\\
 P(BC)&=P(B)\uplus\{t(B)\cup p:p\in P(C)\},\\
 S(BC)&=S(C)\uplus\{s\cup t(C):s\in S(B)\},\\
 t(BC)&=t(B)\cup t(C).
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{5.0a}
\]

Entries may be truncated above a chosen rank only when every discarded
target has a separately named retained witness.

### Theorem 5.1 (port certificate)

For fixed cycle interiors, these choices give a valid one-oriented
downstream carrier in the declared maximal-erosion/common-guard
carrier--compiler architecture exactly when all of the following hold.

1. The six joins are Johnson edges between the exposed endpoints and the
   endpoint order is one Hamilton path through the seven components.  On a
   fixed predecessor face, every directed join `T->V` also obeys

   \[
                         M_0(T\cap V)=T.                  \tag{5.0b}
   \]

   If `M_0` is allowed to change, one perfect incidence matching extending
   all protected rooted pins must instead be chosen jointly; mask-level
   lower-label recycling alone is insufficient.
2. Put

   \[
    \mu^-_\Gamma(L)=\#\{i:T_i\cap T_{i+1}=L\},\qquad
    H^-(\Gamma)=\{L:\mu^-_\Gamma(L)=0\}.                 \tag{5.1}
   \]

   The missing lower colours admit an injection into the two boundary
   cells.  Explicitly, a colour sent left must satisfy

   \[
       T_0\setminus T_1\subseteq L\subseteq T_0,           \tag{5.2}
   \]

   and the reflected condition holds at the right endpoint.  In particular
   `|H^-(Gamma)|<=2`.  Since

   \[
    \sum_L\mu^-_\Gamma(L)=W-1,
    \qquad |H^-(\Gamma)|=
      1+\sum_L(\mu^-_\Gamma(L)-1)_+,                     \tag{5.2a}
   \]

   the stronger one-hole face occurs exactly when the six join lower labels
   are six distinct cut labels.  On the fixed-`M_0` face their rooted tails,

3. The `I` coordinate of the product (5.0a) contains every rank-10 through

   \[
    n_F^+(R)-\#\{i:u(e_i)=R\}+\#\{j:u(f_j)=R\}\ge1
    \quad\text{for every rank-10 }R.                      \tag{5.3}
   \]

   Equation (5.3) is sufficient but is not necessary for interval-deck
   rank-10 coverage, because a longer interval may supply the same target.
4. The product of the seven `R_d` summaries has no internal short-positive
   bit, its two exposed boundary states lie in the declared one-sided
   acceptors, every required positive coordinate run has the required

   maximal-erosion letter is nonempty, and erosion reconstructs each

       |N_{H_Q}(X)|\ge |X|\qquad

#### Proof

## 6. An exact collar-capacity lower bound for residence repair

For a positive run, call its entering edge, all factor edges internal to the
run, and its leaving edge its **closed edge collar**.  Let `D` be the set of baseline
factor edges absent from the final path after cuts and interior rethreading.

### Lemma 6.1 (unchanged-collar runs survive)

Any old positive run whose complete edge collar avoids `D` remains a run of
the same length after arbitrary permutation or reversal of the retained
path segments.

#### Proof

Every adjacency inside the run and both adjacencies at which its bit changes
are retained.  Segment permutation can move or reverse the whole block but
cannot split it or alter either boundary.  \(\square\)

For an edge `AB` of `J(k,r)`, a coordinate can have that edge in the collar
of a positive run only when it lies in `A union B`, and in a zero-run collar
only when it lies outside `A cap B`.  Therefore one old edge belongs to at
most

\[
 r+1\text{ positive},\qquad k-r+1\text{ zero},
 \qquad k+2\text{ signed run collars}.                   \tag{6.1}
\]

If `B_+` bad positive runs and `B_0` bad zero runs remain internal after
the chosen component cuts, every successful interior rethread consequently
satisfies

\[
 |D_{\rm int}|\ge
 \max\left\{
  \left\lceil {B_+\over r+1}\right\rceil,
  \left\lceil {B_0\over k-r+1}\right\rceil,
  \left\lceil {B_++B_0\over k+2}\right\rceil
 \right\}.                                                \tag{6.2}
\]

For the current factor the independently replayed best seven openings leave
`5760` short internal **positive** runs.  Thus

\[
                 |D_{\rm int}|\ge576.                     \tag{6.3}
\]

A simple incidence `C_6` switch deletes exactly three projected Johnson
edges.  Hence a residence repair from those fixed openings using only such
switches needs at least

\[
                              192                          \tag{6.4}
\]

switch applications.  This is a scoped lower bound for the fixed-opening
architecture; counting its seven cut edges gives at least `583` absent
baseline edges in total.  In a co-designed opening/rethread, the original
`5783` bad positive runs give `|D|>=579`; if `p` old component cuts and `q`
simple `C_6` switches are the only final baseline deletions, then
`p+3q>=579` (so `p=7` gives `q>=191`).  Overlapping moves or reintroducing
an old edge cannot increase the number of baseline edges absent at the end,
and therefore cannot evade this final-collar hitting bound.

This proves that component ordering, reversal, and one cut per component
cannot finish residence.  A positive construction must move inside the
central exact fibre (4.2), not merely solve a seven-port Hamilton path.

The weaker bounds (6.2)--(6.4) remain useful dimension-free checks; (6.7)
