# Selective same-parity receiver supercolumns: Hall charge, Rado packing, protected partition-Hamilton topology, and the downstream gate

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** exact selective packing theorems under stated representations, two
sharp finite obstructions, and an exact joint topology/downstream interface.
No all-dimensional receiver supply, bounded-state recurrence, resident factor,
source word, or compiler is claimed.

## 0. Verdict

The split-piece theorem does not require every old root to be split.  Fix any
selective split pattern and bundle every activated zero foot and pure-`xy` head
which must remain terminal-atomic into one **whole compound packet**.  Such a
packet may carry `O(d)` literal obligations and may use more than one internal
path.  Native internal absorption exports zero tasks.  If the packet remains
unabsorbed and a declared terminal atlas assigns it one completed macro, that
macro is one task; its masks, feet, heads, and collar rows are not additional
tasks.

On that face there are four distinct mathematical gates.

1. For a fixed capacity-faithful role--tail table, the exact obstruction to
   reserving a selected family of receiver columns is the aggregate Hall
   escape-charge family

   \[
      b_Z(v)\le a(v),\qquad
      \sum_{j\in Z}\chi_j(Y)\le\kappa(Y).
                                                              \tag{0.1}
   \]

   These inequalities are necessary and sufficient for the unreserved roles
   to complete.  They are separable by one residual bipartite min-cut after
   the columns have been fixed.

2. Separate anchor, zero, and `xy` Hall projections are not an exact bulk
   packing theorem.  Two packet demands on three binary unit-capacity shores,
   with only even-parity triples admitted, pass every one-role and two-role
   projection and have a feasible fractional packing, but have no integral
   packing.  Thus arbitrary accepted receiver columns form a four-partite
   hypermatching problem, not a totally-unimodular circulation.

3. There is an exact positive replacement when the complete typed columns
   have a capacity-faithful linkage representation.  If `M` is the strict
   gammoid of the accepted product-state network and `A_i` is the entrance
   set for whole packet `i`, the exact number of packets which must escape is

   \[
      q^*=\max_{X\subseteq\mathcal D}
        \left(|X|-r_M\!\left(\bigcup_{i\in X}A_i\right)\right).
                                                              \tag{0.2}
   \]

   Hence at most `H` whole packets remain unabsorbed exactly when

   \[
      r_M\!\left(\bigcup_{i\in X}A_i\right)\ge |X|-H
      \qquad(X\subseteq\mathcal D).                         \tag{0.3}
   \]

   This counts whole packets, not their `O(d)` payload.  It becomes a task
   count only through a declared one-terminal-macro-per-unabsorbed-packet
   interface, or another explicitly audited bundling map.  The network
   representation and the rank inequalities are additional hypotheses; the
   frozen Pascal residual theorems do not prove either one.

4. Packing is independent of topology and terminal acceptance.  A variable
   old-Boolean-hex column has three synchronized marked port states, not one
   untyped contracted vertex.  The selected fragments must satisfy the exact
   partition-Hamilton fibre cuts, or certified maximum-merger columns must
   form an incidence tree.  Deep witnesses, flat-source letters, and the
   common-cap compiler must then be replayed on that same selected cyclic
   order.  Local acceptance does not tensorize across columns.

The clean positive architecture is consequently **carrier first**: plant a
protected partition-Hamilton/incidence-tree carrier with dummy payloads, make
literal payload substitution a capacity-faithful gammoid linkage, and require
every native substitution to induce exactly the dummy column's boundary
state morphism.  Then (0.3) leaves at most `H` complete packets for a terminal
ticket atlas.  No frozen artifact supplies this carrier-plus-gammoid-plus-
transparent-state theorem at `k=17 -> 19`.

## 1. Selective split and occurrence-labelled columns

Let `D` be only the activated compound origins left after a declared selective
split pattern and after all baseline paths have been fixed, and write
`mathcal D:=D` in the displayed min--max formulas.  Origins which
remain unsplit are not silently charged to `D`.  If several comparable
origins are to share a ray, first bundle them into a whole packet; otherwise
retain them as distinct packets.  In either case a foot and head belonging to
one terminal-atomic origin are never counted as two exported tasks.

Let `J` be an exhaustive occurrence-labelled column catalogue.  A column
`j` records:

* the whole packet set `D_j` which it absorbs;
* every root/path slot and its true capacity use;
* one literal compatible anchor/zero/`xy` role triple for each planted
  receiver core it uses;
* owner, lower-facet, palette, immediate/deep upper, positive/negative
  history, cap-current and cap-prefix, exterior-`J`, topology, voltage,
  star, protected/private, source, and compiler incidence; and
* its ordered product-state word.

With binary `z_j`, the selective cover and literal capacity rows include

\[
 \sum_{j:i\in D_j}z_j=1\quad(i\in D),\qquad
 \sum_j q_{tj}z_j\le b_t\quad(t\in\mathcal T).          \tag{1.1}
\]

Optional local pattern variables link every variant primitive to exactly one
unsplit or split pattern as in the frozen split-piece theorem.  Equation
(1.1) therefore does not split all roots.  It optimizes only over the origins
which the chosen pattern activates.

The rows (1.1) are a set-partition master in general.  They become a path
flow only after a path/column bijection and capacity fidelity have been
proved.  Product-state vertex copying by itself is not enough: two state
copies of one physical occurrence still share one physical capacity.

## 2. Exact aggregate Hall charge for a fixed role--tail table

Let `I` be the occurrence-role bank and `V` the literal tail-state bank.
Role `i` has fixed head `h_i`, nonempty legal tail list `N(i)`, and a payload
which is constant on that list.  Put

\[
 a(v)=|\{i:h_i=v\}|,
 \quad f(Y)=|\{i:N(i)\subseteq Y\}|,
 \quad \kappa(Y)=a(Y)-f(Y).                              \tag{2.1}
\]

Assume the unreserved table has a balanced selector, equivalently
`kappa(Y)>=0` for every `Y`.  A receiver column `j` reserves distinct roles
`R_j` and a legal tail `t_i` for every `i in R_j`.  Define

\[
 b_j(v)=|\{i\in R_j:t_i=v\}|,
 \qquad
 \chi_j(Y)=|\{i\in R_j:t_i\in Y,\ N(i)\not\subseteq Y\}|. \tag{2.2}
\]

Selected columns must be role-disjoint.  For their family `Z`, put
`R_Z=dot-union_(j in Z) R_j`, `b_Z=sum_j b_j`, and
`chi_Z=sum_j chi_j`.

### Theorem 2.1 (selective Hall-charge packing)

The assignments reserved by `Z` extend to a balanced selector on all roles
if and only if

\[
 b_Z(v)\le a(v)\quad(v\in V),
 \qquad
 \chi_Z(Y)\le\kappa(Y)\quad(Y\subseteq V).              \tag{2.3}
\]

Consequently, for binary column variables the complete lower/path Benders
master contains

\[
 \sum_j b_j(v)z_j\le a(v),\qquad
 \sum_j\chi_j(Y)z_j\le\kappa(Y)                         \tag{2.4}
\]

in addition to cover, role, path, and physical-resource rows.  For fixed
`z`, a violated inequality is exactly a residual Hall shore and is found by
one bipartite min-cut.

#### Proof

For `Y subseteq V`, let

\[
 r_Z(Y)=|\{i\in R_Z:N(i)\subseteq Y\}|.
\]

The residual roles whose entire legal list lies in `Y` number
`f(Y)-r_Z(Y)`, and their residual tail capacity in `Y` is
`a(Y)-b_Z(Y)`.  Hall's theorem therefore gives

\[
 f(Y)-r_Z(Y)\le a(Y)-b_Z(Y)\quad(Y\subseteq V).          \tag{2.5}
\]

Because every reserved tail is legal,

\[
 b_Z(Y)-r_Z(Y)=\chi_Z(Y).                                \tag{2.6}
\]

Rearranging (2.5) and using (2.1) and (2.6) gives the shore row in
(2.3); nonnegative residual point capacities give its first row.  The same
argument reverses, proving sufficiency.  Additivity in (2.2) gives (2.4).
\(\square\)

### Corollary 2.2 (private singleton columns)

If every reserved assignment has `N(i)={t_i}`, then `chi_Z(Y)=0` for every
shore.  Any role-disjoint selection respecting point and physical capacities
extends.  Thus a spanning bank of occurrence-private singleton-tail columns
is a genuine zero-Hall-charge absorber.  None of the frozen Boolean-hex or
fixed-`z` theorems constructs such a Catalan-scale bank.

### Proposition 2.3 (individual Hall safety does not compose)

Let `V={a,b,c}`, let the three heads be `a,b,c`, and take

\[
 N(1)=\{a,c\},\qquad N(2)=\{b,c\},\qquad N(3)=\{a,b\}.  \tag{2.7}
\]

Every nonempty proper shore has `kappa(Y)=1`.  Reserving `1->a` extends via
`2->c,3->b`; reserving `2->b` extends via `1->c,3->a`.  Reserving both fails:
on `Y={a,b}` it has `chi_Z(Y)=2>1=kappa(Y)`.  Thus columnwise Hall acceptance
cannot replace the aggregate cuts (2.4).

The same table has exactly the two displayed balanced selectors.  One is a
loop at `a` plus the `b,c` two-cycle; the other is a loop at `b` plus the
`a,c` two-cycle.  Its allowed state digraph is strongly connected but has no
connected balanced selector.  Lower matching and topology are already
independent in this three-state example.

## 3. Sharp three-role correlation obstruction

Theorem 2.1 is exact only when all correlated choices have genuinely been
expanded into one bipartite role--tail table.  Separate Hall tests on the
three receiver roles do not ensure that the three choices belong to one
accepted literal column.

### Proposition 3.1 (binary parity obstruction)

Take two whole packet demands `i=0,1`.  Give each of the anchor, zero, and
`xy` resource shores the two unit-capacity tokens `0,1`.  For each packet
admit exactly

\[
 E_i=\{(a,z,x)\in\mathbf F_2^3:a+z+x=0\pmod2\}
     =\{000,011,101,110\}.                               \tag{3.1}
\]

Then:

1. every one-role and every two-role projection of `E_i` is complete;
2. the fractional assignment `x_(i,e)=1/4` for all `i` and `e in E_i`
   satisfies both demand rows and every unit role capacity; but
3. no integral packing exists.

#### Proof

Fixing any one or two coordinates in (3.1) determines or leaves a choice for
the remaining coordinate, proving item 1.  In the four even triples, each
coordinate takes each value twice.  Across two packet rows the assignment
`1/4` therefore loads every role token by exactly one, proving item 2.

An integral unit-capacity packing of two triples must use both values in
every role, so the second triple is the coordinatewise complement of the
first.  Complementing three bits changes parity.  The complement of an even
triple is odd and is not in (3.1), proving item 3.  \(\square\)

Within the prescribed three-role binary unit-capacity class, this uses the
minimum two demands and two tokens per shore: one demand is trivially
feasible, and a one-token unit-capacity shore already fails its ordinary
two-demand capacity row.  Complete projection onto each pair of roles needs
at least four triples, attained by (3.1).  Thus it is the smallest such
three-role obstruction which is simultaneously invisible to all one-role
and two-role projections.  It proves that arbitrary three-role columns form
a four-partite hypermatching
(packet, anchor, zero, `xy`).  Their incidence matrix is not automatically
integral, and no scalar Hall, min-cost circulation, or Farkas certificate for
the marginal relaxation proves a literal packing.

## 4. Exact positive theorem under a capacity-faithful linkage hypothesis

Fix the selective bundling `D`.  In this section all multi-origin and
multihead content is bundled **before** `D` is defined, and one linkage path
represents one whole packet.  Thus the Rado specialization has `|D_j|=1`.
The general hypercolumns of Section 1 with `|D_j|>1` are not covered by
Theorem 4.1.  Assume a directed accepted product-state network `N` with a
native receiver-port sink bank has been constructed so
that:

1. each packet `i` has a finite entrance set `A_i`;
2. an entrance-to-port path is in bijection with a complete literal
   anchor/zero/`xy` receiver column for `i`;
3. several columns are simultaneously accepted if and only if their paths
   form a capacity-respecting linkage in `N`; and
4. every shared owner, path, occurrence, palette, history, cap, `J`, voltage,
   star, protected, deep, source, and compiler resource relevant to that
   claim is represented by a genuine shared capacity/state element of `N`.

Clone a capacity-`q` resource into `q` unit resources.  Let `M` be the strict
gammoid on `E=union_i A_i`: a set is independent when its entrances can be
linked to distinct native ports.  Thus `r_M(S)` is an integral maximum
linkage value and is computable by max-flow in the capacity-expanded network.

### Theorem 4.1 (Rado receiver-supercolumn theorem)

The maximum number `nu` of whole packets admitting mutually compatible
native receiver columns is

\[
 \nu=\min_{X\subseteq\mathcal D}
       \left(|\mathcal D\setminus X|
       +r_M\!\left(\bigcup_{i\in X}A_i\right)\right).    \tag{4.1}
\]

Equivalently, the exact number of unabsorbed whole packets is (0.2), and it is
at most `H` if and only if (0.3) holds.  Translating this packet deficit into
a terminal task count requires a declared packet-to-ticket interface.

#### Proof

Parallel-extend `M` to occurrence-labelled copies `(i,e)` for `e in A_i`.
Intersect that matroid with the partition matroid allowing at most one copy
from each packet block.  The matroid-intersection min--max theorem, or
equivalently Rado's independent-transversal theorem, gives (4.1).  Subtracting
from `|D|` gives (0.2), and `q^*<=H` is algebraically equivalent to (0.3).
Each selected independent transversal is, by the capacity-faithful network
hypothesis, exactly a set of compatible literal complete columns.  \(\square\)

The theorem counts one prebundled packet even when it carries `O(d)` masks,
feet, heads, histories, or support.  Multihead pieces must therefore be
prebundled into complete comparable rays, or one must separately prove a
rectangular lane-completion theorem saying that atomic choices always
concatenate to a legal whole path.  Arbitrary weighted head packing is not a
gammoid merely because each head has a path.

The network hypothesis is load-bearing.  A product-state lift which gives
the same physical token one independent copy in every state is not capacity
faithful.  Conversely, identifying state copies through an untyped bottleneck
may permit an illegal state change.  The required proof is a bijection between
literal columns and paths and between simultaneous literal acceptance and
linkage.

Element-weighted selection can use weighted matroid intersection once its
literal cost is additive.  Path-dependent costs require a separately proved
min-cost linkage encoding.  Neither case is automatically an ordinary
min-cost circulation.  Bellman--Ford/Farkas applies to an ordered finite
product-state return graph only after all bounded resource coordinates and
accepting terminal states have actually been materialized.

## 5. Protected partition-Hamilton coupling

### 5.1 Carrier-first form

The safest topology architecture fixes a globally ledger-feasible protected
partition-Hamilton carrier containing dummy-filled receiver cores.  A native
payload column may replace a dummy only if it uses the same marked boundary
ports and induces the same complete boundary state morphism.  Such a
substitution preserves the outside state transversal and outside cycle arcs,
and supplies an exact-one internal path/fibre cover for the marked composite.
Its internal transversal may differ from the dummy's.  The Rado network then
packs payloads on a fixed outside topology.

This order matters.  A protected safe-switch incidence tree starts from an
already materialized globally feasible cycle factor.  It can fuse those
cycles; it does not create the three receiver paths or prove their typed
columns exist.

### 5.2 Exact joint partition-Hamilton form

If receiver selection itself chooses the topology, let binary `z_j` select a
macro.  Let `Lambda_j` be its list of **complete literal realizations**: one
`lambda` fixes the three boundary states, every internal fibre and forced
arc, its primitive cover and resource vector, cumulative ledger, and its
original-cycle footprint.  Use binary `u_(j,lambda)` with

\[
 \sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda_j}u_{j,\lambda}=z_j.           \tag{5.1}
\]

For each role `rho in {a,0,xy}`, link the state selected in the macro-specific
marked port fibre `p(j,rho)` to that same `u` variable:

\[
 y_{p(j,\rho),\sigma}
   =\sum_{\lambda:\lambda_\rho=\sigma}u_{j,\lambda}.    \tag{5.2}
\]

Thus the three port states are synchronized by one literal column choice.
If alternative macros share one physical port fibre, the right side of
(5.2) is aggregated over its mutually exclusive alternatives rather than
duplicating the `y` row.  Three separate exact-one rows without the common
`u` coupling would recreate the parity error of Proposition 3.1.

Every macro-specific internal activation `q` is linked to the same complete
realization by

\[
 q=\sum_{\lambda:q\text{ occurs in }\lambda}u_{j,\lambda},       \tag{5.2a}
\]

with the analogous aggregate over mutually exclusive macros for a shared
physical object.  Cover and resource rows use the incidence vector of that
same `lambda`.  This forbids splicing the boundary state of one realization
to the internal path or ledger of another.

Let `s_p` activate a physical fragment fibre `Sigma_p`; fixed fragments have
`s_p=1`, and macro-created marked fibres have the appropriate linked `u` or
`z` sum.  All activation variables `s_p`, state variables `y_(p,sigma)`, and
accepted arc variables `x_e` are binary.  The exact activated-fibre master is

\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_p}y_{p,\sigma}=s_p,
 \qquad
 x(\delta^+(\sigma))=x(\delta^-(\sigma))=y_{p,\sigma}.  \tag{5.3}
\]

For every physical-fragment set `A`, every `f in A`, and every `g notin A`,
impose

\[
 x\!\left(\delta^+\!\left(\Sigma(A)\right)\right)
       \ge s_f+s_g-1.                                    \tag{5.4}
\]

Together with (5.2a), the cover, protected-arc, and complete ledger rows,
(5.1)--(5.4) are necessary and sufficient for one cycle on all activated
fibres.  Indeed binary (5.3) is a cycle cover; if it has two cycles, take `A`
to be the fibres of one selected cycle and (5.4) is violated.  A single cycle
crosses every activated proper shore.

A complete old Boolean hex therefore cannot be contracted to one ordinary
vertex.  Its anchor, zero, and `xy` corridors are either expanded literally
or represented by three synchronized marked port states.  Any forced
internal path is contracted directionally: retain only arcs entering its
initial state and leaving its terminal state, delete its internal fibres, and
carry its cumulative ledger exactly once.

### 5.3 Safe-switch alternative

Suppose instead that a globally feasible selected-state cycle factor `F_0`
is already materialized.  Index each selected literal realization by
`alpha=(j,lambda)`, put `v_alpha=u_(j,lambda)`, and let `e_alpha` be its fixed
set of original cycles.  Under pairwise-disjoint extended supports or
hereditary applicability, selected literal columns fuse all original cycles
`C` to one exactly on the maximum-merger face when

\[
 \sum_\alpha(|e_\alpha|-1)v_\alpha=|C|-1,               \tag{5.5}
\]

and

\[
 \sum_{\alpha:e_\alpha\subseteq W}(|e_\alpha|-1)v_\alpha\le |W|-1
 \qquad(\varnothing\ne W\subsetneq C).                 \tag{5.6}
\]

If every realization of macro `j` has the same footprint, these rows may be
aggregated to `z_j`.

These are the incidence-tree equations.  For pure ternary hexes,
`|e_alpha|-1=2`, so `|C|` must be odd.  The two individually valid footprints

\[
 e_1=\{1,2,3\},\qquad e_2=\{1,2,4\}                    \tag{5.7}
\]

already violate (5.6) on their four-element union: `4>3`.  After either
three-cycle merger, the other no longer meets three distinct current cycles.
Thus local three-role packing and even connected projected topology do not
imply a protected incidence tree.  Binary pulls may remove the parity
restriction but still require their graphic tree and literal compatibility.

## 6. Downstream deep/source/compiler state

The selected receiver columns and topology must be accepted on one common
chronology.  At minimum the protected boundary state contains

\[
 \Xi=\bigl(
   \text{owner/facet/palette};\Delta,b;
   \mathcal R_d^+,\mathcal R_d^-;
   \text{reserve},J,\text{opening},\nu,\star,
   \text{private};\mathsf W,\mathsf S,\mathsf C;
   \mathsf R_{\rm res}
   \bigr),                                               \tag{6.1}
\]

where `(Delta,b)` is the cap-prefix monoid, `R_d^+` and `R_d^-` are the two
directed-history relations, `mathsf W` is the occurrence-level deep-witness
relation, `mathsf S` is the flat-source relation for the selected opening,
`mathsf C` is the conjunction of common-cap compiler clauses, and
`mathsf R_res` is a Markov-sufficient residence/descent trace.  Each literal
transition or macro morphism separately carries its exact additive weight
`c_Phi`; an accepting return restores (6.1) and tests the total weight,
normally `sum c_Phi<=-1`.  The cost is not a state coordinate required to
return to its initial value.  Additive terminal cap balance is not enough: for
ordered words `A,B`,

\[
 (\Delta_A,b_A)\star(\Delta_B,b_B)
 =\bigl(\Delta_A+\Delta_B,
        \max\{b_A,b_B-\Delta_A\}\bigr).                 \tag{6.2}
\]

After the final owner order and opening are fixed, let the owner path have
length `W`, assume `d<r`, and put `L=W+d`.  For `0<=p<L`, the maximal
flat-source letters are

\[
 P_p=\bigcap_{\max(0,p-d)\le i\le\min(W-1,p)}T_i.       \tag{6.3}
\]

They form an antecedent exactly when every `P_p` is nonempty and

\[
 \bigcup_{p=i}^{i+d}P_p=T_i\qquad(0\le i<W).            \tag{6.4}
\]

For a linear Johnson path, (6.4) is exactly the absence of internal positive
runs of length at most `d`.  Partition-Hamilton topology alone does not imply
this history condition.  Strict upper coverage is the nonwrapping
consecutive-owner-union deck on the same opening.  Strict lower coverage
requires the exact common-cap CNF on the shared source letters; marginal
Hall/DM is only its one-cell projection.

Deep upper rows are equally global.  For every target `U`, an exhaustive
occurrence-labelled witness catalogue `W_U` may use binary variables
`w_(U,I)`.  Let `K(I)` contain every selected join, macro, marked state, and
contracted internal-state activation required by the literal witness.  Then

\[
 \sum_{I\in\mathcal W_U}w_{U,I}\ge1,\qquad
 w_{U,I}\le q\quad(q\in K(I)).                          \tag{6.5}
\]

together with the state, seam, and boundary legality of that literal
interval.  Counting witnesses without retaining their occurrence identities
is not sufficient.

### Proposition 6.1 (minimal downstream noncomposition)

The following scoped abstract banks show that local acceptance is not closed
under union.

1. One deep target has exactly two witnesses `I_1,I_2`.  Column `j_1`
   destroys only `I_1` and column `j_2` only `I_2`.  Each column separately
   preserves the target; their union destroys every witness.
2. At one source position let the maximal palette be `P_p={a,b}`, with the
   nonempty clause `x_(p,a) or x_(p,b)`.  One local ledger adds
   `not x_(p,b)` and the other adds `not x_(p,a)`.  Each is source-feasible
   separately; together they violate the nonempty clause.  This is the
   clause-exact form of the common-source incompatibility.
3. On two physical cap coordinates let packet `A` have current `(1,-1)`,
   packet `B` have `(-1,1)`, and let the initial duplicate slack be `(0,1)`.
   The order `AB` has safe first prefix `(1,0)` and returns to `(0,1)`;
   `BA` fails immediately on its first coordinate.  Both complete currents
   sum to zero, and the terminal combined current is zero.  Terminal additive
   equality does not replace (6.2).

These examples do not claim that the current Pascal residual realizes the
abstract ledgers.  They prove that no theorem from upstream marginal rows
alone can infer the terminal replay.

## 7. Transparent substitution and the regenerative corollary

Let a protected carrier have ordered dummy slot morphisms

\[
 \omega_{j-1}\xrightarrow{\phi_j}\omega_j              \tag{7.1}
\]

in the complete product state (6.1).  A payload substitution is
**transparent** when it has the same marked physical boundary, the same
literal shared-resource usage, and exactly the same boundary morphism
`phi_j`, including cap-prefix legality and all claimed witness/source/compiler
relations and the residence trace.  It also has the same attached exact
weight `c_Phi` as the dummy morphism.

### Theorem 7.1 (carrier substitution)

Any capacity-compatible family of transparent substitutions leaves the
carrier's complete terminal replay unchanged.

#### Proof

Replace the corresponding factors in the ordered composite
`phi_1 phi_2 ... phi_n`.  Every replacement is the same morphism on the same
boundary, so associativity gives the identical composite.  Literal capacity
compatibility prevents a hidden shared-occurrence collision.  \(\square\)

For an additive coordinate, a sufficient special case is a coboundary
increment `psi(omega_j)-psi(omega_(j-1))`, which telescopes around the carrier.
This does not by itself handle cap prefixes, witness survival, or compiler
clauses; those require the full morphism equality above.

### Corollary 7.2 (exact bounded-export regenerative route)

Suppose at every reachable same-parity state with positive descent potential:

1. a protected dummy-filled partition-Hamilton carrier is available;
2. the selected whole packets have a capacity-faithful linkage network whose
   gammoid satisfies (0.3) for one absolute `H`;
3. all linked native substitutions are transparent; and
4. each of the remaining at most `H` whole packets is assigned one completed
   terminal macro, and the common ordered replay of all substitutions and
   terminal macros returns (6.1) with total union weight
   `sum c_Phi<=-1`.

Then one transition exports at most `H` compound repair tasks, returns to the
same protected state class, and strictly descends.  Iteration gives the
corresponding regenerative recurrence.  In a complete finite materialization,
trim the Markov-sufficient lifted graph to states reachable from the root and
co-reachable to acceptance, then add the frozen lifted-return theorem's
zero-cost reset arcs from accepting states to the root.  Failure to find the
required negative accepting return has the exact Bellman--Ford/Farkas
vertex-potential certificate for that declared envelope.

This is not the circular assertion “if task birth is bounded, then it is
bounded.”  Equation (0.3) is the exact structural rank condition which makes
the bound true on whole compound packets.  Proposition 3.1 proves that it
cannot be deduced from the current three marginal role cuts.

There is also a sharp reason transparency is necessary.  Let `n` otherwise
valid native columns emit debts `u_1,...,u_n` tied to pairwise private repair
sockets.  If one completed ticket has support for at most `p(d)` such private
sockets, at least

\[
                         \left\lceil n/p(d)\right\rceil \tag{7.2}
\]

compound tasks are required.  This counts independently anchored sockets,
not masks within one ticket, and remains unbounded even when `p(d)=O(d)`.
It is a scoped abstract countermodel, not an authenticated Pascal residual.
