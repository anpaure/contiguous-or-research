## 2. The atomic packet--footprint object

Let \((\mathcal L_0,\mathcal L_{\rm succ})\) be one frozen authenticated
library epoch.  Entries of \(\mathcal L_0\) are unconditional q1
contradictions; entries of \(\mathcal L_{\rm succ}\) are valid only after a
named pivot-footprint contraction.  A conditional entry is never used as an
unconditional anchor.  Both manifest hashes are part of the theorem epoch.
Let `Q` be one packet in either declared class and let

\[
                    P\in\Sigma_{B_Q}(U_0(B_Q))           \tag{2.1}
\]

be one exact pivot footprint in its complete final bank.  The atomic object
for every exterior and cylinder theorem is

\[
                              z=(Q,P),                    \tag{2.2}
\]

not `Q` alone.

For radius three, \(\Delta_z\) is reconstructed from the complete subset
truth tables and the joint final comparison.  It contains every added,
removed and relabelled atom, row, role, capacity, conflict, complement,
guard, blocker and protected q1 state.  For a `C8`, \(\Delta_z\) is the
atomic before/after joint toggle with the same fields.  In both cases it also
contains every contraction and residual-capacity change caused by `P`.
Every endpoint and resource is an immutable occurrence, never a mask or
dense component number.

Fix complete declared embedding/derivation manifests for both libraries.
An \(\mathcal L_0\) entry **closes** a final atlas only when an authenticated
closure certificate occurs in the complete declared unconditional scan.
An \(\mathcal L_{\rm succ}\) entry closes \(B_Q/P\) only after the named
footprint contraction and an authenticated successor certificate.  A missing
guarded-minor leaf or bicycle arc is an `OPEN_MANIFEST` witness for that
declared derivation, not semantic non-entailment; semantic openness requires
a countermodel or a complete entailment procedure.  An incomplete manifest
is `UNKNOWN`.

Call `z` **library-open relative to the declared manifests** when the final
joint zero-265 vector vanishes, no entry of \(\mathcal L_0\) closes in the
complete final scan, and

\[
 \forall K\in\mathcal L_{\rm succ},\qquad
 K\text{ does not close }B_Q/P\text{ in its complete declared scan}. \tag{2.3}
\]

A packet `Q` is library-open exactly when at least one `z=(Q,P)` is.  All
open footprints must be retained: two footprints of one packet may have
different exterior, upper and later compiler behaviour.  Passing (2.3) is
not q1 feasibility.

Independently of the local zero rows, `Q` is **q1-certified by the library**
when an authenticated scan proves

\[
 \left(\exists K\in\mathcal L_0:K\text{ closes }B_Q\right)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \left(\forall P\in\Sigma_{B_Q}(U_0(B_Q))\ 
       \exists K\in\mathcal L_{\rm succ}:
       K\text{ closes }B_Q/P\right).                    \tag{2.4}
\]

An authenticated unconditional proof or the complete-footprint successor
theorem makes (2.4) an exact q1-UNSAT certificate.  A zero-265 failure is a
local rejection but, by itself, is not a q1 claim.
`LIBRARY_OPEN_NOT_Q1_VERDICT` is inconclusive, whereas
`REJECT_Q1_CERTIFIED` is a proved rejection.

## 3. Strongest unconditional common-exterior theorem

Place all final banks on one immutable occurrence/factor universe.  For
`z`, let \(W_z\) contain every object of \(\Delta_z\) and every object whose
literal invariance after the contraction is not certified.  Close \(W_z\)
under the declared local factor incidences.  For any finite cell
\({\cal C}\) of packet--footprint objects put

\[
             W_{\cal C}=\operatorname{cl}
                 \left(\bigcup_{z\in{\cal C}}W_z\right). \tag{3.1}
\]

Every factor outside \(W_{\cal C}\) is then literally identical in every
member.  This complement is the common exterior.  Let \(\Gamma_{\cal C}\)
record every crossing tail, head and colour capacity, orientation pair,
genuine protected resource, and protected Boolean in a crossing clause.

Let \(\sigma_I,\sigma_E\) be the interior and exterior signatures.  For each
member \(z\in{\cal C}\), write \(R_z(x,y)\) for its conjunction of all
crossing constraints on side-legal selections, where side-legal means that
every noncrossing factor on that side is already satisfied.  The member tag
is retained only to select \(R_z\); the signatures record the values and
residual capacities of every input needed to evaluate its crossing factors.

### Theorem 3.1 (finite footprint-relative common exterior)

Restriction and gluing through the proposed signatures are exact uniformly
over the cell if and only if, for every \(z\in{\cal C}\), \(R_z\) is constant
on every product of realized signature fibres and the accepted fibre pairs
are exactly the declared complementary pairs for `z`.

Recording the complete typed factor separator \(\Gamma_{\cal C}\) is a
sufficient structural certificate.  It is not necessary when an omitted
crossing is entailed or determined by retained coordinates.

#### Proof

For each member, constancy on signature fibres makes the crossing conjunction
a well-defined relation on signature pairs.  If this relation is
complementarity, two
side-legal selections glue precisely when their signatures are
complementary.  Conversely, an exact signature gluing rule cannot assign
different legality to two pairs in the same product of fibres.  Recording
every crossing factor input makes every crossing truth value a function of
the signatures and is therefore sufficient. \(\square\)

The theorem is unconditional and finite at `k=17`.  It gives no useful size
bound: \(W_{\cal C}\) may be the whole formula and the common exterior may be
empty.  Bounded edit arity controls neither \(|W_{\cal C}|\) nor
\(|\Gamma_{\cal C}|\).

## 4. Exact successor-kernel cylinders

For `z=(Q,P)`, take Lane A's complete finite, library-indexed kernel

\[
 \Lambda_z=
 \operatorname{Ker}_{\mathcal L_0,\mathcal L_{\rm succ}}(B_Q,P). \tag{4.1}
\]

It includes the pivot footprint, every residual demanded support, every
provider seam meeting such a support, every outside resource in each
provider footprint, and every guarded-minor/bicycle dependency cone after
contraction.

The member record retains the immutable packet/footprint key as provenance.
Define the **canonical pointed kernel payload** \(\kappa(z)\) to contain

* the packet type and canonical local packet/footprint roles;
* the complete typed kernel hypergraph;
* the authenticated `(library part, core, embedding/derivation)` index,
  demanded-row and pivot roles;
* protected names which must be fixed pointwise; and
* the typed transport from canonical local roles to the member's immutable
  occurrences.

Class equality compares the canonical payload after applying this transport,
not equality of the raw member key.

### Theorem 4.1 (declared-library kernel transport)

Suppose \(\kappa(z)\) and \(\kappa(z')\) are related by a typed isomorphism
which fixes protected names and induces a bijection on the authenticated
`(library part, core, embedding/derivation)` records of the same frozen
library.  Then each unconditional closure bit and each footprint-conditional
closure bit transports under that bijection.  Resource-saturation
matchings transport in both directions.  Missing guarded-minor leaves and
bicycle arcs transport only as `OPEN_MANIFEST` records for the declared
derivations.  Hence the complete declared close/open-manifest vector, and
therefore (2.3), agrees up to the authenticated record bijection.

#### Proof

For a resource entry, closure depends only on provider seams meeting the
residual demanded rows and on their complete resource conflicts.  These are
exactly the pointed kernel.  A typed isomorphism preserves the maximum
simultaneously saturated demand.  Guarded minors and bicycles depend, for
the named derivation, only on their recorded clauses, guards, blockers and
implication leaves.  The library-indexed bijection ensures that a transported
core is still an authenticated entry rather than a merely isomorphic absent
formula.  The claims follow record by record. \(\square\)

Thus canonical kernel classes are exact reusable cylinders for the complete
declared proof manifests.  They are semantic cylinders only when those
manifests implement complete entailment.  This does not make them full-q1
exterior cylinders:
an atom outside every stored dependency cone can create a new obstruction or
a new completion.  Lane A proves that \(\Lambda_z\) is finite, not that its
size is dimension-uniformly bounded.

## 5. A genuinely bounded typed-cylinder theorem

The following is the strongest positive compression statement available
without deciding the cases.

Partition a family of atomic objects into at most `T` pointed-exterior
templates.  A template \(\theta\) supplies typed transports fixing every
protected name and satisfies

\[
 \|W_\theta\|_{\rm phys}\le s,\qquad
 |\Gamma_\theta|\le b.                                  \tag{5.1}
\]

In addition, its actual boundary signature has dimension at most `b`.
Its boundary-conditioned orientation/head--colour master has at most `r`
coordinates.  The `b` boundary coordinates and the `r` master coordinates
all have alphabet at most `q`; inactive coordinates have an explicit symbol.
Assume Theorem 3.1's exact boundary gluing certificate.  Assume also that,
after a **realizable side-legal exterior/master state** and full word are
fixed, the remaining q1 completions are bijective with perfect matchings of
the displayed finite bipartite residual predecessor graph.  Finally, a
word determine that graph up to the same typed isomorphism for every member
realizing the word.

### Theorem 5.1 (transported full-word alternative)

Under these hypotheses, all q1-UNSAT members have a reusable common
Hall-cylinder cover
with

\[
                         K\le Tq^{b+r}.                  \tag{5.2}
\]

There is one canonical deficient shore for each realized template--word
whose graph is imperfect, transported to every member in that cylinder.
Conversely, for each member, a realizable side-legal exterior/master state
whose residual graph has a perfect matching is an exact q1-SAT witness.

#### Proof

Fix a template and a realized full word.  The graph-determinacy hypothesis
gives one canonical residual graph.  If it has no perfect matching, Hall
gives one deficient shore, and typed graph isomorphisms transport it to
every member.  If it has a perfect matching, the completion-bijection and
the member's realizing exterior/master state transport the matching and
Theorem 3.1 glues the complete q1 selection.  There are at most
\(q^{b+r}\) words per template. \(\square\)

The graph-determinacy condition is load-bearing.  Merely bounding a list of
shore names does not bound the number of cylinders, and case-dependent
shores can leave a proof payload proportional to the number of packets.
Lane A's anchor theorem supplies none of `s,b,T,r` or graph determinacy.

## 6. Exact wandering-support obstruction

Anchor containment bounds enumeration arity, not physical locality.  The
remaining free slots may occur throughout the primitive factor.

For a fixed causal anchor `A`, let \(Z_A\) be the set of **labelled atomic**
objects \(z=(Q,P)\) extending it.  Footprints with the same free occurrence
support remain different members.  For \({\cal C}\subseteq Z_A\), put

\[
 c({\cal C})=
 \left\|\operatorname{cl}\left(\bigcup_{z\in{\cal C}}W_z\right)
 \right\|_{\rm phys},
 \qquad
 \kappa_s(Z_A)=\min\left\{m:
 Z_A=\bigcup_{j=1}^m{\cal C}_j, c({\cal C}_j)\le s\right\}. \tag{6.1}
\]

If some singleton has \(c(\{z\})>s\), set \(\kappa_s(Z_A)=+\infty\).

### Theorem 6.1 (support-local cell criterion)

The minimum number of literal common-exterior cells of physical support at
most `s` needed to cover the anchored packet--footprint family is exactly
\(\kappa_s(Z_A)\).  In particular, with

\[
 M_s(Z_A)=\max_{c({\cal C})\le s}|{\cal C}|,
\]

and the convention \(M_s(Z_A)=0\) when no nonempty admissible subfamily
exists, one has

\[
             \kappa_s(Z_A)\ge {|Z_A|\over M_s(Z_A)}.     \tag{6.2}
\]

The displayed finite lower bound is asserted only when \(M_s(Z_A)>0\).

#### Proof

Each literal support-`s` cell is a labelled subfamily whose joint closure
has cost at most `s`; conversely absorbing that closure gives one literal
common exterior by Theorem 3.1.  Thus (6.1) is exactly the cell cover, and
the largest cell covers at most \(M_s\) members. \(\square\)

One may project \(Z_A\) to a completion hypergraph only after every
footprint-specific contraction shadow becomes a labelled vertex, or after
proving one footprint per free subset and support saturation.  In that
simple unit-cost case the coarse bounds are \(M_s\le\mu s\) for a size-two
radius-three anchor, \(M_s\le\mu{s\choose2}\) for a singleton radius-three
anchor, and \(M_s\le\mu{s\choose3}\) for a one-slot `C8` anchor, where
\(\mu\) is the maximum footprint multiplicity.  The labelled definition
(6.1) is the proof-safe finite obstruction.

There is a second sharp obstruction.  Suppose `t` candidate traces have
pairwise distinct private named capacities \(q_j\), each costing one
physical support unit or one distinct native boundary coordinate, and for
every `j` there
are two side-legal exterior states differing only at \(q_j\) plus a
side-legal interior trace which glues to exactly one.  Any exact cell
containing trace `j` must absorb \(q_j\) or expose it as a boundary
coordinate.  A cell containing all `t` traces therefore obeys

\[
                              s+b\ge t,                   \tag{6.3}
\]

and a cover by `T` cells obeys \(T(s+b)\ge t\).  Define
\(\tau_{\rm reusable}\) to be the minimum number of cylinders, each carrying
one shore transported uniformly to all its members.  A Hall fooling family
of `t` valid traces on which no transported shore is deficient twice forces

\[
                         \tau_{\rm reusable}\ge t.        \tag{6.4}
\]

Singleton localization attains these bounds.  No growing private-capacity
or Hall-fooling family has yet been authenticated in the actual next-shell
catalogue.  Therefore a bounded actual interface is open, while boundedness
from edit arity alone is refuted.

## 7. Exact bounded upper sidecars

Two limited protected-upper interfaces are already available.

### Proposition 7.1 (radius-three cut-liability bank)

For three distinct-base one-for-one cut replacements, write \(C_0,C_1\) for
the old and new cuts and \(I(C)\) for old-piece internal interval-union
values.  At horizon rank at most \(9+u\), a value in
\(I(C_0)\setminus I(C_1)\) must have an old witness crossing a new cut.  Each
new-cut suffix--prefix product has at most \((u+1)^2\) distinct union values.
Hence the complete liability superset has size at most

\[
                             3(u+1)^2.                   \tag{7.1}
\]

#### Proof

Removing an old cut joins pieces and cannot destroy an internal interval.
Every witness destroyed after inserting the three new cuts crosses at least
one of them.  Cutting the witness at its first such edge writes it as one
old suffix followed by one old prefix.  Each nested ray has at most `u+1`
distinct unions of rank at most \(9+u\). \(\square\)

This is a pre-q1 liability superset; final seams may redeliver every listed
target.  It is not a hard rejection or an exterior all-width theorem.

### Proposition 7.2 (fixed-horizon `3+1`-`C8` sidecar)

Assume the old factor covers every target in the declared horizon \(U_Q\).
For a fixed literal common-exterior `STAR` or corresponding
`OCTAHEDRAL`-square `3+1` `C8`, owner/lower degrees and the complete
immediate-upper multiplicity vector are unchanged.  Through strict-upper
horizon `Q`, the exact casualty/absorber-demand set is

\[
                  Z_F(D)\setminus N_{F'}(D')             \tag{7.2}
\]

and has at most \(10(Q+1)^2\) distinct values.  At `k=17`, the base rank is
`r=9`; taking ranks through 17 gives `Q=8` and the bound `810`.

This is a bounded distinct-value casualty sidecar for fixed `Q`; it does not
construct an absorber.  It does not bound signed occurrence multiplicity on
long plateaux, the q1 successor
kernel, the number of physical C8 locations, the all-width deck, or the
terminal compiler.

## 8. Frozen-library epoch theorem

For two epochs on the same candidate universe suppose

\[
 \mathcal L_0\subseteq\mathcal L'_0,
 \qquad
 \mathcal L_{\rm succ}\subseteq\mathcal L'_{\rm succ}.   \tag{8.0}
\]

Let \({\rm Open}(\mathcal L_0,\mathcal L_{\rm succ})\) be the zero-265-clean
packet--footprint objects with no authenticated unconditional closure and
satisfying (2.3).

### Theorem 8.1 (monotone portfolio ingestion)

\[
 {\rm Open}(\mathcal L'_0,\mathcal L'_{\rm succ})
 \subseteq
 {\rm Open}(\mathcal L_0,\mathcal L_{\rm succ}).         \tag{8.1}
\]

An anchor catalogue generated from an unconditional reference core
\(K_*\in\mathcal L_0\) retained in both epochs remains a safe candidate
superset after the library grows.  Conditional cores never justify this
anchor step.  Kernel-derived serializations, open witnesses and cylinder
labels are epoch-specific and must be revalidated or recomputed.  A physical
exterior template proved independently of the library may remain unchanged.

#### Proof

If one footprint is open against every entry of the larger pair, it is open
against each sublibrary.  A packet escaping all later unconditional cores
also escapes the retained reference core, so the old anchor generator still
contains it.  New demanded supports, embeddings and blockers can enlarge the
kernel and change its derived classes, proving the final warning.
\(\square\)

The exact-3,664 terminal q1 verdict is bound to this chronology through the
frozen row-provenanced proof manifest above.  It does **not** enter
\((\mathcal L_0,\mathcal L_{\rm succ})\) as a typed core library merely by
being frozen: the current integer-variable cores lack native OccKey and
dependency maps.  A raw DRAT core
with encoding auxiliaries is not automatically a typed resource core; it
must retain its clause/auxiliary map or be decoded to the native proof
system.  Live result counts and resource exits are never theorem inputs.
