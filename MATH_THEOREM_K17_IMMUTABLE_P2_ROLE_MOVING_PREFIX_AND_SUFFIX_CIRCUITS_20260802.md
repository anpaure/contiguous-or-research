## 2. The prefix-circuit theorem

Let two physical P2 rows be

\[
             a:(S,R),\qquad b:(T,U),                 \tag{2.1}
\]

with their physical owners, roots, and carrier incidences fixed.  Suppose

\[
                         T\subsetneq R,qquad
                         S\subsetneq U.               \tag{2.2}
\]

Then

\[
       (S,R)+(T,U)\longmapsto(T,R)+(S,U)              \tag{2.3}
\]

is an exact target-table rethread.  It is the alternating `C4` of the
bipartite containment graph between the two named lower targets and the two
physical root hosts.

### Theorem 2.1 (exact preservation)

The move (2.3) preserves:

1. the complete named lower-target multiset;
2. strictness of every target chain;
3. every physical root and owner and hence both owner phases;
4. the length histogram;
5. every H suffix, H bottom token, hard/free receiver, selected hard supplier,
   and private-H row pointwise; and
6. the authenticated owner--root carrier incidence and its connectivity,
   since only the lower target attached below each of two already-fixed roots
   changes.

It is support-minimal among nonidentity exact rethreads.

#### Proof

Equation (2.2) proves the two new chains are strict.  The before and after
target decks are both the multiset `{S,T,R,U}`.  Each maximum remains on its
old row, so its root and owner are unchanged, and both rows remain length two.
No H row is touched, proving item 5.  The owner--root carrier uses the fixed
incidences, proving item 6 in that precise sense.  A one-row nonidentity move
would delete one named target and duplicate another, contradicting exact
target use, so two rows are minimal.  QED.

The theorem does not say that every such square improves socketability.  That
is an edge weight on (2.3), and it is exactly replayed for rows 565 and 649.

## 3. Why rank seven forces a suffix circuit

### Proposition 3.1 (P2--H bottom-swap cut)

A two-row bottom swap

\[
 (S,R)+(B,M,U)\longmapsto(B,R)+(S,M,U)               \tag{3.1}
\]

requires `S proper_subset M`.  Every mutable H middle in the frozen K17
table has rank seven.  Consequently every rank-seven P2 role is isolated on
the left of the P2-to-current-long-H bottom-swap graph.

The exact union-zero rank histogram contains 623 such roles.  Hence this
restricted graph has matching deficiency at least 623, independently of the
new-role socket predicate.

### Proposition 3.2 (no two-row rank-seven middle swap)

Let `(S,R)` and `(M,U)` be two distinct matched edges of the Boolean inclusion
graph between rank-seven and rank-eight sets.  If both cross edges existed,
then both `R` and `U` would contain `S union M`.  Distinct rank-seven sets that
fit in a common rank-eight set have union of size eight, so

\[
                         R=U=S\cup M.                 \tag{3.2}
\]

But every rank-eight target occurs exactly once, so two distinct physical
rows cannot have this common root.  Thus the graph has no nontrivial `C4`.
Any rethread moving a rank-seven matched target has support at least three.

### Theorem 3.3 (support-three suffix `C6`)

Let `C` have rank six and let `a,b,c` be distinct outside `C`.  Put

\[
\begin{aligned}
 S_0&=C+a,&R_0&=C+a+b,\\
 S_1&=C+b,&R_1&=C+b+c,\\
 S_2&=C+c,&R_2&=C+c+a.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{3.3}
\]

Suppose the current rows are

\[
        (S_0,R_0),\quad(B_1,S_1,R_1),\quad(B_2,S_2,R_2),       \tag{3.4}
\]

and additionally

\[
                         B_1\subsetneq S_2,qquad
                         B_2\subsetneq S_0.           \tag{3.5}
\]

Then

\[
 (S_0,R_0)+(B_1,S_1,R_1)+(B_2,S_2,R_2)
 \longmapsto
 (S_1,R_0)+(B_1,S_2,R_1)+(B_2,S_0,R_2)               \tag{3.6}
\]

is an exact support-three suffix rethread.  It is the alternating `C6` in
the rank-7/rank-8 incidence graph and is support-minimal by Proposition 3.2.

The proof is immediate from (3.3)--(3.5): all new chains are strict, the
rank-seven target deck is cyclically permuted, all other targets and every
row maximum remain fixed, and the two hard bottoms stay on their physical
rows.

For the frozen rows `16269,16267,16271`, the two changed H hosts are absent
from every short/predecessor/successor host field of all `1748` private
tickets.  Their frozen outer bottoms remain legal under the new middles, so
the complete `18646`-edge outer matching survives with `16898` occupied H
and `1748` F receivers.  The raw-warm47 `16898/16898` supplier matching also
survives literally; its three affected selected edges retain nonzero masks:

Again, this supplier replay is raw-warm47 scoped, while socket positivity is
checked separately in both authenticated `s7` owner phases.

The complete supplier projections were also regenerated on the two changed

## 4. Exact scope and next gate

The two-row square already proves that the `1412/1413` global-union zeros are
not invariant under exact target-table rethreading.  It is the smallest
proof-safe role-moving escape and gives an exact one-unit reduction in each
phase and in their union.

The rank-seven theorem identifies the smallest architecture outside the
bottom-relocation face: one P2 row plus two long H suffix rows on an
alternating rank-incidence `C6`.  It gives an explicit authenticated role
escape while retaining the frozen supplier and private-H certificates.

What remains open is not local existence.  For repeated suffix descent one
must regenerate the complete mode catalogue after each changed H suffix,
recompute the entire P2 zero set, and replay all phase-relative supplier and
state/topology rows.  The current artifacts make no such regenerative or
compiler claim.
