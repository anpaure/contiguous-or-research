### Theorem 1.1 (exact protected-resource ledger)

The union of (1.5)--(1.6) is a two-component linear forest with:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{changed A owners}&2b\\
 \text{all protected owners}&2b+4\\

\end{array}                                           \tag{1.7}

Its `2b+2` lower colours are distinct.  Its `2b+2` upper colours are

#### Proof

Coordinate conjugation preserves Johnson adjacency.  The collar endpoints
are fixed, so (1.5)--(1.6) are simple paths with `b+1` edges each.  Every
owner of the left path contains `q^-` and omits `q^+`; every owner of the
right path contains `q^+` and omits `q^-`.  The same signature belongs to
every intersection and union of adjacent owners on its path.  Hence the
owner, lower-colour, and upper-colour resources of the two paths are
pairwise disjoint.  Within one path, coordinate conjugation of the old
strict cutoff path preserves simplicity and both rainbow palettes.  This
is an all-`b` proof; the finite replay is only corroboration.

The literal source replay proves native lower equality.  Interior owners
of either path have degree two and its two endpoints degree one, giving
(1.7).  Thus assigning the two incidences at a degree-two owner to its two
labelled owner slots lifts the bank to a matching in the four-resource
owner-slot hypergraph.  \(\square\)

The count `2b` is the changed-owner count, not the diamond count.  The bare
`A--A` rails have only `2b-2` internal diamonds.  Both joins of both rails
must be included to obtain the literal protected bank in (1.7).

## 4. Why no remote mate path is required now

The old local-delta formulation started from a complete frozen chronology.
Replacing its `X/Y` rails by the new `A` rails duplicated owners already
used remotely and omitted the displaced one-tag owners, so it demanded an
inverse remote exchange.

The protected-bank quantifier chooses the global factor around the new bank
instead.  The new `A` owners are fixed once, at the protected slots.  Every
old one-tag owner remains an ordinary unprescribed vertex of the spanning
host and is completed elsewhere by the residual factor.  There is no
duplicate and no omitted owner.  Thus, at central owner/q1 scope, the remote
mate-path condition has moved entirely into the ordinary global
synchronization/completion problem.  Old-target/source-sector transport
from a frozen parent remains a separate recurrence gate.

What is still not automatic is an occurrence-labelled source realization
of that completion.  The protected slots must include lower/upper tickets
and, for the full residence conclusion, the ordered packet segment and its
endpoint states.  Protecting owner values alone is insufficient.

## 5. Nominal accumulation warning, not a rolling theorem

For the untouched original pair born at jump `i`, its nominal deadline is
`t=b+2i-1`.  If one applies the same oldest/newest transposition formula at
that deadline without accounting for earlier services, its two changed
`A` rails contain `b+i-1` owners each, hence

\[
                         2b+2i-4                    \tag{5.1}
\]

internal rail diamonds.  The formal multiplicity sum over the first `J`
such nominal packets is

\[
 \sum_{i=1}^{J}(2b+2i-4)
       =J^2+(2b-3)J.                                 \tag{5.2}
\]

Including both collars of both nominal rails changes the formal sum to

\[
                         J^2+(2b+1)J.                \tag{5.3}
\]

These formulas do not describe an authenticated sequential rolling bank.
Earlier donors re-enter the deadline queue (already for `b=2`, the first
donor fails again at the next collision), and different-generation banks
have not been proved resource-disjoint.  Their set union can be smaller
than the multiplicity sum or can fail the protected-matching antecedent.

Conditionally, if a construction retained all these nominal packets as
pairwise resource-disjoint protected data, the `o(m)` size antecedent would
require

\[
                         J(J+b)=o(m).                \tag{5.4}
\]

For `J,b=Theta(sqrt(m))`, the formal multiplicity is `Theta(m)`, outside
that antecedent.  Thus a successful recursive construction should
absorb/release earlier refreshed rails rather than plan to freeze this
entire nominal calendar.  Equations (5.2)--(5.4) are only a conditional
size warning; they neither describe the actual post-service deadline queue
nor obstruct rebuilding the factor around only the current refreshed
packet.

The distinct-fresh-label actuator in
`MATH_THEOREM_H2_SPLIT_CORE_ROLLING_RESIDENCE_REFRESH_AND_MUTABLE_OCCURRENCE_GATE_20260801.md`
has a stronger authenticated rolling statement: its historical supports
nest and its **current** changed phase consolidates to two paths of `2t`
diamonds.  That theorem supersedes any attempt to use (5.2) as a general
rolling obstruction.  The present section asserts only the stated nominal
multiplicity warning for the oldest/newest coordinate-transposition
fixtures.
