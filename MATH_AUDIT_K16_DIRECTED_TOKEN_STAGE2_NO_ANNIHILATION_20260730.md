## Result

Starting from the seven authenticated `c1` upper-complete, one-lower-blocker
chronologies reached by the first directed rethread layer, the complete
**cofacet-host directed 3-opt construction census described below** produced
21,856 upper-complete carrier chronologies.  Exact static lower-host replay
found no zero-free chronology.

This is a scoped move-class theorem, not a K16 impossibility theorem.  The
observed blocker graph is not closed: the move class transports the blocker
to many new rank-7 masks.

## Exact scope

- Source roots: first-layer blockers
  `0x0d35, 0x4b38, 0xd838, 0x2d31, 0x0975, 0x3871, 0x0579`.
- Every new seam used two rank-8 cofacets of the current rank-7 blocker.
- All eight standard directed 3-opt segment patterns were admitted.
- All three rethreaded segments had length at least 20.
- Cuts lay strictly after the first flat and before the two fixed tail flats;
  therefore every changed seam was an isolated depth-2 seam.
- Every retained chronology passed literal three-flat, capacity, maximal
  envelope, middle replay, current-blocker host, and all-upper-mask tests.
- The lower audit enumerated every legal proper-prefix cell from exact
  mandatory cores and maximal envelopes, then tested all 26,332 lower targets.

The cofacet restriction is a sufficient construction domain.  All 240 exact
first-layer `c1` blocker-hosting seams were cofacet seams, but this report does
not claim a proof that hypothetical non-cofacet blocker-hosting seams cannot
exist at later roots.

## Interpretation

Within this large exact neighbourhood, a directed rethread almost always
**moves** the unique lower defect rather than removes it.  This motivates
separating the cross-side experiment: two first-layer atoms have zero lower
blockers but one upper blocker.  Their upper-targeted repairs are not part of
the theorem above and must be audited separately.
