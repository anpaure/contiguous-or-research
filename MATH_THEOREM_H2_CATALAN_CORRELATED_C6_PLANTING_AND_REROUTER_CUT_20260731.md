# Correlated C6 planting: a conditional all-parameter cover-down and the exact rerouter cut

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: exact target-subbank criterion, exact outer-fibre and slot-lattice
obstructions, a conditional favourable-common-basis survival lemma, and a
conditional one-chain cover-down theorem.  This does not prove that a
positive density of Delcourt--Postle records is target-aligned.

## 0. Verdict

The new suspended transparent hex catalogue makes the abstract
bank-flexible theorem concrete, but it does not remove its correlation gate.
There is an unpunctured, pairwise-resource-disjoint bank of more than

\[
                              P/54                                      \tag{0.1}
\]

literal C6 packets.  Each packet has a target atom `T`, a planted two-atom
off phase `O`, and a three-atom on phase `N` satisfying

\[
                \operatorname{res}(N)
                  =\operatorname{res}(O)\mathbin{\dot\cup}
                    \operatorname{res}(T).                         \tag{0.2}
\]

Thus a body whose full four-resource leave is a subbank of the target atoms
is completed exactly by the simultaneous switches `O -> N`.

Sparse `C_{2\ell}` switches, including the `C10`, help only after the outer
leave has aligned.  They preserve every lower and upper colour.  They can
rethread physical owners and change the slot leave, but they cannot move a
wrong lower or upper hole onto a C6 target.  This gives an exact, useful cut:

> the lower and upper holes of the DP record must already be the two outer
> projections of one and the same planted target subbank.

Once that cut passes, the remaining slot alignment is a finite 0/1 lattice
fibre generated by the rerouter slot-displacement columns.  A left-kernel
functional separating the desired slot displacement is an exact scoped
no-go.

The all-parameter positive statement is conditional but sharp.  Along one
recursive chain, if a positive density `a_n` of jointly chosen
bank--`Q`--DP records passes the target-coherence cut, lies in the legal
rerouter fibre, and passes the contracted-graphic, cumulative-reserve and
compiler guards, then one such record has deficiency at most

\[
                            {\eta_n\over a_n}P.                   \tag{0.3}
\]

If the surviving planted bank is at least this large, simultaneous C6
activation gives an exact side factor and the local Boolean common cap.
No individual sector edge or individual packet is required to pay its own
reserve debt.

## 1. Native fixed-`Q` packet notation

Use the side parameters

\[
 P=\binom{2n}{n-2},\qquad N=\binom{2n}{n-1},\qquad
 C=\binom{2n}{n}-P.                                      \tag{1.0}
\]

Work in one normalized four-resource side host

\[
                  \widehat G_Q=G_Q-S_0,                          \tag{1.1}
\]

where the **location** of the affine slot baseline `S_0` is part of the
chosen bank state.  Every atom uses one lower colour, one upper colour and
two literal owner slots.

Let

\[
          \mathcal P=\{(T_j,O_j,N_j):j\in J_0\}                  \tag{1.2}
\]

be a bank of suspended C6 packets supplied by the transparent-hex theorem.
Assume their complete supports are pairwise resource-disjoint.  Write

\[
 T_j=(D_j,V_j;s_j^0,s_j^1),                                    \tag{1.3}
\]

and let `S_j` be its four-resource set.  Equation (0.2) holds for every
`j`.  Resource privacy implies that the target sets `S_j` themselves are
pairwise disjoint.

For `I \subseteq J_0`, put

\[
 T_I=\mathbin{\dot\bigcup}_{j\in I}S_j,
 \qquad O_I=\mathbin{\dot\bigcup}_{j\in I}O_j,
 \qquad N_I=\mathbin{\dot\bigcup}_{j\in I}N_j.                  \tag{1.4}
\]

Here and below a packet is usable only when its two off atoms are present,
its target resources are free, and all six named slots are legal.

## 2. Exact target-subbank coherence

Let `r` be a binary leave vector on the normalized host.

### Theorem 2.1 (four-resource coherence)

The following are equivalent.

1. There is an `I \subseteq J_0` with

   \[
                              r=\chi_{T_I}.                     \tag{2.1}
   \]

2. Every resource outside `\bigcup_j S_j` has leave value zero and, for
   every `j`,

   \[
       r(D_j)=r(V_j)=r(s_j^0)=r(s_j^1)\in\{0,1\}.              \tag{2.2}
   \]

When these conditions hold, `I` is unique.

#### Proof

The target supports are pairwise disjoint.  Hence a union of target columns
has one common binary coefficient on the four resources of each target and
zero outside their union.  Conversely (2.2) chooses exactly the targets
whose common coefficient is one, giving (2.1).  Uniqueness follows from
disjointness. \(\square\)

The outer part of (2.2) is

\[
                         r(D_j)=r(V_j)                            \tag{2.3}
\]

for every target, together with zero outer leave outside the target bank.
It is necessary before slots, topology, reserve, or common cap are
considered.

### Corollary 2.2 (exact planted-body count)

Let `|I|=t`.  If a matching contains the `2t` off atoms `O_I` and has leave
exactly `T_I`, then its exterior part has `P-3t` atoms and covers precisely
the `P-3t` lower colours and `P-3t` upper colours outside the complete C6
supports.  The full off-state matching has size `P-t`.  Conversely, an exact
exterior matching on those complementary resources, adjoined to `O_I`, has
leave `T_I`.

#### Proof

Each complete packet support contains three atoms' worth of resources, while
its planted off phase contains two atoms.  Resource privacy lets the counts
add over `I`.  Exact coverage outside the supports proves both directions.
\(\square\)

## 3. What sparse rerouters can and cannot align

Orient one sparse `2\ell`-cycle switch from its old phase to its new phase.
In the notation of the sparse-cycle theorem, both phases use the same lower
colours `D_i`, the same upper colours `V_i`, and the common owners `H_i`.
The old-only slots are `P_i` and the new-only slots are `Q_i`.  Therefore the
leave displacement is

\[
 \Delta_\gamma^{L}=0,\qquad
 \Delta_\gamma^{U}=0,\qquad
 \Delta_\gamma^{S}=\sum_i\chi_{P_i}-\sum_i\chi_{Q_i}.           \tag{3.1}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 (outer-fibre invariance)

Every sequence of legal sparse-cycle rerouters preserves the lower and upper
leave vectors pointwise.  Consequently, if a DP record violates (2.3), has
an outer hole outside the planted target bank, or uses different target
index sets on the two outer shores, no such rerouter sequence can make its
leave a C6 target subbank.

#### Proof

Each switch has zero lower and upper incidence boundary.  Summing (3.1)
over any legal sequence proves pointwise invariance.  The stated failures
contradict the necessary outer projection of Theorem 2.1. \(\square\)

This is a genuine cut obstruction, not a lack of search.  For example each
functional

\[
                         r\longmapsto r(D_j)-r(V_j)               \tag{3.2}
\]

is invariant on a rerouter orbit and must vanish on every target subbank.

There is a useful form which does not preselect one disjoint packet per
target.  For a fixed `Q` and exterior scaffold `F`, make a bipartite graph

\[
                         \mathcal T_{Q,F}                         \tag{3.3}
\]

between surviving lower colours and upper colours.  Put an edge `DV` when
there is at least one legal suspended-C6 option with target atom `(D,V)`,
plantable off phase, and available nonpacket guards.  Let `L,U` be the two
outer leave sets of a DP record; necessarily `|L|=|U|`.

### Theorem 3.2 (target Hall/Farkas cut)

A necessary condition for the outer leave to be a C6 target bank is that
`\mathcal T_{Q,F}[L,U]` have a perfect matching.  Equivalently,

\[
             |N_{\mathcal T}(X)\cap U|\ge |X|\qquad(X\subseteq L). \tag{3.4}
\]

If a row fails, restrict the target-column system to the induced graph
`\mathcal T[L,U]`.  Equivalently in the full catalogue, weight the lower
vertices in `X` by `+1`, **all** upper vertices in
`N_{\mathcal T}(X)` by `-1`, and every other outer vertex by zero.  Every
available target column has nonpositive weight, while the leave has positive
weight.  This is a literal Farkas separator.  It remains valid after every
sparse-cycle rerouter by Theorem 3.1.

#### Proof

Distinct target atoms covering `L,U` induce a matching saturating both
shores.  Hall gives (3.4) and the converse at the outer-pair level.  The
displayed full-catalogue weights have value zero on an edge leaving `X`,
value at most zero on every other available edge, and demand value
`|X|-|N_{\mathcal T}(X)\cap U|>0` on the demand. \(\square\)

Hall is sufficient only for pairing the two outer leaves.  The chosen C6
options may still collide in auxiliary colours or slots.  For the
resource-private prepacked bank of Section 2, `\mathcal T` is itself a
matching and (3.4) reduces exactly to the coherence equalities (2.3).

Now fix a finite catalogue of oriented rerouters available from a record
`F`, and make the slot-displacement matrix

\[
 B_S=\bigl[\Delta_{\gamma}^{S}\bigr]_{\gamma}.                  \tag{3.5}
\]

Let `I` be the outer target index set forced by (2.3), and put

\[
 b_I=\chi_{S(T_I)}-r_S(F),                                      \tag{3.6}
\]

where

\[
                 S(T_I)=\mathbin{\dot\bigcup}_{j\in I}
                           \{s_j^0,s_j^1\}.                     \tag{3.6a}
\]

The sign convention is adjusted if a catalogue column is oriented in the
reverse direction.

### Corollary 3.3 (slot-lattice cut)

A necessary condition for rerouting `F` to the target slot leave is

\[
                         B_Sx=b_I                                \tag{3.7}
\]

for a phase vector `x` in the actual 0/1 reachable fibre.  In particular,
if an integer row `y` satisfies

\[
                         y^{\mathsf T}B_S=0,
 \qquad                  y^{\mathsf T}b_I\ne0,                  \tag{3.8}
\]

then the planted cover-down is impossible in this rerouter catalogue.

The integer left-kernel rows test the rational-span obstruction.  Smith
normal form of `B_S` supplies the additional torsion congruences for exact
integer-lattice membership.  Every sparse-cycle column has total slot sum
zero, so total slot count is the first automatic invariant.

If all rerouter supports are mutually private, every old phase is present,
and every new phase is internally compatible, `x in {0,1}^m` is the exact
algebraic phase cube.  Exterior owner capacity, graphic independence and
reserve remain additional rows; lattice membership alone does not certify
them.

## 4. Favourable common bases retain a linear raw supply

Let `mu` be the exact uniform-marginal distribution on synchronized common
bases.  A fixed lower colour is punctured with probability at most

\[
             p_n={C\over N}={2(2n+1)\over n(n+2)}.                \tag{4.1}
\]

The raw packet theorem supplies a disjoint bank of order

\[
 T_n>{P(n+2)\over54n}>{P\over54}.                              \tag{4.2}
\]

Let `\mathcal G_n` be any family of common bases satisfying the upstream
requirements one wishes to impose before the DP body is chosen—for example
the fixed-host fractional-capacity row and a declared terminal/reserve
state—and put

\[
                         \beta_n=\mu(\mathcal G_n).               \tag{4.3}
\]

Assume `\beta_n>0`.

### Theorem 4.1 (conditional favourable-`Q` survival)

If each packet risks three punctured lower colours, some
`Q in \mathcal G_n` retains at least

\[
                  T_n\left(1-{3p_n\over\beta_n}\right)          \tag{4.4}

packets.  For a coupled two-shore packet with six risk colours, replace
`3p_n` by `6p_n`.

#### Proof

Let `X(Q)` count invalid packets.  Resource disjointness is not needed for
this expectation: the union bound and the one-point marginal give

\[
                            \mathbb E_\mu X\le3p_nT_n.            \tag{4.5}
\]

Since

\[
 \mathbb E[X\mid Q\in\mathcal G_n]
 ={\mathbb E[X\mathbf1_{\mathcal G_n}]\over\beta_n}
 \le {3p_nT_n\over\beta_n},                                    \tag{4.6}
\]

one favourable basis attains at most that conditional average.  Subtract
from `T_n`.  The two-shore form is the same six-event union bound.
\(\square\)

The bound is useful when `\beta_n>3p_n` (or `6p_n`).  If it is not, the
argument is silent; it does not prove that every favourable basis kills the
bank.  In particular this is a conditional intersection theorem, not an
arbitrary-`Q` packet-supply claim.

## 5. Conditional correlated-planting theorem

Fix the cycle cutoff `L` used by the Delcourt--Postle conflict system, let
`0<\alpha<1` be its imported DP colouring exponent, and put

\[
 D=2(n+1)(n+2),\qquad s=\lceil D^{1-\alpha}\rceil,\qquad
 K_D=D+s,
 \qquad \rho_n={s+16n+16+4/n\over D+s}.                 \tag{5.0}
\]

Fix a risk count `r\in\{3,6\}` and set

\[
             B_n=T_n\left(1-{r p_n\over\beta_n}\right).         \tag{5.0a}
\]

Let `\mathcal H_n\subseteq\mathcal G_n` be the family of favourable common
bases retaining at least `B_n` packets.  Theorem 4.1 makes
`\mathcal H_n` nonempty whenever this lower bound is invoked; it asserts no
positive measure for `\mathcal H_n`.  The padded averaging universe below
uses only bank states over `\mathcal H_n`, which is legal because the DP
estimate is uniform in `Q`.

Let `z_n` be the maximum number of protected host resources and `r_n` the
maximum number of forbidden host atoms among these padded bank states.  Use
the padded whole-colour ledger of item `2300A`, with protected-resource and
cycle-pruning costs included in

\[
 \eta_n=\rho_n+{Dz_n+r_n\over K_D P}+{1\over L+1}.              \tag{5.1}
\]

The averaging universe contains each padded
`(recursive bank state,Q,DP colour)` record exactly once, with one fixed
colouring and pruning for each state.  Call a base record **plantable** when
there exist a subbank `I` of surviving C6 packets and a legal
sparse-rerouter macro such that:

1. its normalized outer leave is exactly
   `\{D_j:j\in I\}` and `\{V_j:j\in I\}`;
2. the rerouter macro is reachable and serializable, ends with every
   `O_j (j\in I)` present, and solves the target slot equation (3.7);
3. after the old phases are deleted, all new rerouter edges and all C6 on
   edges are loopless and independent in the physical graph contracted by
   the retained forest;
4. owner capacities and protected anchors are respected;
5. on both shores the completed macro state passes the exact signed-flux
   inequality

   \[
      h(W)+(2n+1)\bigl(\phi_B(W)-\chi_{\rm inh}(W)\bigr)\ge0   \tag{5.2}
   \]

   for every occurrence neighbourhood; under the strict four-sector
   running-intersection hypothesis this is equivalently certified by
   nonnegative cumulative prefix reserve along both sector paths; and
6. every protected pointwise cap is avoided and every downstream compiler
   guard has zero displacement or an independently proved regeneration; and
7. the packet/rerouter changes are residual-face-internal, the opposite side
   rows are chosen jointly, and in the fixed-four residual atomization every
   final augmented directed cycle contains exactly one complementary
   endpoint-pair reset.

Let `a_n` be the number of plantable **base records** divided by the number
of padded base records.  Witness multiplicity is not counted; after a record
is accepted one may choose any canonical witnessing pair `(I,z)`.

Equivalently, if `\Delta_R` denotes **covered-resource** displacement of the
chosen rerouter macro, the central exact equation is

\[
        \operatorname{leave}(F)-\Delta_R z
             =\sum_{j\in I}\chi_{S_j},
 \qquad O_j\subseteq F^z\quad(j\in I).                         \tag{5.3}
\]

### Theorem 5.1 (all-parameter conditional C6 cover-down)

If `a_n>0`, one plantable record has deficiency

\[
                             t\le{\eta_n\over a_n}P.              \tag{5.4}
\]

Here `t=|I|` is its common lower/upper deficiency.

If its surviving packet bank contains at least `t` targets, simultaneous
activation of the selected C6 packets produces an exact normalized side
matching.  Its physical support is a forest, its terminal state passes the
exact two-shore signed-flux reserve test, and it has an existential local
Boolean common cap.  Under the strict four-sector running-intersection
hypothesis, the reserve conclusion is equivalently the two cumulative-prefix
systems.

If these hypotheses hold from one chosen state at every parameter above a
finite authenticated base, including the jointly required opposite-row
conditions in item 7, the transitions form one exact recursive chain.  No
transition for every parent or every `Q` is needed.

#### Proof

The padded-colour averaging theorem gives (5.4).  Conditions 1--2 and
Theorem 2.1 say that immediately before activation the complete leave is
exactly `T_I` and every off phase is present.  Summing the pairwise-private
identities (0.2) therefore covers every resource exactly once and adds
exactly `t` matching atoms.

Conditions 3--4 give physical matching and graphic legality.  Condition 5
is the exact final/exported-state signed-flux reserve theorem; in the strict
four-sector running-intersection architecture it is equivalently the
cumulative-prefix theorem.  No constituent edge or packet is required to be
reserve-feasible by itself.  Exact outer and slot saturation plus the
physical forest give the local Boolean common cap.
Condition 6 supplies the logically separate protected/compiler row.
Residual-face internality and the one-reset row in condition 7 give the
required complement-geodesic path decomposition, so iteration proves the
last statement. \(\square\)

For fixed `\ell=5`, sparse rerouters exist uniformly once `n>=8`; smaller
parameters are finite base cases.  If `a_n\gg\eta_n` and
`\beta_n\gg1/n`, then (5.4) is `o(P)` while Theorem 4.1 leaves a linear C6
bank.  Thus raw packet **capacity** is not the asymptotic gate.  The missing
theorem is positive density for the coherent outer leave and its reachable
slot/graphic/reserve fibre.

More explicitly, put

\[
              \theta_n={n+2\over54n},\qquad q_n=rp_n,           \tag{5.5}
\]

where `r=3` for one shore and `r=6` for a coupled two-shore packet.  The raw
capacity and favourable-`Q` survival bounds imply the sufficient scalar
inequality

\[
 {\eta_n\over a_n}
   \le \theta_n\left(1-{q_n\over\beta_n}\right),
 \qquad \beta_n>q_n.                                           \tag{5.6}
\]

Here `a_n` is computed on the padded universe over the same high-survival
family `\mathcal H_n`; otherwise the two existential choices of `Q` need not
coincide.  Inequality (5.6) only guarantees enough surviving templates; it
does not prove outer Hall alignment or rerouter reachability.  Moreover the full
`\Theta(P)` raw bank must not be frozen as protected support: doing so would
make `z_n=\Theta(P)` and destroy the small-reserve premise.  Each correlated
state plants and protects only its selected `o(P)` subbank.  That selected
protection is already counted in `z_n,r_n`; hence `\eta_n=o(a_n)` is a
conditional noncircularity requirement, not a conclusion of the packing
bound.

## 6. Exact scoped no-go certificates

Within the move class of Theorem 5.1, any one of the following is a rigorous
no-go certificate for a proposed record.

1. **Outer coherence cut:** a target equation (2.3) fails, an outer hole is
   outside the target bank, or the lower and upper target index sets differ.
2. **Slot-lattice cut:** a row `y` satisfying (3.8).
3. **C6 catalogue cut:** for a target
   `A=(D,V;(D+p,i),(D+q,j))`, every packet uses a slot at one of the `n`
   owners

   \[
                              \mathcal W_A=\{V-b:b\in D\}.       \tag{6.1}
   \]

   Saturating or protecting all residual slots in `\mathcal W_A` kills the
   complete `2n(n-2)` canonical packet menu.
4. **Capacitated packet Hall cut:** for targets `A` and a fixed exterior
   scaffold `F`, let `\mathcal P_A(Q,F)` be the packet options whose
   auxiliary colours survive `Q`, whose off atoms are plantable, and whose
   nonpacket guards are legal.  Each option `p=(b,c,o)` consumes the
   mandatory owner slot

   \[
                         \omega(p)=V_A-b\in\mathcal W_A.         \tag{6.2}
   \]

   If `\kappa_F(w)` literal packet slots remain at owner `w`, every private
   one-packet-per-target selection must satisfy

   \[
       |X|\le\sum_{w\in N_{Q,F}(X)}\kappa_F(w)                  \tag{6.3}
   \]

   for every target subset `X`.  A violation is a solver-free no-go.  The
   cuts are necessary, not sufficient for privacy of the other eleven
   support resources.
5. **Common-basis deletion cut:** let `R_-` and `R_+` be the lower and upper
   packet risk colours.  Let `M_1,M_2` be the two synchronized matroids on
   occurrence ground `E`, with palette maps `\tau,\upsilon`, and restrict
   them to

   \[
     E_0=\{e\in E:\tau(e)\notin R_-,\ \upsilon(e)\notin R_+\}. \tag{6.4}
   \]

   A size-`C` common basis avoiding the risks exists exactly when

   \[
       r_1(X)+r_2(E_0\setminus X)\ge C\qquad(X\subseteq E_0).   \tag{6.5}
   \]

   This is the matroid-intersection min--max.  Uniform marginals alone do not
   imply these deletion-minor cuts, nor the later fixed-host `\rho(Q)=0`
   row.
6. **Fixed-rerouter graphic cut:** after deleting the old rerouter edges and
   contracting the exterior forest, a fixed selected family is legal
   exactly when every new edge is loopless and

   \[
        r_g\left(\bigcup_j E_j^{\rm new}\right)=\sum_j\ell_j.   \tag{6.6}
   \]

   For menus of alternative coupled `\ell_j`-edge blocks, the analogous
   subset-rank inequalities are necessary but are not ordinary Rado
   sufficiency: each option must be selected as a whole block.
7. **Reserve cut:** some final upper or lower sector prefix has negative
   cumulative reserve for every reachable rerouter phase.
8. **Graphic/cap cut:** every reachable phase creates a loop/circuit after
   contraction or changes a frozen pointwise cap.

Items 1--2 are invariant/algebraic; items 3--4 are exact owner-capacity
obstructions from the C6 catalogue; item 5 is an exact common-basis cut;
items 6--8 are physical guard cuts.  None is an unrestricted Catalan or
`nu=B` no-go.

## 7. Scope

This note proves the correct composition law for the new catalogue:

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{favourable }Q
 \;\to\;\text{coherent outer target leave}
 \;\to\;\text{rerouter slot/off-phase fibre}
 \;\to\;\text{C6 activation}
 \;\to\;\text{final reserve/common-cap audit}.}
\]

The order is load-bearing.  Sparse cycles cannot manufacture outer
alignment, the word “transparent” does not imply strict-occurrence reserve
transparency, and an existential final Boolean cap is weaker than a frozen
pointwise or compiler cap.  The theorem is all-parameter conditional and
bank-flexible; the positive-density aligned-record theorem remains open.
