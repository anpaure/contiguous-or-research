# The binary native `C16` escape gives a thirteen-root scaffold but doubles the singleton `G2` loss

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Status:** unconditional for the exact target-relevant native single-cycle
atlas through `C18`, the complete `C6/C8` paired menus, the target-relevant
`C6+C10` menu, the two displayed binary `C16` packets, their all-suffix
component action, and their literal replays through suffix semilength four.
The suffix-local two-target backup and contracted thirteen-role port
realization are reductions, not claimed constructions.

## 0. Result

Start with the frozen typed-safe mixed `C8+C6` creator of `T0`.  The first
owner-disjoint **single** native alternating cycle that repairs every old
typed support value through `q3` has length sixteen.  The exact

```text

     8 typed-q2-safe creators,
     6 fully base-q3-safe creators.
```

The six split sharply.

* Four have an odd five-cycle in their common old/new z-free owner graph.
  Each makes two disjoint component merges, of seven and six old roots.
* Two admit a common binary phase and merge exactly thirteen old roots
  into one.  They have the favorable exact topology

  ```text

  ```

  Their physical thirteen-role sign vector is

  ```text
                   (-,+,-,+,-,-,-,+,+,-,+,+,+)       (0.2)
  ```

  up to simultaneous reversal.

Thus the binary `C16` pair is a genuine positive topology/common-phase
scaffold.  It is nevertheless not an all-suffix `q3` repair.  Besides the
frozen boundary debt

```text

```

each `C16` contributes one more debt with the same suffix target:

```text

 +000010111111[G2(V)] - 100010101111[G2(V)],

candidate 5:
 +000010111111[G2(V)] - 100010011111[G2(V)].        (0.3)
```

For a `G2` fibre of size `f`, every displayed coefficient is `+f` or
`-f`.  At suffix semilength two the unique fibre has size two and both
negative targets have load `5 -> 3`.  At suffix semilength three the
singleton

```text

```

has load `1 -> 0` at **both** negative prefixes.  Hence each binary
`C16` tensor has exactly two `q3` casualties at `m=9`, and four at `m=10`.
There is no hidden fibrewise surplus or untouched-provider injection.

Neither the next single-cycle menu nor the first successful longer paired
menu removes this obstruction.

* The complete target-relevant `C18` atlas has `15` base-`q3`-safe
  candidates, eight with common binary phase.  Every one still loses
  `100011010111[110111]` at `m=9`; every binary candidate loses at least
  one further row.  The best six have two casualties, exactly the same
  minimum as `C16`.  Their topology reduces thirteen components per suffix
  copy, but uses a longer native cycle.
* The complete target-relevant `C6+C10` menu has five typed-`q3`-safe
  pairs, all with common binary phase.  Each has exactly two casualties at
  `m=9`, one of them again `100011010111[110111]`, and reduces eleven
  components per suffix copy.  It is therefore a useful shorter paired
  scaffold but not a suffix repair.

The sharp constructive continuation is therefore not another prefix
cycle.  Keep either binary thirteen-root scaffold and add a suffix-local
two-channel backup, keyed by the ordered pair `(c0(V),c1(V))` (or the
injective mark `c0(V)`), which creates both negative rows in `(0.3)` only
on low-surplus fibres while having zero typed current through `q2`.

## 5. Exact conditional thirteen-role port interface

Contract each thirteen-root block from Section 3.  Give it one global sign
`tau_V`; its terminal roles then have the relative signs `(0.2)`.  For
every chosen quotient edge, a later port theorem must select one of these
thirteen physical roles and one of the thirteen candidate starts, and must
verify:

1. a complete source block at that signed role survives every `C16` cut;
2. the endpoint data satisfy the common-history multiway criterion at the
   same start;
3. coalesced requests use one literal word with transitive history
   consistency;
4. distinct starts at one supernode are separated by at least `d+1`; and
5. the selected quotient is connected.

The numerical coincidence "thirteen roles and thirteen starts" gives no
automatic pigeonhole theorem.  Packet cuts and sign compatibility still
have to be audited.  Section 5 is an exact sufficient interface, not a
claim that the native thirteen-port theorem descends.

## 6. Minimal marked-collar reduction

The binary candidate 5 scaffold should be retained.  A repair is needed only for
the two negative channels

```text
N0[G2(V)],  Nj[G2(V)]                                (6.1)
```

on fibres whose complete prefixed old load is at most the fibre size.  A
proof-safe suffix-local packet must therefore:

1. be keyed physically by the ordered pair `(c0(V),c1(V))`, or by the
   injective first-colour mark `c0(V)`, even though its positive target is
   the collapsed union `G2(V)`;
2. create both targets in `(6.1)` on every selected low-surplus fibre;
3. have zero owner, upper-`q1`, lower-`q1`, lower-`q2`, and upper-`q2`
   current, or provide literal backups for every negative value;
4. preserve the common binary phase of the thirteen-root scaffold;
5. be topology-neutral after suppression, or have an exact suffix-disjoint
   contractible action incorporated into `(3.1)`; and
6. expose a terminal compatible with the conditional interface of
   Section 5.

At the first bad suffix `V=110100`, this becomes a finite two-target native
`C6/C8` search on the suffix-side provider paths.  Failure of that exact
menu is the threshold for a genuinely nonnative doubled lead/resident
collar.

Candidate 5 has a structural advantage.  Its two prefix targets

```text
A=100011010111[G],   B=100010011111[G]
```

differ by one Johnson exchange (prefix label `5` is replaced by label
`8`).  Candidate 4's two targets differ by two exchanges.  The following
adjacent-window normal form therefore applies to candidate 5.  Write
`A=H+a`, `B=H+b`.  Choose distinct `h1,h2,h3` in `H`, put

```text
O1=H-h1,   O2=H-h2,   O3=H-h3,
O0=O1-u+a, O4=O3-v+b,
```

with `u in O1`, `v in O3` chosen so that all five vertices are distinct.
Then `O0,O1,O2,O3,O4` is a simple Johnson path, and

```text
O0 union O1 union O2 union O3 = A,
O1 union O2 union O3 union O4 = B.                  (6.2)
```

Thus five owners are enough, and are minimal within the consecutive
four-owner-window architecture, to create both missing `q3` targets.
The suffix mark must choose `h1,u,v` as a function of the injective
`c0(V)`, not merely of the collapsed value `G2(V)`.  Equation `(6.2)` is
only the open rail.  Closing it to an alternating native `C6/C8` or marked
resident packet while making every typed current through `q2` vanish is
the remaining local gate.
