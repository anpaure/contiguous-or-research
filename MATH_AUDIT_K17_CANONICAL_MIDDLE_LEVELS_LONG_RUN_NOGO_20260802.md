## 3. Oriented flip-gap audit

Orient the alternating cycle as

\[
T_i\xrightarrow{p_i}X_{i+1}\xrightarrow{q_i}T_{i+1},
\]

where `p_i` is a deletion and `q_i` is an insertion.  For every occurrence
of every coordinate, the audit records its polarity, physical flip position,
next occurrence, cyclic distance, and induced owner-run length.

The minimum distance between consecutive flips of one coordinate is

\[

\]

In the orientation relevant to positive residence, the short part of the
exact insertion-to-next-deletion histogram is

```text
flip distance 3       5695 occurrences       owner-run length 2
flip distance 5       1598 occurrences       owner-run length 3
```

There is no positive owner run of length one.  This is the automatic gain
from the perfect rank-8 first shadow.  Nevertheless, for `d=3` the long-run
reduction requires every insertion-to-next-deletion distance to be at least

\[
                         2d+1=7.
\]

The canonical cycle therefore has exactly

\[
                         5695+1598=7293
\]

cyclic positive residence defects.  A second direct scan of all coordinate
runs reproduces the flip-gap histogram exactly.

The dual deletion-to-insertion audit is also frozen.  In particular it has
`6851` zero gaps of length one, so this fixture is even farther from
simultaneous positive/negative residence.  That dual failure is not needed
for the present positive-residence no-go.
