## 1. Implication audit

The conditional proof is exact.

1. Every odd and even terminal certificate now has its own
   dimension-appropriate literal scaffold of length at most `B(k)+C`.
2. Row 1 supplies all middle and upper targets on that same branch
   chronology.
3. Row 2 materializes candidate lower target--cell incidences; it no longer
   identifies a candidate system with an integral target assignment.  The
   injective selection is explicitly delegated to Row 3.
4. Row 3 fixes one cap state, one common ticket ground set, one exhaustive
   target/background partition, one background model, all physical
   capacities, terminal types, and global product closure.  It requires
   either the exact all-subset Rado rank bounds or the complete balanced
   private-prefix hypotheses needed to promote a bounded-overload suffix
   catalogue to those bounds.
5. The fixed-state Rado/Edmonds theorem therefore bounds the complete
   terminal omission set by

   \[
   \beta+|Z|+k_0+k_1\le C.
   \]

6. Appending one literal letter for every omitted nonempty mask gives length

   \[
   B(k)+C+C=B(k)+2C.
   \]

7. The odd transition certificates export one compatible sequence
   `g_m -> g_(m+1)`.  The odd terminalization covers dimension `2m+1`, and
   the separate dimension-appropriate even terminalization covers `2m+2`.
   Terminal repair is not exported.  Hence all sufficiently large
   dimensions are covered, and the finite initial segment changes only the
   absolute constant.

Thus `PDDS(C)` implies `nu(k)<=B(k)+O(1)` with the advertised coarse
constant `2C`.

## 2. Necessary-versus-sufficient scope audit

The synthesis keeps the theorem classes separate correctly.

- Architecture-free endpoint chains, dense `gamma_d`, and critical flag
  codegrees are used only as necessary/static diagnostics.
- The dense fractional optimum is not promoted to integral named-target
  rounding or a sliding Ferrers cocycle.
- The protected Catalan theorem is quoted with its exact protected-row
  hypotheses: pairwise distinct protected colours and distinct colour bank
  size `<(m-1)/2`.
- The odd diagonal `q1` theorem is used only after a `q1`-exact equal-shore
  row and compatible unused typed sockets are supplied.  It is not applied
  to the even branch.
- The even owner/upper chronology and lower physicalization are explicit
  `PDDS` premises, not consequences attributed to the odd Catalan/Ferrers
  theorems.
- Frozen private background deletion is not conflated with adaptive matroid
  contraction.
- A port overload bound is not conflated with logical ticket rank without
  balanced port-complete private prefixes.
- Two marginal occurrence linkages are not combined without one cap state,
  allocated shared capacities, terminal typing, and global product closure.
- Per-dimension existence is not conflated with a compatible regenerative
  spine.

No row marked closed retains a hidden construction claim.

## 3. Exact open statement

The only promoted construction hypothesis is `PDDS(C)` itself: one
compatible odd auxiliary spine with dimension-appropriate odd/even terminal
certificates in which owner/upper chronology, literal lower
physicalization, the common two-coordinate compiler, and regeneration are
coinstantiated with a uniform bound.  None of the cited component theorems
proves this joint existence.

Accordingly,

\[
\boxed{\text{the conditional implication is proved, but }
       \nu(k)\le B(k)+O(1)\text{ remains open}.}
\]
