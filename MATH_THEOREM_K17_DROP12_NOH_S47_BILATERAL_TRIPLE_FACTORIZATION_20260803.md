Fix one S source `s`, phase `phi`, declared key `k`, and unordered helpers

For a ticket `w`, let `F(w)` be its physical provider-row footprint.

Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
B_{s,\phi}^{f,g}(k)
 &=\{w\in W_s^\phi(k;\{s\}):d_f,d_g\notin F(w)\},\\
G1_{s,\phi}^{f\mid g}(k)
 &=\{w\in W_s^\phi(k;\{s,f\}):h_f\in F(w),\ d_g\notin F(w)\},\\

\[
