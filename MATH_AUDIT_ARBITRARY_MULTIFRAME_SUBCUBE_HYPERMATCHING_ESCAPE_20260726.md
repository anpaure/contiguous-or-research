# Audit: arbitrary multiframe subcube hypermatchings

Date: 2026-07-26

Method: pure mathematics only.  The valid recursive context-array factor is
used only through whole-packet legality and simultaneous literal trace
injectivity.  No crossed recursion is used.

## 0. Audited verdict

The complete-quartet connected-\(U_{\boldsymbol\kappa}\) obstruction does
**not** apply to arbitrary owner-disjoint subcube packets selected from
different frames.  It applies only when the allowed integral variable is a
whole shore on each component generated by the status-cell partitions.

The failure of the stronger assertion is already literal on \(J(4,2)\).
The packets

\[
 \{12,13\},\qquad \{14,24\},\qquad \{23,34\}
\tag{0.1}
\]

partition \(\binom{[4]}2\), while they extend respectively to the frames

\[
 23|14,\qquad 12|34,\qquad 24|13,
\tag{0.2}
\]

whose edge union is \(K_4\).  Thus connected frame union does not force a
global frame in the unrestricted packet-matching model.

For \(r\)-dimensional orientation packets the exact owner hypergraph is
regular:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\mathscr P_r|
   &={ (2m)!\over (m-r)!^2\,2^r r!},\\
 D_r&=\binom mr^2r!,\\
 {D_r(j)\over D_r}
   &={\binom rj\over\binom mj^2}\qquad(1\le j\le r),
\end{aligned}
\tag{0.3}
\]

where \(D_r(j)\) is the codegree of owners at Johnson distance \(j\);
it is zero for \(j>r\).  These counts remove the owner-side global-frame
obstruction, but do not settle target coverage.

There are two equivalent exact formulations of the surviving target gate.

1. In the quota-marked augmented hypergraph, a matching with owner leave
   \(L_0=o(W/H)\) and total mandatory-target leave \(L_T=o(W)\) suffices.
2. In the unthinned cover-with-surplus formulation, if \(L=W-S\) owners
   are uncovered and
   \[
      Q_j=\sum_T(Z_{j,T}-c_j)(Z_{j,T}-c_j-1),
      \qquad c_j=\left\lfloor{S\over N_j}\right\rfloor,
   \tag{0.4}
   \]
   then
   \[
      2HL+\sum_jQ_j=o(W)
   \tag{0.5}
   \]
   suffices.

The exact cross-depth spread condition is therefore not ordinary small
owner codegree.  It is the target-overlap covariance inequality

\[
 \sum_j\sum_{P\ne P'}K_j(P,P')\pi_{PP'}
 \le
 \sum_j\bigl(2c_jS-N_jc_j(c_j+1)\bigr)+o(W),
\tag{0.6}
\]

where \(K_j(P,P')\) is the number of common depth-\(j\) targets and
\(\pi_{PP'}\) is the joint selection probability.  Equation (0.6), together
with \(HL=o(W)\), implies that one deterministic matching has aggregate
target leave \(o(W)\).

There is also a sharp warning against a generic nibble.  In the balanced
quota hypergraph of whole subcubes, every accepted depth-\(q\) trace is
incident with exactly the \(2^q\) packet owners obtained by freely
orienting its \(q\) varied pairs.  Consequently

\[
 {d_{\mathrm{end},q}\over D}= {2^q\over\binom{m+q}q},
 \qquad
 {\Delta_2\over D}\ge {2\over m+1}\quad(q=1).
\tag{0.7}
\]

A whole \(Q_r\) packet has \(2^r\) owner vertices.  Simultaneous validity
through \(H\) for the present compiler implies, in particular, \(r\ge2H\).
Hence

\[
 K{\Delta_2\over D}\ge {2^{2H+1}\over m+1}.
\tag{0.8}
\]

At \(H=A\sqrt m\), this diverges exponentially.  Thus every proposed
growing-rank theorem requiring \(K\Delta_2/D=o(1)\) is inapplicable.
This is not a nonexistence theorem: a chronological, target-anticorrelated
matching or absorber remains open.

## 1. The packet hypergraph \(\mathscr P_r\)

Put \(n=2m\), \(\Omega_m=\binom{[n]}m\), and
\(W=|\Omega_m|\).  Choose a frozen-one set

\[
                         F\subset[n],\qquad |F|=m-r,
\tag{1.1}
\]

and an \(r\)-edge matching \(M\) disjoint from \(F\).  The unused
\(m-r\) coordinates are frozen to zero.  Define

\[
 \mathcal P(F,M)
 =\{F\cup X:X\text{ chooses one endpoint of every edge of }M\}.
\tag{1.2}
\]

This is a literal \(Q_r\) of \(2^r\) middle owners.  Let
\(\mathscr P_r\) contain every distinct set (1.2) once.

### Theorem 1.1 (exact owner census)

The edge count, degree, and distance codegrees are (0.3).

#### Proof

Choose \(F\), choose the \(2r\) active coordinates among the remaining
\(m+r\), and match them:

\[
 |\mathscr P_r|
 =\binom{2m}{m-r}\binom{m+r}{2r}{(2r)!\over2^rr!}
 ={(2m)!\over(m-r)!^2\,2^rr!}.
\tag{1.3}
\]

For a fixed owner \(X\), choose \(r\) active coordinates in \(X\), \(r\)
outside \(X\), and biject them.  This gives

\[
                         D_r=\binom mr^2r!.
\tag{1.4}
\]

Let \(X,Y\) have Johnson distance \(j\).  In a common packet, the \(j\)
coordinates of \(X\setminus Y\) must be bijected to the \(j\) coordinates
of \(Y\setminus X\).  Choose and biject the remaining \(r-j\) active
coordinates from \(X\cap Y\) and from the complement of \(X\cup Y\).
Thus

\[
 D_r(j)
 =j!\binom{m-j}{r-j}^{\!2}(r-j)!
\tag{1.5}
\]

for \(j\le r\), and it is zero otherwise.  Dividing (1.5) by (1.4) gives
the last line of (0.3). \(\square\)

For \(r\le m/4\), the ratios decrease with \(j\), because

\[
 {D_r(j+1)\over D_r(j)}
 ={(r-j)(j+1)\over(m-j)^2}<1.
\tag{1.6}
\]

Hence \(\Delta_2/D_r=r/m^2\) in that range.

## 2. Why the component no-go has narrower scope

For \(m=2,r=1\), the three choices

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal P(\{1\},\{23\})&=\{12,13\},\\
 \mathcal P(\{4\},\{12\})&=\{14,24\},\\
 \mathcal P(\{3\},\{24\})&=\{23,34\}
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.1}
\]

are disjoint and exhaust \(\Omega_2\).  Extending their active edges by
the frozen coordinate pairs gives exactly the frames in (0.2), whose six
edges are all of \(K_4\).

The exclusion theorem says that the status cells of all three complete
frames generate one equivalence component.  A *component recoupling*
therefore has one variable and chooses one complete shore.  The family
(2.1) uses three independent packet variables instead.  Owner disjointness
is checked on the literal sets, not on their generated equivalence
closure.  This proves the claimed scope separation.

The example is not confined to one-dimensional packets.  Let
\(r_\star=8\cdot2^t\) be any admitted physical active dimension and let
\(C\cong Q_{r_\star-1}\) be a common orientation cube on disjoint reservoir
pairs.  Then

\[
             \mathcal P(\{1\},\{23\})\times C,\quad
             \mathcal P(\{4\},\{12\})\times C,\quad
             \mathcal P(\{3\},\{24\})\times C
\tag{2.2}
\]

are three disjoint \(Q_{r_\star}\) packets and partition
\(\binom{[4]}2\times C\).  Extend each local frame by the same reservoir
matching.  The relevant occupancy fibre—two selected local coordinates
and one endpoint of every reservoir pair—is connected under the generated
status moves, while (2.2) is still a mixed-frame packet partition of it.
Thus the scope counterexample persists at every valid recursive packet
dimension, where the context-array factor may be installed literally.

## 3. Exact full-orbit augmented census

Let \(\mathcal I=\{(q,\epsilon):1\le q\le H,\epsilon\in\{-,+\}\}\).
A valid packet type \(\alpha\) has an owner set \(P_\alpha\), of size
\(B_\alpha\), and injective traces

\[
 \tau_{\alpha,a}:P_\alpha\longrightarrow
 \binom{[n]}{m+\epsilon q},
 \qquad a=(q,\epsilon).
\tag{3.1}
\]

Put \(p_q=N_q/W\), where
\(N_q=\binom{2m}{m-q}=\binom{2m}{m+q}\).
Choose \(M_\alpha\) integral joint markings so that each packet start is
accepted exactly \(M_\alpha p_q\) times at depth \(q\).  This is possible:
average all subsets of the two adjacent sizes
\(\lfloor B_\alpha p_q\rfloor,\lceil B_\alpha p_q\rceil\) in the unique
rational proportions with mean \(B_\alpha p_q\), take the product over
the finitely many typed depths, and clear denominators.

For every marking and every \(g\in S_n\), make one augmented edge containing
all owners \(gP_\alpha\) and all marked targets
\(g\tau_{\alpha,a}(x)\).  Then every resource vertex, in every typed
class, has degree

\[
                         D=(m!)^2\sum_\alpha M_\alpha B_\alpha.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Indeed a base owner maps to a fixed owner under \((m!)^2=n!/W\)
permutations.  A marked rank-\((m\pm q)\) target maps to a fixed target
under \(n!/N_q\) permutations, and

\[
                         p_q{n!\over N_q}={n!\over W}=(m!)^2.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Thus weight \(1/D\) on every indexed augmented edge is an exact
simultaneous fractional perfect matching.

For resource types \(a,b\), ranks \(r_a,r_b\), and intersection size \(t\),
let \(I_{ab}(t)\) be the number of ordered marked base-resource pairs of
that relation, summed over packet types and markings.  Their exact
codegree is

\[
 d_{ab}(t)
 =I_{ab}(t)t!(r_a-t)!(r_b-t)!(n-r_a-r_b+t)!.
\tag{3.4}
\]

This follows because the four Venn cells have the displayed factorial
orders and may be permuted independently.  Formula (3.4) is the promised
exact reduction of every owner--target and cross-depth target--target
codegree to one finite internal packet census.

## 4. The unavoidable chronological codegree

Fix an accepted depth-\(q\) trace in a whole \(Q_r\) packet.  On the
\(r-q\) unvaried active pairs the containing owner is forced, while the
\(q\) varied pairs may be oriented arbitrarily.  Hence its lower target is
contained in exactly \(2^q\) packet owners, and its upper target contains
exactly \(2^q\) packet owners.

A fixed lower target lies in exactly \(\binom{m+q}q\) middle owners; a
fixed upper target contains the same number.  Double-counting the accepted
target--incident-owner pairs in the full orbit therefore gives (0.7).
This uses no assumption on frame, packet location, or correlations among
the quota markings.  If the packet edge retains only one cyclic necklace,
the \(q+1\) consecutive window owners instead give the weaker bound
\((q+1)/\binom{m+q}q\); the depth-one obstruction is unchanged.

Every augmented edge contains the whole owner cube, so \(K\ge2^r\).  The
current physical compiler actually uses the stronger range
\(H\le r/4-1\); the weaker consequence \(r\ge2H\) already yields (0.8).
The conclusion is only that crude pair-linearized nibble criteria fail.
Chronological correlations could still make a matching possible.

## 5. Exact sufficient target-spread condition

Let \(\mathcal M\) be an owner-disjoint family of whole packet states,
covering \(S=W-L\) owners.  At signed depth \(j\), let
\(A_j(P)\) be the packet's distinct target set and

\[
 Z_{j,T}=\sum_{P\in\mathcal M}\mathbf1_{\{T\in A_j(P)\}},
 \qquad \sum_TZ_{j,T}=S.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Put \(c_j=\lfloor S/N_j\rfloor\) and define \(Q_j\) by (0.4).
For integral \(z\), \((z-c_j)(z-c_j-1)\ge0\).

If \(c_j\ge1\), every missing target contributes
\(c_j(c_j+1)\ge2\) to \(Q_j\).  If \(c_j=0\), writing
\(U_j=|\{T:Z_{j,T}>0\}|\) gives

\[
 S-U_j=\sum_{Z_{j,T}>0}(Z_{j,T}-1)
 \le {1\over2}\sum_TZ_{j,T}(Z_{j,T}-1)={Q_j\over2}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

In both cases,

\[
 \#\{T:Z_{j,T}=0\}\le(N_j-S)_++{Q_j\over2}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

Since \(N_j\le W\), the first term is at most \(L\).  Summing over the
\(2H\) typed depths proves that (0.5) implies aggregate target leave
\(o(W)\).

Finally set

\[
 K_j(P,P')=|A_j(P)\cap A_j(P')|.
\tag{5.4}
\]

For a law on fixed-owner-mass packet matchings, with
\(\pi_{PP'}=\Pr(P,P'\in\mathcal M)\),

\[
 \mathbb E Q_j
 =\sum_{P\ne P'}K_j(P,P')\pi_{PP'}
   -2c_jS+N_jc_j(c_j+1).
\tag{5.5}
\]

Therefore (0.6) is exactly \(\sum_j\mathbb E Q_j=o(W)\).  Since every
\(Q_j\ge0\), one outcome has \(\sum_jQ_j=o(W)\), and (5.3) completes the
proof of the sufficient theorem.

## 6. Why ordinary pseudorandomness is the wrong spread

At one signed depth, the uniform orbit point has target load
\(\lambda=W/N=c+\theta\), \(0\le\theta<1\), and owner degree \(D\).
If a packet law has the same target-overlap kernel as independent
rounding up to \(o(W)\), its expected off-diagonal overlap is

\[
                         N\lambda^2-{W\over D}+o(W).
\tag{6.1}
\]

Subtracting the floor baseline in (5.5) gives

\[
                         N(c+\theta^2)-{W\over D}+o(W).
\tag{6.2}
\]

At \(q=A\sqrt m+O(1)\), \(\lambda=e^{A^2+o(1)}\), so (6.2) is
\(\Omega_A(W)\).  Thus catalogue regularity, small owner codegrees, or
near-independent selection does not imply (0.6).  The selected packets
must have a directed negative covariance in the physical target-overlap
kernels of linear order.

## 7. Precise proved boundary

The unrestricted packet escape is valid and remains open:

* the old complete-quartet/global-frame no-go does not apply;
* owner degrees and codegrees are exactly (0.3);
* the full marked orbit is an exact simultaneous fractional perfect
  matching, with all augmented pair codegrees reduced to (3.4);
* the exact sufficient integral gate is (0.5), equivalently (0.6), or the
  quota-matching leaves \(L_0=o(W/H)\), \(L_T=o(W)\);
* ordinary pair-linearized nibbling is excluded by (0.7)--(0.8); and
* no target-overlap cut proving (0.6) impossible is currently known.

The next constructive lemma is therefore a correlated chronological
packet matching/absorption theorem which achieves (0.6), not another
whole-frame heat estimate.
