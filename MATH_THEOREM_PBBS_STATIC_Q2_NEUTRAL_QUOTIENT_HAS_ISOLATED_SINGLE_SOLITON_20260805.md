# The static q2-neutral PBBS component quotient is disconnected

**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Method:** synthesis of the rigid-edge C6 classification and exact switch
topology; no computation or search  
**Status:** unconditional for `m>=4` within the clean common-core C6 atlas.
It rules out a static spanning loose-forest proof on the original PBBS
factor.  Any repair of the single-soliton cycle must be dynamic or use a
higher circuit outside this atlas.

## 1. The static quotient

Let `F_PBBS` be the centered PBBS step-two factor equipped with the
max-height q1 section.  Define the static q2-neutral component
multihypergraph `Q_m^(2)` as follows:

* its vertices are the `g=f^2` components of `F_PBBS`;
* an incidence is a selected clean common-core C6 whose three unchanged
  companion rows delete one common pivot;
* its projected component multiset records the three PBBS components
  containing the old C6 edges;
* a genuine quotient hyperedge is retained only when those three
  components are distinct.

The common-deletion theorem says that these and only these clean C6s are
q2-multiset neutral.

Let `C_ss` denote the single-soliton component generated by the rooted
mountain `1^m0^m`, and `C_ts` the two-soliton component with partition
`(m-1,1)` appearing in the rigid C6 calculation.

## 2. Isolation theorem

### Theorem 2.1

For every `m>=4`, the vertex `C_ss` has degree zero in the genuine
three-component part of `Q_m^(2)`.

More precisely, every selected q2-neutral clean common-core C6 containing
an edge of `C_ss` has projected old-component multiset

\[
                         \boxed{\{C_{ss},C_{ts},C_{ts}\}.}
\tag{2.1}
\]

#### Proof

Every edge of `C_ss` is a cyclic translate of the normalized rigid edge
with q1 colour `1^(m-1)0^(m+2)`.  The exact rigid-edge classification in
`MATH_THEOREM_PBBS_RIGID_EDGE_Q2_NEUTRAL_C6_UNIQUENESS_AND_GRAPHIC_NOGO_20260805.md`
shows that, after normalizing that edge, q2 neutrality forces one common
companion deletion and uniquely forces the other two active labels.  Up to
cyclic translation and reversal, the only possible clean C6 is therefore
the displayed rigid C6.

The exact component calculation in
`MATH_THEOREM_PBBS_CLEAN_C6_Q2_NEUTRAL_GRAPHIC_NEUTRAL_20260805.md`
places its other two old edges on the same component `C_ts`.  Hence every
normalized possibility has multiset (2.1).  Translation and reversal
commute with PBBS and do not change component equality.  No genuine
three-distinct quotient edge is incident with `C_ss`.  `square`

### Corollary 2.2

The static genuine q2-neutral quotient `Q_m^(2)` is disconnected.  In
particular it has no spanning loose forest, regardless of the abundance or
expansion of its other action/angle sectors.

Thus a proof which chooses a physically disjoint family of clean
q2-neutral C6s entirely in the original PBBS factor cannot close the
graphic q1 gate.

## 3. Exact dynamic interface

Applying the unique rigid C6 does not lower the component count: it cuts
one edge of `C_ss` and two edges of `C_ts` and reconnects the two original
cycles into two hybrid cycles.  Because every switched row remains in the
transported q1 section, both hybrid cycles are still wholly selected.

Call them `H_1,H_2`.  A subsequent genuine three-component clean C6 which
uses only one edge in `H_1 union H_2` can absorb at most one of these two
cycles into a component carrying an omission.  The other remains a closed
selected cycle.  Therefore any dynamic clean-C6 repair requires at least
one of the following:

1. two later genuine connectors, one incident with each of `H_1,H_2`;
2. one later connector having two old edges on opposite hybrid cycles and
   a third edge on an omission-bearing donor component; or
3. a higher q2-neutral circuit not decomposable into one static clean C6.

This is a topology lower bound, not an existence theorem for any of the
three options.

## 4. Relation to the action-profile descent results

The parametric and all-hook q2-neutral C6 families prove that the quotient
away from `C_ss` contains many genuine strict profile-descent edges.  They
do not contradict Theorem 2.1.  Action-profile connectivity cannot see the
rigid-edge incidence multiplicity: the obstruction lives at the physical
angle/component level.

Accordingly the exact remaining PBBS theorem is dynamic:

> after the forced neutral split of `C_ss union C_ts`, place one protected
> q2-neutral straddling circuit (or two one-hybrid connectors) whose donor
> side already contains an omitted q1 occurrence, and then regenerate the
> quotient atlas.

No invariant depending only on the soliton partition can prove that step;
the static obstruction and its possible escape are both occurrence-level.
