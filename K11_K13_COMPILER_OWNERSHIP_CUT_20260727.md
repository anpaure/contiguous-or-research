# Exact carrier cuts for the depth-three OR compiler

This note isolates the compatibility condition that ordinary target-to-cell
Hall matching misses, audits it on the exact `k=11` compiler and several
`k=13` middle cycles, and records the exact residence obstruction of the
all-shadow-perfect `res104` cycle.

## 1. The exact assignment-to-word criterion

Fix a middle path (T_0,ldots,T_{W-1}) and compiler depth (d).  Let

\[
P_p=\bigcap_{\max(0,p-d)\le i\le\min(p,W-1)}T_i
\]

be the maximal linear erosion.  A short cell (c) is an interval of at most
(d) consecutive entry positions.  Suppose an injective map \(\phi\) assigns
each requested lower target (X) to a short cell.  Define

\[
J_p=P_p\cap\bigcap_{X:\,p\in\phi(X)}X.
\]

For every middle demand ((i,x)), put

\[
C(i,x)=\{p\in[i,i+d]:x\in P_p\},
\]

with the indexing orientation adjusted in the obvious way if the derivative
uses backward windows.

**Exact criterion.**  The assignment lifts to a nonzero word (A) with
(A_p\subseteq P_p), (D^dA=T), and (D^{|c|-1}A(c)=X) on every assigned
cell if and only if:

1. (J_p\ne\varnothing) for every entry (p);
2. for every assigned (X,c) and every (x\in X), some (p\in c) has
   (x\in J_p);
3. for every middle demand ((i,x)), some (p\in C(i,x)) has (x\in J_p).

Necessity follows because every realizing word satisfies (A_p\subseteq J_p).
For sufficiency, take (A_p=J_p).  Condition 1 makes entries nonzero;
condition 2 gives every assigned union exactly; condition 3 reconstructs the
middle row.

Thus the missing information beyond ordinary Hall is a family of positive
**carrier clauses**.  At depth three every clause has arity at most four.
An assignment to (X) kills coordinate (x\notin X) at all entries in its
cell.  A set of assignments is illegal precisely when those kills cover a
whole protected carrier clause (or empty an entry).

## 2. Exact `k=11` compiler: why it works

Using the first actual occurrence of every rank-4 target in
`sigma_sat_k11_465.word` gives:

- 330 assigned targets, all at depth one;
- occurrence multiplicities (1^{209}2^{110}3^{11});
- entry owner loads (0^{32}1^{206}2^{227});
- 227 overlapping assignment pairs, every overlap one entry and every target
  intersection of rank three;
- joint ranks (3^{461}4^4);
- only 8 of the 1407 allowed entry-coordinate incidences killed;
- no empty entry, no target-witness failure, and no middle-carrier failure.

The decoded word lies inside the joint envelope.  The remaining joint-minus-
word slack is (0^{396}1^{58}2^{10}3^1).  This is a quantitatively very loose
carrier realization despite the tight target count.

## 3. The `c756` cycle: exact localization of the failure

For all 1287 rank-5 targets, and separately for the 663-target orbit MUS, the
progressive SAT ladder is:

| stage | status |
|---|---|
| ordinary cell injection plus nonempty entry intersections | SAT |
| plus every assigned-target witness clause | SAT |
| plus every middle carrier clause | UNSAT |

Therefore the failure is not Hall and not pairwise target overlap.  It is the
simultaneous central-carrier condition.

The 169 rank-5 masks absent from the native lower-(q=2) shadow have 429
genuine internal (D^2) candidates.  Adding the exact same-colour zero-run
pair conflicts still gives SAT.  The decoded assignment covers all 169 but
loses 105 of the 1118 native masks, ending at 1182/1287.  Thus the missing-169
interval obstruction alone is not decisive; the gate is retaining the native
(D^1) colours while installing them.

Two optimization models must be kept distinct:

- In the restricted “missing (D^2) plus native-(D^1) retention” model,
  bound 56 is UNSAT and bound 60 is SAT; bounds 57--59 were not resolved.
- In unrestricted (D^0/D^1/D^2) rank-5 coverage, zero holes is UNSAT and a
  15-hole word is SAT.  Hence the certified interval is currently
  (1\le h\le15).

## 4. New joint seeds: the flat-(D^2) interface

The exact audit is in
`scratch/k13_joint_seed_carrier_cut_audit.json` and is generated by
`scratch/audit_k13_joint_seed_carrier_cuts.py`.

For both the rank-5 seed and the `r45` seed:

- the maximal cyclic erosion has rank four at all 1716 entries;
- every rank-6 mask has exactly one central-safe (D^2) cell;
- these 1716 assignments biject the 1716 (D^2) cells;
- their joint envelope is exactly (P), so flat rank six itself kills no
  carrier incidence.

Flat rank six nevertheless adds positive witness clauses not implied by the
middle clauses.  Every genuinely new clause has arity three.

| seed | new arity-3 clauses | quotient orbits | failed by current word |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| rank-5 | 2158 | 166 | 325 = 25 orbits |
| `r45` | 2288 | 176 | 312 = 24 orbits |

The more immediate obstruction is cell capacity.  Flat rank six occupies all
(D^2) cells, so ranks 1--5 must use (D^0,D^1).  The candidate graph then
has exactly the following isolated rank-5 targets:

| seed | isolated rank-5 masks | orbits | maximum-matching defect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| rank-5 | 156 | 12 | 156 |
| `r45` | 143 | 11 | 143 |

There is no further Hall deficit: the maximum-matching defect equals the
number of isolated masks.  Every isolated target is a rank-5 value currently
realized in (D^2).  Hence flat-(D^2) rank six plus the full lower ideal is
impossible on either fixed cycle before higher-order carrier interactions are
considered.  The `r45` interface is better by one quotient orbit.

The `r45` word also misses 13 rank-3 masks.  They form one rotation orbit with
representative \(\{0,2,6\}\).  This is one **target** orbit, but not one
literal carrier-cut orbit:

- 120 flat-(D^2) candidates per target and none is central-safe;
- their violations meet 332 distinct quotient carrier-clause orbits;
- the 52 candidates with only one violated clause form four candidate orbits
  and four clause orbits;
- unrestricted over (D^0,D^1,D^2), there are 13 safe assignment orbits:
  nine in (D^0), four in (D^1).

Selecting any of those 13 orbits equivariantly passes the exact (J_p)
criterion, both alone and together with all flat-rank-6 assignments.  A
(D^0) orbit kills only 13 envelope incidences; a (D^1) orbit kills 52.
Thus the missing rank-3 orbit is cheap.  The eleven (D^2)-only rank-5
orbits are the real flat-interface obstruction.

## 5. The all-shadow-perfect `res104` cycle

The exact audit is in
`scratch/k13_shadowneutral_res104.carrier_audit.json`, generated by
`scratch/audit_k13_res104_carrier_residence.py`.

This cycle covers every lower and upper shadow, but its maximal depth-three
erosion has 299 **empty** carrier demands at 273 middle positions:

| carrier arity | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| demands | 299 | 3900 | 2938 | 1768 | 3107 |

The empty demands are exactly the incidences in 104 short coordinate runs:
13 runs of length two and 91 of length three.  Equivariantly this is eight
run orbits (one length-two and seven length-three), or 23 empty-demand orbits.
No compiler assignment can repair an empty carrier; the middle cycle itself
must change.

### Residence equivalence

Let (X_0,ldots,X_{W-1}) be a cyclic rank-(r) Johnson sequence and (d<r).
Set (P_i=\bigcap_{j=0}^dX_{i+j}).  Every (P_i) is nonempty because (d)
Johnson steps delete at most (d) elements from the first (r)-set.  For a
coordinate (x\in X_i), the maximal reconstruction contains (x) at (i)
iff a block of (d+1) consecutive occurrences of (x) covers (i).
Consequently

\[
D^dP=X
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every maximal cyclic coordinate run has length at least }d+1.
\]

Since every other preimage is entrywise contained in (P), this is also
equivalent to existence of a nonzero depth-(d) preimage.

For an open middle path with word length (W+d), the same condition is needed
only for runs strictly internal to the path.  Prefix and suffix runs are
supplied by the boundary erosion entries.  The distinction is real but small
for `res104`: exhaustive checking of all 1716 cuts finds 13 symmetric best
cuts.  A best cut absorbs two adjacent length-three runs, hence six empty
demands, but leaves 293 empty demands at 267 middle positions.  Thus the
(B=W+3) boundary does not rescue this seed.

### Search pre-score

For a central move or trade, use the lexicographic score

\[
(\text{all-shadow holes},\ \#\text{empty carriers},\
  \#\text{short runs},\ \#\text{singleton carriers}).
\]

For `res104` it is \((0,299,104,3900)\).  The first coordinate is a hard
feasibility gate.  Conditional on zero shadows, the second is the exact
depth-three structural obstruction.  The third clusters those obstructions;
the fourth measures fragility under later ownership cuts.

This score is local.  If a move changes middle positions (S), a (q)-shadow
window can change only when its start lies in (S-[0,q]); the erosion entries
lie in (S-[0,3]); reconstructed middle demands lie in (S+[-3,3]).  Hence
the score can be maintained from a bounded collar.  Equivariant moves change
the empty-demand count in multiples of 13.  The search must remove all eight
short-run orbits without ever driving a shadow load to zero.

## 6. The residence and flat-\(D^2\) fronts are globally different

`scratch/compare_k13_res104_d2fronts.py` compares the eight `res104` defect
collars with the seven bad quotient vertices of
`sigma_jointcompiler_k13_d2descent_b7_r2`; its full output is
`scratch/k13_res104_vs_d2b7_fronts.json`.

- Raw quotient sigma assignments differ on 128 of 132 lower-mask orbits.
- After exhausting all affine relabelings of \(\mathbb Z_{13}\), the best
  multiplier is 6 and the assignments still differ on 123 of 132 orbits.
  Translation is inert after quotient canonicalization.
- Under that best assignment alignment, no D2-bad vertex lies in a `res104`
  collar.
- Optimizing the defect masks alone, multiplier 3 puts only two of seven bad
  vertices into two distinct collars.
- Quotient chronology alone aligns at most three bad positions by rotation,
  or four if reversal is also allowed.
- Requiring mask and quotient position to agree simultaneously gives at most
  one of seven, even after affine relabeling, rotation, and reversal.

Thus the two fronts are not the same local obstruction in different
coordinates.  They occur on globally separated sigma assignments, so a merge
must transport structure across a macroscopic symmetric-difference support
rather than splice two nearby local repairs.
