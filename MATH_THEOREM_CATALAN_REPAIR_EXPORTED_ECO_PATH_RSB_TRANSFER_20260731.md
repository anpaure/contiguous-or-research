# Repair-exported ECO path backbones: exact local channels and RSB transfer

Date: 2026-07-31  
Status: exact all-dimension conditional transfer theorem on the private path
face; exact finite-state integrality; exact local RSB data separator; exact

## 0. Verdict

After the repair endpoint has been fixed, the strongest currently justified
uniform theorem is a **path-backbone transfer theorem**.  It is not a new
coherent-supply theorem.

Fix an ordinary path on the repaired factor components.  A binary ECO atom
is a length-one interval tile and a ternary ECO atom is a length-two interval
tile.  For every atom `Z` and every ownership state

\[
                         Q\subseteq E_{\rm sh}(Z),
\]

the reusable local export must literally contain

\[
 Q\longmapsto
 (N_{Z,Q},S_Z,T_{Z,Q},\iota_{Z,Q},B_{Z,Q},V_Z(Q),\sigma_{Z,Q}),       \tag{0.1}
\]

where `N` is the directed unit-capacity occurrence network, `S_Z` consists
of `rho(Z)` distinct sources, `T` is a unit sink bank, `iota` embeds the
network in the parent router, `B` is the complete forbidden bank, `V_Z(Q)`
is the assigned-away port set, and `sigma` is the literal unit-edge or
grouped-rank semantics.  The exact local row is the ownership-resolved
vertex-capacitated max-flow test, with every already protected RSB resource
also deleted.

This is still not an RSB state.  To compose through shadows and the lower
compiler, append the actual physical seam delta and a finite
occurrence-labelled boundary transition carrying:

* the depth-two last-provider occurrences and their spans;
* the depth-`d` rank and wall collar;
* the fixed compiler skeleton, common matching, changed cells and the
  pairing-resolved augmenting linkage; and
* every active protected footprint which a later atom must avoid.

Once those records are exported, path integrality survives intact.  Replace
the path position `i` by a pair `(i,beta)`, where `beta` is the finite RSB
boundary state.  Every certified ECO transition is a forward arc.  An
accepted repair--ECO--RSB execution exists exactly when the resulting
layered directed acyclic graph has a source--sink path.  Its unit-flow
polytope is integral.  Thus arbitrary finite RSB boundary memory does not
reintroduce the determinant-two selection obstruction on a component path.

Two limits are sharp.

1. The statement is not without loss of generality in the component
   skeleton.  On `K_(1,3)`, the three pair-of-arm ternary tiles have the
   determinant-two half cover and no integral tiling.
2. The ownership-indexed channel export (0.1) alone cannot determine an RSB
   transition.  A palette-neutral incidence hexagon can preserve both
   immediate palettes while changing a unique depth-two occurrence.  The
   missing datum is precisely its occurrence span/last-provider record.

There is also a literal finite separation at the repaired project-`m=5`
positive base.  The singleton `g1=Z(101100)` passes the compiled rank-one
channel, component merge, owner and 42-socket closure rows.  Its same
singleton 42-path lift nevertheless contains `32` positive coordinate runs
of length two bounded strictly inside paths.  For `d(10)=2`, no intact-path
ordering, reversal or socket closure can be resident.  Hence even the best
current central singleton does not export the downstream RSB state.

The live all-`m` obligation is therefore concrete:

\[
 \boxed{\text{construct one repaired path bank whose owned ECO records
 and RSB boundary transitions contain an accepting layered path.}}       \tag{0.2}
\]

No further abstract Hall or generic router theorem is needed after that
object is supplied.

## 1. The repaired path face

Let `P` be one controlled repair/rethread packet and let `F_P` be its
accepting endpoint.  Intermediate states of `P` may carry debt; all data
below are read at the regenerated endpoint.  Fix:

1. one occurrence-labelled decoration `(I,M)` at `F_P`, including the full
   matching-boundary transfer rather than only root coverage;
2. the factor components

   \[
                         C_0,C_1,\ldots,C_s;
   \]

3. the ordinary component path

   \[
        A=C_0C_1\cdots C_s,\qquad e_i=C_{i-1}C_i\quad(1\le i\le s);
                                                                    \tag{1.1}
   \]

4. one collision-free fixed-rotation ECO subbank, or a stronger explicitly
   certified common physical cube; and
5. one orientation `omega` of the fixed-rotation ECO support-conflict path
   forest, used only to allocate shared occurrence-router resources.

For an ECO atom `Z`, let `H_Z` be the distinct components containing its
three old factor edges and put

\[
                         \rho(Z)=|H_Z|-1.                         \tag{1.2}
\]

The atom is an `A`-interval tile when, for some `0 <= a < b <= s`,

\[
                  H_Z=\{C_a,C_{a+1},\ldots,C_b\},
                  \qquad b-a=\rho(Z)\in\{1,2\}.                 \tag{1.3}
\]

Its tile is

\[
                         J_Z=\{e_{a+1},\ldots,e_b\}.             \tag{1.4}
\]

The physical record must say more than (1.3).  It contains the six literal
ports, old/new matching phases, external labels, the strict binary phase
when `rho=1`, component faithfulness, and the occurrence-labelled owner and
matching-seam data on the declared prefix cube.  Ternary strictness is
automatic only when the three old edges lie in three distinct current
components.  Binary strictness remains a literal phase test.

Let `Q_omega(Z)` be the subset of the at-most-two shared old edges assigned
away from `Z`.  Exporting all ownership states makes a bank portable: the
orientation may be fixed before the eventual interval tiling is selected.
For one frozen execution, only its realized state `Q_omega(Z)` is logically
necessary.

## 2. Exact repair-exported local-channel record

For every `Q subseteq E_sh(Z)`, export the tuple (0.1) with the following
literal meanings.

1. `N_(Z,Q)` is a finite directed network with unit vertex capacities.
2. `S_Z={s_1,...,s_rho}` consists of distinct protected sources.
3. `T_(Z,Q)` is a sink bank with unit sink capacities.
4. `iota_(Z,Q)` embeds every network vertex into the parent occurrence
   router.  Equality of abstract vertex names is not enough.
5. `B_(Z,Q)` contains every forbidden child-router vertex and every fixed
   protected footprint not allowed to share this channel.
6. `V_Z(Q)` is the literal set of the two port occurrences deleted for each
   assigned-away shared old edge.
7. `sigma_(Z,Q)` identifies the `rho(Z)` channel units with the `rho(Z)`
   edges of the interval tile (1.4), or exports the complete grouped atomic
   rank/kill relation instead.

Let `A(beta)` be the active protected occurrence footprints in an incoming
RSB boundary state `beta`.  Split every allowed vertex into an in/out pair
with a unit arc.  The transition-local channel test is

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname {maxflow}\bigl(
 N_{Z,Q}-B_{Z,Q}-V_Z(Q)-A(\beta);S_Z,T_{Z,Q}\bigr)=\rho(Z).}
                                                                    \tag{2.1}
\]

With prescribed unit/sink identities, (2.1) is replaced by the exact
pairing-resolved linkage relation.  The paths chosen by a passing
transition are included in its outgoing protected record.

The semantics in Item 7 is load-bearing.  For a literal unit expansion it
must state that loss of one channel unit removes at most its named edge of
`J_Z`.  If `Z` remains an indivisible physical toggle, export instead a
surviving component-rank function `r_(Z,Q)(Y)` and prove

\[
        \rho(Z)-r_{Z,Q}(Y)\le |Y\cap\Omega_{Z,Q}|                 \tag{2.2}
\]

for every deletion set in its complete owned bank `Omega_(Z,Q)`.  Two
abstract disjoint paths do not themselves turn one all-or-none ternary
toggle into two independently live component edges.

After the conflict-path ownership is fixed, the complete owned atom banks
are pairwise disjoint.  Therefore (2.1), together with the exact unit or
grouped semantics, is already the full occurrence-channel input needed by
the selected path backbone.  It does not provide any shadow or compiler
guard.

## 3. The RSB boundary transition appended to each atom

For a fixed dimension the boundary alphabet may be large, but it is finite
and occurrence-labelled.  A state `beta` contains the following fields for
the downstream RSB rows.

1. The accepted physical chronology fragments and phase, including the
   exact spans of named occurrences.
2. For every currently protected depth-two target, a chosen last-birth
   occurrence and its transported edge span.
3. The depth-`d` erosion boundary letters and every rank/wall test whose
   owner window meets an active seam collar.
4. The fixed compiler skeleton, its current target-saturating matching, and
   the complete signatures of all active changed cells.
5. Every active guard occurrence, controller footprint, augmenting-path
   vertex, channel path and reserved child bank.
6. Any additional accepted run/residence, trace, reachability,
   socket/voltage or Pascal-boundary record required by the intended RSB
   recursion.  These extra coordinates are not inferred from Items 1--5.

For a candidate atom `Z`, an exported transition

\[
                         \beta\xrightarrow{Z,Q}\beta'            \tag{3.1}
\]

contains:

1. the actual old-only and new-only physical seam sets and the common-
   fragment transport map;
2. a replacement new boundary occurrence, or a surviving common occurrence,
   for every last provider destroyed by the old-only seams;
3. all new depth-`d` rank and wall collar tests, and the literal
   run/residence check when it is part of the accepting state;
4. a maximum matching `M_0` of the common compiler graph and the
   pairing-resolved family of vertex-disjoint augmenting paths from all
   exposed targets to distinct free new cells;
5. the complete changed-cell/controller signatures; and
6. a new protected-footprint ledger which prevents every named witness,
   channel and compiler repair from being silently reopened later.

Finally, (3.1) must pass the physical strictness, fixed-decoration owner,
matching-seam and local-channel test (2.1) at the same literal atom.  This
is a joint transition record, not an assertion that independently feasible
component, matching, route and guard witnesses can be pasted together.

A nonlocal representative-changing packet is a regeneration transition:
its output `beta'` is recomputed in full.  Its intermediate states need not
belong to the accepting alphabet.  A phase-aligned ECO step uses (3.1)
without regeneration.

## 4. Exact layered-path theorem

Fix the repaired endpoint, component path `A`, collision-free bank and
ownership orientation above.  For every `0 <= i <= s`, let `Sigma_i` be the
finite set of exported boundary states after the first `i` component-path
edges have been tiled.  Construct a directed graph `L` whose vertices are

\[
                         (i,\beta),\qquad\beta\in\Sigma_i.        \tag{4.1}
\]

If `Z` has component support `C_i,...,C_j`, add

\[
                    (i,\beta)\longrightarrow(j,\beta')          \tag{4.2}
\]

exactly when `j-i=rho(Z)`, all repair-exported physical/owner rows pass, and
`beta -> beta'` is a certified transition (3.1) at
`Q=Q_omega(Z)`.  Every arc moves strictly to the right, so `L` is acyclic.

### Theorem 4.1 (repair-exported ECO--RSB path transfer)

Let `beta_0 in Sigma_0` be the regenerated repair state and let
`Sigma_s^acc` be the required accepting RSB boundary states.  Within the
private/frozen-exterior class of Sections 1--3, the following are
equivalent.

1. There is a left-to-right component-path ECO tiling which is physically
   executable, owner-aligned, locally channel-valid with its declared unit
   or grouped semantics, and preserves the exported RSB state at every
   accepting step.
2. In `L` there is a directed path from `(0,beta_0)` to some
   `(s,beta_f)` with `beta_f in Sigma_s^acc`.

For every path in Item 2, the selected ECO atoms have component--atom
incidence graph a tree.  Their occurrence channels and private RSB guards
compose, and the final state satisfies every coordinate explicitly carried
by `beta_f`.

#### Proof

Suppose Item 2 holds and write its successive position coordinates as

\[
                    0=i_0<i_1<\cdots<i_h=s.                       \tag{4.3}
\]

The atom on arc `r` tiles exactly the interval edges

\[
                       e_{i_{r-1}+1},\ldots,e_{i_r}.              \tag{4.4}
\]

These intervals are disjoint and cover `E(A)`.  The ordinary-tree tiling
theorem therefore makes the component--atom incidence graph a tree.  In
the left-to-right order, each atom meets the component already exposed at
`C_(i_(r-1))` and introduces its one or two new component branches.  Its
strict physical signature makes that step a clean merge.

The collision filter and common-cube record give simultaneous physical
compatibility.  The common matching seam and owner records transport the
same decoration.  The owned occurrence banks are pairwise disjoint; (2.1)
supplies the required local paths after the realized ownership deletions,
and the semantic field makes their component-rank meaning literal.  For any
router deletion set, sum the unit losses, or (2.2), over these disjoint
banks.  The lost rank on the path is at most the number of deleted vertices,
so the exported channel coordinate is already closed.

Induct on `r` in (4.3).  The transition record replaces every destroyed
last provider, checks the new erosion collar, repairs the compiler matching
by its named augmenting linkage, and adds its footprints to the outgoing
protected ledger.  The next transition deletes that ledger in (2.1) and
checks it against its physical seam set.  Hence there is no hidden reuse or
reopening.  The guarded RSB gluing theorem applies at every step.  This
proves Item 1 and the final assertion.

Conversely, an execution in Item 1 partitions the path edges into its
binary and ternary interval tiles.  Reading the tiles from left to right
gives (4.3).  Record the actual exported boundary state after every step.
Each executed atom passes the defining local tests of (4.2), so these
states and atoms form a directed path in `L`.  This proves Item 2. \(\square\)

### Theorem 4.2 (finite boundary memory preserves integrality)

The nonnegative unit-flow polytope from `(0,beta_0)` to the accepting sink
obtained by joining all `(s,beta_f)` to one supersink is integral.  Thus any
fractionally feasible repair-exported ECO path flow is a convex combination
of integral accepted executions.

#### Proof

`L` is a directed graph, and its node--arc incidence matrix is totally
unimodular.  Equivalently, every nonnegative unit flow in the acyclic graph
decomposes into source--sink paths. \(\square\)

The theorem does not assert that `L` contains a path.  It says that once
the exact local records are exported, there is no further integral
correlation gap caused by the path tiling or by finite RSB state memory.

### Corollary 4.3 (static private-guard collapse)

Suppose every candidate carries a static all-future-seams RSB guard, all
compiler repairs use private banks, and its local-channel record passes for
every ownership state.  Then the boundary state does not affect tile
eligibility.  Delete every failing tile and map an accepted interval
`[a+1,b]` to the arc `a -> b` on `0,...,s`.  An accepted execution exists
if and only if `s` is reachable from `0`.

Since intervals have length one or two, this is the exact recurrence

\[
 R_0=1,\qquad
 R_j=(R_{j-1}\wedge B_j)\vee(R_{j-2}\wedge T_j),                 \tag{4.5}
\]

with the second term omitted for `j=1`; `B_j` says that some accepted
binary tile covers `e_j`, and `T_j` says that some accepted ternary tile
covers `e_(j-1),e_j`.  Witness identities are retained on the corresponding
arcs.

## 5. Why the path hypothesis is exact

The state expansion above does not make an arbitrary component tree
integral.  On the three-edge star with arms `e_1,e_2,e_3`, take only the
three ternary tiles

\[
       \{e_1,e_2\},\qquad\{e_2,e_3\},\qquad\{e_3,e_1\}.          \tag{5.1}
\]

The exact-cover matrix is

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\1&1&0\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix},             \tag{5.2}
\]

with determinant two.  Its unique fractional cover is
`(1/2,1/2,1/2)`, and there is no integral tiling.  Giving all three atoms
the same trivial passing local-channel and RSB state leaves this obstruction
unchanged.  Thus Theorem 4.2 is a genuine path-backbone theorem, not a
generic hypertree theorem in disguise.

## 6. Sharp RSB separator from one incidence hexagon

The ownership-indexed local-channel object (0.1) is not a sufficient
boundary state for Theorem 4.1.

Let `|H|=r-2` and choose distinct `a,b,c,x,p` outside `H`.  Put

\[
 E_a=H+a+x,\quad E_b=H+b+x,\quad E_c=H+c+x,                      \tag{6.1}
\]

\[
 Q_{ab}=H+a+b,\quad Q_{bc}=H+b+c,\quad Q_{ca}=H+c+a.             \tag{6.2}
\]

Replace the old seams

\[
 E_aQ_{ab},\quad E_bQ_{bc},\quad E_cQ_{ca}                       \tag{6.3}
\]

by

\[
 E_aQ_{ca},\quad E_bQ_{ab},\quad E_cQ_{bc}.                      \tag{6.4}
\]

Both immediate turn palettes are unchanged.  The local ECO atom, component
effect, ownership bits and every network in (0.1) may therefore be held
fixed.  If the retained predecessor of `E_a` is `R_a=H+x+p`, however, the
depth-two occurrence changes from

\[
              R_a\cup E_a\cup Q_{ab}=H+p+x+a+b                    \tag{6.5}
\]

to

\[
              R_a\cup E_a\cup Q_{ca}=H+p+x+a+c.                   \tag{6.6}
\]

In one exterior let (6.5) have a second surviving occurrence; in another
let it be the unique provider.  The complete `Q -> local-channel` exports
are identical, but only the first exterior retains that depth-two target.
Therefore no predicate of (0.1), the interval tile and the immediate owner
data can decide the RSB transition.  At minimum one must export the named
last-provider occurrence/span relation of Section 3.

This is the first local alternating-circuit separator: consecutive Boolean
levels have no four-cycle, so the incidence hexagon is minimal.  The claim
is a data-separation theorem; uniqueness of (6.5) is an exterior state, not
asserted for every Hamilton factor.

## 7. Literal repaired project-`m=5` separation

At the synchronized repaired project-`m=5` endpoint, the singleton

\[
                            g1=Z(101100)                           \tag{7.1}
\]

has:

* component effect `120+132 -> 252`;
* six ports `51,53,55,57,59,61` and no fixed-rotation support conflict;
* the same marked occurrence sets, with a re-derived unique leaf-forest
  owner matching at the endpoint;
* `rho(g1)=1`; and
* the compiled local-channel arc `s_g1 -> z_g1`.

The independent singleton lift has `210` edges on `252` vertices and
exactly `42` path components.  Its authenticated `42` colour-injective
connectors give one Hamilton cycle.  Reconstruct those same paths and scan
each coordinate along each path.  The positive runs bounded by zeroes
strictly inside a path have histogram

\[
        2^{32}3^9 4^{25}5^3 6^9 7^1 8^4 10^2,                   \tag{7.2}
\]

and the `32` length-two runs have coordinate multiplicities

\[
                         (5,5,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,1).                 \tag{7.3}
\]

For `k=10`, the required depth is `d=2`, so every internal positive run
must have length at least three.  Path reversal preserves a bounded internal
run, while path ordering and endpoint sockets change only boundary runs.
Thus every intact-path closure of this exact singleton retains all `32`
violations.

This is a literal same-endpoint separation:

\[
 \boxed{\text{repair + owner-aligned ECO + compiled local channel
 + socket closure}\ \not\Rightarrow\ \text{RSB residence}.}     \tag{7.4}
\]

It does not exclude an interior rethread, a different repaired decoration,
or a different ECO bank.  It proves that the repair export must append a
new residence/RSB transition after such a rethread; the central singleton
certificate cannot be promoted unchanged.

## 8. Exact proved/open boundary

Proved here:

1. the exact ownership-resolved local-channel interface needed by a
   repair-exported path bank;
2. the exact finite RSB boundary transition which must be appended to that
   interface;
3. an equivalence between accepted private path-backbone executions and
   reachability in the layered state graph;
4. integrality after arbitrary finite boundary-state expansion;
5. the static private-guard recurrence;
6. the determinant-two obstruction to dropping the path hypothesis;
7. the palette-neutral hex separator showing that local channels do not
   determine RSB preservation; and
8. the literal `32`-run residence obstruction for the repaired singleton
   `g1` lift.

Not proved:

1. a controlled repair/rethread endpoint with an accepted path bank for
   every dimension;
2. existence of a collision phase whose filtered ECO interval tiles span a
   chosen component path;
3. uniform production of the networks and semantics in (0.1);
4. uniform production of the RSB boundary transitions in Section 3;
5. the interior rethread needed by the repaired project-`m=5` singleton;
   or
6. residence, deeper-shadow, compiler, reachability, socket and voltage
   closure unless those fields are literally present in the accepted
   boundary state.

Thus the strongest honest all-dimension statement is the transfer theorem,
not existence.  A successful induction must construct a nonempty layered
path, or supply a stronger private static bank reducing it to (4.5).
