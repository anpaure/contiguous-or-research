## 3. The exact role projection

Let `W_2` be the `3899` original length-two rows.  In owner phase `phi`, the
role key of `w` is

\[
             \kappa_\phi(w)=(w,B_w,R_w,T_w^\phi),       \tag{3.1}
\]

where the row is the strict chain $B_w\subsetneq R_w$ and $T_w^\phi$ is its
owner.  Let $\Omega_\phi(\kappa)$ be the complete one-short hyperarc DNF used by
the global-union census.  Then

\[
             Z_\phi=\{w:\Omega_\phi(\kappa_\phi(w))
                              =\varnothing\}.          \tag{3.2}
\]

The word *global* here refers to the endpoint-mode union, not to arbitrary
role rethreading.  The role key (3.1) is an input parameter of the census.

Let `C_old` be the integer column set generated by the authenticated outer
bottom matching, its alternating relay circuits, the fixed-role one-short
columns, and fixed-role two/three-short state columns.  Define `Pi_2` to be
the signed incidence map on occurrence-labelled role keys.  Every column
`c in C_old` satisfies

\[
                         \Pi_2 c=0.                  \tag{3.3}
\]

Indeed:

1. `x/p/S` outer assignments change only `H`/`F` bottom placements; every
   original `P2` row remains fixed.
2. Bottom-relay `C6` and `C8` columns are alternating circuits in that same
   matching fibre.
3. A one-, two-, or three-short state column selects transitions through
   already named roles.  Its directed state boundary may cancel without
   changing any key (3.1).
4. A planted fixed-suffix `C10` is a matching circuit on the same suffix
   shore.  It changes a carrier `P2` key only if that carrier prefix
   assignment is explicitly made movable.  No such typed `C10` occurs in
   the frozen suffix catalogue, whose saturated-`C10` count is zero.

Therefore every integral packet `d` composed from old columns, regardless of
overlap or prefix order, obeys

\[
                         \Pi_2d=0.                   \tag{3.4}
\]

Since the endpoint universe in (3.2) was already exhaustive on that fibre,
the zero-set objective is constant on (3.4).

This is a role-movement invariant, not a global state impossibility theorem.
A two/three-short grammar can place a fixed role next to another short and
thereby use a local pattern not present in $\Omega_\phi$.  Such a column still
does not reduce (3.2).  Ruling out all compound grammars would require their
own complete catalogue and is not claimed here.

## 5. Prefix role matchings and circuit decomposition

Create a bipartite prefix graph.  Its left vertices are occurrence-labelled
bottom targets and its right vertices are fixed suffix slots.  For the
`P2`-only subgraph a slot is `(R_w,T_w^phi)` and

\[
       B_i\sim (R_j,T_j^\phi)
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       B_i\subsetneq R_j.                            \tag{5.1}
\]

The current `P2` assignment is the diagonal perfect matching `M_2`.  If
`M_2'` is another target-exact prefix assignment, then

\[
       z=\mathbf1_{M_2'}-\mathbf1_{M_2},
       \qquad A_2z=0,                                \tag{5.2}
\]

where `A_2` is the bipartite degree matrix.  Thus `z` decomposes into
alternating even circuits.  Conversely, toggling any alternating circuit
preserves every bottom and suffix occurrence once.  This is the exact
cycle-space formulation of role movement.

In a simple bipartite graph a nonzero matching difference has length at
least four.  Hence a cross-contained two-row square is the absolute minimum
prefix role move.

\[
       w=(S,R),\qquad h=(B,M,U),                     \tag{7.1}

\[
       (S,R)+(B,M,U)
          \longrightarrow (B,R)+(S,M,U).            \tag{7.2}

\[
                    B\subsetneq R,\qquad S\subsetneq M.      \tag{7.3}

For the `226` rank-seven rows, the `P2`-only graph supplies no off-diagonal

\[
                 N(X_*)=\{\text{the 226 current diagonal slots}\}. \tag{8.1}

improvement.  It identifies the exact next expansion needed after harvesting

suffix key, change chain length, or enlarge the local state grammar.

## 9. The minimum rank-seven middle-layer escape

There is a canonical smallest expansion which moves a member of `X_*`:
uncontract the assignment of rank-seven targets to rank-eight roots.  This
shore contains both the bottom of every rank-seven `P2` row and the middle of
every long `H` row.

The Boolean incidence graph between distinct rank-seven targets and
rank-eight roots has no `C4`.  Indeed, if two distinct rank-seven sets were
both contained in two distinct rank-eight sets, their union, of size at
least eight, would be contained in the intersection of the two roots, of
size at most seven.  Therefore the smallest nonzero alternating circuit in
this layer is a `C6`.

An exact such circuit is supported on rows


```text

```

Row `16269` has structural degree one in the `P2`-only graph and belongs to
`Z_0-Z_1`.  Thus it lies in the residual set excluded by Section 8.  The
three-row circuit preserves the complete target deck, roots, owners, and
