## 2. Verdicts

The orbit theorem is exact with orbit-size weights.  Equal sizes, not
freeness itself, cancel those weights; freeness is additionally required by
the translate-by-translate coherent bundle lift.  Varying stabilizers do not
admit one naive voltage group: matching remains governed by weighted Hall,
while topology needs stabilizer/coset transition data.

For a prescribed simple degree-two bank `P` in the odd middle-level graph,
the guarded surcharge deficiency

\[
 \delta_{\rm star}(P,Z)=
   \max_C\left[
    \sum_U(d_C(U)+z_{L-C}(U)-n+2-p_C(U))_+
       -\sum_{x\in C}(2-d_P(x))
   \right]_+                                                   \tag{2.1}
\]

is exactly zero iff `P` extends to a spanning two-factor avoiding the fixed
edge catalogue `Z`.  This is an owner/lower-`q1` theorem; nonlocal or
selection-dependent upper/common-cap conflicts require a joint model.

The `n-1` disjoint two-edge paths around one unused near-complete upper star
give `2n-2` prescribed incidence edges and violate (2.1) by one.  Hence
pairwise owner/lower/paired-upper disjointness, polynomial size, and maximum
degree two do not imply extension asymptotically.

The upper-decorated selector is a valid polynomial integral face only after
the forced second matching is resource-independent and graphic-acyclic,
the first colour extends to a **spanning perfect matching** avoiding the
same forced edges and fixed `Z`, and all unit resource families are laminar.
The connector count, component Hall rows, orientation compatibility, and
final residual matching are separate necessary hypotheses.  Arbitrary-width
upper coverage must designate literal surviving witnesses; it cannot be
replaced by a vacuous bundle declaration.
