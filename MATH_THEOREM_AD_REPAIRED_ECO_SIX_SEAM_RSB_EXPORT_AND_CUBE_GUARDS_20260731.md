# Repaired coherent ECO atoms have an exact six-seam physical lift

Date: 2026-07-31  
Status: exact downstream physical-lift, all-depth collar, trace, erosion, and
fixed-skeleton Hall theorem; conditional common-connector/common-core cube;
no all-dimension repair, ECO-bank, connector, residence, or compiler

## 0. Verdict and scope

This note starts **after** a controlled repair has produced a fixed joint
middle-levels decoration.  It does not revisit raw ECO recursion and it does
not strengthen the central component-hypertree theorem.

On the all-six face, one coherent ECO incidence toggle has a much sharper
physical effect than an arbitrary transparent rethread.  Under the decorated
diamond lift it replaces exactly six Johnson edges:

* three edges on the physical rail avoiding the distinguished coordinate;
* three edges on the physical rail containing it.

The old six edges and the new six edges are matchings on the same twelve
physical vertices.  The residual cross matching is unchanged.  Therefore
the physical degree vector, and in particular the set of path endpoints, is
unchanged.  Both rank-one colour multisets are unchanged as well.

This does **not** finish the recursive state.  Four independent rows remain.

1. A joint alternating SDR is only a perfect diamond matching.  Its physical
   lift can contain a cycle.  The exact additional condition is the binary
   trace-forest test, or a protected literal `0^4` breaker on every current
   middle-levels component.
2. A common endpoint set does not imply a common Hamilton connector set.  If
   the same connector set Hamiltonizes both physical forests, then the final
   owner cycles differ in exactly six seams.  With different connector sets,
   the connector symmetric difference must be charged separately.
3. Coherence is rank-one neutral but not depth-two neutral.  The first
   unrecorded external neighbour already changes a depth-two union.  Thus an
   ECO atom must export its physical boundary rays, not only its six ports
   and common labels.
4. For a prepared common cube with separated dependency halos, all flag and
   compiler deltas add atom by atom.  This gives exact negative-part tests
   for simultaneous coverage and Hall over **every** cube state.  Without
   halo separation, singleton guards are not compositional.

The useful downstream package is consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{c}
 \text{fixed decoration + gap-owner state + trace breaker}\\
 {}+\text{ six-edge physical block + common connector/socket state}\\
 {}+\text{ oriented boundary rays + erosion walls}\\
 {}+\text{ fixed-skeleton local compiler columns/Hall linkage}.
 \end{array}}
 \tag{0.1}
\]

The repaired project-`m=5` singleton proves the central merge and replays the
decoration, gap forest, trace forest, and a terminal connector chronology.
Its exact path interiors contain 32 positive coordinate runs of length two,
so every intact-path closure fails depth-two residence.  Existing files do
not replay a residence-safe singleton all-depth/compiler state relative to
one common pre/post connector chronology.  Hence the finite central theorem
remains unconditional, while its downstream RSB promotion is explicitly
negative on the intact-path face and open after an interior rethread.

## 1. Literal decorated ECO lift

Let

\[
 \Omega=[2m-1],\qquad \widehat\Omega=\Omega\sqcup\{\infty\},
 \qquad m\ge3.
 \tag{1.1}
\]

Fix

\[
 |H|=m-2,qquad e\in H,qquad
 a,b,c,d\in\Omega\setminus H
 \tag{1.2}
\]

with `a,b,c,d` distinct.  Put

\[
 L_x=H+x,qquad U_{xy}=H+x+y.
 \tag{1.3}
\]

Use the old and new incidence matchings

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_0&=\{L_aU_{ca},L_bU_{ab},L_cU_{bc}\},\\
 E_1&=\{L_aU_{ab},L_bU_{bc},L_cU_{ca}\}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{1.4}
\]

Assume all six ports are selected by one fixed Catalan decoration, every
nonhexagon neighbour of `L_x` is `H+x+d`, and every nonhexagon neighbour of
`U_xy` is `H-e+x+y`.  These are exactly the all-six coherence equations with
common external-add label `d` and common external-delete label `e`.

The physical rank-`m` layer on `\widehat\Omega` has the two rails

\[
 {\cal B}={\Omega\choose m},\qquad
 {\cal A}=\{\infty+A:A\in{\Omega\choose m-1}\}.
 \tag{1.5}
\]

Define the following six old physical edges:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 {\cal E}^{0}_{\cal B}=\{&
 (H+a+d,H+a+c),
 (H+b+d,H+a+b),\\
 & (H+c+d,H+b+c)\},                                  \tag{1.6}
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
 {\cal E}^{0}_{\cal A}=\{&
 (\infty+H-e+a+b,\infty+H+b),\\
 & (\infty+H-e+b+c,\infty+H+c),\\
 & (\infty+H-e+c+a,\infty+H+a)\}.                    \tag{1.7}
\end{aligned}
\]

The corresponding six new edges are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 {\cal E}^{1}_{\cal B}=\{&
 (H+a+d,H+a+b),
 (H+b+d,H+b+c),\\
 & (H+c+d,H+c+a)\},                                  \tag{1.8}
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
 {\cal E}^{1}_{\cal A}=\{&
 (\infty+H-e+a+b,\infty+H+a),\\
 & (\infty+H-e+b+c,\infty+H+b),\\
 & (\infty+H-e+c+a,\infty+H+c)\}.                    \tag{1.9}
\end{aligned}
\]

Here and below an ordered display `(X,Y)` denotes the unordered Johnson
edge `XY`.

### Theorem 1.1 (exact six-edge lift)

Let `P_0,P_1` be the two physical diamond lifts of the fixed decoration
before and after (1.4).  Then

\[
 E(P_1)=
 \bigl(E(P_0)\setminus{\cal E}^{0}\bigr)\mathbin{\dot\cup}{\cal E}^{1},
 \qquad
 {\cal E}^{i}={\cal E}^{i}_{\cal A}\mathbin{\dot\cup}
              {\cal E}^{i}_{\cal B}.                \tag{1.10}
\]

Moreover:

1. \(|{\cal E}^0|=|{\cal E}^1|=6\) and
   \({\cal E}^0\cap{\cal E}^1=\varnothing\);
2. each of `E^0,E^1` is a matching on the same twelve vertices;
3. the degree of every physical vertex is the same in `P_0` and `P_1`;
4. the residual cross matching on unmarked middle-levels positions is
   literally unchanged; and
5. the lower-intersection and upper-union multisets of `E^0` and `E^1`
   agree separately.

#### Proof

At a selected `L_x` position, the decorated-diamond lift uses the physical
edge between its two rank-`m` neighbours.  One is the fixed external
neighbour `H+x+d`; the other is its incident hexagon `U`-port.  Substituting
the old and new incidences (1.4) gives exactly (1.6) and (1.8).

At a selected `U_xy` position, the lift uses the edge between the two
`infinity`-rail neighbours.  One is the fixed external neighbour
`infinity+(H-e+x+y)`; the other is the incident `infinity+L` port.
Substitution gives (1.7) and (1.9).

Every other selected turn has the same two factor neighbours.  Every one of
the six ports is marked, so none is incident with the residual cross
matching.  Deleting the marked positions therefore leaves exactly the same
unmarked path graph, with exactly the same forced path matching.  This
proves (1.10) and item 4.

The external label `d` distinguishes the three external vertices on the
`B` rail, while omission of `e` distinguishes the three external vertices
on the `A` rail.  Since `a,b,c,d` are distinct, the displayed old and new
edges are twelve distinct edges, and each side pairs the same six vertices
on each rail.  This proves items 1--3.

On the `B` rail, both sides have lower colours

\[
                 \{H+a,H+b,H+c\}                    \tag{1.11}
\]

and upper colours

\[
       \{H+d+a+b,H+d+b+c,H+d+c+a\}.                 \tag{1.12}
\]

On the `A` rail, both sides have upper colours

\[
 \{\infty+H+a+b,\infty+H+b+c,\infty+H+c+a\}         \tag{1.13}
\]

and lower colours

\[
 \{\infty+H-e+a,\infty+H-e+b,\infty+H-e+c\}.        \tag{1.14}
\]

This proves item 5. \(\square\)

### Corollary 1.2 (endpoint invariance)

If `P_0` and `P_1` are linear forests, their degree-one and degree-zero
vertex sets agree.  Hence every fixed connector edge on old path endpoints
is still degree-compatible after the toggle.  It need not have the same
component effect; Hamiltonicity must still be checked.

## 2. A final joint SDR still needs the trace-forest row

A Catalan decoration supplies exact turn palettes, selected-shore
alternation, and the forced residual matching.  It does not by itself say
that the physical lift is a linear forest.

For one middle-levels component write its cyclic selected/unselected trace
as

\[
                  1^{a_1}0^{b_1}\cdots1^{a_s}0^{b_s},             \tag{2.1}
\]

where alternation of selected shores forces every `b_i` to be positive and
even.  The exact trace theorem says that the physical lift contains a cycle
if and only if

\[
             b_i=2\ \hbox{for every }i,
 \qquad     a_i\ \hbox{is odd for every }i.           \tag{2.2}
\]

Thus the additional forest condition is precisely that every component
violates (2.2).  A convenient literal sufficient certificate is a retained
path of four consecutive unmarked middle-levels occurrences, called a
protected `0^4` breaker.

### Proposition 2.1 (smallest decoration-only obstruction)

Take \(m=2\), \(\Omega=\{0,1,2\}\), and the middle-levels Hamilton cycle

\[
 0, 01, 1, 12, 2, 02, 0.                       \tag{2.3}
\]

Select the `A`-position `0` and the `B`-position `12`.  The selected upper
turn is `012`, the selected lower turn is the empty set, and the two selected
shore types alternate.  Hence this is a Catalan decoration.  Its trace is

\[
                            100100,                   \tag{2.4}
\]

which satisfies (2.2).

Indeed the physical lift contains the four-cycle

\[
              02-01-(\infty1)-(\infty2)-02           \tag{2.5}
\]

and has the other two rank-two vertices `infinity0` and `12` isolated.
Therefore a final joint SDR, even with exact residual matching, is not a
Catalan linear matching until the trace-forest row is imposed.

#### Proof

The marked `A` turn gives `02-01`, the marked `B` turn gives
`infinity1-infinity2`, and the two residual cross edges are
`01-infinity1` and `02-infinity2`.  These are exactly (2.5). \(\square\)

### Lemma 2.2 (protected breakers survive a strict ECO hypertree)

Suppose every initial middle-levels component contains a named common-core
`0^4` path, disjoint from every selected atom port and every edge changed by
the common cube.  Suppose also that every atom is subsetwise
component-faithful and only merges components.  Then every component in
every cube state contains a protected `0^4` path, and hence every decorated
physical lift is a linear forest.

#### Proof

No toggle changes an edge or mark on a named breaker, so the four unmarked
occurrences stay consecutive.  A merge takes unions of old component
fragments and deletes no breaker.  Every current component contains at
least one initial component and therefore at least one breaker.  The trace
criterion excludes (2.2) on every current component. \(\square\)

This is a sufficient common-core condition, not a claim that `0^4` is the
only possible trace certificate.  Exact state compression may instead carry
the cycle-face bit or the full trace automaton.

## 3. From the six-edge forest delta to a six-seam chronology delta

Let `C_0,C_1` be connector sets on the common endpoint set of Corollary 1.2,
and assume

\[
                         T_i=P_i\cup C_i              \tag{3.1}
\]

is a Hamilton cycle for `i=0,1`.  Connectors are disjoint from the physical
forest in their own state.

### Theorem 3.1 (connector-charged seam bound)

Put

\[
 c=|C_0\setminus C_1|=|C_1\setminus C_0|.            \tag{3.2}
\]

Then the changed-seam count satisfies

\[
 |E(T_0)\setminus E(T_1)|
 =|E(T_1)\setminus E(T_0)|\le6+c.                    \tag{3.3}
\]

If `C_0=C_1`, equality holds and the six old-only and six new-only seams are
exactly (1.6)--(1.9).

#### Proof

Every old-only cycle edge belongs either to `E(P_0)\setminus E(P_1)`, of
which there are six, or to `C_0\setminus C_1`.  This proves the inequality.
The two Hamilton cycles have the same number of edges, so the two one-sided
differences agree.  If the connector set is common, it is disjoint from both
physical forests and no cancellation is possible; Theorem 1.1 gives exactly
six. \(\square\)

Assume henceforth that the connector set is common.  Delete the six old
seams from `T_0`.  They are a matching, so the six common path fragments
have at least two vertices each.  If their orders are `n_1,...,n_6`, define

\[
                         b_q=\sum_{i=1}^6\min\{q,n_i\}.            \tag{3.4}
\]

### Theorem 3.2 (exact all-depth ECO collar)

For every reversal-invariant statistic `f` on `q+1` consecutive physical
owners and every value `Z`,

\[
 \operatorname{mult}_{T_1}(f=Z)-
 \operatorname{mult}_{T_0}(f=Z)
 =B^+_{q}(Z)-B^-_{q}(Z),                              \tag{3.5}
\]

where `B_q^-` and `B_q^+` are the old and new windows whose edge spans meet
a changed seam.  Their exact sizes are

\[
 |B_q^-|=|B_q^+|=b_q\le6q.                           \tag{3.6}
\]

In particular,

\[
                         b_1=6,qquad b_2=12.         \tag{3.7}
\]

Equation (3.5) holds simultaneously for lower intersections and upper
unions at every depth.  At depth one their target multisets agree exactly
by Theorem 1.1.  At depth two exactly twelve old and twelve new occurrences
form the complete possible delta.

#### Proof

The common-edge graph is the six path fragments.  A `q`-edge window wholly
inside one fragment transports, possibly reversed, and intersection and
union are reversal-invariant.  The number of internal windows in a fragment
of order `n_i` is `max(n_i-q,0)`, giving (3.4)--(3.6).  Since the changed
edges are a matching, `n_i>=2`, proving (3.7).  Depth-one equality is the
palette calculation (1.11)--(1.14). \(\square\)

For a linear word, the chosen cut and its prefix/suffix correction must be
exported separately.  A common cyclic connector does not by itself fix the
linear opening.

## 4. Coherence does not determine the depth-two delta

The six-port ECO record contains no information about the common physical
neighbour on the far side of one endpoint.  That omission is already fatal
at depth two.

Choose

\[
 p\in\Omega\setminus(H\cup\{a,b,c,d\})               \tag{4.1}
\]

and suppose the common neighbour of `H+a+d` away from the ECO seam is

\[
                           R=H+d+p.                   \tag{4.2}
\]

Use the inverse orientation of (1.4), so the changed edge at that endpoint
is

\[
 (H+a+d,H+a+b)\longrightarrow(H+a+d,H+a+c).          \tag{4.3}
\]

The immediate lower and upper seam-colour multisets of the complete atom
are unchanged, but the displayed depth-two upper window changes from

\[
 R\cup(H+a+d)\cup(H+a+b)=H+a+b+d+p                  \tag{4.4}
\]

to

\[
 R\cup(H+a+d)\cup(H+a+c)=H+a+c+d+p.                 \tag{4.5}
\]

The two masks are different.  Changing `p` changes the lost and gained
depth-two targets without changing any ECO port or common label.  By
complementation, the same phenomenon occurs for lower depth-two
intersections.

Thus coherence, owner alignment, component faithfulness, and even exact
rank-one palette preservation do not determine the first RSB row.  One
complete literal export for depth `q` is the two oriented common rays of
length `q-1` beyond every changed seam endpoint, truncated when another
changed seam is met.  Any quotient state must at least distinguish the
window values induced by different choices such as (4.2).  For an
erosion/compiler dependency of owner span `R`, the literal-ray export has
length `R-1`.

This is a local data-separation theorem.  It does not assert that the target
in (4.4) is globally unique in an arbitrary completed chronology.

## 5. Exact erosion and compiler constants for one atom

Fix erosion depth \(\delta\), and define

\[
 P_i=\bigcap_{j=0}^{\delta}T_{i+j}.                  \tag{5.1}
\]

Assume `T_0` is erosion-safe.  By Theorem 3.2, at most

\[
                           b_\delta\le6\delta         \tag{5.2}
\]

old and new erosion letters change.  The complete consecutive-wall test
uses \(\delta+2\) owners, so at most

\[
                  b_{\delta+1}\le6(\delta+1)         \tag{5.3}
\]

old and new wall records require recomputation.

Let one named compiler-cell type use `h` consecutive erosion letters in a
fixed occurrence-exact skeleton.  Its owner dependency span is
\(\delta+h-1\) seams.  Hence the old and new exposed dependency-boundary
banks have exact size

\[
             b_{\delta+h-1}\le6(\delta+h-1).         \tag{5.4}
\]

For `a_h` types of width `h` anchored at every start, multiply (5.4) by
`a_h`; for several widths sum the bounds.  Actual signatures may coincide
across the toggle, so the genuinely changed cells form a subset of these
exposed banks.  These are right-vertex bounds, not incidence-edge bounds.

The output chronology is erosion-safe if and only if every new collar
intersection/union has its intended rank and every new deepest consecutive
pair satisfies the return-wall rank.  A fixed-skeleton compiler saturates
if and only if its common matching deficiency is discharged by the exact
pairing-resolved augmenting linkage.  No scalar target-positivity test
replaces Hall.

All constants here are the general physical-window identities with the new
exact ECO seam value `s=6`.  If the connector set changes, replace `6` by
the actual seam count, bounded by `6+c` in (3.3).

## 6. A prepared ECO cube and exact negative-part guards

Let \({\cal T}\) be a finite family of pairwise physical-port-disjoint,
all-six coherent ECO atoms on one repaired state.  Assume:

1. every subset \({\cal U}\subseteq{\cal T}\) is a literal fixed-decoration,
   component-faithful common-cube state;
2. every subset passes the exact trace-forest condition of Section 2;
3. one connector set `C` is legal and Hamiltonizing for every physical
   forest in the cube; and
4. the twelve-vertex physical blocks supporting distinct atoms are
   pairwise disjoint.

For \({\cal U}\subseteq{\cal T}\), write
\(T_{\cal U}=P_{\cal U}\cup C\).  Then for any two cube states

\[
 |E(T_{\cal U})\setminus E(T_{\cal V})|
       =6|{\cal U}\triangle{\cal V}|.                \tag{6.1}
\]

At depth two the exact boundary count is

\[
 b_2({\cal U},{\cal V})=12|{\cal U}\triangle{\cal V}|. \tag{6.2}
\]

For a maximum flag depth \(Q\), erosion depth \(\delta\), and maximum
compiler width \(h_*\), put

\[
       R=\max\{Q,\delta+1,\delta+h_*-1\}.             \tag{6.3}
\]

Call the cube **`R`-separated** when, in every cube state, every cyclic edge
span of length at most `R` meets the changed-edge block of at most one atom,
and the fixed compiler skeleton has no cell whose complete dependency meets
two atom blocks.  On retained fragments the occurrence-exact skeleton and
all controller/core choices must transport under the permitted fragment
orientation.  Equivalently, the prepared common core exports disjoint
oriented `R`-collars for the atoms together with its exact transport map.
This is stronger than port disjointness.

For `q<=Q`, target `Z`, and atom `t`, let

\[
 \Delta_{q,t}^{\cup}(Z)=B_{q,t}^{+,\cup}(Z)-B_{q,t}^{-,\cup}(Z),
 \qquad
 \Delta_{q,t}^{\cap}(Z)=B_{q,t}^{+,\cap}(Z)-B_{q,t}^{-,\cap}(Z).  \tag{6.4}
\]

The `R`-separation makes these singleton deltas independent of all other
cube bits.

### Theorem 6.1 (exact all-state flag guard)

For either \(\star\in\{\cup,\cap\}\) and every cube state \({\cal U}\),

\[
 \mu_{q,{\cal U}}^{\star}(Z)
 =\mu_{q,\varnothing}^{\star}(Z)
  +\sum_{t\in{\cal U}}\Delta_{q,t}^{\star}(Z).       \tag{6.5}
\]

Consequently every cube state covers every target through depth `Q` if and
only if

\[
 \boxed{
 \mu_{q,\varnothing}^{\star}(Z)
 +\sum_{t\in\mathcal T}\min\{0,\Delta_{q,t}^{\star}(Z)\}\ge1}
 \tag{6.6}
\]

for every `q<=Q`, every target `Z`, and both statistics.

#### Proof

Every window wholly in the common core transports.  By `R`-separation, a
noncommon `q`-window belongs to one atom collar and its value depends only on
that atom's bit.  The signed multiset delta is therefore the disjoint sum of
the singleton deltas, proving (6.5).  For fixed `q,Z,star`, the minimum of
the right side over all subsets `U` is obtained by choosing exactly the
atoms with negative delta.  This gives (6.6), with necessity and
sufficiency. \(\square\)

The same separation gives an exact local erosion test: every cube state is
erosion-safe if and only if the base is safe and both local phases of every
atom collar pass all rank and wall records.  No multi-atom wall can occur
under the definition of `R`-separation.

Now fix a lower-target set \({\cal L}\).  Let `K_t^0,K_t^1` be the old and
new named compiler-cell banks in atom `t`'s private dependency collar, and
let `K_*` be the stable common bank.  All these right-vertex banks are
pairwise disjoint.  For \(X\subseteq{\cal L}\), put

\[
 \sigma_0(X)=|N_{K_*}(X)|+
              \sum_t|N_{K_t^0}(X)|-|X|,              \tag{6.7}
\]

\[
 \delta_t(X)=|N_{K_t^1}(X)|-|N_{K_t^0}(X)|.          \tag{6.8}
\]

### Theorem 6.2 (exact all-state compiler Hall guard)

Every cube-state compiler graph has a matching saturating \({\cal L}\) if
and only if

\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_0(X)+\sum_{t\in\mathcal T}\min\{0,\delta_t(X)\}\ge0
 \qquad(X\subseteq\mathcal L).}                      \tag{6.9}
\]

#### Proof

For a state \({\cal U}\), disjointness of the named right-vertex banks gives

\[
 |N_{\cal U}(X)|-|X|
 =\sigma_0(X)+\sum_{t\in{\cal U}}\delta_t(X).        \tag{6.10}
\]

Hall must hold for every `X` and every \({\cal U}\).  For a fixed `X`, the
minimum over \({\cal U}\) again selects exactly the negative deltas, yielding
(6.9).
\(\square\)

Theorem 6.2 is an exact simultaneous Hall statement, not a claim that the
exponentially indexed inequalities have a compact separator in every
representation.  Pairwise-disjoint local augmenting linkages are a stronger
constructive sufficient certificate.

### Why separation is load-bearing

If one dependency window meets two atoms, its output can depend on the pair
of bits.  The corresponding Boolean function has a genuine mixed Möbius
coefficient and cannot be recovered from the two singleton deltas.  Likewise
one named compiler cell may be stable in both singleton comparisons and
change when both atoms are toggled.  In that case (6.5), (6.10), and the
negative-part criteria are invalid; one must export the full interacting
collar table or insert a regeneration checkpoint.

## 7. Exact downstream exported state

For a repaired ECO atom, the following boundary state is sufficient.  The
data-separation results prove that its trace, connector, external-ray, and
compiler coordinates cannot simply be inferred from ECO genealogy; they do
not rule out a smaller problem-specific quotient automaton.

1. **Central state:** the fixed occurrence-labelled decoration, gap-owner
   matching/forest state, literal owner-aligned six ports, and subsetwise
   component-faithful ECO cube.
2. **Trace state:** the exact cycle-face bit on every current
   middle-levels component, or one protected common-core breaker per initial
   component.
3. **Physical state:** the six old and six new edges (1.6)--(1.9), plus the
   connector matching, endpoint pairing, one-cycle/voltage result, and the
   linear opening with its suffix/prefix convention.
4. **RSB rays:** for every changed physical endpoint, the two oriented
   common rays out to the largest owner dependency in (6.3), with collisions
   between atom collars recorded rather than suppressed.
5. **Compiler state:** the occurrence-exact skeleton, core/controller
   footprints, changed named cell banks, wall results, and either all Hall
   cuts (6.9) or a pairing-resolved augmenting linkage.

The ECO genealogy determines item 3's six forest edges.  It determines none
of the connector choice, external rays, linear cut, or compiler incidence.
The local-channel state

\[
 Q\longmapsto(N_{Z,Q},S_Z,T_{Z,Q},B_{Z,Q},\text{unit semantics})
\]

is upstream occurrence routing and is logically orthogonal to items 2--5.
A compiled occurrence arc does not imply a trace breaker, a depth-two
witness, or a compiler augmenting path.
