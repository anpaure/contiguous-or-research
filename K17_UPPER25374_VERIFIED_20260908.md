## Why acceptance proves the upper bound

\[
 R_j=\{A_j\}\cup\{S\cup A_j:S\in R_{j-1}\}.
\]

Induction shows that these are exactly all suffix unions ending at j.
Keeping one start position for each OR value is sufficient: equal unions
remain equal after any common extension. The union of the R_j therefore
contains exactly all ordinary interval targets. The verified word has
every mask1,...,131071 in that union.
