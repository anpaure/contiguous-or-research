# Audit of the `B+1` one-token regenerative overlay

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Audited file:**
`MATH_THEOREM_BPLUS1_ONE_TOKEN_REGENERATIVE_OVERLAY_AND_FIXED_FIBRE_OBSTRUCTION_20260803.md`  
**Method:** pure symbolic dependency and scope audit.  No computation,

## 0. Verdict

**PASS as a conditional state-compression theorem.**

The theorem does not assert the extension-surjective reset-host lemma.  It
proves that this lemma would need to regenerate only one named aperture
token, plus at most `C` named common-cap omissions, and identifies two exact
obstructions to any fixed-fibre substitute.

The one-token claim concerns the additional protected overlay.  The full
integral Pascal parent/four-sector payload remains the core induction
object and is not compressed to one token.

## 1. Rank and deadline audit

At odd parameter `m`, the state token has rank `m-1`.  The child has owner
rank `m+1`, so adding one fresh coordinate gives a rank-`m` incoming token,
which is exactly child owner rank minus one.

The exact Pascal recurrence gives `d_(m+1) in {d_m,d_m+1}`.  Since
`d_m=Theta(sqrt(m))`, child owner rank exceeds child depth by at least two
for all sufficiently large `m`.  The one-in/one-out aperture construction
therefore has enough complement coordinates.  Its formula realizes the
tagged incoming token and emits one new rank-`m` aperture colour.  No
assumption that the literal parent packet survives is used.

For fixed child parameters `(R,D)`, the incoming token splits as
`B_0 dotcup Lambda` of sizes `R-D,D-1`; the output is `B_0 dotcup P`, where
`P` is any `(D-1)`-set in the complement.  This proves both directions of
the exact-sphere characterization and its binomial count.  In the
connectivity proof, `s=D-1<=R-3=K-2` guarantees that the common block and
the `s-1` outside elements used in the two-step realization both exist.
Thus no parity or orbit assertion is being inferred from a numerical menu
alone.

The bounded-bank corollary is the direct two-shore selection test: an
output avoids `F` precisely when the retained `(R-D)`-subset of `H` and the
new `(D-1)`-subset of its complement can each avoid `F`.  Its two
inequalities are therefore necessary and sufficient, not a union-bound
estimate.

## 2. Boundary-coalescence audit

A cyclic `q1`-exact chronology has one lower colour per cyclic seam.  A
linear opening deletes exactly the seam colour at the cut.  The diagonal
router has one port per same seam, so the only wrap route lost at that cut
has the same lower label.  The one-aperture theorem identifies its sole
unrealized colour with the intersection at the aperture seam.  Opening at
that seam therefore makes all three logical labels equal.

The theorem does not identify equal-valued occurrences silently.  It
explicitly assumes one occurrence-labelled complete exterior bundle whose
entry has that value and whose terminal is the accepted upper occurrence.
Hence the coalescence is logically exact and its physical realization
remains a host premise.

## 3. Topology audit

After forced pivot arcs are contracted, a perfect matching of
`B_(S,T)` gives one directed `S`-to-`T` path plus cycles.  The protected
octagon theorem absorbs one accessible cycle at a time without changing
the path endpoints or immediate palettes.  Therefore the positive topology
criterion is exact under its all-width/pivot guards.

On a fixed fibre, every exact port-preserving equal-size switch preserves
the selected tail/head banks and connector cardinality.  The path-count
identity `p=C-|A|` proves invariance.  Hall deficiency greater than one
prevents a `C-1` matching, hence prevents one path plus cycles.  A closed
`C6` induces a 3-cycle on the factor permutation and preserves component
parity.  These are correctly stated as fixed-fibre obstructions, not global
nonexistence results.

## 4. Common-cap audit

The diagonal theorem closes one occurrence coordinate only when the
existing upper-turn occurrence is accepted in the terminal role.  The
overlay theorem retains the exact two-Rado deficiency formula and leaves
the second coordinate, shared-capacity allocation, product closure, and
background transport as hypotheses.

The omitted set has size at most `C` only after one fixed common cap state
and one maximum common independent set are chosen.  The reset-host
definition explicitly requires the child omitted set to replace, not be
adjoined to, the incoming omitted set.  This is the necessary
nonaccumulation condition.

## 5. Word-length audit

The preword has baseline length `B`, and the pivot contributes exactly one
inserted source position.  The logical token is not an appended letter; it
is discharged by the coalesced exterior bundle.  Only the at most `C`
terminal common-cap omissions are appended at the final requested
dimension.  Therefore the claimed conditional length is `B+1+C`, not
`B+2+C` and not a sum over recursion depth.

## 6. Minimality scope

The claim of one-token sharpness is restricted to the one-aperture relay.
It follows from the exact local colour `J` and the unique lower boundary of
a Hamilton path.  It is not a universal lower bound on every conceivable
construction and is not a constant-bit claim.

The theorem deliberately rebuilds the child host.  It does not contradict
the no-finite-crossing-state theorem or the inherited shared-bank residence
horizon, both of which apply to lossless literal transport of a frozen
interface.

## 7. Unproved rows retained explicitly

The theorem still assumes:

1. a fresh protected upper/all-width/resident child host;
2. endpoint component Hall and guarded cycle absorption, or direct ordered
   Hall;
3. a literal coalesced boundary bundle;
4. two-coordinate/background common-cap all-cut bounds; and
5. one-star reconstruction/consolidation.

Accordingly it proves a smaller exact recursive interface and a conditional
induction, not `nu(k)<=B(k)+O(1)`.
