## 0. Scope and outcome

The frozen round-02 Hamming-two face is proof-closed at the local/q1
interface.  The next declared move classes are therefore

1. three distinct-base one-for-one recuts, and
2. one atomic aligned role-relocating `3+1` Boolean `C8`.

They are incomparable classes.  In particular an atomic `C8` is not four
independent recuts.  This note gives a fail-closed adapter and a common
post-q1 verifier for either class.  It accepts no packet merely because a
catalogue calls it open: a separately produced total SAT assignment must be
replayed, decoded to a literal chronology, and then pass residence, upper
deck and topology checks.

No materialized radius-three or active atomic-`C8` q1 survivor is presently
authenticated.  Thus the present result is a launch-ready verifier, not a
positive `k=17` construction.

\[
 E_{p(o)}=S(o),\qquad
 \bigvee_{t=a(w)}^{b(w)}E_t=T(w).                         \tag{1.2}

The address record includes its cyclic/linear boundary type and exact slot

## 2. Immutable packet schema

Every packet directory contains one strict `packet.json`, one
class-specific operation table, the upstream survivor row, and all named
manifests to which that row refers.  Mutable worker ids and numeric component
ids are never physical keys.

The launch command also supplies an **external frozen authority-manifest
SHA-256**.  The packet cannot choose this value.  The authority manifest
binds the packet key and move class to the generator survivor row, the
occurrence/direction/age and role registries, all eight radius-three subset
replays (when applicable), the zero-ledger source, and the complete
`L0/Lsucc` embedding/nonclosure manifests.  The adapter requires exact hash
agreement for every referenced registry.  Thus a packet cannot authenticate
fabricated age, role, subset or `OPEN` evidence by merely listing its own
hashes.  Until a generator freezes such a manifest, the packet status is
`UNKNOWN`.

The only promotable upstream status is

```text
LIBRARY_OPEN_NOT_Q1_VERDICT
```

and this launch accepts the exact open scope

```text
OPEN_MANIFEST_DERIVATION_RELATIVE
```

Here “open” says only that every derivation in the named, complete,
authenticated finite manifest fails.  A missing clause or arc is not
semantic non-entailment.  A future semantic-open extension would require a
countermodel or complete entailment replay and is outside this schema.
Missing shards, hashes, embeddings,
reconstruction fields, or replay results yield `UNKNOWN`, never promotion.

### 2.1 Radius three

The physical key is

\[
 R3:(r_0,r_1,r_2),\qquad r_0<r_1<r_2,                     \tag{2.1}
\]

and each `r_i` repeats its immutable primitive triple

\[
                     (b_i,q_0(b_i),q_i).                  \tag{2.2}
\]

The bases are pairwise distinct.  The adapter starts from the frozen
round-02 bank and, for every subset `J subseteq {0,1,2}`, constructs the cut
vector directly by choosing `q_i` precisely for `i in J`.  It does not apply
stored deltas sequentially.  The eight resulting bank digests must equal the
eight declared subset records, and the final bank must equal the independently
provided final-bank bytes.

Proper subsets are allowed to be locally dirty.  The exact first-applicable
mechanism field is one of

1. `CLEAN_SUBSET_EXTENSION`;
2. `LOCAL_DEBT_COMPLETION`;
3. `DISTRIBUTED_CERTIFICATE_COVER`; or
4. `PRIMITIVE_TERNARY_CONSTRUCTION`.

Only the final joint zero ledger gates promotion.  Consequently the adapter
does not accidentally delete the local-debt-completion class.

### 2.2 Atomic aligned `3+1` `C8`

Let indices be modulo four.  The two Boolean families are reconstructed,
not trusted, from core `S` and ordered distinct petals `a_0,...,a_3`:

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & C_i & O_i\\ \hline
\mathrm{STAR} & S\cup\{a_i\} & S\cup\{a_i,a_{i+1}\}\\
\mathrm{OCTAHEDRAL} & S\cup\{a_i,a_{i+1}\}
 & S\cup\{a_i,a_{i+1},a_{i+2}\}.
\end{array}                                                   \tag{2.3}
\]

The two alternating matchings are

\[
                 \{C_iO_i:i\in\mathbb Z_4\},\qquad
                 \{C_iO_{i-1}:i\in\mathbb Z_4\}.           \tag{2.4}
\]

The declared active phase must be exactly the matching selected in the old
factor; the opposite four incidences must be absent.  The adapter removes
the first four and inserts the second four atomically, then verifies:

1. the declared final factor is exactly this symmetric difference;
2. all lower and owner degrees remain two and no undeclared edge changes;
3. no removed incidence is protected;
4. the four removed turns have literal component pattern `3+1`;
5. after deleting them, the first-return permutation is
   `s=(0 1 2)(3)` in the declared active directed order, and the actual
   cross-matching `h` satisfies `c(sh)=1`;
6. the old and new forced-role keys are recomputed from immutable occurrence
   and endpoint fingerprints, are unequal, and are not numeric component
   labels; and
7. every new turn `x_i -> y_{h(i)}` obeys the literal depth-three age
   recurrence

\[
 C_{j+1}(y_{h(i)})=C_j(x_i)\setminus C_0(y_{h(i)})
                       \quad(j=0,1,2).                     \tag{2.5}
\]

The occurrence sidecar therefore stores the complete `C_0,...,C_3` masks,
not just owners.  An absent role resolver, age flag, active phase, aligned
order or protected-turn field is fail-closed.  The raw Boolean key count is
not an active-packet count.

The atomic packet keeps the frozen round-02 cut-colour manifest.  A `C8`
combined with an independent recut is outside this declared face.

## 3. Content-keyed q1 instance

The two adapters emit the same normalized objects:

* the packet-derived factor;
* the frozen physical cut-colour set;
* every required protected incidence; and
* a packet/factor/cut digest binding.

A q1 instance then supplies full ordered path traces, an orientation map,
the complete seam map, a DIMACS formula and a purported total SAT model.
Path identity is its complete ordered owner trace (up to the explicitly
declared orientation), not a mutable builder row.  A seam identity contains
the two endpoint occurrence keys, their orientations, the physical lower
colour and its upper union.

### Theorem 3.1 (generic total chronology reconstruction)

Suppose the verifier establishes all of the following.

1. The normalized paths are vertex-disjoint subpaths of the packet factor,
   their union is all `24,310` rank-nine owners, and deleting exactly the
   frozen cut colours produces exactly those paths.
2. Both orientations of every path are present and the submitted seam map
   equals the independently regenerated complete legal seam map as a set of
   content keys.
3. The DIMACS assignment is total and satisfies every clause.
4. It selects one orientation, one incoming seam and one outgoing seam for
   every path, and one seam for every frozen cut colour.

Then the selected seams form a permutation of the paths.  Concatenating its
cycles gives a unique total physical chronology.  This conclusion has no
hard-coded component, path, candidate or seam count and therefore applies
to both packet classes.

#### Proof

Condition 4 gives indegree and outdegree one at every path, so the selected
relation is a permutation.  Condition 2 makes every selected arc a literal
endpoint-compatible Johnson seam with the claimed colour.  Condition 1
makes path interiors disjoint and exhaustive.  Hence each permutation cycle
concatenates to one closed physical component and the union of these
components contains every owner exactly once.  Uniqueness follows because
the selected orientation and successor of every path are fixed. `square`

The decoder additionally replays rather than infers:

* all `24,310` owners, each once and at rank nine;
* every adjacency is Johnson;
* all `24,310` rank-eight intersections exactly once;
* all `19,448` rank-ten unions at least once; and
* every required protected incidence.

For atomic `C8`, the packet adapter retains a separately hash-bound
occurrence/role/age sidecar carrying `C_0,...,C_3`, while the generic decoder
emits an occurrence-rich chronology carrying the content-canonical path and
seam provenance.  The packet sidecar certifies (2.5); the final owner
chronology independently certifies product residence.  An owner-only packet
record would not certify the former.

## 4. Total deep guards

The final guard compares the chronology with the **frozen old factor**, not
with the packet-derived factor.  Thus an atomic `C8` cannot erase its own
casualties by changing the reference deck.

It checks:

1. cyclic depth-three product residence, including both joins of every
   path and every seam;
2. actual interval-union coverage at ranks `11,...,17`;
3. retained old-witness preservation and inclusion in the audited
   per-active-edge `9x9` cross-deck **superset**;
4. physical component sizes and either `FACTOR_OK` or `HAMILTON` topology;
5. quotient voltage only when the complete physical edge set is exactly
   `Z_17`-invariant (or when a separately bound quotient manifest is
   supplied).

Physical connectivity is definitive.  A numeric voltage is neither required
nor meaningful for a non-equivariant chronology.

The transition-monoid casualty bound is a safe prefilter only.  Three recuts
have layerwise liability bounds

\[
              27,48,75,108,147,192,243                 \tag{4.1}
\]

at ranks `11,...,17`; four changed `C8` turns have

\[
              36,64,100,144,196,256,324.               \tag{4.2}
\]

Selected seams may redeliver a liability, so neither nonzero bound is an
infeasibility certificate.  The total guard always enumerates the final
deck.  It also recomputes the full active-edge set `A` and the safe
`81|A|` cross-deck-superset bound.  The superset certificate is not an exact
enumeration of crossing intervals; actual final ranks `11,...,17` are still
replayed exactly.

The atomic-`C8` upper-interface theorem gives the packet-local identity

\[
 D_{\rm old}\setminus D_{\rm new}=Z_F(D)\setminus N_{F'}(D')             \tag{4.3}
\]

through ranks `11,...,17`.  The present acceptance path does not rely on a
submitted evaluation of (4.3): it recomputes the old and final decks in the
global guard.  A packet-local (4.3) table is therefore diagnostic and cannot
replace the global final replay.

## 5. Fail-closed statuses

Structural absence, hash drift, a partial model, map mismatch, incomplete
manifest, missing age/role data, or execution failure returns
`ERROR_FAIL_CLOSED`/`UNKNOWN`.  A completed chronology receives exactly one
of the following substantive deep statuses:

```text
REJECT_OPEN_OR_UNBOUND_TOPOLOGY
REJECT_Q1_REPLAY
REJECT_DEPTH3_RESIDENCE
REJECT_RETAINED_HIGHER_SHADOW
REJECT_TOPOLOGY_POLICY
PASS_RETAINED_GUARDS_WITH_INTRINSIC_HOLES
PASS_ALLWIDTH_DEEP_GUARDS
```

Only `PASS_ALLWIDTH_DEEP_GUARDS` is a total residence/ranks-11--17/topology
pass.  Common-cap, terminal compiler and literal source-word realization
remain separate gates.

## 6. Exact proved boundary

The pipeline proves the following conditional implication:

> An immutable packet in either declared class, a separately verified total
> q1 model on its reconstructed physical instance, and a
> `PASS_ALLWIDTH_DEEP_GUARDS` output yield a literal owner/q1/resident/

It does not assert existence of such a packet or model.  It does not

claim semantic openness from a derivation manifest, accept `C8+recut`, or
claim a compiler/common-cap/source word.
