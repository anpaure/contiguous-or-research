# Proof-body audit: lanes W, Y, Z, AA, and AC

Date: 2026-07-25

Every current report named below was read in full.  Imported results which
carry a decisive integrality claim were checked against their proof source.
This note distinguishes an exact reduction from the still-unproved positive
estimate at its endpoint.  Nothing here proves MWB or the constant-one
contiguous-OR theorem.

## W — correlated physical atoms and star-perfect quota rounding

**Verdict: sound exact rounding theory, conditional on a missing physical
selection theorem.**

The balanced factorial-floor identities, arbitrary-packet covariance
ledger, independent-product localization theorem, and samplewise
exact-ownership rigidity are algebraically exact.  In particular,
independent packets whose union owns every middle target exactly once must
have deterministic disjoint middle-owner cells.  The full physical
reservoir has connected co-block graph, so a full-support product
atomization really does collapse to one global packet.

Once fixed disjoint physical cells are supplied, the dependent-rounding
theorem is valid.  In the two-state case, the coordinatewise pair-sum
identity gives

\[
 \sum_jw_j\Delta_j
 \leq B_w/2+\frac18\sum_i\|z_i\|_w^2.
\]

The coupled-diamond action and its SCD-radius summation have the advertised
constant.  The literal-word conclusion, however, still assumes both an
almost-spanning aligned diamond atomization and a small pair-sum floor;
the present diamonds do nothing at depth one, so the first shadow is a
separate missing input.

The star report correctly repairs a different gap.  A simple
\(P_v\)-trace has exactly two full lifts, and full \(Q_v\)-compatibility is
an exact 2-SAT problem, not an automatic consequence of star saturation.
The fixed \(Q_v\)-core and middle/depth-one incidences have normalized
codegree \(2/m\), so the projected \(O(m^{-2})\) codegree cannot be used as
a black-box factor theorem.  For one already exact factor, survival packets
turn quota-safe deletion into a hitting-set problem.  Threshold rounding
loses at most the constant packet rank \(2+c_{\lceil A\sqrt m\rceil}\),
and deletion from the factor is automatically extendible.  Thus the
unproved statement is exactly the common multidepth LP bound
\((SPC_A)\), not an additional completion theorem.

**Dependencies and scope.**  The necklace word theorem imports the audited
physical block linearization and outer-tail construction.  Its conclusion
is unlabelled and uses one terminal exact-owner family; it does not provide
common-owner labelled synchronization.  No implication from projected
star codegree to \((SPC_A)\) is proved.

**Cap recommendation: retain.**  W is the cleanest exact rounding/LP lane.
If only one of its two packages is kept active, prioritize the factor-first
survival-packet formulation and preserve the two-state rounding theorem as
a reusable lemma.

## Y — exact-factor class connectivity and stationary completion counts

**Verdict: proof package sound; Y5 is live, while Y4's full augmentation is
an oracle-scale closure.**

For two exact factors, the ownership-overlay components are genuine
packing circuits.  Their subset switches give precisely the exact factors
supported on the two endpoints.  Column injectivity excludes one-for-one
noncommon components, yielding at most \(\lfloor B/2\rfloor\) components.
The hub-tree connectivity, Gram-antagonistic split recursion, factor
\(2/B\) comparator gain, and augmented-Graver primitivity follow.  These
statements are exact but quantify over comparisons with arbitrary completed
factors.  Consequently they prove only that the augmented local minima are
the unknown global minima.

The stationary report contains a more useful exact reduction.  Uniform
measure on every original heat class is stationary and the classes are
\(S_n\)-stable.  Double counting shared lower intervals gives

\[
 \mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathscr C}}\mathcal Q_H
 =B\,\mathsf G_H(\mathscr C)-2\Lambda_H.
\]

The deterministic spill identity

\[
 (q+1)Q_q(F)=\Sigma_q^{\max}-\Sigma_q(F)
\]

also checks exactly.  It uses literal middle ownership and the fact that a
noninterval lower set can be contained in at most \(q\) middle windows of
one row.  The maximum-entropy benchmark and its compatible-pair refinement
have the right factors: pairwise middle exclusion removes only
\(O(W/m)\), whereas a successful stationary law must create an additional
\(W/2-O_A(W/\sqrt m)\) depth-one covariance deficit.  The stationary
correlation tax on a transposition cell is also exact.

The missing assertion is \((SCC_A)\), or the stronger whole-fibre
completion-count estimate \((GCC_A)\).  Stationarity, reversibility, and
class enlargement do not imply either estimate.

## Z — positive corridor augmentation and exchange closure

**Verdict: Z5 is a sound live conditional route; Z6 soundly closes generic
exchange and static-orbit shortcuts.**

For a fixed-point involution, bundling ownership components by its action
preserves exact positivity and makes every moved target a two-load system.
The scalar formula

\[
 \chi_c((M+D)/2)+\chi_c((M-D)/2)
 =\max\{(2c-M)_++(M-2c-2)_+,|D|-1\}
\]

is correct.  Therefore the midpoint--locking--synchronization decomposition
is an identity, not a heuristic.  The fixed-point-involution spectrum,
level-set mixing bound, greedy fragmentation bound, signed-incidence
frustration formulation, Gaussian hyperplane rounding, and support-mass
constant all check.  The steepest legal gain is consequently bounded by
ideal mixing minus two explicit ownership residues.  The unproved step is
the restitution-gap inequality controlling those residues above the
\(HB\) scale.

The exchange-closure reports are also correct.  The top MSW ownership
component is a genuine primitive of degree
\((5/16+o(1))B\); hence no universal comparator-conformal \(o(B)\)-move
theorem exists.  Full lifted Graver moves are an exact test set for every
separable discrete-convex load objective and Gram-antagonistic packets are
an exact quadratic test set.  This yields local equals global, not an
absolute bound on the global optimum.  The full coordinate-orbit
reblocking identity and the point-stabilizer owner-block theorem likewise
show that static orbit smoothing restores all endpoint discrepancy.

**Chronology scope.**  Every descent endpoint is one literal exact factor,
and only the terminal factor is fed to the wreath-to-word theorem.  Keeping
the union of all visited rows would require a separate \(o(B)\) novel-row
bound, which is not proved.

**Cap recommendation: retain Z5; merge/archive Z6.**  Z5 leaves an explicit
positive ownership inequality.  Z6 is a valuable no-go and consistency
check but no longer a live construction lane.

## AA — exact-ownership holonomy and packet signing

**Verdict: sound black-box countermodels, but deliberately noncyclic.**

The two-factor hole formula is exactly signed NAE frustration.  Targets
owned on both sides of one component are safe; one-component targets have
an immutable one-hole floor; all remaining one-sided targets are signed
NAE hyperedges.  The optimized doubled-\(K_n\) construction has literal
exact middle ownership in every child, actual rank-\((m-1)\) target labels,
distinct row labels, exact balanced \(\{1,2\}\) fractional loads, and exact
point margins.  The neutral Hall completion and rectangle inventory check,
and every signing has \((1/4-o(1))W\) holes.

This is not an actual wreath construction: the rows are arbitrary
\(n\)-target incidence blocks.  It therefore disproves generic
common-sign discrepancy theorems using only ownership, marginals, and point
degrees, but it gives no lower bound for the genuine cyclic fibre.

The two-sheet packet report is similarly exact.  Packet switch cost is the
signed-graph frustration index, and the three-path braid has optimum
\(g\) and forces both colors to have linearly many runs.  Its local LP has
zero cost.  However, the braid contains a directed triangle, while a
positive-radius rotor has directed girth exactly \(2d+2\).  Thus the
countermodel cannot be inserted into the actual rotor system.  The report
correctly retains the joint quantifier: holes and chronology toll must be
small for the same final signing.

**Cap recommendation: archive after merge.**  Preserve the NAE identity,
the \(1/4\) abstract method boundary, and the rotor-girth warning in the
handoff.  AA has no surviving genuine-cyclic positive route under a
sixteen-task cap.

## AC — decorated semigroups and Graver-hole bypass

**Verdict: sound algebraic separation; generic route closed.**

The all-wreath incidence constants, uniform-ray denominator, mobile
floor/ceiling cone and lattice witnesses, finite saturation multiplier,
factor-resolution index, conflict-graph coloring equivalence, and orbit
refactorization identities are correct.  The signed-lattice claims use the
previously proved simultaneous cyclic-selector surjectivity theorem.  Its
oriented proof and the integral projection obtained by adding coefficients
on reversal pairs were checked; no factor of two is lost.

The actual MSW top component remains primitive after both the histogram and
mobile decorated lifts and has degree greater than \((5/16)B\).  Its
restricted unsigned-incidence semigroup is normal and its exact fibre has
only the two endpoint factors, so normality does not bound Graver degree.
The coordinate-specific linear objective makes this macro move the first
improving move, but it is not the symmetric MWB objective.

The abstract \(K_4\) family separately shows that saturation index two and
an exact constant-support Graver basis can coexist with overload \(W_0/4\)
and deletion cost \((1/8+o(1))W_0\).  It is not a wreath construction.  On
the actual universal orbit, minimizing average constituent repair is
exactly the original one-factor optimum, so colored IDP is no shortcut.
No genuine unrestricted semigroup hole is exhibited.

**Cap recommendation: archive after merge.**  Preserve the actual
macroscopic primitive and orbit-no-gain statements in Z/B.  The proposed
generic polynomial-Graver/normality bypass is closed and leaves no live
positive line.
