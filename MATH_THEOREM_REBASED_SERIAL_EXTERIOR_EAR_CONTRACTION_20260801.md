# Rebased additive-constant reduction: all local packet algebra is closed, and only a regenerative exterior-ear rank remains

Date: 2026-08-01  
Status: exact synthesis and exact conditional contraction theorem.  The
U1--U4 menu and conflict estimates below are unconditional statements about
the prospective planted catalogue.  The final regenerative exterior-ear
rank inequality is not proved.  Consequently this note does **not** claim
`nu(k) <= B(k)+O(1)`.

## 0. Verdict

The buffered-hexagon proposal should no longer be stated as requiring one
simultaneous independent transversal of `Theta(m^2)`-element packet lists.
That was a useful sufficient route before the serial safe-carrier theorem,
but it is not the weakest remaining theorem.

The current packet results divide the lower-compiler discrepancy into two
parts.

* **Cycle part.**  The planted adjacent-order breakers generate every
  depthwise quadratic current.  The four-packet twisted cube cancels all
  depths while preserving owners, both immediate palettes, all upper OR
  support, residence and simple topology.  An induced occurrence-labelled
  even cycle is routed nonnegatively by one returned catalyst atom.
* **Path part.**  Every one of those operations preserves the used physical
  cell basis.  None can increase matching rank.  A deficient compiler can
  improve only through a physical **open exterior ear**, ending at a
  previously unused cell, in one legal terminal common-cap state.

Serial composition means that packet supports and the returned catalyst may
be reused.  The compiler need not exist at intermediate chronologies and is
solved once at the end.  Therefore no simultaneous cross-list degree bound
is needed on the weakest route.

The exact remaining quantitative statement is a rank inequality for a
regeneratively planted exterior-ear network.  In its most compressed form it
is

\[
 \boxed{
   \max_{X\subseteq U_k}
      \bigl(|X|-r_{\mathcal M_k}(P_X)\bigr)
          \le \rho\Phi_k+C,\qquad \rho<1.}
                                                               \tag{0.1}
\]

Here `U_k` is the child source set, `P_X` is the set of legal entrances for
sources in `X`, and `M_k` is the strict gammoid of **physically certified
U1--U4-safe exterior ears** to the unused terminal compiler cells.  The
maximum in (0.1) is Rado's exact deficiency, not a heuristic expansion
surrogate.

A convenient stronger row is

\[
 |U_k|\le a\Phi_k+b,
 \qquad
 r_{\mathcal M_k}(P_X)
   \ge \left(1-A{d(k)\over k}\right)|X|-\gamma
       \quad(X\subseteq U_k),                                 \tag{0.2}
\]

with absolute `a,b,A,gamma`.  It gives

\[
 \Phi_{k+2}\le aA{d(k)\over k}\Phi_k+O(1).                   \tag{0.3}
\]

In the four-descendant normalization used in the earlier buffered-C6
reduction, `a=4`, so the coefficient is `4A d(k)/k=O(k^(-1/2))`.
Eventually it is at most any fixed `rho>0`, in particular `1/2`.

Thus the old requested estimates

```text
list size Omega(k^2), cross-conflict O(k d)
```

are sufficient for a parallel Haxell route but are not the exact missing
gate.  What is still missing is the physical exterior-ear/U5 inequality
(0.1), including its same-parity planting and cap-state quantifier.

## 1. What is now unconditional above U5

Fix rank `r`, residence depth `d`, and an ambient ground set `Omega`.  Put

\[
                           q=|\Omega|-r-2.                     \tag{1.1}
\]

For the canonical mixed coatom tensor, freeze the core, filler order, and
the four endpoint-active roles.  Vary the two active roles absent from the
endpoints.

### Theorem 1.1 (exact prospective U1--U4 atlas)

There are exactly

\[
                              L=q(q-1)                          \tag{1.2}
\]

labelled packet pairs with the same endpoints and clipped boundary state.
Every pair is a reversible U1--U4 replacement.  Its two phases have equal

1. owner set and simple Johnson topology;
2. adjacent-intersection and adjacent-union multisets;
3. prefix-OR, suffix-OR and complete internal interval-OR support; and
4. internal residence and clipped boundary state.

The `L` old words are distinct.  Hence (1.2) is a **prospective planted
atlas**, not `L` replacements of one frozen incumbent word.

Among the owner, lower-q1, upper-q1, prefix and suffix resource tokens, one
option uses at most

\[
                              s=16d+64                           \tag{1.3}
\]

noncommon tokens.  Every fixed noncommon token occurs in at most

\[
                              h=2(q-1)                          \tag{1.4}
\]

options of one other atlas.  Consequently, if the common anchor skeletons
of two prospective atlases are private, then one option conflicts through
U1--U4 tokens with at most

\[
                         \delta_{12}\le(32d+128)(q-1)          \tag{1.5}
\]

options of the other atlas.

#### Proof

The exact planted-boundary tensor theorem gives (1.2) and all four local
rows.  Its token table gives respectively

\[
 (8d+23)+(8d+20)+16+2+3=16d+64
\]

noncommon owner/lower/upper/prefix/suffix tokens.  A noncommon token contains
one or both variable active labels.  With one such label fixed, it occupies
at most two roles and the other label has `q-1` choices; with both fixed,
there are at most two orders.  This proves (1.4).  Union bounding over the
tokens of one option gives (1.5).  \(\square\)

### Corollary 1.2 (bounded prospective banks are not the asymptotic gate)

For `H` source-private prospective atlases, the U1--U4 conflict graph has

\[
             \Delta_{1:4}\le(H-1)(32d+128)(q-1).              \tag{1.6}
\]

Haxell's independent-transversal row `L>=2 Delta_(1:4)` holds whenever

\[
                         q\ge2(H-1)(32d+128).                  \tag{1.7}
\]

For fixed `H`, central `q=Theta(k)` and `d=Theta(sqrt(k))`, this holds for
all sufficiently large `k`.

This corollary is exact but has two important scope restrictions.

* Source-private common anchors must actually be planted.  A shared common
  owner makes two lists completely conflicting.
* The options have different old words and generally different lower
  actions.  Equation (1.7) is not a fixed-incumbent or prescribed-action
  menu theorem.

### Theorem 1.3 (the actual prescribed-action U1--U4 menu is linear)

For one canonical common-order flag action with fixed endpoints, varying the
action-invisible active label gives exactly

\[
                         q_{\rm act}=|\Omega|-r-3              \tag{1.8}
\]

prospective packets with that same all-depth action.  With source-private
common banks, a previously selected task excludes at most

\[
                              9d+33                             \tag{1.9}
\]

candidates when owners, both immediate palettes, and one-step lift shadows
are made private.  Thus `H` bounded prescribed actions can be selected
greedily whenever

\[
             q_{\rm act}>t+(H-1)(9d+33),                       \tag{1.10}
\]

where `t` counts declared candidate-specific exterior exclusions.

This is the strongest unconditional fixed-action list statement presently
available.  The endpoint-planted adjacent-order breaker does **not** yet
have an action-invisible linear, let alone quadratic, menu.  Therefore the
old slogan “every repair task has `Omega(k^2)` whole packets” remains
unproved and must not be used in an `O(1)` argument.

## 2. What the breaker, cube and catalyst close

The following implications use only proved results.

### 2.1 Signed all-depth action

The complete relabelled canonical-plus-breaker catalogue has, integrally in
its adjacent-order product-lattice form and rationally in the full harmonic
form, every depthwise zero-point-degree lower action.  In particular no
common-order reflection moment survives the planted adjacent-order
breakers.

This is a catalogue statement.  It is not nonnegative physical reachability
inside one carrier.

### 2.2 A strict-rainbow zero-action macro

The four-packet twisted cube is a literal prepared-slot macro for every
`d>=2` once `r>=max(d+4,14)`.  Its packets have pairwise-disjoint owners,
lower-q1 palettes and adjacent-union supports.  They may be toggled serially
in any order, preserve U1--U4 at each intermediate word, and have zero
terminal lower occurrence action at every depth.  Their native cells give
a common identity-cap partial compiler basis of size `8(d-1)`.

Thus all-depth companion cancellation and immediate-palette collision are
closed on a prepared four-slot bank.

### 2.3 Nonnegative occurrence cycles

Let two compiler tables of equal source and endpoint marginals differ on a
chordless `2 ell` occurrence cycle.  One exterior matched catalyst atom
connects them in exactly `ell+1` legal quadratic switches and is returned
unchanged.  For an induced `C6`, four switches are optimal.

After the corresponding rectangles are physically certified, the same
catalyst may be reused serially through arbitrarily many cycles.  Hence an
unbounded catalyst **count** is not required.

### 2.4 The exact boundary

Every quadratic switch, every twisted-cube cancellation, and every returned-
catalyst cycle route preserves

* source marginals;
* target/cell marginals;
* matching cardinality; and
* the used physical cell basis.

They can connect equal-rank tables, but cannot augment a deficient matching.
The first operation not contained in the proved catalogue is an open ear
whose terminal endpoint is an unused physical compiler cell.

### 2.5 A complete endpoint chain transporter is now explicit

There is a useful new exact gadget between cycle routing and an open ear.
It transports one whole nested coatom chain with zero loss.

Let `d>=2`, put `F={f_0,...,f_d}` and
`C={infinity,a,b,c}`.  Define the endpoint coatom block

\[
             T_i=K\cup C\cup(F-\{f_i\}),\qquad 0\le i\le d.  \tag{2.1}
\]

For `epsilon in {a,b}`, define an antecedent word
`A^epsilon=(A_0^epsilon,...,A_(2d)^epsilon)` by

\[
\begin{aligned}
 A_0^\epsilon&=K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,\epsilon,f_1\},\\
 A_j&=\{f_{j+1}\} &&(1\le j\le d-2),\\
 A_{d-1}&=\{a,b,f_d\},\\
 A_d&=K\cup C,\\
 A_{d+t}&=K\cup C\cup\{f_0,\ldots,f_{t-1}\}
                                  &&(1\le t\le d).
                                                               \tag{2.2}
\end{aligned}
\]

Then

\[
                         D^dA^a=D^dA^b=(T_0,\ldots,T_d).       \tag{2.3}
\]

All contiguous unions of the two antecedents agree except the left prefixes
of lengths `1,...,d-1`, and those prefixes are exactly

\[
 K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,a\}\cup\{f_1,\ldots,f_h\}
 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,b\}\cup\{f_1,\ldots,f_h\},
            \qquad 1\le h\le d-1.                             \tag{2.4}
\]

The length-`d` prefix is common.  Reversing the construction gives the
suffix chain.

#### Proof

Every length-`d+1` window starting at `i`, `0<=i<=d`, contains `A_d`, so it
contains `K union C`.  Its filler singletons before `A_d` are
`f_(i+1),...,f_d`; the maximal-erosion tail after `A_d` contributes
`f_0,...,f_(i-1)`.  Hence its filler union is exactly `F-{f_i}`, proving
(2.3).  The only letter depending on `epsilon` is `A_0`.  Therefore only
prefixes can change.  Before `A_(d-1)` enters, their values are precisely
(2.4).  At length `d`, `A_(d-1)` supplies both `a,b` and `f_d`, so the two
prefixes agree; every longer prefix also agrees.  \(\square\)

This closes a genuine part of the former exterior-ladder hypothesis.  In a
prepared endpoint block, all `d-1` phase-old chain values are replaced
cellwise by the `d-1` phase-new chain values, with one common length-`d`
cell left over.  A private mirrored block does the same for the suffix
chain.

For one isolated packet the two target sets differ, so this statement alone
does **not** imply `ell=alpha`: a lost old target need not have another new
neighbour.  For a coefficientwise-cancelling macro such as the twisted
cube, however, use the fixed cancellation pairing of its chain values and
give every paired packet its endpoint transporter.  The union of all old
reserved assignments and the union of all new reserved assignments then
cover the same target set on the same number of reserved physical cells.
After deleting the old reserved edges, the new reserved edges are disjoint
length-one augmenting paths.  On that reserved macro bank one has exactly
`alpha=ell`, so the nested chains cause no compiler loss.

It is still not the open ear of Section 4.  The transported assignments use
the same physical prefix/suffix basis.  Unless the common extra cell is
proved to be an unused, trace-guarded sink in the **same legal cap state**
and the packet hard-task row is co-selectable there, the gadget gives
`alpha=ell`, not `alpha>ell`.  Thus it strengthens the U5 shell while leaving
the basis-changing exterior endpoint as the exact missing resource.

There is a second, often cleaner endpoint choice which does not force the
length-`d` cell to be common.

### Corollary 2.6 (fresh-q1 endpoint transporter)

Replace the filler order by

\[
                    F^x=(f_0,f_1,\ldots,f_{d-1},x)             \tag{2.5}
\]

with fresh `x`, and let `T_i^x` be its coatom block in this order.  In
(2.2), replace the exceptional letters by

\[
 A_0^\epsilon=K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,\epsilon,f_1\},
 \quad A_j=\{f_{j+1}\}\ (1\le j\le d-2),
 \quad A_{d-1}=\{x\},
 \quad A_d=K\cup C,                                          \tag{2.6}
\]

and again use maximal erosion after `A_d`.  Then

\[
                              D^dA^a=D^dA^b=T^x.              \tag{2.7}
\]

The two antecedents differ exactly on their prefixes of lengths
`1,...,d`.  At lengths `1,...,d-1` they give the same packet chains (2.4).
At length `d` they give the fresh endpoint-contained q1 pair

\[
 K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,a,x,f_1,\ldots,f_{d-1}\}
 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 K\cup\{\mathord\infty,c,b,x,f_1,\ldots,f_{d-1}\}.           \tag{2.8}
\]

The length-`d+1` prefix is common.  Hence the deeper packet chains are
transported exactly as before, while q1 is exported to a fresh endpoint
pair rather than forced through one common length-`d` cap.  This is the
appropriate version when the regenerative state tracks `delta_top` or a
fresh B1 endpoint bank.

#### Proof

For windows starting at `i<d`, the singleton letters before `A_d` contribute
the suffix of `(f_1,...,f_(d-1),x)` beginning immediately after the omitted
filler, while the maximal-erosion tail contributes `f_0,...,f_(i-1)`.
The start-`d` window contains `A_d` and the full tail
`f_0,...,f_(d-1)`, omitting `x`.  Thus every window has exactly the filler
set `F^x` minus its indexed omission, proving (2.7).  Only `A_0` depends on
`epsilon`; before `A_d` enters, direct union gives (2.4) and (2.8).  Once
`A_d` enters, both `a,b` are present and the prefixes agree.  \(\square\)

The fresh-q1 variant is still basis-preserving.  It changes which q1 target
is carried by the same length-`d` physical cell; it does not itself make a
new sink cell or prove the q1 endpoint-cap Hall inequality.

## 3. The exact scalar form of the remaining lemma

Let `T^-` be a safe carrier, choose an optimizing legal cap state `theta^-`,
and let `M` be a maximum compiler matching.  Consider any serial U1--U4-safe
macro ending at carrier `T^+` and legal cap state `theta^+`.  Delete from `M`
the edges absent at the terminal state.  Let

\[
 \ell=\text{number of deleted old matching edges},\qquad
 \alpha=\text{maximum number of vertex-disjoint augmenting paths}. \tag{3.1}
\]

The exact terminal compiler theorem gives

\[
             \lambda_d(T^+)-\lambda_d(T^-)=
             \min_{\theta^+}\bigl(\ell-\alpha\bigr).          \tag{3.2}
\]

Consequently the logically weakest one-step contraction statement is:

> **Exterior-ear descent, scalar form.**  For every relevant regenerative
> child state there is a serial physically planted macro and a legal
> terminal cap state such that
> \[
>      \alpha-\ell\ge(1-\rho)\lambda_d(T^-)-C.                \tag{3.3}
> \]

Equations (3.2)--(3.3) imply

\[
                         \lambda_d(T^+)\le
                         \rho\lambda_d(T^-)+C.                 \tag{3.4}
\]

No packet-list cardinality or conflict graph is logically required for
(3.3).  Conversely, equal lower counters, full quadratic span, a returned
catalyst, or the native twisted-cube basis can give at best
`alpha=ell`; none implies positive descent.

## 4. The exact all-cut exterior-ear form

The scalar row becomes checkable when the augmenting ears live in one
directed network.

Let `U` be the exposed source tasks after retaining an inherited compiler
matching.  Let `P_u` be the set of entrance vertices of task `u`.  Build a
directed network whose sinks are unused physical compiler cells.  Every
source-to-sink path must carry:

1. its literal exterior basis-changing endpoint;
2. every cap-state and trace-guard resource;
3. a physical serial lift of its occurrence cycles using planted breakers
   and, when necessary, the returned catalyst router;
4. twisted-cube/all-depth companion cancellation where required; and
5. the exported residence and upper-witness guards.

Let `M_ear` be the strict gammoid represented by vertex-disjoint paths to
the sink bank, and put `P_X=union_(u in X) P_u`.

### Theorem 4.1 (exact Rado deficiency)

The maximum number of source tasks that can receive pairwise compatible
ears is

\[
 \min_{X\subseteq U}\bigl(|U-X|+r_{\mathcal M_{\rm ear}}(P_X)\bigr),
                                                                    \tag{4.1}
\]

and the number left unmatched is exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 \delta_{\rm ear}(U)=
       \max_{X\subseteq U}\bigl(|X|-r_{\mathcal M_{\rm ear}}(P_X)\bigr).}
                                                                    \tag{4.2}
\]

#### Proof

This is Rado's transversal theorem applied to the task sets `P_u` in the
strict gammoid.  A gammoid-independent transversal is precisely a set of
entrances admitting vertex-disjoint paths to distinct sinks.  Subtracting
the maximum transversal size from `|U|` gives (4.2).  \(\square\)

Thus (0.1) is not merely sufficient: within a proposed physical ear network
it is the exact cut inequality needed for contraction.

### Corollary 4.2 (the `d/k` contraction)

Suppose

\[
 |U|\le a\Phi_k+b                                             \tag{4.3}
\]

and for all `X subseteq U`,

\[
 r_{\mathcal M_{\rm ear}}(P_X)
       \ge(1-\varepsilon_k)|X|-\gamma.                         \tag{4.4}
\]

If every unmatched source costs at most `w_0` units of regenerated literal
defect and all noncompiler rows are U1--U4-exact, then

\[
\Phi_{k+2}\le
      a w_0\varepsilon_k\Phi_k+O(1).                           \tag{4.5}
\]

In particular, with `epsilon_k=A d(k)/k`,

\[
 \Phi_{k+2}\le a w_0 A{d(k)\over k}\Phi_k+O(1)               \tag{4.6}
\]

and the coefficient tends to zero.

#### Proof

Equations (4.2) and (4.4) give

\[
 \delta_{\rm ear}(U)\le\varepsilon_k|U|+\gamma.
\]

Use (4.3), multiply by `w_0`, and absorb `b,gamma` and the bounded terminal
sidecar into `O(1)`.  \(\square\)

When `a=4,w_0=1`, this is the earlier coefficient
`4A d(k)/k`, now with the packet algebra and positivity rows rebased onto
the proved coatom catalogue.

## 5. Why this is weaker than the old buffered-Haxell hypothesis

The old parallel route asked for one packet from every list at once.  It
therefore needed:

* `Omega(k^2)` options in every task list;
* global cross-list conflict degree `O(kd)`;
* pairwise nonconflict implying global composition; and
* one compiler state surviving all packets simultaneously.

The present serial route asks only for the linkage rank (4.2).  Different
ears may be exposed and applied one after another, their physical supports
may overlap at different times, the one catalyst is returned, and the
compiler is rebuilt on the terminal carrier.  This strictly removes global
packet conflict degree and an intermediate common compiler basis from the
weakest sufficient theorem.

The U1--U4 counts in Section 1 remain useful in two places.

1. They show that any **bounded** bank of prospectively planted packet
   shells has ample asymptotic supply.
2. They reduce a future proof of (4.4) to the exterior endpoint, cap tickets
   and their vertex cuts: the local owner/palette/upper/residence shell is
   no longer the density bottleneck.

They do **not** prove (4.4), because the candidate fibre over one fixed old
word is one and a common cap cut-vertex can collapse arbitrarily many large
formal lists to gammoid rank one.

## 6. The single remaining theorem, stated without hidden hypotheses

The appropriate target is the following.

> **Regenerative exterior-ear rank lemma.**  For every sufficiently large
> same-parity Pascal step and every reachable state of defect `Phi_k`, one
> can prospectively plant the required corrected endpoint coatom slots and
> form a terminal compiler network such that:
>
> 1. every path is a literal U1--U4-safe serial macro;
> 2. all occurrence-cycle segments are realized by planted adjacent-order
>    breakers, twisted-cube companion cancellation and the returned-catalyst
>    router;
> 3. every path has one genuine exterior unused-cell endpoint and all paths
>    coexist in one legal terminal cap state;
> 4. the exposed source set obeys `|U|<=a Phi_k+b`; and
> 5. its strict-gammoid rank obeys (4.4) with
>    `epsilon_k=O(d(k)/k)` and `gamma=O(1)`.

This one lemma implies `Phi_(k+2)<=rho Phi_k+O(1)` and hence
`nu(k)<=B(k)+O(1)` by the existing terminal compiler reduction.

Everything before the words **exterior unused-cell endpoint** is now
available as exact local algebra or exact conditional physical routing.
The unproved content is physical occurrence placement at the current
carrier and the U5 all-cut/common-cap rank.  Calling that gap another
quadratic-span, reflection, list-size, or ordinary LLL problem is no longer
accurate.
