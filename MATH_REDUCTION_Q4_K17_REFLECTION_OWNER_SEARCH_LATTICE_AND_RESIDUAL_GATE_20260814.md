# The q4 k17 reflection owner search has no small-prime cut and reduces to a 540-row residual gate

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Status:** exact search-boundary reduction plus capped H100 outcomes.  No
owner factor is asserted unless a literal residual certificate is decoded.

## 1. Exact Benders interface

Fix one perfect matching in the 36-regular distance-two graph on the 70
fixed owner bracelets.  Choose one strong self-column lift above each of
its 35 edges.  In an exact owner cover, the four nonfixed bracelet rows of
these lifts must be pairwise disjoint, so they occupy 140 of the 680
nonfixed rows.  What remains is exactly:

\[
 \boxed{\text{cover the remaining 540 rows by 54 disjoint reflected-pair
 configurations of size 10}.}                                  \tag{1.1}
\]

Conversely, any solution of `(1.1)`, together with its 35 self lifts,
gives the 35-self plus 54-pair bracelet cover and hence the full owner
factor.  This is an exact Benders decomposition, not a relaxation.

### Proposition 1.1 (defect-vertex-cover patches are complete)

Let `S` be any incumbent with one lift in each fixed-matching group, 54
paired configurations, and nonfixed row loads in `{0,1,2}`.  Form its
defect multigraph `Gamma`: selected configurations are vertices and every
row of load two gives one edge between its two incident configurations.
For any vertex cover `R` of `Gamma`, the outside family `S-R` is simple.
Let `F_R` be the rows not covered outside.  If `R` contains `s_R` self
lifts and `p_R` pairs, incidence conservation gives

\[
                         |F_R|=4s_R+10p_R.                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Replacing `R` is therefore the finite exact problem of covering `F_R`
once, using one new lift in each destroyed self group and exactly `p_R`
new paired configurations, all contained in `F_R`.

This family of patch problems is complete.  For any exact solution `S*`,
the set `R=S-S*` is a vertex cover: `S*` cannot retain both endpoints of a
doubled-row edge.  The new configurations `S*-S` give the required exact
patch of `F_R` with the same self-group and pair profile.  Conversely any
such patch, joined to `S-R`, is an exact solution.  Thus enumerating vertex
covers and their patches is an exact decomposition of the fixed-matching
master, not merely a local-search heuristic.  Minimum or varied covers
give smaller subproblems; destroying every defect-incident vertex is only
the maximal first patch.

There is also an exact Benders expansion rule.  If `F_R` has a zero row
`r`, any target patch must choose some catalogue configuration `C` which
contains `r`.  Every retained incumbent configuration in `S-R` which
intersects `C` is then a blocker and must be added to `R`.  Branching over
the distinct blocker sets of all `C` containing `r` is exhaustive.  The
same rule applies when a destroyed self group has no lift contained in
`F_R`: branch over that group's lifts and add all of their retained
blockers.  Choosing a minimum-blocker branch is a useful heuristic;
retaining every distinct branch is the complete decomposition.

For the frozen sample-zero perfect matching, there are 3,749 self-lift
variables.  One random disjoint self bank leaves 2,748 eligible pair
configurations on the 540 residual rows, with row degrees from 24 through
86.  Its exact-one CNF has 29,688 variables and 80,820 clauses.

## 2. Exact modular ranks

For the full strong reflection master, the incidence matrix has 750 rows
and 163,464 columns.  Its exact ranks are

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
p&2&3&5\\ \hline
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}&748&750&749,
\end{array}                                                       \tag{2.1}
\]

and the all-one target lies in the column space in every field.

The complete mod-two left nullspace is

\[
 \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}
 \left\{\mathbf1_{\rm all\ 750},\ \mathbf1_{\rm fixed\ 70}\right\}.
                                                                    \tag{2.2}
\]

Indeed, every strong self column has reduced size six and meets the fixed
rows twice, while every paired configuration has size ten and meets the
fixed rows zero times.  The two displayed vectors therefore annihilate
all columns; rank 748 proves they span the whole nullspace.  Both pair
evenly with the target.

After fixing one perfect matching, replace its 70 fixed rows by 35
one-lift-per-matching-edge rows.  The resulting face has 715 rows and
30,757 columns.  Its ranks are

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
p&2&3&5\\ \hline
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}&714&715&714,
\end{array}                                                       \tag{2.3}
\]

again with a consistent target.  Its complete mod-two left nullspace is
generated by the indicator of the 680 nonfixed rows: a self-lift column
meets those rows four times and a paired column ten times.  Rank 714 proves
there is no further mod-two component invariant.  Thus exchanges confined
to the 680 nonfixed rows are the natural exact search space.

In both `(2.1)` and `(2.3)`, the mod-three rank equals the number of rows.
Hence a maximal integer minor is nonzero modulo three and therefore
nonzero over the rationals.  Both matrices have full rational row rank:
there is no rational left-null cut.  Congruence cuts at other primes and
nonnegative-semigroup cuts remain separate.

For the frozen sample-zero 540-row residual pair system, the corresponding
ranks are

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
p&2&3&5\\ \hline
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}&539&540&539.
\end{array}                                                       \tag{2.4}
\]

Its target is consistent in all three fields.  The mod-two and mod-five
nullities are exactly the forced all-row relation from column weight ten;
the full mod-three rank again proves full rational row rank.  Thus freezing
this particular self bank creates no new small-prime or rational cut.

## 3. Capped search evidence

The strongest retained joint state on one fixed matching has

```text
energy sum(load-1)^2 = 116
load histogram: 58x0, 564x1, 58x2
```

It is not a certificate.  A 305.55-second complete hinted CP-SAT repair
returned UNKNOWN.  Twenty random disjoint self banks left dense residual
systems with 2,654--2,916 eligible pairs and minimum row degree 19--29;
twenty ten-second CP-SAT runs all returned UNKNOWN.  On the frozen
2,748-column residual, a 32-thread exact-cover DFS returned UNKNOWN after
120 seconds and 20,246,016 nodes.  These are capped outcomes, not UNSAT
evidence.

The defect-cover decomposition gives sharper finite diagnostics.  The
maximal cover, which destroys all 62 configurations incident with a
doubled row, has 518 patch rows, 662 eligible self lifts, and 1,981 eligible
pairs; its 300-second CP-SAT run was UNKNOWN.  Two minimum covers are too
tight and fail before solving:

```text
minimum incidence weight: 11 self + 18 pairs, 224 rows,
  4 zero self groups and 162 zero rows
minimum vertex count: 4 self + 24 pairs, 256 rows,
  1 zero self group and 200 zero rows
```

Intermediate exact vertex covers show the blocker expansion quantitatively:

```text
40 vertices: 14 self + 26 pairs, 316 rows, 69 zero rows
50 vertices: 18 self + 32 pairs, 392 rows, 3 zero rows
52 vertices: 19 self + 33 pairs, 406 rows, 2 zero rows
```

These structural zeros are properties of the specified E116 incumbent and
chosen covers, not global obstructions.  Proposition 1.1's blocker branches
are the exact way to expand them.  Stronger incumbents from other search
lanes are outside this artifact unless bound to their own replay.

The exact residual Kissat run is recorded only once it terminates.  A
running solver and a low-energy state do not change the theorem status.

## 4. Boundary and next atoms

The rank calculations eliminate mod-2, mod-3, and mod-5 cuts on both the
full strong master and the fixed-matching face.  They do not eliminate a
Hall cut, a higher-prime lattice cut, or a nonnegative-semigroup
obstruction for a particular self bank.

The globally lifted reflection face still cannot satisfy exact lower-`q1`
coverage.  Quotient-twisted self columns, symmetry-breaking lower-token
transport, and the ordered lower-`q2` phase assignment remain separate.
Every eventual owner certificate must retain `(core,ordered sigma)`, not
only its owner edge, so all ten ordered `L^(3)` tickets survive for the
canonical 143-rotation phase gate.
