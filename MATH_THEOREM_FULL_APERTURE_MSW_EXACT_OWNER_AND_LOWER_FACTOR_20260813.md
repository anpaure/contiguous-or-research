# Full-aperture MSW cycles exactly factor the middle owners and, in odd dimension, the immediate lower row

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** unconditional exact integral theorem for the named middle-owner
row, with a literal depth-`d` antecedent and exact lower-row factor in odd
ambient dimension.  It abandons the minimal-aperture pure-rail restriction.
It does not by itself solve global fusion, all lower flags, upper coverage,
or the common-cap compiler.

## 1. Cyclic intervals and the MSW input

For a cyclic order `sigma` on an `n`-set, let

\[
 \mathcal D_s(\sigma)=\{I_i^s(\sigma):i\in\mathbb Z_n\} \tag{1.1}
\]

be its all-start cyclic `s`-window deck.

The Mütze--Standke--Wiechert odd-graph factor theorem, together with the
minimum-odd-cycle--wreath equivalence, says that for every `h>=1`, the
complete layer

\[
 {[2h+1]\choose h}
\]

has a partition into families `D_h(sigma)`.  Each family has `2h+1`
members.

Fix the physical depth by

\[
 q=d+1.                                               \tag{1.2}
\]

The constructions below use **full aperture**: the owner windows have
length about half the ambient cycle, while only `q` consecutive source
letters are unioned to produce one owner.

## 2. Odd ambient dimension

Let

\[
 k=2m+1,\qquad R=m+1,\qquad 2\le q\le m.             \tag{2.1}
\]

Take an MSW wreath factor `F` of `binom([k],m)`.  For each cyclic order
`sigma` occurring in the factor, put

\[
 a=m-q+2=R-d                                           \tag{2.2}
\]

and define the cyclic source word

\[
 A_i(\sigma)=I_i^a(\sigma),\qquad i\in\mathbb Z_k.  \tag{2.3}
\]

### Theorem 2.1 (odd exact factor)

The source cycles (2.3), one for every wreath of `F`, have all of the
following properties.

1. Every source letter has the required rank `R-d`.
2. The width-`q` owner row is
   \[
    O_i=\bigcup_{t=i}^{i+q-1}A_t=I_i^{m+1}.          \tag{2.4}
   \]
   These owner rows partition the entire layer `binom([k],m+1)`.
3. The immediate lower row is
   \[
    L_i=\bigcup_{t=i}^{i+q-2}A_t=I_i^m.              \tag{2.5}
   \]
   These lower rows partition the entire layer `binom([k],m)`.
4. Every owner cycle is a simple Johnson cycle.  Every coordinate has
   owner trace `1^(m+1)0^m`, up to cyclic shift, and is therefore both
   positively and dually resident at threshold `q`.
5. The immediate upper row is `I_i^(m+2)` and is simple within every
   component.  More generally, for
   \[
    1\le ell\le m+q,
   \]
   one has
   \[
    \bigcup_{t=i}^{i+\ell-1}A_t=I_i^{m-q+\ell+1}.    \tag{2.6}
   \]
   Hence every interval-union row of rank less than `k` is simple within
   the component; at `ell=m+q` the union first becomes `[k]`.

#### Proof

Formula (2.2) is immediate from `d=q-1`.  Consecutive cyclic intervals
with consecutive starts have a union whose length is the old length plus
the number of additional starts, until the full cycle is reached.  This
gives (2.4)--(2.6).

The MSW factor partitions all `m`-sets into the supports
`D_m(sigma)`, proving the lower assertion (2.5).  On a `(2m+1)`-cycle,
the complements of all `m`-windows are exactly all `(m+1)`-windows, with
a fixed cyclic shift of their starts.  Complementation is a bijection
between `m`-sets and `(m+1)`-sets.  Therefore the families
`D_(m+1)(sigma)` also partition the entire owner layer, proving (2.4).

Consecutive `(m+1)`-windows exchange one endpoint, hence form a simple
Johnson cycle.  A fixed cyclic label belongs to `m+1` consecutive owner
windows and is absent from the other `m`; (2.1) makes both runs at least
`q`.  Proper cyclic intervals of a fixed length determine their starts,
proving all asserted within-component simplicity.  `square`

### Corollary 2.2 (positive semigroup closure in the odd owner/lower projection)

For odd `k`, the simultaneous demand

\[
 \mathbf1_{\binom{[k]}{m+1}}
 \oplus
 \mathbf1_{\binom{[k]}m}
\]

lies in the positive semigroup generated by literal closed depth-`d`
source cycles.  No terminal correction, lattice argument, fractional
rounding, or nibble is needed for these two rows.

This is stronger than the minimal-aperture central-shell near-factor: it
has zero owner leave.

## 3. Even ambient dimension

Let

\[
 k=2m,\qquad R=m,qquad 2\le q\le m-1,              \tag{3.1}
\]

fix one coordinate `x`, and put

\[
 T=[k]\setminus\{x\},\qquad |T|=2m-1.               \tag{3.2}
\]

Take one MSW wreath factor `F` of `binom(T,m-1)`.  Use two source cycles
for each order `sigma` in `F`.

The no-`x` source is

\[
 A_i^0=I_i^{m-q+1}(\sigma),                         \tag{3.3}
\]

and the with-`x` source is

\[
 A_i^1=\{x\}\cup I_i^{m-q}(\sigma).                \tag{3.4}
\]

Both have rank

\[
 m-q+1=R-d.                                         \tag{3.5}
\]

### Theorem 3.1 (even exact owner factor)

The cycles (3.3)--(3.4) exactly partition the full rank-`m` owner layer.
More precisely,

\[
 O_i^0=\bigcup_{t=i}^{i+q-1}A_t^0=I_i^m(\sigma),    \tag{3.6}
\]

and the supports `D_m(sigma)` partition all `m`-subsets of `T`, since
they are the complements in `T` of the MSW-factor supports
`D_(m-1)(sigma)`.  Also

\[
 O_i^1=\bigcup_{t=i}^{i+q-1}A_t^1
 =\{x\}\cup I_i^{m-1}(\sigma),                     \tag{3.7}
\]

and these partition all rank-`m` owners containing `x`.

The two shores are disjoint, so together they partition
`binom([k],m)`.  Their toggle traces are respectively

\[
 1^m0^{m-1},\qquad1^{m-1}0^m,                       \tag{3.8}
\]

while `x` is constant in the second type.  Thus (3.1) gives positive and
dual residence.  Both owner rows are simple Johnson cycles and have
literal rank-`R-d` antecedents.

#### Proof

The interval-union calculation is the same as in Theorem 2.1.  The MSW
factor partitions `binom(T,m-1)`.  Complementation within the odd set `T`
maps each `(m-1)`-window deck to the corresponding `m`-window deck and
partitions `binom(T,m)`.  Adjoining `x` to the original factor partitions
the other owner shore.  The trace statement follows by counting cyclic
window incidences.  `square`

## 4. Exact even lower-palette ledger

For the no-`x` type,

\[
 L_i^0=\bigcup_{t=i}^{i+q-2}A_t^0=I_i^{m-1}.        \tag{4.1}
\]

These rows are exactly the original MSW factor, and hence partition all
rank-`m-1` targets not containing `x`.

For the with-`x` type,

\[
 L_i^1=\{x\}\cup I_i^{m-2}.                         \tag{4.2}
\]

Each row is simple within its component, but the aggregate is not an
exact factor for cardinality reasons.  Its total number of occurrences is

\[
 {2m-1\choose m-1},                                 \tag{4.3}
\]

whereas the number of possible named targets is

\[
 {2m-1\choose m-2}.                                 \tag{4.4}
\]

The average multiplicity is therefore

\[
 {m+1\over m-1}.                                    \tag{4.5}
\]

For a fixed `(m-2)`-set `S subset T`, its multiplicity in (4.2) is at
most `m+1`.  Indeed, form the disjoint union of the natural Johnson
cycles on the selected `(m-1)`-window supports.  The occurrences of `S`
are exactly the cycle edges induced by the `m+1` supersets

\[
 \{S\cup\{y\}:y\in T\setminus S\}.
\]

That induced graph has maximum degree at most two, hence at most `m+1`
edges.  It may have no edge; coverage of every with-`x` lower target is
not asserted.

Thus the even construction closes the owner row exactly and the no-`x`
lower shore exactly.  Its remaining local lower question is a concrete
choice/refinement of the MSW factor ensuring coverage or a designated
matching on the with-`x` shore.

## 5. Why minimal aperture was not necessary

The earlier pure rails imposed source letters of the form

\[
 C\cup\{z_i\},
\]

so their owners were a fixed core plus `q`-windows.  That is a convenient
normal form, not a consequence of depth alone.

For a general cyclic owner row, the exact unpinned antecedent criterion is
that every positive owner-membership run have length at least `q` and
that every intersection of `q` consecutive owners be nonempty.  The full
aperture cycles satisfy this visibly.  Their canonical source letters are
exactly the intersections of `q` consecutive owners, namely (2.3),
(3.3), and (3.4).  Their ranks are `R-d` by construction.

Therefore there is no hidden source-rank or residence axiom forcing owner
aperture `q`.  What is lost is the fixed-core singleton-toggle form and
some of its local port freedom; what is gained is an exact global owner
factor.

## 6. Remaining scope

The theorem proves a major carrier result, but not the whole compiler.

1. Distinct components can still collide in non-middle interval rows,
   even though their owner supports are disjoint.
2. Odd `k` has an exact immediate-lower factor; deeper designated lower
   flags must still be aligned with the same antecedent.
3. Even `k` has the explicit with-`x` lower ledger (4.2)--(4.5), not yet
   an exact lower factor.
4. The MSW components form a two-factor-scale cycle family, not one fused
   occurrence circuit.
5. Protected phases, sockets, histories, upper witnesses, and the common
   cap remain joint constraints.

Within those limits, the full named-owner positive-semigroup gate is
closed for both parities, and for odd ambient dimension the immediate
lower row is closed simultaneously with zero leave.
