# A two-leg caterpillar does not force a Sperner ideal lattice

2026-09-08. Pure proof, with no mathematical execution. Root independently
checked the polynomial and antichain and passed the argument. This is a
structural scope obstruction, not a counterexample to the near-balanced
four-axis staircase.

## 1. A literal counterexample, including the extra chain

Let R have five elements a,b,c,d,e and precisely the relations generated by

    a<c, b<c, c<d, c<e.

Its cover graph is a tree: choose a<c<d as the junction spine, with one
lower leg b<c and one upper leg c<e at the middle junction. Thus it is
already in the general class of junction chains with lower and upper
chain legs. Let [2]={0<1} and put L=J(R) x [2], equivalently the ideal
lattice after adjoining one independent join-irreducible to R.

The ideals of R, grouped by cardinality, are exactly

    {}, {a},{b}, {a,b}, {a,b,c},
    {a,b,c,d},{a,b,c,e}, {a,b,c,d,e}.

Consequently its rank polynomial is

    1+2q+q^2+q^3+2q^4+q^5,

and L has rank sequence

    (1,3,3,2,3,3,1).

Nevertheless the following four points form an antichain in L:

    ({a},1), ({b},1), ({a,b,c,d},0), ({a,b,c,e},0).

The two points at each second coordinate are incomparable. For a cross
pair, the smaller ideal has the larger second coordinate, so neither
product-order comparison holds. Thus width(L)>=4>3, its largest rank.

For completeness, width(L)=4 exactly. Partition J(R) into the chains

    A: {} < {a} < {a,b} < {a,b,c} < {a,b,c,d} < {a,b,c,d,e},
    B: {b} < {a,b,c,e}.

For any finite chain X, partition X x [2] into the chain consisting of
all (x,0), followed by (max X,1), and the chain of all remaining (x,1).
Applying this separately to A and B gives four chains covering L.

## 2. Exact implication for the current staircase task

The desired lattice Q_p has four coordinate chains of 2s-1
join-irreducibles, an ordered chain of their four designated junctions,
and an independent fifth chain of 2s-1 join-irreducibles. Three cuts are
balanced and the fourth is displaced by a small amount.

The example above does not have those equal lengths and near-balanced
cut hypotheses. It therefore neither disproves Sperner for Q_p nor
changes its known finite matching evidence. It does disprove any
proposed justification based only on the junction-tree/two-leg
caterpillar shape, including a blanket claim that adjoining a chain
repairs that shape. A successful theorem or flow construction must
exploit additional quantitative hypotheses satisfied by Q_p.

## 3. Bounded literature check

No applicable chain-partition or matching theorem was identified in
this bounded check. This is a report of the check, not a claim that no
such theorem exists in the literature.

The primary paper [Chainlink Polytopes](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2023/67.pdf)
defines a different system of cyclic interchain relations (Definition
2.2). Its rank-matrix methods prove rank symmetry and fence rank
unimodality; the cited results do not provide the needed matching in
this threshold-junction lattice.

The primary paper [Counting Linear Extensions of Posets with
Determinants of Hook Lengths](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2021/60.html)
introduces mobile posets and proves linear-extension formulas. Those
formulas are not a chain partition of their ideal lattices. No
additional argument converting them into the required adjacent-rank
flow for Q_p was found.

The previously inspected fence results likewise require path-shaped
join-irreducibles; the internal junctions of Q_p have degree four. Rank
unimodality or an identity for its rank polynomial would not establish
the missing width upper bound. The near-balanced four-junction
Sperner/chain-partition gate remains open.
