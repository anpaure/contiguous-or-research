\[
 Q_p=E_p\cap\bigcap_{\alpha:p\in I_\alpha}T_\alpha.
\]

each (Q_p) is nonempty and

\[
                         \bigvee_{p\in I_\alpha}Q_p=T_\alpha

for every row.  The maximal cells (x_p=Q_p) prove sufficiency.  This theorem
does not protect an undeclared witness: every relied-on interval must be in

lower target.  The theorem therefore correctly requires a linear upper atlas
