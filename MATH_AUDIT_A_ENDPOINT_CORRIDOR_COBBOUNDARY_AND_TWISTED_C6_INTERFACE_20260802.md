before the ambient common base is selected.  The augmented table carries

* `3n` roots;
* `3n-1` retained lower and `3n-1` retained upper occurrences;
* both exported history ends;
* the omitted lower/upper pair and private pump closure; and
* every cap/history/private occurrence.
