# k=15 phase-factor safe-opening audit

## Purpose

A cyclic phase-quotient factor is not yet a path carrier.  Every physical
component must be opened at one edge.  The opening deletes one occurrence of
each upper shadow generated by that edge (and longer windows crossing it), so
an exact cyclic factor can become unusable if some component has no opening
whose losses are absorbed by global multiplicity.

`scratch/audit_k15_phasefactor_safe_openings.py` performs this audit exactly.
For a replacement factor for original cycles 1 and 12 it:

1. computes the global upper-q1 through upper-q3 multiplicities after adding
   the untouched original cycles;
2. computes the exact shadow losses of every possible opening edge;
3. optionally restricts openings to those that cut every cyclic depth-3
   residence violation;
4. chooses exactly one opening per component;
5. enforces, for every upper target, that total selected loss is at most its
   multiplicity minus one.

The final selection problem is SAT with weighted at-most constraints.  The
output records the chosen cuts, removed lower colours, the resulting paths,
and the full post-opening upper-shadow audit.

## Positive control

On the original physical cycles 1 and 12, the audit is `SAT_VERIFIED` through
q3.  It selects

```text
cycle 1:  cut 5141, removed lower colour 967
cycle 12: cut 134,  removed lower colour 6826
```

and leaves zero upper-q1, q2, or q3 holes.  Artifact:

```text
scratch/k15_original_1_12.openings_q3.json
```

This is a control for the loss accounting and SAT encoding.

## Exact obstruction for the current upper-q2 factor

For

```text
scratch/k15_phasequotient_upperq2_orbit.factor.json
```

the audit fails already at q1, even with residence ignored.  Components 30,
44, and 51 have no individually safe opening: every edge of each component
is the unique surviving occurrence of at least one upper-q1 target.

Artifacts:

```text
scratch/k15_phasequotient_upperq2_orbit.openings_q1.json
scratch/k15_phasequotient_upperq2_orbit.openings_q2.json
```

Hence this factor cannot be converted to a path family merely by choosing
better cuts.  A future factor must either encode cuttability during factor
search or be paired with an explicit seam-repair operation that replaces the
lost unique colours.

## Interface criterion

For a factor with components `C_1,...,C_s`, let `L_e(t)` be the number of
occurrences of target `t` destroyed by opening component edge `e`, and let
`mu(t)` be its global cyclic multiplicity.  The exact cuttability condition is

\[
  \exists e_i\in E(C_i)\quad
  \sum_i L_{e_i}(t)\le \mu(t)-1
  \qquad\text{for every audited target }t.
\]

The individually-safe pruning used by the script is logically exact: if a
component has no edge satisfying `L_e(t) <= mu(t)-1` for every `t`, then no
simultaneous choice of openings can work, since all losses are nonnegative.
