## 4. Complete sharp radius-three census

For a blocker state `U=W(y,v)`, a one-cell substitution can create a new
`A` occurrence only if its new nonzero cell is a submask of `A`.  Indeed,
`A` was absent before the substitution, so every new `A` witness must contain
the edited cell, and every cell in an interval whose OR is `A` is a submask
of `A`.  Conversely, testing all 255 nonzero submasks of `A` at every position
therefore contains every possible one-cell completion.

For a substitution which covers all old holes, define its debt to be the set
of formerly covered targets whose last occurrences it destroys.

### Theorem 4.1 (sharp-fibre no completion and exact minimum layer)

For every one of the 2048 blockers `W(y,v)`:

1. all `12874*255=3,282,870` position/value assignments were evaluated;
2. exactly 27,926 assignments cover `A`;
3. no assignment has empty debt;
4. the minimum debt size is exactly one;
5. exactly 17 assignments attain it, namely:

   ```text
   p6439: v -> z,  z in Z, debt {H};                 16 rows,
   p12873: y -> A,          debt {E};                  1 row.
   ```

Thus the complete fibre has 34,816 minimum rows and zero universal words.

formerly covered targets whose multiplicity becomes zero.  No witness is

### Corollary 4.2 (retained-portal Hamming-support floor)

Fix any final terminal value `y in Y` and final central value `v in V`, and
retain those two portal coordinates.  A universal word agreeing with
`W(y,v)` outside at most one additional coordinate does not exist.  Therefore
at least two additional distinct coordinates are required; the final word has
Hamming distance at least four from the frozen source.

This is a final Hamming-support statement under the retained-portal
hypothesis, not a sequential edit-count theorem and not a lower bound for
arbitrary block replacement, insertion, deletion, or chronology rethreading.

## 5. Why no state quotient was assumed

The 2048 labelled occurrence states are genuinely distinct.  At the terminal
site, occurrence counts restricted to the `Y` masks recover `y`: intervals
through the central site all contain bit 13 and cannot contribute a `Y` mask,
whereas the terminal singleton contributes `y` and the terminal seam
contributes `E`.  Once `y` is known, occurrence counts restricted to `V`
recover `v`: the central singleton contributes `v`, while every longer
interval through that site contains an extra fixed bit and is not in `V`.

Consequently neither locality nor a coordinate symmetry justified collapsing
the 2048 inputs before the census.  Posterior to enumeration, their normalized
17-row minimum shapes are identical.  Their full debt histograms are not all
identical: they form 252 empirical classes.  The theorem uses the exhaustive
2048-state result, not those posterior classes.

## 6. Constructive target: a protected `A` backup

an `A` witness into an `H` witness.  Theorem 4.1 says that a single further
cell cannot create a replacement `A` witness without losing another target.
Hence the smallest still-open sharp construction is:

1. keep `p12872=0x4e61` and choose any terminal `y in Y`;
2. keep a sharp central provider `v in V` at `p6439`;

## 8. Precise boundary and caveats

Proved here:

* the exact two-hole prefix ledger;
* the 128 terminal sharp states;
* the 2048 sharp blockers;
* the complete third one-cell substitution census for every blocker;
* the exact 17-row minimum layer and absence of a sharp radius-three
  completion;
* the protected-prefix sufficient theorem and the retained-portal requirement
  of at least two further distinct support coordinates after a blocker; and
* certified UNSAT for exactly two further edits inside the pinned
  40-position Hamming-neighbour provider catalogue.

Not proved:

* that no nonsharp second move can lead to a shorter repair;
* that no simultaneous multi-cell packet outside the 40-position catalogue
  exists;
* that every protected-prefix packet must literally create an `A` witness
  before changing the central site, rather than by a coupled simultaneous
  change;
* a universal word inside this frozen five-phase sharp corridor, an optimal
  length-12873 word, or a global no-go at length 12873.

Subsequent global work has produced the literal universal word
`answers/k16_upper12874.word`, SHA256

current global bracket is `12873 <= nu(16) <= 12874`; the catalogue-relative
