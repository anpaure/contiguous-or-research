## 2. What the existing PBBS theorem actually supplies

Theorem 21.2 of `PBBS_RESIDENCE_PACKING_REDUCTION_20260725.md` supplies complete correct-rank support at every depth on the complete lower-owner PBBS factor. The literal conversion and the overlap identities are given in its Section 19.1, equations (19.4)--(19.7).

Write the original lower rank-eight states as A_t, put X_t=[17] minus A_t, and use the step-two upper-owner order. The overlap identity is

    X_t intersect X_(t+2) = A_(t+1).

Consequently, for q>=1,

    intersection(j=0,...,q) X_(t+2j)
      = intersection(j=0,...,q-1) A_(t+2j+1).

Thus upper triple intersections give the first lower shadow, and upper fourfold intersections give the second lower shadow. In particular, the complete factor has all rank-seven upper triple-intersection targets, and all rank-six correct upper fourfold-intersection targets.

This is not a theorem that every restriction of the factor retains those targets. Removing the six bad cycles, opening cycles, replacing them by a path, or changing source letters requires a new preservation argument or a check on the fixed finite data. The census below answers that restriction question at k=17; it does not assume it away.

For a retained cyclic upper-owner sequence T, all positive coordinate runs have length at least four. Define its backward maximal antecedent by

    E_i = T_(i-3) intersect T_(i-2) intersect T_(i-1) intersect T_i,

with cyclic indices. Eroding each positive run by three positions and dilating it by successive unions gives

    E_i union E_(i+1)
      = T_(i-2) intersect T_(i-1) intersect T_i,
    E_i union E_(i+1) union E_(i+2)
      = T_(i-1) intersect T_i,
    E_i union E_(i+1) union E_(i+2) union E_(i+3)
      = T_i.

For completeness, on one coordinate a positive owner run [a,b] produces the antecedent run [a+3,b]. The unions of two, three, and four consecutive antecedent entries have runs [a+2,b], [a+1,b], and [a,b], respectively. This proves the identities coordinatewise, including cyclic runs. In the actual retained PBBS cycles the respective ranks are six for E, seven for the first union, eight for the second, and nine for the owner replay. The finite verifier checks these ranks and all replay identities at every occurrence.

## 4. Exact individual-host criterion

Let E be a cyclic source or a linear source, and let i be one of its positions. A nonempty target S is an individually legal single-letter host at i precisely when

    S is a subset of E_i,
    S union E_j = E_i union E_j for every existing neighbor j of i.

For a linear endpoint there is just one existing neighbor. Define

    C_i = union over existing neighbors j of (E_i minus E_j).

Then the complete legal-host interval is exactly

    C_i subseteq S subseteq E_i, S nonempty.

Indeed, under S subseteq E_i, the equation S union E_j = E_i union E_j holds if and only if E_i minus E_j is a subset of S. Intersecting these requirements over the existing neighbors gives the stated criterion. At an interior or cyclic position this core can also be written E_i minus (E_(i-1) intersect E_(i+1)).

The verifier enumerates every subset of E_i minus C_i and adds C_i, retaining nonempty targets of rank at most six. It counts indexed hosts separately even when their actual letter sets coincide. No sampling, random choices, floating point, matching, or satisfiability solver is involved.

## 5. What remains in the lower compiler

For any simultaneous replacement preserving every pair union, every interval of length at least two retains its original union, since its union is the union of its consecutive pair unions. This protects the good-cycle rank-seven pair witnesses. It does not protect original one-letter rank-six witnesses or the prefix's 102 indispensable rank-seven letters.

Every rank-at-most-six target must therefore be explicitly supplied or reserved in the lower assignment, along with protection for those prefix exceptions. Individual hosts are necessary data, not a simultaneous construction. Two individually legal neighboring installations can jointly erase an element carried by the original pair; distinct demanded targets also cannot occupy the same position.

`EXACT_PAIR_UNION_BINARY_HOST_COMPILER_20260908.md` gives an exact conflict formulation and a 2-SAT compiler when each target is supplied with at most two host choices. The present census does not choose such domains or prove their simultaneous feasibility. Installing every minimum core at once is not justified either.

Finally, cutting and fusing the 140 cyclic sources into Q can change the maximal source letters, the protected pair palette, and host domains near each join. The present positive bank result must be transported through those changes. It also does not remove the active prefix's independent rank-eight and upper-recapture obligations. This is a concrete pre-fusion lower interface, not a proof of nu(17)=B(17) or an all-dimension theorem.
