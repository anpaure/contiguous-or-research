# Terminal decoration after ECO and the exact joint RSB transition state

Date: 2026-07-31
Status: exact stage-separation and associative-transition theorem; exact
scope obstructions; no all-dimension accepting-state construction

## 0. Verdict

There are two logically different uses of an ECO family.

1. **Optional undecorated Hamiltonization.**  Apply physically legal ECO
   toggles to a repaired factor until it is one Hamilton cycle.
2. **Witness transport.**  Freeze a decoration and its auxiliary witnesses
   before the ECO toggles, and require every toggle to transport them.

The first route is strictly weaker than witness transport, but it is itself
stronger than the minimal central requirement.  A separate terminal
operation may decorate any spanning 2-factor; it need not merge its
components.  In either terminal-only route the ECO stage
does **not** need forced-port owner alignment, a leaf-forest matching splice,
an occurrence local-channel network, fixed-decoration transparency, a
protected trace breaker, sockets, residence, deeper shadows, or a
common-\(Q\) compiler.  Those are not physical Hamiltonization predicates.

The central decoration stage needs only a terminal Catalan-linear-matching
witness.  In the present trace language this may be a spanning 2-factor with
globally bijective turn representatives and componentwise alternating
marks.  Every marked component must contain an unmarked occurrence and lie
off the binary cycle face; unmarked components use either residual phase.
A direct ordered four-transversal may use still less.  Historical ECO ports
need not be selected by that new witness.

For RSB, the terminal decorated lift must additionally choose one literal
linear opening and one physical chronology carrying residence, every upper
target, and one common-\(Q\) compiler.  These fields have an exact
associative interface, but the interface is one **correlated relation**, not
the Cartesian product of separately feasible projections:

\[
 \boxed{
   (\text{run collar},\ \text{deep-service state},\
    \text{protected trace},\ \text{opening/ports},\
    \text{common-}Q\text{ relation}).}                 \tag{0.1}
\]

Residence and upper service have deterministic concatenation monoids.  A
protected trace is transported by a named occurrence span.  Opening choices
must remain explicit because the two global endpoints are residence
exemptions and deleting the opening seam removes all cyclic witnesses which
cross it.  The compiler coordinate is the exact maximal-common-cap boundary
relation; it composes by natural join followed by existential projection.

Consequently there are again two exact downstream architectures.

* **Terminal regeneration:** start from any spanning 2-factor, rethread to an
  accepting decorated factor, and recompute the whole central and RSB state
  on its physical path lift.  Optional ECO Hamiltonization may be used but
  is not required.  The private owner/router guards never return as
  hypotheses.
* **Prefix transport:** carry one witness through the ECO path backbone.
  Then owner alignment, matching splice, local channels and no-hidden-
  reopening guards return as proof-sufficient transport coordinates, and
  (0.1) must be appended to them.

The existing repaired project-\(m=5\) singleton closes the central physical
toggle and a compiled rank-one channel.  It exports no accepting state of
(0.1).  Thus it is a central base, not an RSB base.

## 1. Three separate acceptance languages

Let \(F\) be a repaired spanning two-factor, and let
\(Z_1,\ldots,Z_g\) be literal ECO atoms evaluated in their current physical
contexts.  Put

\[
                 F_i=F_{i-1}\mathbin\triangle Z_i.     \tag{1.1}
\]

### 1.1 Physical ECO Hamiltonization

Call (1.1) an **A-execution** when:

1. every deleted atom edge is present in the current \(F_{i-1}\), and the
   symmetric difference is degree preserving;
2. all simultaneous-support and commuting claims are literal, or the atoms
   are tested sequentially in their current contexts;
3. every atom is component faithful; and
4. the selected component--atom incidence graph is a tree, equivalently it
   admits the ordinary component-tree tiling of the incidence-tree theorem.
   On the path-backbone subface, a supplied interval tiling covers every
   edge of that fixed component path once.  The path subface is sufficient,
   not without loss of generality.

These rows imply that every step merges the declared current blocks and the
terminal two-factor has one component.  Hence it is Hamilton.  No selected
occurrence, gap owner, router path, trace mark, or compiler cell appears in
this implication.

### 1.2 Terminal central decoration

Let \(F_A=F_g\).  A **B-stage** either leaves \(F_A\) fixed or applies a
correlated degree-preserving alternating-circuit packet through spanning
two-factors and ends at a spanning 2-factor \(F_B\).  Requiring the endpoint
or every intermediate factor to be Hamiltonian is an optional stronger
normal form.
A **B-certificate** is any terminal witness which proves the Catalan Linear
Matching conclusion on \(F_B\).  On the current trace face it is enough to
give globally bijective upper/lower turn representatives and componentwise
alternating selected shores, require at least one unmarked occurrence on
every marked component, and exclude the ordinary binary cycle face there.
Wholly unmarked components use either residual cross phase; wholly marked
components are forbidden because they lift to two rail cycles.
Leaf-peelability and a protected trace breaker are convenient sufficient
certificates, not logically necessary terminal axioms.  A direct
ordered four-transversal is another allowed B-certificate only when its
physical linear lift is the designated terminal object (equivalently, on
the middle-levels-resolvable face its forced support is carried by the
chosen \(F_B\)).  An unrelated abstract four-transversal proves central CLMT
but is not a witness *on* \(F_B\) and cannot feed the RSB chronology below.
Only the accepting B-endpoint must carry the displayed witness.
Intermediate B states need not be decorated unless that packet is also
being used as a prefix-transport proof.

### 1.3 Terminal RSB acceptance

Fix \(k,r,d\), put \(h=d+1\), and let \(T\) be the literal linear
rank-\(r\) chronology obtained after the physical path lift, connector
selection and one chosen opening.  An **R-certificate** consists of:

1. exact middle ownership by \(T\);
2. internal \(h\)-residence, with only its two global endpoint arms exempt;
3. an interval-union witness for every required upper target;
4. a chain-aligned schedule, caps and one integral lower-target assignment
   satisfying the maximal-common-cap equations; and
5. the protected terminal data needed by the next Pascal lift.

This is precisely the terminal content of RSB, independently of how \(T\)
was constructed.

### Theorem 1.1 (stage separation)

The following implications are exact.

1. An A-execution produces a terminal Hamilton cycle.
2. A B-certificate on any terminal spanning 2-factor proves the central
   Catalan Linear Matching conclusion; an A-execution is optional.
3. A B-certificate followed by an R-certificate proves the corresponding
   RSB instance; again no component-merging antecedent is necessary.

In Items 2--3, no owner or local-channel condition at a historical ECO port
is necessary unless that condition is used to prove transport of the chosen
terminal witness through the A-execution.

#### Proof

Item 1 is the incidence-tree component-faithfulness theorem: an atom node
touches distinct current blocks, lowers the component count by its rank, and
the total rank is one less than the initial component count.  Degree two is
preserved, so the connected terminal factor is a Hamilton cycle.

Item 2 applies the corrected decorated-2-factor theorem componentwise.  Its
hypotheses refer to the terminal occurrence witness, not to connectivity or
marks which might have been placed on earlier ECO ports.  Item 3 applies the exact
carrier--compiler criterion to the terminal R-certificate.  Again the
claim is terminal.  Prefix owner and channel data enter only in the
stronger proof which identifies a terminal witness with the transport of a
chosen earlier witness. \(\square\)

The distinction is visible already in the repaired project-\(m=5\) bank.
All five fixed-rotation ECO atoms survive and individually Hamiltonize the
repaired two-component factor, whereas only four are all-six marked and
owner-aligned in the transported decoration.  Thus the transported-owner
filter is strictly stronger than A-execution.  The audit does not assert
that the fifth endpoint has another B-certificate; it proves only the
claimed separation of predicates.

## 2. The deterministic downstream word monoids

The terminal or transported RSB state is easiest to define after cutting at
every seam which any future operation may change.  A resulting **sealed
fragment** is an oriented literal word

\[
                         P=(P_0,\ldots,P_{n-1})       \tag{2.1}
\]

of rank-\(r\) owner occurrences.  A future step may join or reverse whole
sealed fragments, but may not silently delete an internal edge.  Every
occurrence carries its physical identifier.  This is the phase-aligned
physical-lift requirement: the actual old/new chronology seams are exported.
An abstract ECO circuit size or a bound on the number of augmenting paths
does not imply it, because changing a residual matching phase or following
one long augmentation can rethread an arbitrarily long retained fragment.

### 2.1 Residence

For every coordinate \(x\), store

\[
 (\lambda_x(P),\rho_x(P),\omega_x(P)),              \tag{2.2}
\]

where the first two entries are the initial and terminal positive-run
lengths capped at \(h\), and

\[
 \omega_x(P)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 x\in P_i\ \text{for every }0\le i<n.               \tag{2.2a}
\]

Thus “the whole fragment contains \(x\)” means that the positive run of
\(x\) spans every owner of the fragment, not merely that \(x\) occurs
somewhere in it.  Also store the bit that every positive run internal to
\(P\) has length at least \(h\).

For two internally accepted fragments, the concatenation \(PQ\) is
accepted exactly when, for every \(x\),

\[
 \rho_x(P)+\lambda_x(Q)=0,
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \omega_x(P)\vee\omega_x(Q),
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \rho_x(P)+\lambda_x(Q)\ge h.                       \tag{2.3}
\]

The new collar is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \lambda_x(PQ)&=
 \begin{cases}
  \min(h,\lambda_x(P)+\lambda_x(Q)),&\omega_x(P),\\
  \lambda_x(P),&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}\\
 \rho_x(PQ)&=
 \begin{cases}
  \min(h,\rho_x(P)+\rho_x(Q)),&\omega_x(Q),\\
  \rho_x(Q),&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}\\
 \omega_x(PQ)&=\omega_x(P)\omega_x(Q).
                                                               \tag{2.4}
\end{aligned}
\]

These formulas retain exactly the two endpoint exemptions.  They form an
associative partial monoid because they reconstruct the literal collar of
concatenation.

### 2.2 Arbitrary-width upper service

Put

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname {Tot}(P)&=\bigcup_iP_i,\\
 \operatorname {Pre}(P)&=\left\{\bigcup_{i=0}^jP_i:0\le j<n\right\},\\
 \operatorname {Suf}(P)&=\left\{\bigcup_{i=j}^{n-1}P_i:0\le j<n\right\},\\
 \operatorname {Deck}^+(P)&=
  \left\{\bigcup_{i=a}^bP_i:0\le a\le b<n\right\}.
                                                               \tag{2.5}
\end{aligned}
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname {Tot}(PQ)
   &=\operatorname {Tot}(P)\cup\operatorname {Tot}(Q),\\
 \operatorname {Pre}(PQ)
   &=\operatorname {Pre}(P)
     \cup\{\operatorname {Tot}(P)\cup A:A\in\operatorname {Pre}(Q)\},\\
 \operatorname {Suf}(PQ)
   &=\operatorname {Suf}(Q)
     \cup\{A\cup\operatorname {Tot}(Q):A\in\operatorname {Suf}(P)\},\\
 \operatorname {Deck}^+(PQ)
   &=\operatorname {Deck}^+(P)\cup\operatorname {Deck}^+(Q)\\
   &\qquad\cup
     \{A\cup B:A\in\operatorname {Suf}(P),
                     B\in\operatorname {Pre}(Q)\}.   \tag{2.6}
\end{aligned}
\]

Every interval is internal to one side or uniquely crosses the new seam,
so (2.6) is exact and associative.  Intersections have the dual formulas.
If fixed-width depth labels rather than arbitrary intervals are required,
append the last and first \(q\) literal masks and the already-covered debt
bank.  This is again an exact associative state.  A one-seam provider list
is not exact when a witness may cross several short fragments.

### 2.3 Protected trace

In the binary-trace construction, store a named occurrence span which is a
literal breaker of the unique cycle face, together with the declaration
that no remaining operation deletes an edge of that span.  For a positive
zero-run of length at least four, the span contains that whole zero-run.  For
an even maximal marked run, it also contains the two bounding unmarked
occurrences and their incident transition edges; otherwise a later join
could extend the marked run and change its parity.  With these literal
boundaries a breaker is inherited under every later concatenation or
whole-fragment reversal.
If a step spends the old breaker, it must create and name a replacement in
its new collar.  Equivalently, use the last-birth rule: every terminal
breaker is either a protected base occurrence or was born at one unique
collar and its transported span avoids every later deleted seam.

This protected-witness coordinate is sufficient and compositional.  If no
single breaker is reserved, replace it by the exact finite trace automaton;
a terminal truth bit without its occurrence span is not a transition
state.

## 3. Openings and the common-\(Q\) relation

### 3.1 The opening is a mode, not a terminal afterthought

For every eligible oriented opening \(o\) of a cyclic chronology, let
\(T_o\) be the resulting linear word.  Keep \(o\), its two formal endpoint
occurrences, and all states of Section 2 indexed by the same value of \(o\).

This is necessary for two reasons.

1. A short cyclic positive run may become two legal endpoint arms only when
   the cut lies in that run.
2. At depth \(q\), deleting the cut removes exactly the \(q\) cyclic
   \((q+1)\)-windows whose spans cross it.  A sole upper witness may be one
   of them.

Thus cyclic residence and cyclic shadow coverage do not determine linear
acceptance.  The opening mode must be chosen jointly with the witness bank.

### 3.2 Exact compiler fibre

For one opening \(o\), fix a chain-aligned schedule \(I\), caps \(\Gamma\),
protected rows and the complete lower-cell atlas.  Let \(M\) be an injective
lower-target assignment.  Its maximal common cap is

\[
 A_p(M)=\Gamma_p\cap
        \bigcap_{(S,C)\in M:\ p\in C}S.              \tag{3.1}
\]

The exact compiler fibre \(\mathfrak C_o\) is the set of all tuples
\((I,\Gamma,M,A(M))\) for which:

\[
 A_p(M)\ne\varnothing,                               \tag{3.2}
\]

every scheduled middle and protected row has its exact union under
\(A(M)\), and every selected lower cell \(M(S)\) has union exactly \(S\).
By the maximal-common-cap theorem, \(\mathfrak C_o\ne\varnothing\) is
equivalent to existence of one common-\(Q\) compiler for that opening.

Marginal target--cell Hall, scalar slack and individually admissible caps
are only projections of \(\mathfrak C_o\); they are not substitutes for it.

### 3.3 Exact boundary composition

The compiler can be carried through a path decomposition without selecting
its final matching in advance.  Take the exact Boolean common-cap system.
For a processed module \(P\), expose every variable which occurs in a
constraint not wholly contained in the sealed interior of \(P\), and let

\[
 \mathcal R_P={\text{boundary valuations extendible to a satisfying
                     interior valuation}\}.          \tag{3.3}
\]

This includes used target/cell identities, cap bits at exposed positions,
protected-row bit obligations, and every selected-cell equation which can
still meet a future module.  For a legal seam \(e:P\to Q\), let
\(\mathcal R_e\) contain its new cross-boundary schedule, envelope, cell and
guard equations.  Then the exact update is

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal R_{PQ}
  =\pi_{\partial(PQ)}
       (\mathcal R_P\Join\mathcal R_e\Join\mathcal R_Q),}          \tag{3.4}
\]

where \(\Join\) is natural join on common variables and \(\pi\) existentially
forgets variables which have become internal.

### Lemma 3.1 (compiler relation is associative)

Formula (3.4) is independent of parenthesization, provided every constraint
which crosses a current cut is represented on that cut.

#### Proof

Natural join is conjunction of the represented constraints, and projection
is existential quantification of internal variables.  Both
\((R_P\Join R_e\Join R_Q)\Join R_f\Join R_R\) and
\(R_P\Join R_e\Join(R_Q\Join R_f\Join R_R)\) are the same conjunction.
Existential quantifiers over disjoint variables commute.  Hence both
parenthesizations give exactly the boundary valuations extendible to a
satisfying assignment of the same full subsystem. \(\square\)

This is finite in every fixed dimension, as required by RSB.  It is not
automatically bounded width.  Under an occurrence-fixed compiler skeleton
whose cells use \(h_c\) consecutive depth-\(d\) erosion letters, a cell has
owner span \(d+h_c-1\); only spans crossing the cut need be exposed.  If
cells have unbounded span, representatives or phases may be reoptimized, or
future moves may reopen hidden interiors, the full live relation is the
safe state.  There is no anonymous constant-width compression theorem in
that generality.

## 4. Exact joint transition theorem

For a sealed fragment \(P\), let \(\mathfrak S(P)\) be the set of tuples

\[
 (o,\mathcal R_h,\mathcal U,\beta,
       \eta_-,\eta_+,\mathcal R_{\rm comp})           \tag{4.1}
\]

which arise from one literal physical realization of \(P\).  Here
\(\mathcal R_h\) is the residence collar, \(\mathcal U\) is the exact
upper-service state, \(\beta\) is a protected trace occurrence or exact
trace state, \(\eta_-,\eta_+\) are the occurrence-labelled endpoint ports,
and \(\mathcal R_{\rm comp}\) is (3.3).  The same opening \(o\) and the
same physical occurrence identifiers are used in every coordinate.

Do not replace \(\mathfrak S(P)\) by the product of its coordinate
projections.  If two coordinates share a port, seam, occurrence or compiler
position, their feasible choices remain correlated in (4.1).

### Theorem 4.1 (associative private RSB transition)

Suppose a terminal B-stage or a regeneration node exports sealed fragments
and their exact joint relations (4.1).  Suppose every later path-backbone
transition:

1. joins occurrence-compatible endpoints by a literal Johnson seam;
2. applies (2.3)--(2.6), protects or replaces \(\beta\), and applies (3.4);
3. exposes every resource which a future transition may delete or reuse;
   and
4. has private interiors, or otherwise carries the full overlap relation.

If the transitions are physical lifts of earlier ECO atoms, the premise
includes their occurrence-labelled changed-seam sets and retained-fragment
phases.  Otherwise the node is a regeneration node and its output relation
is recomputed rather than inferred from the abstract atom.

Then:

1. the state obtained at the root is independent of the parenthesization
   of the path-backbone composition;
2. every accepting root tuple gives one literal chronology satisfying
   residence, complete upper service, the trace guard, the chosen opening
   and one common-\(Q\) compiler simultaneously; and
3. if the fragment and transition catalogues are exhaustive, every such
   chronology yields an accepting root tuple.

#### Proof

Endpoint concatenation is associative.  Equations (2.3)--(2.4) are the
exact residence collar of the literal concatenation, and (2.6) is its exact
interval deck.  Protected named spans survive precisely under Item 3; a
replacement is another literal occurrence and obeys the same last-birth
induction.  Lemma 3.1 gives associativity and exactness of the compiler
relation.  Natural join on the shared opening and occurrence identifiers
prevents choices from different physical realizations from being mixed.
This proves Items 1--2.  Conversely, restrict a literal accepting
chronology and its compiler valuation to every cut.  Those restrictions
belong to the exact fragment and seam relations and reconstruct the root
tuple, proving Item 3. \(\square\)

The theorem is a preservation theorem after a B-stage export.  If the ECO
Hamiltonization is deliberately undecorated, there is no RSB witness to
preserve during that A-stage; Theorem 4.1 begins only after the terminal
regeneration has produced one.

## 6. Sharp counter-scopes

### 6.1 Central transparency does not preserve depth two

Let \(|H|=r-2\) and choose distinct \(a,b,c,x,p\notin H\).  Put

\[
 E_a=H+a+x,\quad E_b=H+b+x,\quad E_c=H+c+x,           \tag{6.1}
\]

\[
 Q_{ab}=H+a+b,\quad Q_{bc}=H+b+c,\quad Q_{ca}=H+c+a. \tag{6.2}
\]

Replace

\[
 E_aQ_{ab},\ E_bQ_{bc},\ E_cQ_{ca}
\quad\text{by}\quad
 E_aQ_{ca},\ E_bQ_{ab},\ E_cQ_{bc}.                 \tag{6.3}
\]

Both immediate upper and lower seam-colour multisets are unchanged.  With
retained predecessor \(R_a=H+x+p\), however, one depth-two value changes
from

\[
 R_a\cup E_a\cup Q_{ab}=H+p+x+a+b
\]

to

\[
 R_a\cup E_a\cup Q_{ca}=H+p+x+a+c.                  \tag{6.4}
\]

Hence even an immediate-palette-transparent incidence hexagon can destroy a
sole deeper witness.  The incidence hexagon is the first nontrivial
alternating circuit between consecutive Boolean levels.  This is a local
scope obstruction, not a claim that (6.4) is globally unique in every
Hamilton completion.

### 6.2 Two opening modes are the minimal projection obstruction

Let the allowed opening modes be \(\{o_0,o_1\}\).  Suppose residence accepts
only \(o_0\), while a protected sole upper witness or the compiler accepts
only \(o_1\).  Every displayed marginal projection is nonempty, but the
joint relation (4.1) is empty.  With one opening mode nonempty projections
cannot have this failure, so two modes are minimal.

The pattern has literal word meaning.  Cutting inside a unique short cyclic
positive run can be the only way to split it into two legal boundary arms,
while the same cut can delete the unique cyclic witness whose span crosses
that edge.

### 6.3 Marginal Hall is not common-\(Q\)

At positions \(0,1\), take one middle row with

\[
             \Gamma_0=\Gamma_1=\{o,a,x\},            \tag{6.5}
\]

the two singleton cells, and lower targets

\[
             S_0=\{o\},\qquad S_1=\{o,x\}.           \tag{6.6}
\]

All four target--cell incidences are individually sound.  The marginal
graph is \(K_{2,2}\), is convex, has a permanent owner \(o\), satisfies
Hall, and has two perfect matchings.  Every perfect matching caps both
positions by labels omitting \(a\), so the middle row loses \(a\).  No
common-\(Q\) compiler exists.  This is the smallest two-position
matching/common-cap obstruction and proves that the compiler coordinate of
(4.1) cannot be replaced by marginal Hall data.

## 7. Exact remaining obstruction

If a repaired component path and an integral ECO interval tiling are used,
their bare selection is already a unit-flow problem.  The minimal terminal
route does not require either object.  After the A/B separation, the exact
remaining downstream statement is not another owner-Hall or router theorem.
It is one of the following two supply statements.

1. **Regeneration form.**  The chosen recursive family contains an accepting
   decorated spanning 2-factor whose physical path lift exports a nonempty
   relation (4.1), and that relation regenerates the corresponding child
   relation for the next Pascal step.  Alternating-circuit universality makes
   the starting factor immaterial.
2. **Transport form.**  The state-expanded ECO path graph contains a source
   to accepting path after the fixed-decoration owner/channel rows and all
   five correlated downstream coordinates (4.1) are imposed on each
   transition.

The first form avoids every intermediate owner/router guard but asks for a
global terminal regeneration theorem.  The second localizes the proof but
keeps the larger correlated state.  Neither follows from coherent ECO
supply, collision-path independence, component-path integrality, owner
alignment, or the local max-flow oracle separately.

At repaired project \(m=5\), all five ECO atoms pass A, four pass the
transported owner row, and the isolated atom \(101100\) has the compiled
rank-one channel.  The existing files do not export a root-accepting tuple
of (4.1).  Separately, the audited repaired standard physical lift has
thirty-one bounded coordinate runs of length two in eighteen intact forest
paths, so its intact-path closure cannot be a depth-two RSB chronology.
This is a downstream obstruction, not a reopening of raw ECO or central
CLMT.
