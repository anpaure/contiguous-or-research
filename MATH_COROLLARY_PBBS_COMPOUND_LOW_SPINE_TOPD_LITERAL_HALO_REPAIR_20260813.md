# The compound PBBS low spine has an unconditional protected top-`d` literal-halo repair

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** unconditional composition theorem for the **named crossing
casualties**.  The polynomial compiler occurrences cut by the compound
low-spine replacement admit simultaneous clean paired packages and full
monotone source halos in one simple spanning middle-levels two-factor.
Their target equalities are literal in the maximal candidate antecedent and
become actual compiler cells in every `d`-resident completion.  This is a
prospective factor extension, not a relative extension preserving every
uncut edge or occurrence of the old PBBS factor.  The theorem does **not**
make the unprotected completion resident, solve the deep payload atlas, or
join/open the final chronology.

## 1. Parameters and the low-spine casualty profile

Work on the odd middle-levels ground set of size `2R-1`.  Let `d` be the
deadline and assume

\[
                    d^2=O(R),\qquad d=o(R),\qquad R\ge3d+3.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The compound low-spine theorem replaces the direct PBBS edges

\[
                         U_hU_{h+1},\qquad4\le h\le d,
\tag{1.2}
\]

by the one protected palette path

\[
 U_4-B_4\leadsto C_4-U_5-B_5\leadsto\cdots
        \leadsto C_d-U_{d+1}.
\tag{1.3}
\]

It restores the deleted immediate lower and upper values exactly, is
prospectively biresident internally, has `O(dR)` incidences and exposure
`O(d)`, and exports clipped flags only at its two exterior ports.

There are `p=d-3` deleted old edges.  Define `D_q` to be the submultiset of
selected canonical **correct-rank** depth-`q` occurrences, of target rank
`R-q`, whose `(q+1)`-owner interval crosses at least one deleted edge.  At
owner-intersection depth `q`, one deleted edge lies in at most `q` cyclic
intervals of length `q+1`.  Hence

\[
                         |\mathcal D_q|\le pq\le dq
                         \qquad(1\le q\le d),
\tag{1.4}
\]

and

\[
 \sum_{q=1}^{d}|\mathcal D_q|
 \le { (d-3)d(d+1)\over2}=O(d^3).
\tag{1.5}
\]

Depth one is already restored by the palette half-edges in (1.3).

## 2. Simultaneous literal-halo repair

### Theorem 2.1

For every sufficiently large parameter satisfying (1.1), one can choose,
simultaneously for every occurrence in

\[
                         \bigsqcup_{q=2}^{d}\mathcal D_q,
\tag{2.1}
\]

a clean complement-paired facet-geodesic package with four length-`d+1`
monotone arms such that:

1. all selected owner and lower-incidence resources are mutually disjoint
   and avoid the compound low-spine path;
2. the complete protected bank is a path forest with both all-occurrence
   exposures below `R/3`;
3. every target `S in D_q`, `|S|=R-q`, has a protected source interval of
   width `d-q+1` whose union in the maximal candidate word is exactly `S`;
4. the paired path simultaneously retains a protected consecutive-owner
   union witness for the proper-upper target `[2R-1]\setminus S`; and
5. the union extends to a simple spanning middle-levels two-factor.

The selection order is part of the assertion.  First fix the `O(d)` seam
owners and facets, including all choices of `z_h`, and use them as the
endpoint bank.  Choose every depth-two package and its arms avoiding that
bank; then route the long compound bridges avoiding the depth-two halos.
Finally choose the central packages of depths at least three and expose
their arms.

#### Proof

Equation (1.4) is precisely the task bound

\[
                         |\mathcal D_q|\le C_0dq
\]

of the ordered polynomial source-halo theorem, with `C_0=1`.  Choose the
fixed seam-resource bank first and call it `P_end`; it has size and
exposure `O(d)`.  The ordered halo theorem chooses the at most `2d`
depth-two packages and arms avoiding `P_end`.  Their union has
`O(d^2)=O(R)` resources and exposure `O(d)`.  The clean-arm avoidance
lemma therefore routes every long bridge of (1.3) around this already
protected bank: at a fixed seam endpoint, first choose the fresh whisker
event outside its `O(d)=o(R)` incident forbidden resources, and then the
remaining same-signature forbidden bank has total size `O(R)`.  The result
is the disjoint union of the one compound path and the depth-two halo paths,
hence a path forest.  It is the intermediate base `P_0`, with `O(dR)`
incidences and exposure `O(d)`.

Apply the ordered halo theorem to depths `3,...,d`.  It gives mutual
resource disjointness, both exposure bounds, and a simple protected-factor
extension.  On the complementary central path

\[
                         W_0,\ldots,W_q
\]

the owner intersection is `S`.  If the central block begins at owner
index `i` and

\[
                         P_t=\bigcap_{a=0}^{d}T_{t-a},
\tag{2.2}
\]

then the protected halo identity is

\[
                         \bigcup_{h=q}^{d}P_{i+h}=S.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The interval in (2.3) has width `d-q+1`; both of its endpoint owner
windows lie wholly inside the two protected length-`d+1` arms, so (2.3)
is independent of the exterior factor completion.  In the reverse factor
orientation the reflected halo identity supplies the corresponding
interval.  The clean package's first shore is a consecutive owner path
whose owner union is the complement of `S`, giving the protected
proper-upper owner-path witness claimed in item 4.  Depth one was restored
locally before this construction.  This proves all five items. `square`

## 3. Exact implication and remaining quantifier

### Corollary 3.1

If the spanning factor in Theorem 2.1 is chosen so that every completed
owner component is `d`-resident, then its maximal candidate word is a
valid nonempty antecedent on every component and every occurrence in
`(2.1)` is an actual literal compiler cell.  Consequently the compound
low-spine surgery has zero defect on its **named crossing-casualty bank**
and zero named paired-upper defect.

#### Proof

Residence turns (2.2) into the valid maximal antecedent.  Equation (2.3)
then gives the literal target equality; exact-value fibres for distinct
targets are automatically occurrence-disjoint.  If the protected upper
owner path occupies consecutive owner positions `t,...,t+q-1` and has
owner union `Z`, then

\[
 Z=\bigcup_{j=t}^{t+q-1}T_j
  =\bigcup_{p=t}^{t+q+d-1}P_p.                    \tag{3.1}
\]

Thus under the same residence premise it is also a literal source-interval
upper witness. `square`

The qualification in Corollary 3.1 is essential.  The polynomial
protected-factor theorem controls owner/lower simplicity and exposures; it
does not constrain coordinate run lengths on its unprotected edges.  Thus
this composition removes the former **simultaneous halo-planting gate**,
but leaves the following global rows:

1. a relative rethread or a new global fan theorem preserving/replacing
   the canonical top-`d` occurrences which did **not** cross (1.2);
2. a resident completion and residence-safe fusion/opening;
3. the remote interval anti-cover/chart factor for depths `q>d`;
4. arbitrary-width upper protection outside the named paired bank; and
5. the final typed/regenerative interface.

In particular, an arbitrary protected-factor completion may discard old
PBBS occurrences outside `D_q`.  The present corollary repairs every named
correct-rank occurrence that crosses a deleted role-zero edge; it does not
by itself prove completeness of the entire top-`d` compiler on the new
factor.
