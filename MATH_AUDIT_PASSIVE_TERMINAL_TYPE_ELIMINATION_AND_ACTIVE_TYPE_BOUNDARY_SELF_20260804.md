## 1. Quantifier audit

The conservative extension is applied only after one physical state and
one deterministic complete bundle bank have been fixed.  It does not infer
a common state from separate phase states and does not turn a prospective
menu into a frozen-state menu.

The direct theorem likewise assumes one mutually feasible physical bank and
one residual matching.  It does not use equality of marginal ranks to infer
a joint bank.

## 2. Literal-target audit

The native nested pair is never substituted for the incomparable ray
targets.  Exact target identity stays at the two upstream aligned ray
occurrences.  This respects the incomparability no-go in the folded-C8
theorem.

The polarity bit distinguishes logical roles only.  It is not used to
change any interval OR, activate an unavailable occurrence, or alter a

## 3. Capacity audit

The selected native route remains physically charged.  Only its separate
acceptance label is erased.  Ordinary service on an identical occurrence
fact coalesces inside the charged complete record, and every other bundle
capacity remains in the forbidden residual bank `B_*`.

Therefore the theorem does not make a physical route free and does not
double-use a unit occurrence.

The stronger direct-ray corollary omits the native socket only from the
target-coverage proof.  Its `2(d-1)` ray target values and addresses are
pairwise distinct and simultaneously realized, so they are legitimate
forced compiler edges.  The corollary explicitly retains the socket whenever
another compensation, topology, or routing theorem uses it; it therefore
does not erase a load-bearing physical resource.

## 4. Routing audit

The terminal two-Rado and regular-factor theorems genuinely need types when
they optimize over alternative representatives: an integral linkage may
permute sinks.  The new theorem does not weaken those results.  A
deterministic full bundle removes representative ambiguity but does not make
an otherwise rejected typed sink legal.  The type row is bypassed only when
the final proof abandons that non-target router and uses the direct ray
matching plus a separately proved complementary matching.

The wrong-sink example proves this boundary sharply.

## 5. Regeneration audit

At the final word there is no downstream consumer of the polarity code, so
it is passive.  At an inductive boundary this is not automatic.  A future
transition which distinguishes two type labels makes the type active and
requires either an explicit compatibility proof or a proof that the
continuation factors through the type-free physical record.

Thus no regenerative theorem is claimed.
