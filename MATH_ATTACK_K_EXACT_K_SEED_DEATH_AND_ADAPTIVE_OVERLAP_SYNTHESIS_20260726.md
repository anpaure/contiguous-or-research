# Exact arbitrary-\(k\) pair-type death and adaptive overlapping recoupling

Date: 2026-07-26

Method: pure mathematics only.  No computation, finite search, solver, or
web input is used.

## 0. Verdict

The normalized \(k\)-seed hypothesis is true for every integer \(k\ge1\).
There is an explicit middle-layer carrier \(\mathcal U_k\), of size

\[
                         |\mathcal U_k|=16(2k+1),
\tag{0.1}
\]

and \(2^k\) literal exact \(Q_2\)-factor corners on that same owner set
with lower and upper ledgers

\[
 L^-_\epsilon
 =16(k+|\epsilon|)f_0+
  16(k+1-|\epsilon|)f_1,
\tag{0.2}
\]

\[
 L^+_\epsilon
 =16(k+|\epsilon|)f_1+
  16(k+1-|\epsilon|)f_2.
\tag{0.3}
\]

Thus every seed has background-independent signed drift

\[
                         16(e_0-e_1),\qquad
                         16(e_1-e_2),
\tag{0.4}
\]

and the all-new corner has the literal pointwise death density

\[
                         \boxed{\delta_k={k\over2k+1}.}
\tag{0.5}
\]

The same \(\mathbb Z_4\) colouring phases every cell of every corner, so
all \(2^k\) factors suspend on identical support to physical
\(C_{2h}\)-factors for every \(h\ge2\).

There is no \(k=3\) obstruction.  The construction gives exactly

\[
 |\mathcal U_3|=112,\qquad
 28\ Q_2\text{-cells per corner},\qquad
 \delta_3={3\over7}.
\tag{0.6}
\]

Moreover, the carrier can be arranged on a chain of overlapping pair
coordinates.  A rank-rotated recornering then makes the identical
32-owner sector switch new, old, and new again along a nested logical
path, while every logical step has net one-seed drift and changes at most
three physical seed shores on the exhibited path.

The qualification is exact: the repeated-owner realization is adaptive.
It is not generated by \(k\) fixed context-independent physical
involutions, and one owner never belongs simultaneously to two tagged
seed sectors in a static corner.  Exact finite labelled target balance
and all-depth collision control are not consequences of this theorem.

## 1. Chain carrier

Let

\[
                         P_j=\{a_j,b_j\},
 \qquad 0\le j\le k,
\tag{1.1}
\]

be the chain pairs and let \(S=\{s_0,s_1\}\) be a common splitter pair.
Put

\[
                         t=\lceil\log_2(k+1)\rceil
\tag{1.2}
\]

and take \(t\) tag pairs.  Choose \(k+1\) distinct split tag
orientations \(G_0,\ldots,G_k\).  Finally take reservoir pairs

\[
                         R_1=\{u,v\},\qquad R_2=\{w,x\},
\tag{1.3}
\]

whose split-orientation square is

\[
                         Q_R=(uw,vw,vx,ux).
\tag{1.4}
\]

The ambient block has

\[
                         n_k=2(k+t+4)=2k+2t+8
\tag{1.5}
\]

coordinates.

For seed \(i\in[k]\), freeze every chain pair other than
\(P_{i-1},P_i\) at its \(a\)-endpoint and freeze the tags at \(G_i\).
On \(P_{i-1}\cup P_i\cup S\), define the death states

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_i=\{&
a_{i-1}b_{i-1}s_0,\ a_{i-1}b_{i-1}s_1,\\
&a_i b_i s_0,\ a_i b_i s_1\}
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.6}
\]

and buffer states

\[
\begin{aligned}
Z_i=\{&
b_{i-1}a_i s_0,\ a_{i-1}b_i s_0,\\
&b_{i-1}a_i s_1,\ a_{i-1}b_i s_1\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.7}
\]

For \(\eta\in\{0,1\}\), these states form the physical square

\[
Q_{i,\eta}=
(a_{i-1}b_{i-1}s_\eta,\,
 b_{i-1}a_i s_\eta,\,
 a_i b_i s_\eta,\,
 a_{i-1}b_i s_\eta),
\tag{1.8}
\]

with axes

\[
 a_{i-1}\leftrightarrow a_i,\qquad
 b_{i-1}\leftrightarrow b_i.
\tag{1.9}
\]

Thus seed \(i\) and seed \(i+1\) share the coordinate pair \(P_i\);
all seeds also share \(S\).  This is literal sequential coordinate
overlap.

Let \(\mathcal S_i\) be the eight embedded states \(D_i\dot\cup Z_i\)
with their frozen chain context and tag \(G_i\).  Let
\(\mathcal S_0\) be a tag-\(G_0\) copy of the four \(D_1\)-states.
Distinct tags make

\[
                         \mathcal S_0,\ldots,\mathcal S_k
\tag{1.10}
\]

pairwise owner-disjoint.  Define

\[
\mathcal U_k=
\{X\cup Y:X\in\mathcal S_0\dot\cup\cdots\dot\cup\mathcal S_k,\ 
Y\in\{uw,vw,vx,ux\}\}.
\tag{1.11}
\]

Every state before the reservoir has size \(k+t+2\), and \(Y\) has size
two.  Hence every owner has rank \(k+t+4=n_k/2\).  Also

\[
|\mathcal U_k|
=4(4+8k)=16(2k+1).
\tag{1.12}
\]

This gives the recursive count

\[
|\mathcal U_{k+1}|=|\mathcal U_k|+32;
\tag{1.13}
\]

one new seed is one new 32-owner exact trade sector.

## 2. Exact factor cube and ownership

On seed sector \(i\), the old shore consists of the eight vertical cells

\[
                         X\cup Q_R,\qquad
                         X\in D_i\dot\cup Z_i.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The new shore consists of the eight horizontal cells

\[
                         Q_{i,\eta}\cup Y,\qquad
                         \eta\in\{0,1\},\quad
                         Y\in\{uw,vw,vx,ux\}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Both shores partition the identical 32 owners: (2.1) fixes a local
state and varies the reservoir square, while (2.2) fixes a reservoir
orientation and varies one of the two disjoint local squares.  The
survivor sector uses its four vertical reservoir cells.

For \(A\subseteq[k]\), choose the new shore on sector \(i\) exactly when
\(i\in A\), and denote the resulting factor by \(\mathscr F_A\).
Tag-disjointness gives, without any fractional ownership,

\[
                         \mathscr F_A
\text{ partitions }\mathcal U_k
\text{ into }4(2k+1)\text{ physical }Q_2\text{'s}.
\tag{2.3}
\]

The \(2^k\) factors are distinct because the old and new partitions of
each seed sector differ.

## 3. Exact two-sided ledgers

Measure full-pair type in the fixed matching consisting of all chain,
splitter, tag, and reservoir pairs.  All frozen contexts are split.

On an old seed shore, the four \(D_i\)-states contribute \(16f_1\)
below and the four \(Z_i\)-states contribute \(16f_0\).  Every lower edge
of a new square has intersection among

\[
b_{i-1}s_\eta,\quad a_i s_\eta,\quad
b_i s_\eta,\quad a_{i-1}s_\eta,
\tag{3.1}
\]

so the new shore contributes \(32f_0\).  The survivor contributes
\(16f_1\).  Summing proves

\[
                         L^-_A
=16(k+|A|)f_0+16(k+1-|A|)f_1.
\tag{3.2}
\]

Every old vertical upper edge fills one reservoir pair.  It therefore
has type two above \(D_i\) and type one above \(Z_i\).  Every new
horizontal upper union contains exactly one of \(P_{i-1},P_i\), so it
has type one.  The survivor has upper type two.  Therefore

\[
                         L^+_A
=16(k+|A|)f_1+16(k+1-|A|)f_2.
\tag{3.3}
\]

In the all-new factor, the owner occurrences split into four pointwise
classes:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{class}&\text{number}&(\text{source},\text{lower},\text{upper})\\ \hline
\text{buffer}&16k&(0,0,1)\\
\text{death}&16k&(1,0,1)\\
\text{survivor}&16&(1,1,2).
\end{array}
\tag{3.4}
\]

Hence the death classes are literally disjoint and

\[
                         {16k\over16(2k+1)}
                         ={k\over2k+1}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

Upper birth is pointwise \(B=1-D\), not merely equal in expectation.

## 4. Common phase and exact local operator

Assign \(0,1,2,3\) cyclically around every embedded square
\(Q_{i,\eta}\).  This is consistent because all such owner sets are
disjoint.  If

\[
                         y_0=uw,\ y_1=vw,\ y_2=vx,\ y_3=ux,
\tag{4.1}
\]

colour an owner by

\[
                         c(X\cup y_j)=g(X)+j\pmod4.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Every vertical cell and every horizontal cell is cyclically coloured.
Thus this one colouring phases every cell in every \(\mathscr F_A\).
The coloured-square suspension consequently gives exact common-support
\(C_{2h}\)-factors for all \(h\ge2\).

Rotate the chart on the \(s_1\)-square once relative to the
\(s_0\)-square.  At every fixed abstract \(Q_2\) vertex, a corner of
weight \(r\) then has exact conditional rates

\[
                         p_r^-={r\over2k+1},\qquad
                         p_r^+=1-p_r^-.
\tag{4.3}
\]

At the all-new corner the exact one-block joint probability-generating
polynomial is

\[
\boxed{
\Xi_k(x,y,z)={kz+kxz+xyz^2\over2k+1},}
\tag{4.4}
\]

where \(x,y,z\) record source, lower, and upper full-pair type.  A
product-transversal window touching \(q\) distinct carrier blocks has

\[
                         D_q\sim
\operatorname{Bin}\left(q,{k\over2k+1}\right),
\qquad B_q=q-D_q.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Its mean lower death is

\[
{qk\over2k+1}
={q\over2}-{q\over2(2k+1)}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

Thus the residual \(q/2\) drift gap is exactly

\[
                         {q\over2(2k+1)}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Letting \(k\to\infty\) removes that normalized Gaussian pair-type
obstruction.

## 5. Adaptive repeated-owner cube

The base cube uses one fixed physical shore bit per tag sector.  To force
literal repeated action on the same owners, let

\[
                         \rho=(1\ 2\ \cdots\ k)
\tag{5.1}
\]

and, for every logical subset \(S\subseteq[k]\), define

\[
                         \Psi(S)=\rho^{|S|}(S),\qquad
\widetilde{\mathscr F}_S=\mathscr F_{\Psi(S)}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

On Boolean rank \(r\), \(\rho^r\) is a permutation of the \(r\)-subsets.
Hence \(\Psi\) is a rank-preserving bijection of the Boolean lattice.
All \(2^k\) recornered factors are therefore distinct and exact.
Because (3.2)--(3.3) depend only on rank, every logical edge
\(S\to S\cup\{j\}\) still has precisely the signed drift (0.4).

Put \(r=|S|\).  The number of physical seed shores changed by that edge
is exactly

\[
\boxed{
d_j(S)
=|\Psi(S)\mathbin\triangle\Psi(S\cup\{j\})|
=2r+1-2|S\cap\rho S|
-2\mathbf1_{\{\rho j\in S\}}.}
\tag{5.3}
\]

Indeed, after applying \(\rho^{-r}\),

\[
|\Psi(S)\cap\Psi(S\cup\{j\})|
=|S\cap(\rho S\cup\{\rho j\})|.
\tag{5.4}
\]

It follows that \(d_j(S)\) is odd and

\[
1\le d_j(S)\le
\min\{2r+1,\,2k-2r-1\}.
\tag{5.5}
\]

The edge turns on \((d_j+1)/2\) sectors and turns off
\((d_j-1)/2\), so the net ledger is exactly one forward seed.

For \(k\ge3\), take the nested path

\[
S_0=\varnothing,\qquad
S_r=\{k,1,2,\ldots,r-1\}\quad(1\le r\le k).
\tag{5.6}
\]

Then

\[
\Psi(S_1)=\{1\},\qquad
\Psi(S_2)=\{2,3\},\qquad
\Psi(S_k)=[k].
\tag{5.7}
\]

Thus the identical 32 owners of sector 1 are old at \(S_0\), new at
\(S_1\), old at \(S_2\), and new again at \(S_k\).  The first and last
edges of the path have \(d=1\); every interior edge has \(d=3\).
Therefore the repeated-owner action is literal and costs at most

\[
                         96\text{ owner recouplings}
\tag{5.8}
\]

per interior local step.

This recornering is adaptive: the physical toggle set
\(\Psi(S)\triangle\Psi(S\cup\{j\})\) depends on \(S\).  It does not prove
the stronger fixed-generator same-owner cube.

## 6. Growing-\(k\) packing

The exact unbiased eligibility probability of one chain block is

\[
\boxed{
p_k={16(2k+1)\over2^{2k+2t+8}}
={2k+1\over2^{2k+2t+4}}.}
\tag{6.1}
\]

Partitioning the \(2m\) ordinary coordinates into

\[
                         B=\left\lfloor{2m\over n_k}\right\rfloor
\tag{6.2}
\]

blocks gives

\[
                         Bp_k=
\Theta\left({m\over k^2 4^k}\right).
\tag{6.3}
\]

More explicitly,

\[
 {m\over1536\,k^2 4^k}
 <Bp_k<
 {3m\over16\,k^2 4^k}
\tag{6.4}
\]

once \(m\ge2(k+t+4)\).  Therefore

\[
                         k^2 4^k=o(m^{1/3})
\tag{6.5}
\]

permits \(r=m^{2/3+o(1)}\) eligible blocks per retained owner.  The
first-\(r\)-eligible selector is stable under variation inside
\(\mathcal U_k^r\), and its leave satisfies

\[
 {W-G\over W}
 \le2(m+1)\exp\left(-{Bp_k\over8}\right)=o(1).
\tag{6.6}
\]

Tensoring the local factors and applying the standard Hamming factor
gives literal \(C_{4r}\)-factors on the retained owners with

\[
                         {G\over4r}
\tag{6.7}
\]

components.  Through \(H=O(\sqrt m)\), the certified collar is

\[
                         O(HW/r)=o(W).
\tag{6.8}
\]

For \(q\le H\), (4.5) is exact conditional on the product component and
Hamming phase.  If \(k\to\infty\) under (6.5), the centre error

\[
                         O_A(\sqrt m/k+1)=o(\sqrt m)
\tag{6.9}
\]

and the \(O(q)\) decrement variance is negligible on the ambient
\(\Theta(m)\) pair-type variance scale.

## 7. The audited \(k=3\) boundary

The chain theorem gives the matched two-sided \(112\)-owner carrier in
(0.6).  A more symmetric untagged attempt on

\[
                         \binom{[8]}4\times Q_R
\tag{7.1}
\]

was also audited.  Twelve vertex-disjoint horizontal \(Q_2\)-tiles do
give eight exact factor corners, a common phase, and lower death
\(120/280=3/7\).  But the six discrepancy-three tiles each have lower
death three and upper death one.  Consequently one nominal bit has

\[
\Delta^-=32e_0-24e_1-8e_2,\qquad
\Delta^+=16e_1-8e_2-8e_3,
\tag{7.2}
\]

and the all-new upper death is only

\[
                         {72\over280}={9\over35}.
\tag{7.3}
\]

Changing cyclic orientations cannot alter the intersection/union
multisets.  That particular untagged \(J(8,4)\) retile therefore fails
the simultaneous lower/upper requirement and needs a complement-balanced
replacement.  This is a failure of that natural retile, not an
obstruction at \(k=3\), because the tagged chain carrier already gives
the exact matched \(3/7\) law.

## 8. Exact implication boundary

Proved:

1. arbitrary-\(k\) literal exact common ownership;
2. exact lower and upper affine ledgers;
3. all-new death \(k/(2k+1)\), with pointwise complementary upper birth;
4. adjacent coordinate-overlap of the seed recouplings;
5. one common phase and every-length \(C_{2h}\) suspension;
6. adaptive repeated recoupling of the same owners, at path cost
   \(d\le3\);
7. exact phasewise binomial decrement;
8. growing-\(k\) near-spanning packing under
   \(k^2 4^k=o(m^{1/3})\); and
9. removal of the leading Gaussian \(q/2\) pair-type drift gap as
   \(k\to\infty\).

Not proved:

* a fixed context-independent \(k\)-generator cube with simultaneous
  same-owner multihits;
* exact finite transport of every pair-type orbit law;
* labelled lower/upper target balance at every depth;
* affine all-depth occurrence carrier vectors;
* cross-packet shadow-collision control;
* completion of the negligible packing leave; or
* the constant-one theorem.

Thus the exact local \(k\)-seed gate, including \(k=3\), is positive.
The surviving obstruction is no longer the \(q/2\) Gaussian drift
constant; it is the occurrence-resolved finite and labelled transport
problem.
