# Deterministic obstruction to local \(\Psi\)-descent by the audited conveyor and moving-hole library

Date: 2026-07-27

Method: pure mathematics only. No computation, search, solver, web input,
or probabilistic/entropy argument is used.

## 0. Statement and scope

Put

\[
 n=2m,\qquad M=m+H,\qquad d=m-3H+1,
\]

\[
 N=\binom{2m}{m-H},\qquad
 W=\binom{2m}{m},\qquad dN=\rho W,
\tag{0.1}
\]

and assume

\[
 H=o(m),\qquad H\ge3,\qquad 0\le1-\rho=o(1).
\tag{0.2}
\]

A labelled all-core state chooses one labelled length-\(d\) common-core
tight path at every rank-\(M\) top.  If \(K_D\) is its middle-owner load,
put

\[
 \Psi(K)=\sum_{D\in\binom{[2m]}m}\binom{K_D}{2}.
\tag{0.3}
\]

Since every state has total occurrence mass \(dN\), its common-core floor
energy is

\[
 \Phi_{\rm cc}(K)
 =\sum_D{(K_D-1)(K_D-2)\over2}
 =\Psi(K)+W-dN.
\tag{0.4}
\]

Thus \(\Phi_{\rm cc}\) and \(\Psi\) have exactly the same exchange
derivatives.

Let \(\mathfrak G_{\rm aud}\) be the labelled state graph generated by
the following *proved literal suspensions*:

1. the complete or protected/inert middle-preserving suspensions of the
   three-top/two-base conveyor and its symmetric six-top realization;
2. any two-, four-, or eight-top moving-hole suspension for which the
   proved identical-middle-shore identity is retained; and
3. the catalysed six-frame mixed-placeholder rectangle, suspended to
   length \(d\) as in Theorem 2.3 below.

The theorem of this note is:

### Theorem A (deterministic high-energy local minimum)

For every sufficiently large \(m\), there is an explicit labelled
all-core state \(\mathcal S_\sigma\) which has no incident
middle-nonneutral generator of \(\mathfrak G_{\rm aud}\).  Every incident
middle-neutral generator preserves \(\Psi\).  Hence
\(\mathcal S_\sigma\) is a local minimum in \(\mathfrak G_{\rm aud}\),
and

\[
 \boxed{
 \Psi(\mathcal S_\sigma)
 \ge\left({\rho^2 2^H\over4m}-{\rho\over2}\right)W.}
\tag{0.5}
\]

At the calibrated height
\(H=(1+o(1))\sqrt{m\log m}\), the right side is much larger than \(W\).

This rules out an exchange-descent theorem for the named finite labelled
move library.  It does **not** lower-bound \(\min\Psi\): a zero-energy
change to another labelled representation of the same path monomial may
expose a later catalyst, and no quotient-escape theorem is proved here.

The note also proves an exact load-space obstruction.  Every nonzero
middle derivative in the audited library preserves every singleton
coordinate marginal.  There is a load vector with
\((1/4+o(1))W\) collision energy which is globally minimizing on its
entire marginal fibre.  That formal shield violates a necessary
common-core coordinate cap and is therefore deliberately not claimed to
be a product coefficient.

## 1. Universal deterministic exchange calculus

Let a packet replace aggregate middle vector \(\Gamma^0\) by
\(\Gamma^1\), put

\[
 \Delta=\Gamma^1-\Gamma^0,
\]

and let \(R\) be the load of all untouched paths.

### Lemma 1.1 (exact packet derivative)

If the two shores have equal occurrence mass, then

\[
 \boxed{
 \Psi(R+\Gamma^1)-\Psi(R+\Gamma^0)
 =\langle R,\Delta\rangle
  +{1\over2}\bigl(\|\Gamma^1\|_2^2-\|\Gamma^0\|_2^2\bigr).}
\tag{1.1}
\]

If a coordinate permutation maps one shore to the other, the last term
vanishes and the exact local-minimum inequality is

\[
 \langle R,\Delta\rangle\ge0.
\tag{1.2}
\]

#### Proof

For every nonnegative integral vector \(X\),

\[
 \Psi(X)={1\over2}\left(\|X\|_2^2-\sum_DX_D\right).
\]

The linear terms cancel because the two packet masses agree.  Expanding
the squared norms proves (1.1).  Coordinate conjugacy gives equality of
the two intrinsic norms and hence (1.2). \(\square\)

### Proposition 1.2 (middle-neutral part of the library)

Each proved identical-middle-shore suspension of the following gadgets
has \(\Delta=0\), and therefore preserves \(\Psi\) in every exterior:

1. the three-top/two-base conveyor and symmetric six-top conveyor;
2. the two-top moving-hole transfer; and
3. the squarefree four- and eight-top moving-hole cubes.

#### Proof

For the two-base conveyor, the two positional derivatives satisfy

\[
 d_H(\omega';x,y)=-d_H(\omega;x,y),
\]

while the path telescope gives their sum as the packet derivative.  Thus
the complete middle derivative is zero.  Deleting phases whose packet
derivative is individually zero preserves the identity.

For the two-top moving-hole transfer, equation (2.12) of its theorem is
literal equality of the retained middle-owner vectors.  The four-top and
eight-top theorems similarly prove equality of the two punctured
core-only \(H\)-window families, hence zero alternating middle derivative.
Any suspension included in the present graph is required to retain that
proved identity. \(\square\)

These gadgets may change chronology and signed traces.  They simply do
not move the objective (0.3).

## 2. The catalysed middle-nonneutral primitive

Use the notation of
`MATH_THEOREM_SIX_FRAME_MIXED_PLACEHOLDER_RECTANGLE_AND_MINIMALITY_20260726.md`.
There are six changed frames on tops

\[
 U_i=C\cup\{x_i,x_{i+1},z\}\quad(0\le i<5),
 \qquad U_5=C\cup\{x_5,x_0,w\},
\tag{2.1}
\]

and one fixed closing catalyst on

\[
 V_z=C\cup\{x_5,x_0,z\}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

### Proposition 2.1 (six-frame rectangle derivative)

At the middle row the changed six-frame packet has derivative

\[
 \Delta=d_w^H-d_z^H.
\tag{2.3}
\]

It is the sum of \(2H\) support-disjoint context lifts, up to global
complementation and sign, of

\[
 \eta=
 e_{\{x_5,z\}}-e_{\{x_0,z\}}
 +e_{\{x_0,w\}}-e_{\{x_5,w\}},
\tag{2.4}
\]

and hence

\[
 \|\Delta\|_2^2=8H.
\tag{2.5}
\]

With the fixed catalyst included, the old seven-frame packet has exactly
\(2H\) colliding target pairs and no triple collision, while the new
packet is pairwise disjoint.  Thus, if \(R\) is the untouched load,

\[
 \boxed{
 \Psi(R+L_{\rm new})-\Psi(R+L_{\rm old})
 =-2H+\langle R,\Delta\rangle.}
\tag{2.6}
\]

#### Proof

The phasewise six-edge telescope is exactly
\(d_w^H-d_z^H\).  The separated placeholders give \(H\) contributing
phases through each of the two swapped positions.  Their contexts are
pairwise distinct, and every contribution is (2.4), proving (2.5).
The exact collision statement is the two-collision-fan proposition of
the cited theorem.  It gives the intrinsic change \(-2H\); adding the
same exterior to both shores contributes \(\langle R,\Delta\rangle\),
proving (2.6). \(\square\)

### Proposition 2.2 (singleton-marginal invariant)

For every coordinate \(i\),

\[
 \sum_{D\ni i}\Delta_D=0.
\tag{2.7}
\]

The same holds for every coordinate relabelling, common phase
restriction, and finite sum of audited derivatives.  Consequently no
composition of the audited moves realizes \(e_X-e_Y\) for distinct
middle targets \(X,Y\).

#### Proof

The four coefficients of \(\eta\) sum to zero.  At each of
\(x_0,x_5,z,w\), its two incident coefficients cancel.  Adjoining a
fixed context preserves zero singleton incidence: a context coordinate
sees the total coefficient sum, and an external coordinate sees its
incidence in \(\eta\).  Global complementation replaces a coordinate
incidence by total coefficient sum minus that incidence, so it also
preserves zero.  This proves (2.7) and its stated closure properties.

For distinct \(X,Y\), a coordinate in \(X\triangle Y\) has nonzero
marginal in \(e_X-e_Y\), so such a transfer cannot lie in the generated
derivative lattice. \(\square\)

### Theorem 2.3 (literal length-\(d\) suspension)

For all sufficiently large \(m\), the seven paths in Proposition 2.1
can be chosen as literal length-\(d\) options in the full all-core
common-core catalogue, while retaining all \(2H\) rectangles and the
energy identity (2.6).

#### Proof

It is enough that \(M\ge12H+10\).  Choose a consecutive protected block
\(Q\subset C\) of size \(2H\).  Place the three positional placeholders
in the complementary segment, pairwise more than \(2H\) apart and more
than \(2H\) from both ends.  Use \(Q\) as the core on all seven tops.

The core-safe construction retains

\[
 (M-2H)-2H+1=M-4H+1=d
\]

consecutive phases.  Every middle \(H\)-window through either swapped
placeholder is retained and avoids both the third placeholder and the
protected block.  Swapping the two labels does not change the retained
phase set.  Hence all \(2H\) context rectangles, all \(2H\) old
collisions, and all corresponding new disjoint targets survive the
restriction.  Equations (2.3)--(2.6) remain exact. \(\square\)

This is an all-core statement.  It does not place the move inside a
preassigned core assignment or fixed mechanical atlas.

## 3. The deterministic master-order state

Fix a directed cyclic order \(\sigma\) on \([2m]\) and a cut.  At each
rank-\(M\) top \(U\), write

\[
 \sigma|_U=(u_1,\ldots,u_M).
\tag{3.1}
\]

Choose

\[
 Q_U=\{u_1,\ldots,u_{2H}\},
 \qquad S_U=(u_{2H+1},\ldots,u_M),
\tag{3.2}
\]

with both blocks in their displayed orders, and take the associated
length-\(d\) core-safe path.  Call the resulting labelled product state
\(\mathcal S_\sigma\).

### Lemma 3.1 (restriction-consistency obstruction)

Let \(C\) be a common positional core and let \(A,B\) be two distinct
gaps in its cyclic order.  There are no distinct labels \(x,a,y\notin C\)
such that both

\[
 \theta^+(x,a)\text{ on }C\cup\{x,a\},
 \qquad
 \theta^+(a,y)\text{ on }C\cup\{a,y\}
\tag{3.3}
\]

are restrictions of one cyclic order on \(C\cup\{x,a,y\}\).  The same
holds with both plus signs replaced by minus signs.

#### Proof

Restrict both displayed orders to \(C\cup\{a\}\).  In the first order,
\(a\) occupies the \(B\)-gap; in the second it occupies the distinct
\(A\)-gap.  Restrictions of one ambient cyclic order to the same subset
must agree, a contradiction.  Reversing both placeholder assignments
only interchanges the two gaps. \(\square\)

### Corollary 3.2 (no nonneutral audited shore)

The labelled state \(\mathcal S_\sigma\) contains no shore of

1. a distinct-top two-base conveyor with a path of at least two edges;
2. the three-top conveyor or symmetric six-top realization; or
3. the catalysed six-frame mixed-placeholder rectangle, including its
   three-changed-top factorization and its unrestricted two-opposed-swap
   factorization.

#### Proof

Every selected labelled cyclic frame is the restriction \(\sigma|_U\).
Two consecutive edges in a packet path share an internal label \(a\).
The first frame places \(a\) in one placeholder gap and the second in the
other.  Lemma 3.1 contradicts their being restrictions of the same master
order.

The three-top packet contains one two-edge path; the symmetric six-top
packet contains two three-edge paths.  The catalysed six-frame packet
contains the five-edge common-spectator path in (2.1), while its smaller
protected factorization still contains a two-edge common-spectator path.

In the unrestricted two-top factorization, the two tops share
\(C\cup\{x_0,x_5\}\), but their source frames give the common labels
\(x_0,x_5\) opposite orders in the two placeholder gaps.  Their
restrictions to this common subset therefore disagree, which is likewise
impossible for two restrictions of \(\sigma\).  Thus every listed
nonneutral shore is excluded. \(\square\)

The statement is about the labelled options which are terms of the path
product.  It does not assert that the same path monomials have no alternate
labelled representations exposing a packet.

## 4. Exponential support compression and high energy

Let \(\mathcal A_\sigma\) be the middle sets whose complement contains an
ambient block of \(H\) consecutive coordinates of \(\sigma\).

### Lemma 4.1 (master-order support bound)

Every owner used by \(\mathcal S_\sigma\) belongs to
\(\mathcal A_\sigma\), and

\[
 |\mathcal A_\sigma|
 \le2m\binom{2m-H}{m}
 \le2m\,2^{-H}W.
\tag{4.1}
\]

#### Proof

Write a used owner as \(D=U\setminus J\), where \(J\) is a consecutive
\(H\)-window of \(\sigma|_U\).  The ambient \(\sigma\)-arc from the
first to the last member of \(J\) contains no point of \(D\): its points
outside \(J\) are absent from \(U\), hence also absent from \(D\).  The
arc has at least \(H\) coordinates, so \(D^c\) contains an ambient
consecutive \(H\)-block.

For each of the \(2m\) ambient blocks, exactly
\(\binom{2m-H}{m}\) middle sets avoid it.  The union bound gives the
first inequality.  Finally,

\[
 {\binom{2m-H}{m}\over W}
 =\prod_{j=0}^{H-1}{m-j\over2m-j}\le2^{-H}.
\]

This proves (4.1). \(\square\)

### Proof of Theorem A

The total middle occurrence mass of \(\mathcal S_\sigma\) is

\[
 \sum_DK_D=dN=\rho W.
\tag{4.2}
\]

All nonzero loads lie in \(\mathcal A_\sigma\).  Cauchy--Schwarz and
Lemma 4.1 give

\[
 \sum_DK_D^2
 \ge{(\rho W)^2\over|\mathcal A_\sigma|}
 \ge{\rho^2 2^H\over2m}W.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \Psi(\mathcal S_\sigma)
 ={1\over2}\left(\sum_DK_D^2-\rho W\right)
 \ge\left({\rho^2 2^H\over4m}-{\rho\over2}\right)W.
\]

Corollary 3.2 excludes every middle-nonneutral incident generator.
Every remaining incident generator in \(\mathfrak G_{\rm aud}\) has
zero middle derivative by Proposition 1.2.  Hence every neighbor has the
same \(\Psi\)-value, proving local minimality and (0.5). \(\square\)

No averaging is used: both the state and its support certificate are
explicit.

## 5. A sharp load-space marginal shield

In this section only, use the globally complementary root-target
convention.  Global complementation preserves \(\Psi\).  For a zero-total
derivative it also sends each singleton marginal to its negative, so the
zero-marginal lattice is unchanged.

Let

\[
 \mathcal K=\left\{\Delta\in\mathbb Z^{\binom{[2m]}m}:
 \sum_D\Delta_D=0,
 \quad\sum_{D\ni i}\Delta_D=0\ (1\le i\le2m)\right\}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Every net middle derivative generated by the explicitly defined library
\(\mathfrak G_{\rm aud}\) lies in \(\mathcal K\).

Put

\[
 K_0=W-dN=o(W).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Choose \(\mathcal H\), of size \(K_0\), among the middle sets containing
exactly one of coordinates \(1,2\), and define

\[
 L_D^\sharp
 =\mathbf1_{\{1\in D\}}+\mathbf1_{\{2\in D\}}
  -\mathbf1_{\{D\in\mathcal H\}}.
\tag{5.3}
\]

### Theorem 5.1 (global minimum on the marginal fibre)

The vector \(L^\sharp\) is nonnegative, has total mass \(dN\), and

\[
 \Psi(L^\sharp)=\binom{2m-2}{m-2}
 =\left({1\over4}+o(1)\right)W.
\tag{5.4}
\]

For every \(\Delta\in\mathcal K\) with
\(L^\sharp+\Delta\ge0\),

\[
 \Psi(L^\sharp+\Delta)\ge\Psi(L^\sharp).
\tag{5.5}
\]

#### Proof

The first two indicators in (5.3) have total mass \(W\), and the last
removes \(K_0\) unit cells.  The only load-two cells are the targets
containing both \(1,2\), proving (5.4).

For \(\Delta\in\mathcal K\), its coordinate marginals at \(1,2\) give

\[
 \langle L^\sharp,\Delta\rangle
 =-\sum_{D\in\mathcal H}\Delta_D.
\tag{5.6}
\]

Since \(\Delta\) is integral and has zero total sum,

\[
 \sum_{D\in\mathcal H}\Delta_D
 \le{1\over2}\|\Delta\|_1
 \le{1\over2}\|\Delta\|_2^2.
\tag{5.7}
\]

Using

\[
 \Psi(L^\sharp+\Delta)-\Psi(L^\sharp)
 =\langle L^\sharp,\Delta\rangle
   +{1\over2}\|\Delta\|_2^2
\]

now proves (5.5). \(\square\)

This is an exact obstruction to deriving discrete-convex descent from
the exchange cone alone.  It is not a physical counterexample.

### Proposition 5.2 (the shield violates the common-core marginal cap)

Every genuine length-\(d\) common-core selection satisfies, in the
root-target convention,

\[
 dN{m-H\over2m}
 \le b_i:=\sum_{D\ni i}K_D
 \le dN{m-H\over2m}+HN{m+H\over2m}.
\tag{5.8}
\]

Both endpoints are \((1/2+o(1))W\), whereas

\[
 b_1(L^\sharp)\ge
 {W\over2}+\binom{2m-2}{m-2}-K_0
 =\left({3\over4}-o(1)\right)W.
\tag{5.9}
\]

Thus \(L^\sharp\) is not in the common-core product support.

#### Proof

There are \(N(m-H)/(2m)\) roots containing coordinate \(i\), and all
\(d\) targets selected at each such root contain \(i\).  There are
\(N(m+H)/(2m)\) other roots.  In one injective tight \(H\)-path, a
fixed coordinate belongs to at most \(H\) consecutive windows.  This
proves (5.8).

Formula (5.9) follows directly from (5.3): the first star contributes
\(W/2\), the intersection of the first two stars contributes
\(\binom{2m-2}{m-2}\), and deleting \(\mathcal H\) removes at most
\(K_0\) incidences at coordinate \(1\). \(\square\)

## 6. Exact boundary

Proved:

1. the exact deterministic \(\Psi\)-derivative of every packet;
2. exact middle flatness of the owner-preserving conveyor and moving-hole
   gadgets;
3. the catalysed six-frame derivative, its \(-2H\) intrinsic descent,
   and its zero singleton marginals;
4. a literal all-core length-\(d\) suspension of that catalyst;
5. the explicit master-order state with no labelled nonneutral shore;
6. the deterministic lower bound (0.5), making that state a
   high-energy local minimum of the named labelled move graph; and
7. an exact high-energy minimum on the ambient coordinate-marginal fibre,
   together with the proof that this formal shield is not physical.

Not proved:

1. \(\min\Psi=o(W)\) or \(\min\Psi=\Omega(W)\);
2. local minimality of \(\mathcal S_\sigma\) after quotienting all
   zero-energy alternate representations of one path monomial;
3. a charged-copy theorem for the six-frame catalyst; or
4. coefficient one.

The smallest positive replacement lemma is a **quotient escape theorem**:
after allowing zero-energy changes of labelled representation, every
common-core coefficient with \(\Psi\ge\varepsilon W\) must reach a
representation exposing a catalysed six-frame move with

\[
 \langle R,\Delta\rangle<2H.
\tag{6.1}
\]

Alternatively, one must construct a physically legal middle exchange
whose derivative changes a singleton marginal and then prove a charged
coverage theorem for it.  The presently audited conveyors and moving-hole
rectangles prove neither statement.
