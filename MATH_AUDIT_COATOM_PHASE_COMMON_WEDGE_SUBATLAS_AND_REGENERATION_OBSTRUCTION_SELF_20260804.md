## 1. Set identities and ranks

From the disjoint decomposition in the fixed-active atlas,

\[
 K_{uv}\cup F_{uv}
 =\bigl(H\setminus(\{e,h,u,v\}\cup S)\bigr)
  \cup\{g\}\cup S\cup\{u,v,h\}
 =(H\setminus\{e\})\cup\{g\}.
\]

The right side has rank `r-2`.  Every active block label has rank three and
is disjoint from it, so `Z_*` has rank `r+1`.  Deleting one of `u,v` gives
the two rank-`r` owners; deleting both gives their rank-`r-1` intersection.
Their union is `Z_*`.  These are exactly the lower--owner--upper ranks of a
native q1 turn.

## 2. Literal phase identity

The authoritative active words agree at positions `1,4,5,12`.  The tensor
uses the same positional coatom expansion and the same ordered filler in

block letter by letter, not merely an equal owner set or equal OR support.
The consecutive `u,v` omissions therefore occur at the same two literal
addresses in both phases.

## 3. Census

There are `q(q-1)` ordered pairs.  Reversal changes only the traversal
orientation.  The intersection satisfies

\[

\]

so distinct unordered pairs give distinct lower sources and there are
exactly `binom(q,2)` unoriented wedges.  Every option has the same union
`Z_*`; this common socket is recorded as an obstruction rather than counted
as independent capacity.
