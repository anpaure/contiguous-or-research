# AB/K8 cross-audit: joint TU signing, palette growth, and protected-menu escape

Date: 2026-07-25

Method: pure mathematics only. No web search, finite search, solver, computer
algebra, or long local job is used. Every switching statement concerns complete
freshly recomputed ownership components of literal integral exact middle wreath
factors.

Status: cross-audit plus two exact noncomposition theorems. The conditional
near-TU lemma in K8 is valid, but none of its four geometric hypotheses is
proved for the dependent seam overlay. No conclusion of coefficient one,
MWB, or literal contiguous-OR optimality is drawn from that conditional lemma.

## 0. Verdict

Put

\[
n=2m+1,\qquad W=\binom nm,\qquad
B=\frac Wn=\operatorname{Cat}_m,\qquad
H=\lceil A\sqrt m\rceil ,
\tag{0.1}
\]

where \(A>0\) is fixed. At depth \(q\), write

\[
N_q=\binom n{m-q},\qquad
W=c_qN_q+\rho_q,\quad 0\le\rho_q<N_q,
\qquad b_q=\binom{c_q+1}{2}.
\tag{0.2}
\]

Let \(O_q\) be the exact balanced-quota overload, let

\[
J_A(F)=\sum_{q\le H}\frac{O_q(F)}{c_q},
\tag{0.3}
\]

and let

\[
\Phi_q(F)=
\sum_S\binom{\mu_q^F(S)}2-P_q^{\min},
\qquad
\mathfrak F_A(F)=\sum_{q\le H}\frac{\Phi_q(F)}{b_q}.
\tag{0.4}
\]

The audit gives the following conclusions.

1. **The seam constants pass.** The exact lower bound in K8 is

   \[
   \Sigma_m\ge
   \frac{v_m(2n-4)m(m-4)}{4(2m^2+1)},
   \tag{0.5}
   \]

   where \(v_m=(11/72+o(1))B\). Hence

   \[
   \frac{\Sigma_m}{W}\ge\frac{11}{288}-o(1).
   \tag{0.6}
   \]

   The corresponding fresh seam-incident row mass is at least
   \((11/144-o(1))B\). The notation in (0.6) is a lower bound. The explicit
   right side of (0.5), divided by \(W\), equals \(11/288+o(1)\), with no
   prescribed sign on that \(o(1)\); the actual seam count may be larger.

2. **The all-depth TU theorem passes with its full hypotheses.** One vertically
   concatenated all-depth leakage matrix, one joint deleted row set, and one
   common component signing are essential. Separate depthwise TU statements
   do not compose.

3. **The TU conclusion has a factorial strengthening.** Under all four K8
   profile, unit-leakage, and joint near-TU hypotheses, the signed endpoint
   obeys

   \[
   \boxed{
   \Phi_q\le(c_q+1)^2|\mathcal R_q|,
   \qquad
   \mathfrak F_A\le4|\mathcal R|.
   }
   \tag{0.7}
   \]

   Thus a joint deletion of \(o(W)\) rows would control both \(J_A\) and the
   normalized factorial objective. This is conditional: K8 does not prove the
   profile, unit-leakage, or near-TU hypotheses for its seam construction.

4. **The \(6^k\) orbit ceiling passes and sharpens to \(6^r\).** Here \(r\)
   is the graphic rank of the new transpositions after the native
   size-one/two coordinate blocks are contracted. It satisfies \(r\le k\).
   The bound applies ex post to a fixed-frame adaptive history with \(k\)
   distinct nonnative colours, regardless of reuse or recomputation. It does
   not survive unrestricted reframing without restarting the invariant.

5. **The factorial constant \(8192\) passes.** If

   \[
   d=m-H-2\ge1,\qquad
   \frac{\operatorname{Cat}_H}{(4d+2)6^k}
   \ge4(c_H+1),
   \tag{0.8}
   \]

   then

   \[
   \boxed{
   \frac{\Phi_H}{W}>
   \frac{4^H}{8192\,ndH^4\,6^k}.
   }
   \tag{0.9}
   \]

   For the normalized objective, the exact consequence includes the missing
   normalizer:

   \[
   \boxed{
   \frac{\mathfrak F_A}{W}>
   \frac{4^H}
   {8192\,b_HndH^4\,6^k}.
   }
   \tag{0.10}
   \]

   The finite guard is \(H\le m-3\), not merely \(H\le m-2\), whenever
   (0.9) is invoked.

6. **K8 parts A and B do not compose in the canonical fixed frame.** Its
   dependent preparation uses only native colours and its fresh seam uses one
   further colour. The native private pile remains too concentrated for K8's
   own pair-profile hypotheses. The failure occurs before TU or signing.

7. **K can avoid the AB8 menu at the seam-label level.** If the AB8 menu has
   \(O_A(m)\) colours, the seam averaging may exclude the entire menu and
   still gives \((11/288-o_A(1))W\) seams. This statement concerns the
   canonical packet construction, not the AB8 invariant-fibre minimizer.

8. **Near-TU cannot be the first productive escape from the AB8 cage.** After
   any menu-only history, K8's profile hypotheses fail for every candidate
   next transposition. After \(D\) arbitrary preparatory cuts they remain
   impossible whenever

   \[
   2^{D+2}(c_H+1)<\operatorname{Cat}_H.
   \tag{0.11}
   \]

   Hence applicability of K8's stated TU gate requires at least

   \[
   \boxed{
   D\ge
   \left\lceil
   \log_2\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_H}{c_H+1}
   \right\rceil-2
   =
   2H-\frac32\log_2H-O_A(1)
   }
   \tag{0.12}
   \]

   prior counted cuts. This is a necessary condition for that particular
   profile/TU theorem, not for every possible adaptive balancing method.

## 1. Exact profile language

Fix a factor \(F\), a depth \(q\), and a candidate transposition \(\sigma\).
Call the following the K-profile condition:

\[
\mu_q(S)\in\{c_q,c_q+1\}
\quad\text{for every \(\sigma\)-fixed target }S,
\tag{1.1}
\]

and

\[
\mu_q(S)+\mu_q(\sigma S)
\in\{2c_q,2c_q+1,2c_q+2\}
\tag{1.2}
\]

for every moved pair \(\{S,\sigma S\}\).

### Lemma 1.1 (orbitwise quota induced before signing)

If (1.1)--(1.2) hold, then there is a balanced quota vector \(b\), with
exactly \(\rho_q\) entries \(c_q+1\), such that

\[
\sum_{S\in\mathcal O}\mu_q(S)
=\sum_{S\in\mathcal O}b(S)
\tag{1.3}
\]

on every \(\sigma\)-orbit \(\mathcal O\). Consequently every
\(\sigma\)-invariant target union \(U\) satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\mu_q(U)\le c_q|U|+\min(|U|,\rho_q).
}
\tag{1.4}
\]

#### Proof

On a fixed target use its actual value, which is \(c_q\) or \(c_q+1\). On a
moved pair of total \(2c_q,2c_q+1,\) or \(2c_q+2\), choose respectively

\[
(c_q,c_q),\qquad
(c_q,c_q+1),\qquad
(c_q+1,c_q+1).
\tag{1.5}
\]

This gives (1.3). The selected quota entries have total \(W\), so their number
of ceilings is forced to be \(\rho_q\). A balanced quota puts at most the
right side of (1.4) on \(U\). \(\square\)

This lemma is independent of leakage and TU. It will be the decisive
pre-signing obstruction below.

## 2. Audit of the \(11/288\) seam constant

Let \(v_m\) be the exact contextual packet matching size. The previously
proved matching enumeration gives

\[
v_m=\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_m-O_m}{2}
=\left(\frac{11}{72}+o(1)\right)B.
\tag{2.1}
\]

One matched packet has \(2n\) middle roots, of which exactly \(2n-4\) are
moved by its packet transposition. A moved root has Johnson degree
\(m(m+1)\), and at most \(2n-1\) neighbours remain in its packet cell. Hence
the external degree floor is

\[
d_*=m(m+1)-(2n-1)=m^2-3m-1.
\tag{2.2}
\]

Every fixed transposition colour accounts for at most
\(v_m(2n-4)\) directed external arcs. The native contextual palette has
\(m-1\) colours. Deleting them leaves

\[
v_m(2n-4)\bigl(d_*-(m-1)\bigr)
=v_m(2n-4)m(m-4)
\tag{2.3}
\]

arcs on

\[
\binom n2-(m-1)=2m^2+1
\tag{2.4}
\]

colours. A colour average followed by the pinned dependent bit assignment
loses at most a factor four. This proves (0.5).

Finally,

\[
\frac{v_m}{B}\longrightarrow\frac{11}{72},
\tag{2.5}
\]

and

\[
\frac{(2n-4)m(m-4)}
{4n(2m^2+1)}
\longrightarrow\frac14.
\tag{2.6}
\]

Equations (2.5)--(2.6) prove (0.6). Since a loopless row has owner degree at
most \(n-3\), the seam incidences give at least

\[
|Z_m|\ge
\frac{2\Sigma_m}{n-3}
\ge\left(\frac{11}{144}-o(1)\right)B
\tag{2.7}
\]

fresh seam-incident left rows.

### Theorem 2.1 (forbidden-palette seam selection)

Let \(\mathcal P\) be any forbidden set of \(p\) transposition colours, with

\[
p<d_*=m^2-3m-1.
\tag{2.8}
\]

Then the same canonical dependent packet preparation has a colour
\(\rho\notin\mathcal P\) and a deterministic side assignment producing at
least

\[
\boxed{
\frac{
v_m(2n-4)(d_*-p)
}{
4\left(\binom n2-p\right)
}
}
\tag{2.9}
\]

distinct genuine commutator seams.

#### Proof

Each forbidden colour removes at most \(v_m(2n-4)\) directed arcs. Thus at
least \(v_m(2n-4)(d_*-p)\) arcs remain on
\(\binom n2-p\) allowed colours. Average, then apply the same pinned bits.
The active-arc injection is unchanged. \(\square\)

Let \(\mathcal T_m\) be K8's native palette and let \(\mathcal M_s\) be the
AB8 protected menu. If \(s\le H\), then

\[
|\mathcal T_m\cup\mathcal M_s|=O_A(m).
\tag{2.10}
\]

Applying Theorem 2.1 to

\[
\mathcal P=\mathcal T_m\cup\mathcal M_s
\tag{2.11}
\]

preserves the limiting factor \(1/4\) in (2.6). Hence the canonical packet
construction has a seam colour outside the AB8 menu with

\[
\left(\frac{11}{288}-o_A(1)\right)W
\tag{2.12}
\]

genuine seams.

Equation (2.12) is only a colour/seam statement at a canonical packet-cube
vertex. The AB8 minimizer \(G_{m,s}\) is chosen in a larger invariant fibre
and need not retain K's contextual matching. Therefore (2.12) is not an
escape path starting at \(G_{m,s}\).

## 3. Audit of the one-sign all-depth TU theorem

For one fresh \(\rho\)-overlay, let \(C\) range over its complete ownership
components and let \(p=\{S,\rho S\}\) range jointly over all moved pairs at
all depths. Put

\[
z_{pC}=a_{pC}(S)-a_{pC}(\rho S),
\qquad Z=(z_{pC}).
\tag{3.1}
\]

K8 assumes simultaneously:

1. the fixed-target condition (1.1);
2. the moved-pair condition (1.2);
3. \(z_{pC}\in\{-1,0,1\}\);
4. after one joint row deletion
   \(\mathcal R=\bigsqcup_q\mathcal R_q\), the full vertically concatenated
   matrix \(Z'\) is totally unimodular.

### Theorem 3.1 (joint TU signing, audited)

Under these four hypotheses there is one common whole-component signing such
that

\[
\boxed{
O_q\le(c_q+1)|\mathcal R_q|,
\qquad
J_A\le
\sum_{q\le H}\left(1+\frac1{c_q}\right)|\mathcal R_q|
\le2|\mathcal R|.
}
\tag{3.2}
\]

#### Proof

Put \(s=Z'\mathbf1\) and consider

\[
\left\{
x\in[0,1]^{\mathscr C}:
\left\lfloor\frac{s_p}{2}\right\rfloor
\le(Z'x)_p\le
\left\lceil\frac{s_p}{2}\right\rceil
\right\}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

The half-vector is feasible. Stacking \(Z',-Z',I,-I\) preserves total
unimodularity, so the nonempty bounded polytope (3.3), with integral right
sides, has an integral vertex. For

\[
\varepsilon=\mathbf1-2x,
\tag{3.4}
\]

every retained row has signed sum \(0\) when its total is even and
\(\pm1\) when its total is odd. This remains true for negative row sums.
The exact parity identity

\[
\ell_p\equiv\sum_Cz_{pC}\pmod2
\tag{3.5}
\]

then gives balanced loads on every retained pair. A deleted pair has half
\(\ell^1\)-error at most \(c_q+1\) against the same-total quota pair in
(1.5). Fixed targets and pair totals account for all mass \(W\), so no quota
remainder remains. This proves (3.2). \(\square\)

The vertical quantifier is essential. For example, the one-row matrices

\[
(1,1)\qquad\text{and}\qquad(1,-1)
\tag{3.6}
\]

are separately TU, but their vertical concatenation has determinant \(-2\).
The first row may demand opposite component signs while the second demands
equal signs. Separate depthwise signings therefore do not imply one legal
all-depth factor.

### Proposition 3.2 (conditional factorial strengthening)

The same signed endpoint satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_q\le(c_q+1)^2|\mathcal R_q|
}
\tag{3.7}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F_A
=\sum_{q\le H}\frac{\Phi_q}{b_q}
\le4|\mathcal R|.
}
\tag{3.8}
\]

#### Proof

Use the exact integral identity

\[
2\Phi_q
=\sum_X(\mu_q(X)-c_q)(\mu_q(X)-c_q-1).
\tag{3.9}
\]

Fixed targets and retained moved pairs are quota-balanced and contribute
zero. A deleted pair has total \(2c_q,2c_q+1,\) or \(2c_q+2\). Convexity
shows that its largest possible contribution to \(\Phi_q\), attained at an
endpoint allocation, is respectively

\[
c_q^2,\qquad c_q(c_q+1),\qquad(c_q+1)^2.
\tag{3.10}
\]

This proves (3.7). Since

\[
\frac{(c_q+1)^2}{b_q}
=\frac{2(c_q+1)}{c_q}\le4,
\tag{3.11}
\]

equation (3.8) follows. \(\square\)

Proposition 3.2 strengthens the conclusion of K8's conditional theorem; it
does not establish any of its four hypotheses.

## 4. Audit of \(6^k\), the factorial floor, and the K8 noncomposition

Let

\[
\mathcal T_m=\{(2u+2\ \ 2u+3):0\le u\le m-2\}
\tag{4.1}
\]

be the native palette. Contract its coordinate blocks, which have sizes one
or two. For a set \(E\) of further transpositions, let

\[
r(E)=\sum_j(p_j-1)
\tag{4.2}
\]

where the \(j\)-th final coordinate component contains \(p_j\) native
blocks. This is the graphic rank of the added block edges, and

\[
r(E)\le|E|.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Fix depth \(q\), put \(d=m-q-2\), and let \(\Omega_q\) be K8's native private
target union. It has

\[
|\Omega_q|\le(4d+2)\operatorname{Cat}_d,
\qquad
\mu_q(\Omega_q)\ge
\operatorname{Cat}_d\operatorname{Cat}_q.
\tag{4.4}
\]

Here and throughout this section, the starting factor is a vertex of the full
intrinsic \((2\ 3)\)-component cell of the canonical MSW factor, and subsequent
states are reached by complete component switches using the displayed fixed
palette. The mass assertion in (4.4) is not claimed for an arbitrary exact
factor.

### Lemma 4.1 (graphic-rank orbit ceiling)

For a fixed enlarged palette \(\mathcal T_m\cup E\), every endpoint of such a
palette-supported adaptive history has an invariant union \(U\) with

\[
\boxed{
|U|\le
L_{q,r}:=
\min\!\left\{
N_q,\,
6^{r(E)}(4d+2)\operatorname{Cat}_d
\right\},
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\mu_q(U)\ge\operatorname{Cat}_d\operatorname{Cat}_q.
}
\tag{4.6}
\]

#### Proof

If one enlarged coordinate component contains \(p\) native blocks of sizes
\(b_i\in\{1,2\}\), a native target profile with block counts \(t_i\) expands
by the ratio

\[
\frac{\binom{\sum b_i}{\sum t_i}}
{\prod_i\binom{b_i}{t_i}}
\le\binom{2p}{p}\le6^{p-1}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

The base \(p=2\) gives six, and every subsequent central-binomial ratio is
less than four. Multiplication over final components gives
\(6^{\sum(p_j-1)}=6^{r(E)}\). Enlarged orbit overlaps only reduce the union
size. Componentwise orbit-mass invariance gives (4.6). \(\square\)

Thus \(6^k\) is a correct ceiling but need not be attained. The proof is
uniform in the fixed set \(E\), so after a realized fixed-frame adaptive
history one may take \(E\) to be the set of distinct nonnative colours that
actually appeared. Repeated use costs no further rank. A global reframing
moves \(U\) and is outside this conclusion.

### Corollary 4.2 (exact profile-cap obstruction)

Adjoin a candidate next bridge to the realized palette before computing
\(r\). If the K-profile (1.1)--(1.2) holds for that candidate at depth \(q\),
then necessarily

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cat}_d\operatorname{Cat}_q
\le c_qL_{q,r}+\min(L_{q,r},\rho_q).
}
\tag{4.8}
\]

Whenever the right side is smaller, one of the two profile hypotheses fails
before unit leakage or TU is considered.

#### Proof

The union in Lemma 4.1 is invariant under the candidate bridge. Apply Lemma
1.1. \(\square\)

At \(q=H\), below the whole-layer truncation, the cruder but explicit
necessary condition is

\[
\operatorname{Cat}_H
\le(c_H+1)(4d+2)6^r.
\tag{4.9}
\]

If (4.9) fails, the truncation is automatically inactive: with
\(x=\operatorname{Cat}_H/((4d+2)6^r)>c_H+1\), the untruncated union bound is
\(M_H/x\le W/x<W/(c_H+1)<N_H\).

Therefore profile compatibility requires

\[
\boxed{
r\ge
\max\!\left\{0,\,
\left\lceil
\log_6
\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_H}
{(c_H+1)(4d+2)}
\right\rceil
\right\}.
}
\tag{4.10}
\]

Using the Catalan asymptotic and \(H=\lceil A\sqrt m\rceil\), the right side
is

\[
\frac{\log4}{\log6}H
-\frac{7}{4\log6}\log m+O_A(1).
\tag{4.11}
\]

Here and in (4.11), unlabelled logarithms are natural.

### Proposition 4.3 (factorial constant and normalization)

Assume \(d\ge1\) and

\[
x=\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_q}{(4d+2)6^k}
\ge4(c_q+1).
\tag{4.12}
\]

Then the exact convex floor gives

\[
\Phi_q\ge
\frac{\operatorname{Cat}_d\operatorname{Cat}_q^2}
{32d\,6^k}.
\tag{4.13}
\]

At \(q=H\), this implies (0.9)--(0.10).

#### Proof

Put \(M=\operatorname{Cat}_d\operatorname{Cat}_q\). Catalan concatenation
gives \(M\le\operatorname{Cat}_{m-2}<W\), so under (4.12) the untruncated
union size obeys

\[
L=\frac Mx\le\frac{W}{4(c_q+1)}<N_q.
\tag{4.12a}
\]

Thus the truncation in (4.5) is inactive. For \(L\) bins carrying
mass at least \(M\), put

\[
a=\left\lfloor\frac ML\right\rfloor,\qquad
s=M-aL,\qquad u=a-c_q.
\tag{4.14}
\]

The exact identity (3.9) and discrete convexity give

\[
2\Phi_q\ge Lu(u-1)+2su.
\tag{4.15}
\]

Condition (4.12) gives \(u\ge3x/4\) and \(u-1\ge x/2\). Since
\(4d+2\le6d\) for \(d\ge1\), the safe bound (4.13) follows. Finally,

\[
\operatorname{Cat}_H\ge\frac{4^H}{4H^2},
\qquad
\operatorname{Cat}_{m-H-2}>
\frac B{4^{H+2}},
\tag{4.16}
\]

give the constant \(8192\). Dividing by \(b_H\) gives (0.10). \(\square\)

The threshold

\[
k\ge\frac{\log4}{\log6}H-O_A(\log m)
\tag{4.17}
\]

is necessary only for this certified fixed-frame factorial lower bound to
stop diverging. It is not a necessity statement for \(J_A=o(W)\), MWB, or
coefficient one.

### Corollary 4.4 (K8 seam and TU parts do not compose)

In K8's canonical construction, the dependent preparation uses native
colours and the fresh seam overlay adds only one nonnative colour. For all
sufficiently large \(m\), condition (4.8) fails at \(q=H\). Hence one of the
profile hypotheses (1.1)--(1.2) fails for that fresh overlay, independently
of its leakage matrix.

#### Proof

Take the realized palette together with the fresh seam colour. Its new
graphic rank is at most one. The right side of (4.9) is polynomial in \(m\),
whereas \(\operatorname{Cat}_H\) grows as
\(4^H/(\sqrt\pi H^{3/2})\). Thus (4.9) fails. \(\square\)

This does not say that the seam overlay lacks useful leakage. It says only
that K8's exact floor-compatible TU theorem cannot be invoked on that
construction.

## 5. Interaction with the AB8 adaptive cage

Fix the AB8 colour \(s\), and put

\[
L_s=\operatorname{Cat}_{m-s-H-2}.
\tag{5.1}
\]

The cage factor \(G_{m,s}\) and every endpoint of an AB8 menu-only history
have \(L_s\) pairwise target-disjoint protected pairs, each of total at least
\(\operatorname{Cat}_H\). Let \(P\) be the union of their \(2L_s\) targets.
Then

\[
|P|=2L_s,\qquad
\mu_H(P)\ge L_s\operatorname{Cat}_H.
\tag{5.2}
\]

### Theorem 5.1 (no direct near-TU escape from the protected menu)

For all sufficiently large \(m\), after any finite AB8 menu-only history:

1. no candidate bridge in \(\mathcal M_s\) satisfies the depth-\(H\)
   K-profile;
2. no arbitrary candidate bridge outside \(\mathcal M_s\) satisfies that
   profile either.

#### Proof

If the candidate lies in \(\mathcal M_s\), it fixes the protected targets
individually or swaps the two members of every protected pair. Thus \(P\) is
candidate-invariant. Lemma 1.1 and (5.2) would force

\[
L_s\operatorname{Cat}_H
\le c_H(2L_s)+\min(2L_s,\rho_H)
\le2L_s(c_H+1).
\tag{5.3}
\]

This is impossible once
\(\operatorname{Cat}_H>2(c_H+1)\).

For an arbitrary outside candidate \(\sigma\), put

\[
U=P\cup\sigma P.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Then \(U\) is \(\sigma\)-invariant,
\(|U|\le4L_s\), and it carries at least the mass in (5.2). Lemma 1.1 would
give

\[
\operatorname{Cat}_H\le4(c_H+1),
\tag{5.5}
\]

again impossible for large \(m\). \(\square\)

Theorem 5.1 is stronger than saying that menu bridges preserve the pile: K8's
profile/TU theorem cannot even be used as the first outside-menu escape step.
It says nothing about an outside bridge governed by a different discrepancy
theorem.

### Theorem 5.2 (preparation-depth obstruction for every candidate bridge)

Start at \(G_{m,s}\), perform any \(D\) arbitrary complete freshly recomputed
transposition-component cuts, and then choose any candidate bridge \(\sigma\).
Let

\[
L_D^*=\min\{N_H,\,2^{D+2}L_s\}.
\tag{5.6}
\]

There is a \(\sigma\)-invariant target union \(U_D\) with

\[
|U_D|\le L_D^*,\qquad
\mu_H(U_D)\ge L_s\operatorname{Cat}_H.
\tag{5.7}
\]

Every endpoint \(F'\) in the freshly recomputed complete \(\sigma\)-cell
satisfies the exact overload floor

\[
\boxed{
O_H(F')\ge
\left[
L_s\operatorname{Cat}_H
-c_HL_D^*
-\min(L_D^*,\rho_H)
\right]_+.
}
\tag{5.8}
\]

Consequently, K8's depth-\(H\) profile hypotheses are impossible whenever

\[
\operatorname{Cat}_H>2^{D+2}(c_H+1).
\tag{5.9}
\]

#### Proof

Select exactly \(\operatorname{Cat}_H\) literal occurrence tokens from every
protected pair at the start. The \(L_s\) token groups are disjoint. After
\(D\) cuts, rowwise identity/transposition transport puts their support in a
set \(P_D\) of size at most

\[
|P_D|\le2^{D+1}L_s.
\tag{5.10}
\]

It still carries all \(L_s\operatorname{Cat}_H\) selected tokens. Set

\[
U_D=P_D\cup\sigma P_D.
\tag{5.11}
\]

This is \(\sigma\)-invariant and has the stated size. Its total load is
preserved by every complete cut in the current \(\sigma\)-cell.

Every balanced quota vector puts at most
\(c_H|U_D|+\min(|U_D|,\rho_H)\) mass on \(U_D\). Equal total mass makes
overload at least the excess on this union, proving (5.8). If the K-profile
held, Lemma 1.1 would make the bracket nonpositive. The simpler upper bound

\[
c_HL_D^*+\min(L_D^*,\rho_H)
\le(c_H+1)2^{D+2}L_s
\tag{5.12}
\]

proves (5.9). \(\square\)

Solving (5.9) gives the necessary preparation depth (0.12). Using

=\frac{4^H}{\sqrt\pi H^{3/2}}(1+o(1))
\tag{5.13}
\]

and the fixed-\(A\) bound \(c_H=O_A(1)\) gives its displayed asymptotic.
The subsequent TU component signing would be one further counted cut.

### Proposition 5.3 (exact protected factorial charge)

For all sufficiently large \(m\), so that
\(\operatorname{Cat}_H\ge2c_H+2\), the following holds.

Put

\[
\psi(t)=\left\lfloor\frac{(t-1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
h_c(t)=\psi(t)-ct+c(c+1).
\tag{5.14}
\]

Every menu-only endpoint satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_H\ge
L_s h_{c_H}(\operatorname{Cat}_H).
}
\tag{5.15}
\]

#### Proof

For one protected pair of total \(t\), the minimum of its contribution to
\(\Phi_H\), using the exact identity (3.9), is

\[
\frac12\min_{x+y=t}
\left[
(x-c_H)(x-c_H-1)
+(y-c_H)(y-c_H-1)
\right]
=h_{c_H}(t).
\tag{5.16}
\]

For \(t\ge2c_H+2\), this is nondecreasing. The protected pairs are
target-disjoint, every other summand in (3.9) is nonnegative, and every pair
total is at least \(\operatorname{Cat}_H\). This proves (5.15). \(\square\)

Thus deleting a protected row from a putative leakage matrix does not make
its bad pair total floor-compatible. The cardinal penalty
\((c_H+1)|\mathcal R_H|\) in K8 is valid only because K8 separately assumes
that every deleted pair still has total in
\(\{2c_H,2c_H+1,2c_H+2\}\).

## 6. What the interaction proves and does not prove

The exact picture is now:

1. K's dependent preparation creates the claimed positive-density seam supply,
   and the leading constant \(11/288\) survives exclusion of any
   \(O_A(m)\)-sized forbidden menu.

2. This gives a bridge label outside the AB8 menu on the canonical packet
   construction. It does not transfer the canonical contextual packet
   matching to the AB8 minimizer.

3. K8's joint TU rounding is a correct integral theorem. It uses one common
   sign across all depths and even conditionally controls
   \(\mathfrak F_A\) by \(4|\mathcal R|\).

4. The K8 seam construction does not satisfy the profile gate needed by that
   TU theorem. A one-new-colour palette is far below the exact profile-cap
   threshold (4.10).

5. At the AB8 minimizer, K8's profile gate cannot be the first outside-menu
   move. Before it can become possible for any candidate bridge, at least the
   preparation depth in (0.12) is necessary.

6. None of these statements rules out a robust approximate-profile TU theorem.
   The certified trapped **linear** mass is small. In K8's native pile,

   \[
   \frac{\operatorname{Cat}_{m-H-2}\operatorname{Cat}_H}{W}
   =\Theta_A(m^{-7/4})=o(1),
   \tag{6.1}
   \]

   and the shifted AB8 pile is smaller by the Catalan factor associated with
   \(s\). Thus an additional theorem which pays the actual profile-defect mass,
   rather than only the number of deleted rows, might still prove
   \(J_A=o(W)\). No such theorem is proved here.

7. Divergent factorial collision and sublinear balanced overload are
   compatible. Therefore the palette and AB8 factorial obstructions are not
   counterexamples to MWB or coefficient one.

The precise remaining K/AB composition gate is:

> Starting from every exact factor, after paying all preparatory cuts, find an
> outside-menu fresh overlay whose fixed-target and pair-total profile defect,
> unit-leakage defect, and joint all-depth TU deletion cost have total
> **weighted** contribution \(o(W)\), and realize the resulting one common
> component signing inside that exact factor.

Every clause after “whose” is unproved. In particular, near-TU alone is not a
theorem, seam abundance alone is not a profile theorem, and label-level menu
escape alone is not productive escape.
