## 0. Verdict

The mixed coatom-screen tensor genuinely evades the two earlier **local
geometry** obstructions.

* It is not an arbitrary retained detour: the coatom blocks and typed
  intersection/union screens give pointwise prefix/suffix OR equality,
  complete internal OR-support equality, positive residence, exact owner
  current, a simple Johnson path, and exact old/new immediate palettes.
  Thus the fourteen-owner `0/784` retained-detour certificate does not apply.
* Its two free active labels `c,delta` are absent from both endpoints.  After
  freezing all other labels, a fixed endpoint pair therefore supports

  \[
                            q(q-1)                              \tag{0.1}
  \]

  labelled packets, where `q=|Omega-(L union R)|`.  On the central ground
  set `|Omega|=2r`, this is `(r-2)(r-3)=Omega(r^2)`.  Hence the endpoint
  leakage which killed the eight-owner padded macro is absent.

There is nevertheless a sharp quantifier boundary.  The `q(q-1)` packets
have `q(q-1)` different **old words**.  Once one literal incumbent fragment
is fixed, it reconstructs every tensor label and hence its new phase; the
candidate fibre is exactly one.  Fixing merely the total OR already reduces
the canonical fibre to two, corresponding to the order of `{c,delta}`.

Thus the theorem supplies an exact quadratic **prospective/planted
fixed-boundary atlas**, not a post-hoc quadratic menu for repairing one
frozen chronology.  This is precisely the task/menu embedding gate in the
authoritative theorem.

The canonical quadratic atlas also has an exact favourable variable-token
row: after declaring its common anchor skeleton private, every noncommon
owner, lower/upper seam, or internal-deck token occurs in at most
`2(q-1)` options.  But common skeleton tokens occur in all `q(q-1)` options,
and no compiler deletion token has been constructed.  Cross-anchor privacy,
U5, and regeneration therefore remain indispensable.

## 2. Complete relabelling counts

Let `M=|Omega|`, `k=r-d-4`, and retain one fixed connector row and the fixed
screen schedule (1.2).  Write `(x)_j=x(x-1)...(x-j+1)`.

### Proposition 2.1 (global labelled atlas)

The number of labelled tensors is exactly

\[
              \binom{M}{k}(M-k)_6(M-k-6)_{d+2}.                 \tag{2.1}
\]

This is the untyped count in which `infinity` is one of the six freely
relabelled active roles.  If `infinity` is a globally distinguished fixed
coordinate, the exact count is instead

\[
          \binom{M-1}{k}(M-1-k)_5(M-1-k-5)_{d+2}.               \tag{2.1a}
\]

#### Proof

Choose the unordered core `K`, the ordered images of the six active roles,
and the ordered filler roles.  These choices are disjoint and determine the
two words.  Conversely a literal old word recovers the data, as proved in
Theorem 2.4 below, so no two choices are counted twice.  \(\square\)

The other two authenticated connector rows are cyclic relabellings, not a
factor of three over a fixed old word.

Let `L,R` be compatible ordered rank-`r` endpoints.  Necessarily
`|L intersection R|=r-2`, and put

\[
 I=L\cap R,\quad A=L-R,\quad B=R-L,\quad
 O=\Omega-(L\cup R),\quad q=|O|=M-r-2.                         \tag{2.2}
\]

### Proposition 2.2 (full endpoint fibre)

If all six active roles, including `infinity`, are relabelled, the exact
directed endpoint fibre is

\[
                  4(r-2)_2(r-4)_d(q)_2.                         \tag{2.3}
\]

If `infinity` is a globally distinguished coordinate already specified as
one element of `A`, the factor `4` becomes `2`.

#### Proof

The two elements of `A` are `infinity,f_(n-1)` and the two elements of `B`
are `e,f_0`, giving two orders on each shore.  Choose ordered `a,b` in `I`,
then ordered `f_1,...,f_(n-2)` from the remaining `r-4` elements of `I`;
the rest of `I` is `K`.  Finally choose ordered `c,delta` in `O`.  This gives
(2.3).  A distinguished `infinity` removes the first shore factor two.
\(\square\)

In particular, for `M=2r` and distinguished `infinity`, (2.3) is

\[
                         2(r-2)_2^2(r-4)_d.                      \tag{2.4}
\]

This large number is not yet a useful list because most choices change the
old word, its total OR, and its internal anchor profile.

### Proposition 2.3 (exact interface-fibre lattice)

For a fixed compatible endpoint pair with distinguished `infinity`:

1. endpoints together with the total OR and complete clipped boundary
   profile leave exactly
   eight directed parametrizations (`a/b`, `e/f_0`, and `c/delta`);
2. endpoints together with the owner/internal-support set (without fixing
   the boundary profile) leave `2d!` parametrizations;
3. endpoints together with the owner support and clipped boundary profile
   leave the two opposite `a/b` phases; and
4. fixing the literal ordered old word leaves exactly one directed option.

These are four separate faces of the interface lattice, not a cumulative
chain.  The first and fourth statements are the decisive menu bounds.  The
middle two record the exact loss of filler-order information under
support-only interfaces.

#### Proof

The total OR is

\[
                         L\cup R\cup\{c,delta\}.                 \tag{2.5}
\]

so it fixes the unordered outside pair and leaves its two orders.  The
clipped boundary profile identifies `K` and every middle filler `f_i`; it
does not distinguish `a/b` or `e/f_0`, giving `2^3=8` choices.

The unordered owner support determines the active/filler partition and the
two endpoint fillers, while the `d` middle fillers may be permuted and
`a,b` interchanged, giving `2d!`.  The boundary profile orders the middle
fillers; together with owner support it leaves only the `a/b` interchange.
Finally the ordered first coatom block recovers the ordered filler
sequence and the successive block intersections recover every active
triple, proving uniqueness of the directed old-word lift.  \(\square\)

## 3. A genuine quadratic planted fixed-boundary subatlas

Fix `K`, the ordered filler set, and the four endpoint-active roles
`e,infinity,a,b`.  Only `c,delta` vary over distinct elements of `O`.

### Theorem 3.1 (quadratic planted boundary menu)

This canonical subatlas has exactly

\[
                              q(q-1)                             \tag{3.1}
\]

packets.  They all have the same endpoints and the same complete clipped
boundary-residence state, and every packet satisfies the local U1--U4 rows
of Section 1.  They have `q(q-1)` distinct old words.  For each fixed total
OR there are exactly two packets, and for each fixed old word exactly one.

For `M=2r`, `q=r-2`, so (3.1) is `(r-2)(r-3)=Omega(r^2)`.

#### Proof

The active labels `c,delta` occur in neither endpoint.  Every ordered
distinct pair therefore gives the same endpoints and boundary record, while
the local tensor theorem is equivariant under relabelling.  The old word
contains blocks labelled by both roles, so it recovers their ordered pair;
the words are distinct.  Equation (2.5) depends only on the unordered pair,
giving fibre two at fixed total OR.  \(\square\)

Theorem 3.1 is the precise sense in which the tensor evades the earlier
fixed-boundary leakage no-go.  It is a menu only when the construction may
plant the candidate-dependent off phase jointly with the on phase.  It does
not repair one already frozen off fragment.

## 5. What remains for `B(k)+O(1)`

The coatom tensor closes the local retained-path problem and supplies a real
quadratic planted boundary fibre.  It does not close the recursion.

1. **Task embedding.**  A prospective factor construction must choose the
   off phase jointly.  A post-hoc replacement of one fixed fragment has list
   size one.
2. **Cross-anchor dispersion.**  The common skeleton tokens in Section 4
   must be private or assigned by a spread theorem; per-atlas exposure does
   not imply aggregate exposure.
3. **Compiler U5.**  No option names a deletion cell in one fixed unused
   compiler basis.  On the complete flat maximal-envelope bank, however,
   the q1-valid patterns `0110,1011` now have an exact incidence-sensitive
   footprint `8d^2+24d`; pattern `1011` has the shortest one-prefix Ferrers
   majorant, `12d+33` starts in every row.  This is a quadratic compiler
   exposure, not part of the `16d+64` physical-token count in Section 4.
   Complete trace guards and the Hall row remain uncertified.
4. **Regeneration.**  No Pascal lift is proved to recreate the same prepared
   tensor interface with bounded carried state.

The token-exposure theorem is needed by a simultaneous planted-selector
route.  In the weaker serial safe-move architecture, different moves may
reuse supports at different times and only the final carrier is compiled;
then cross-list Haxell is unnecessary.  The fixed-old-word fibre-one theorem
still matters: at one current slot there is no hidden relabelling menu, so
serial progress must come from many exposed slots or from chronology changes,
not from rechoosing `(c,delta)` inside that same old fragment.

The exact compiler condition remains

\[
 \delta_H(D(\rho^*)\cup D_{mac})=0,                            \tag{5.1}
\]

or an absolute deficiency with a literal bounded repair.  Scalar unused
capacity is insufficient.

For the stronger same-matching face, placing pattern `1011` at an endpoint
and reserving

\[
                  \widehat\rho_h=\max\{\rho_h^*,12d+33\}        \tag{5.1a}
\]

is sufficient provided every selected trace guard outside the incidence
footprint is phase invariant and
`delta_H(D(widehat rho))=0`.  The `O(d^2)` reserve is scalar-feasible for a
bounded number of packets at `B+1`; this is not a Hall or guard proof.  The
exact non-wasteful test minimizes the deficiency of the edgewise common
bank `H^0_(a,eta) intersection H^1_(a,eta)` over placement and screen
pattern.

For the current serialized tensor artifact this row is fail-closed, not
merely unproved: its certified packet-to-cell list is empty.  The augmented
compiler graph therefore has an isolated packet task, so the presently
certified U1--U5 menu has size zero.  This is an artifact/interface
obstruction only; it is not a no-go against a future embedding which
supplies a literal cell list or uses the serial final-compiler architecture.

Therefore the strongest current conclusion is:

\[
 \boxed{\text{an exact }\Omega(r^2)\text{ planted boundary atlas exists,}
 \quad\text{but no }\Omega(r^2)\text{ fixed-old-word/U5 menu is proved}.}
                                                                    \tag{5.2}
\]
