# One-aperture Pascal chains and a literal one-cell `B+1` pivot bridge

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** unconditional local source/port composition, sharp correction
to the proposed automatic repayment, and conditional `B+1` bridge.  One
monotone source insertion literally realizes the persistent
rank-defect-one Pascal anchor, but its opposite fan does **not** realize the
central colour lost at the aperture transition.  The construction carries
exactly one debt under same-parity rank/depth suspension.  Paying it once
requires an exterior occurrence and alignment of that transition with the
global open edge (or an independently budgeted second boundary socket).
Completion of this protected state inside a global Pascal owner path,
one-star consolidation, upper completeness, residence, exterior
chronology, and the terminal common-cap/compiler remain hypotheses.

## 0. Outcome

The rank aperture from
`MATH_THEOREM_K_PASCAL_PORT_RANK_APERTURE_AND_ONE_CREDIT_REGENERATIVE_CHAIN_20260802.md`
is a literal one-cell-pivot object.

The identification is not merely numerical.  If the inserted source
letter is `X` and the next `d-1` source letters form the common ordered rail
`w`, then the longest new right fan is the predecessor head

\[
                         p_*=(X,w),
\tag{0.1}
\]

whose union is the aperture support `Q_*`.  The longest new left fan can
simultaneously be one omitted central lower colour `N`.

There is a sharp warning.  If `Q_* subset N`, then every source letter on
both fans lies in `N`.  No crossing window can have rank `r`, so a flat
rank-`r` pivot is impossible.  The compensating colour must be nonnested.

For every

\[
                         d\ge2,\qquad r\ge d+2,
\tag{0.2}
\]

an explicit nonnested choice exists.  It has

* a right full fan `Q_*` of rank `r-2`;
* a left full fan `N` of rank `r-1`;
* `d+1` pairwise-distinct rank-`r` owner windows forming a Johnson path;
* pairwise-distinct immediate lower and upper transition colours;
* exact monotone transport of every old interval-OR occurrence; and
* one literal common source word for the two fans and the owner path.

The same right fan is the persistent private Pascal predecessor.  Two
child closing roles can be crossed while retaining it.  The left fan is a
central exact-width occurrence, but it is **not** the colour displaced by
the terminal aperture.  The latter is

\[
                         J=Q_*\cup\{\alpha\}.
\tag{0.3}
\]

No interval in the displayed pivot block realizes `J`.  Thus the literal
automatic-repayment conjecture is false.  A corrected one-credit bridge
uses one exterior protected occurrence of `J` and makes the aperture
transition the global opened edge, so that `J` is also the unique
Hamilton-path boundary colour.

The exact global consequence is conditional.  If an owner/target-exact
Pascal scaffold embeds this protected block, supplies the exterior `J`
socket, opens the corresponding transition, consolidates the two child
stars into one parent star, and admits the displayed source letters in one
common cap, then one insertion gives a lower owner/head/topology interface
at length `B+1`.  The anchor state survives every later Pascal splice; the
local debt count stays one rather than growing with the recursion depth.

## 1. The literal asymmetric pivot

Choose pairwise-disjoint objects

\[
 X,\quad \alpha,\ell,\mu,\quad
 \lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{d-1},\quad
 \rho_1,\ldots,\rho_{d-1},
\tag{1.1}
\]

where the Greek letters outside `X` are singleton coordinates and

\[
                         |X|=r-d-1.
\tag{1.2}
\]

The ground-set demand is `r+d`, hence it fits in `[2r-1]` under (0.2).

Before insertion, place the source letters

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{position}&\text{letter}\\ \hline
-d&\{\ell\}\\
-i&\{\lambda_i\}\quad(2\le i\le d-1)\\
-1&X\cup\{\alpha,\lambda_1\}\\ \hline
 i&\{\rho_i\}\quad(1\le i\le d-1)\\
 d&\{\alpha,\mu\}.
\end{array}
\tag{1.3}
\]

Insert the single nonempty letter

\[
                         A_0=X
\tag{1.4}
\]

at the displayed cut.  Empty index ranges below are omitted.

### Theorem 1.1 (flat owner path)

The `d+1` consecutive length-`(d+1)` windows containing the inserted
position have unions

\[
\begin{aligned}
 M_0&=X\cup\{\alpha,\ell\}
          \cup\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{d-1}\},\\
 M_j&=X\cup\{\alpha\}
          \cup\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{d-j}\}
          \cup\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_j\},
                         &&1\le j\le d-1,\\
 M_d&=X\cup\{\alpha,\mu\}
          \cup\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_{d-1}\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.5}
\]

They are pairwise-distinct rank-`r` sets and form a simple Johnson path.
More precisely,

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{step}&\text{deleted}&\text{inserted}\\ \hline
0\to1&\ell&\rho_1\\
j\to j+1&\lambda_{d-j}&\rho_{j+1}
                     \quad(1\le j\le d-2)\\
d-1\to d&\lambda_1&\mu.
\end{array}
\tag{1.6}
\]

The set-theoretic Johnson lower colours `M_j cap M_(j+1)` are pairwise
distinct, as are the immediate upper colours `M_j union M_(j+1)`.  The
literal shared-source row agrees with those lower colours except at the
final aperture transition, audited in Section 2.

#### Proof

Formula (1.5) is the direct union of `d-j` left letters, the star, and `j`
right letters.  Each row contains `X`, `alpha`, and exactly `d` further
singleton coordinates, so (1.2) gives rank `r`.  Consecutive formulas give
the swaps in (1.6); all displayed moving labels are distinct.  Hence the
owners are distinct and Johnson-adjacent.  A transition colour records the
deleted or inserted label of its step, so the two colour families are
separately injective. \(\square\)

### Theorem 1.2 (temporal preimage and all-width transparency)

Before insertion, the `d` crossing length-`(d+1)` windows have values

\[
                         U_j=M_{j-1}\cup M_j,
                         \qquad1\le j\le d,
\tag{1.7}
\]

and rank `r+1`.  The `d-1` old crossing width-`d` cells have values
`M_1,...,M_(d-1)` and rank `r`.

Moreover every old interval-OR occurrence transports to the enlarged word
with exactly the same value.

#### Proof

The old crossing window at transition `j` contains the union of the two
new windows adjacent to the inserted position.  Equation (1.6) shows that
this union has rank `r+1`.  A crossing width-`d` cell omits the star but
contains `A_(-1)`, and therefore still contains `X`; its value is the
corresponding internal row of (1.5).

Finally

\[
                         X\subseteq A_{-1}\cup A_1.
\tag{1.8}
\]

The exact monotone-insertion theorem now transports every old interval
unchanged: a crossing interval already contains `X`, and a one-sided
interval is untouched. \(\square\)

Thus the block has the required nonflat pre-insertion chronology.  It is
not an insertion into an already-flat owner row.

## 2. The two fans are exactly the correction pair

The two longest new fan cells of width at most `d` are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 N&=X\cup\{\alpha,\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{d-1}\},\\
 Q_*&=X\cup\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_{d-1}\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.1}
\]

Therefore

\[
                         |N|=r-1,\qquad |Q_*|=r-2.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The identities are physical:

\[
 N=\bigcup_{i=-(d-1)}^0 A_i=M_0\cap M_1,
 \qquad
 Q_*=\bigcup_{i=0}^{d-1}A_i.
\tag{2.3}
\]

At the other end there is a strict distinction between set intersection
and literal shared-window union:

\[
 M_{d-1}\cap M_d=J:=Q_*\cup\{\alpha\},
 \qquad
 \bigcup_{i=0}^{d-1}A_i=Q_*.
\tag{2.3a}
\]

For the first `d-1` owner transitions the shared `d`-cell equals the set
intersection.  At the final transition it is the deficient aperture
`Q_*`, one rank below the required central colour `J`.

### Lemma 2.1 (no local occurrence of the displaced colour)

No interval of the source block (1.3)--(1.4) has union `J`.

#### Proof

The only source letters containing `alpha` are

\[
 A_{-1}=X\cup\{\alpha,\lambda_1\},
 \qquad A_d=\{\alpha,\mu\}.
\]

An interval using the first also gains the unwanted `lambda_1`; an
interval using the second gains the unwanted `mu`.  Neither belongs to
`J`, so no interval realizes it. \(\square\)

The right occurrence in (2.3) has the ordered head

\[
            p_*=(X,\{\rho_1\},\ldots,\{\rho_{d-1}\}).
\tag{2.4}
\]

It is exactly a rank-defect-one Pascal predecessor token on the common
rail

\[
                         w=(\{\rho_1\},\ldots,
                                  \{\rho_{d-1}\}).
\tag{2.5}
\]

The opposite fan `N` is already the first native lower-q1 colour of the
owner path.  No equality of set values is being used to identify two
different physical addresses: (2.3) is the standard shared-window identity.
Likewise the right interval in (2.3) is the exported token occurrence
`p_*`.  But (2.3a) proves that `N` does not repay `J`.

There is also no new unrestricted compiler coverage in `N`: before
insertion the old one-sided interval `[-(d-1),-1]` already has value `N`.
Insertion promotes it to an exact-width-`d` head occurrence; it does not
create its first occurrence in the interval deck.

### Proposition 2.2 (nested repayment is impossible)

Suppose instead that a monotone one-cell bridge has right full fan `Q_*`
of rank `r-2` and left full fan `N` of rank `r-1`, with `Q_* subset N`.
Then none of the `d-1` internal crossing owner windows can have rank `r`.

#### Proof

Every source coordinate in the nearest `d-1` positions on either side
belongs to its full fan.  The star also belongs to both.  Hence every
internal window through the star, using between one and `d-1` positions
from each side, is contained in `Q_* union N=N`, of rank `r-1`.
The two endpoint windows may use the farther exterior letters, but they do
not repair these `d-1` internal failures. \(\square\)

This is the literal mismatch in the most tempting identification
`N=Q_*+a`.  The construction (1.3) avoids it: `N cap Q_*=X`, and
`|N union Q_*|=r+d-2`, leaving enough moving coordinates for the Johnson
path.

## 3. Two-child private Pascal splice on the same block

Fix `x_0 in X`.  Let

\[
                         R=\{\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_{d-1}\}.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Choose one further coordinate `nu` outside every object in (1.1), and put

\[
                         a_0=\ell,\qquad a_1=\alpha,
                         \qquad a_2=\nu.
\tag{3.2}
\]

The complete two-child bank now uses `r+d+1<=2r-1` coordinates, exactly
under the standing hypothesis `r>=d+2`.

Besides the anchor predecessor (2.4), define one ordinary predecessor with
the same rail and leading block

\[
                         c_1=X\cup\{\alpha\}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Its support is

\[
                         R\cup c_1=J.
\tag{3.3a}
\]

This predecessor is an exterior protected occurrence; it is not a
contiguous interval of the pivot block, consistently with Lemma 2.1.

Define two closing roles `g_0,g_1` with common rail `w`, terminal blocks

\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_0&=(X-\{x_0\})\cup\{\ell,\alpha\},\\
 D_1&=(X-\{x_0\})\cup\{\alpha,\nu\},
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.4}
\]

and owners

\[
\begin{aligned}
 T_0&=X\cup R\cup\{\ell,\alpha\},\\
 T_1&=X\cup R\cup\{\alpha,\nu\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.5}
\]

Both lower cap endpoints are `{x_0}`.

### Theorem 3.1 (literal two-child crossing)

The old assignments `p_* -> g_0` and `p_1 -> g_1` and both crossed
assignments are literal.  Crossing them and freezing `g_0` produces a
private-rooted parent whose exported port is again `p_* -> g_1`.

All displayed rank-`r` owners and rank-`(r-1)` q1 supports are distinct.
The only noncentral predecessor support is `Q_*`.  The ordinary child
predecessor supplies exactly the displaced terminal colour `J`, while the
pivot occurrence `N` supplies a distinct central exact-width value.

#### Proof

From (3.4)--(3.5),

\[
 T_i\setminus(R\cup D_i)=\{x_0\}.
\tag{3.6}
\]

Both `X` and `X+alpha` contain this mandatory point.  Further,

\[
 X\cup\{\alpha\}\subseteq T_0,
 \qquad X\subseteq T_1.
\tag{3.7}
\]

These are exactly the two crossed cap tests.  The
freeze-after-rectangle theorem gives the claimed parent and retains the
same anchor occurrence.

The port-side central supports are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_1&=X\cup R\cup\{\alpha\}=J,\\
 V_0&=(X-\{x_0\})\cup R\cup\{\ell,\alpha\},\\
 V_1&=(X-\{x_0\})\cup R\cup\{\alpha,\nu\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.8}
\]

They all have rank `r-1`.  The signatures `x_0`, `ell`, and `nu`
separate them.  Among the pivot set intersections, only the final one is
`J=Q_1`; it deliberately uses the exterior occurrence (3.3a).  Every other
pivot lower colour has a nonempty lambda signature or lacks the complete
rho bank, so it differs from (3.8).  The owners in (3.5) are separated from
the owners (1.5) by the fresh `nu` and the `R`/lambda/`ell` signatures.
Finally `Q_*` has rank `r-2`, while `N` has rank `r-1`. \(\square\)

The same formula extends to an anchored chain: choose further distinct
`a_j`, put

\[
 c_j=X\cup\{a_j\},\quad
 D_j=(X-\{x_0\})\cup\{a_j,a_{j+1}\},\quad
 T_j=X\cup R\cup\{a_j,a_{j+1}\},
\tag{3.9}
\]

and retain `p_*` after every splice.  The two-child construction above is
the coordinate-economical base case; a longer chain additionally requires
the displayed fresh labels or a collision-audited reuse schedule.

## 4. Exact lower matching and palette ledger

Let the pre-insertion scaffold have a literal strict-lower matching
`M_old`.  The `d-1` crossing width-`d` cells expelled from the band have
rank `r` by Theorem 1.2, so no edge of `M_old` uses them.  Every selected
old edge therefore transports unless it is deliberately replaced by a new
fan occurrence.

For a set `U` of already-covered logical targets whose fan occurrences are
to become the selected representatives, the exact replacement is

\[
 \Phi\bigl(M_{\rm old}-M_{\rm old}(U)\bigr)
   \mathbin{\dot\cup}
 \{S\longmapsto f_S:S\in U\},
\tag{4.1}
\]

where `Phi` is monotone transport and the `f_S` are distinct new fan cells.

### Theorem 4.1 (compiler neutrality and exact-width promotion)

Formula (4.1) is a literal matching of the same logical target bank as
`M_old`.  In particular:

* release the old `Q_*` edge exactly when `[0,d-1]` is chosen as its new
  representative;
* release the old `N` edge exactly when `[-(d-1),0]` replaces it; and
* leave every other transported edge unchanged unless its target is
  assigned to another fan.

The insertion does not newly cover `N` in the unrestricted compiler,
because `[-(d-1),-1]` already had value `N`.  Its genuine gain is the
exact-width-`d` occurrence (2.3), which is the first native q1 head of the
new owner path.  The right fan is the exact-width deficient anchor `Q_*`.
The displaced central colour `J` is supplied only by the exterior child
token (3.3a) or another ambient occurrence.

The displayed post-insertion source is one common-word witness for every
transported edge and every selected fan edge in (4.1), provided all its
letters pass their final envelopes.

#### Proof

The rank argument above proves that every old selected edge transports.
New fan cells are precisely the cells outside the transport image, so
releasing one old representative for each selected equal-valued fan gives
the disjoint union (4.1).  Equations (2.3) give the two full-fan values.
The old occurrence of `N` and Lemma 2.1 prove respectively the promotion
and exterior-`J` assertions.  All chosen edges occur in the one displayed
word, proving the local common-word statement. \(\square\)

Thus the pivot is compiler-neutral and head-palette-positive.  It is not by
itself a one-unit improvement of unrestricted lower-target Hall deficiency.

## 5. Why the credit does not accumulate

There are two elementary suspensions of the local state.

### Proposition 5.1 (fixed-depth rank suspension)

If `r` rises to `r+1` while `d` is fixed, add one fresh coordinate `z` to
`X` and hence to every displayed source letter, support, cap, and owner
which contains `X`.  Leave the ordered rail and moving labels unchanged.
All ranks in (1.5), (2.2), and (3.5)--(3.8) rise by one.  The aperture
still has defect one, every port containment and pivot identity persists,
and there is still exactly one inserted source position.

### Proposition 5.2 (one-step depth suspension)

If `(r,d)` rises to `(r+1,d+1)`, keep `X` fixed and add one fresh lambda
and one fresh rho coordinate to the two ordered moving banks.  Append the
rho block to the common rail and use the corresponding lambda block on the
opposite fan.  The formulas (1.3)--(3.9), with indices raised by one, again
hold.  All owner and central q1 ranks rise by one, `Q_*` remains one below
the central q1 rank, and the same star occurrence is the unique correction
position.

Consequently, along any same-parity depth schedule with increment zero or
one at each rank step, the local port/pivot state regenerates with

\[
                         \text{protected aperture-debt count}=1.
\tag{5.1}
\]

### Theorem 5.3 (exact one-in/one-out debt relay)

Let an incoming carried central value be

\[
              \widehat H\in\binom{[2r-1]}{r-1}.
\]

Partition

\[
 \widehat H=B_0\mathbin{\dot\cup}\Lambda,
 \qquad |B_0|=r-d,\qquad |\Lambda|=d-1,
\tag{5.2}
\]

choose `alpha in B_0`, and put `X=B_0-{alpha}`.  Choose the `d+1`
coordinates `ell,rho_1,...,rho_(d-1),mu` outside `widehat H`.  Then the
construction of Section 1 has

\[
                         N=\widehat H,
\tag{5.3}
\]

and exports

\[
 Q'=X\cup\rho[1,d-1],
 \qquad J'=Q'\cup\{\alpha\}.
\tag{5.4}
\]

Thus one incoming exact-width central obligation is realized and exactly
one new native shared-window obligation is exposed.  If `J'` is designated
as the next carried value, the recurrence is

\[
                         \eta_{\rm next}=\eta_{\rm old}=1.
\tag{5.5}
\]

If an ambient child token supplies `J'` as in (3.3a), the local head row is
closed instead.  The construction is available for every `r>=d+2`, since
the complement of `widehat H` has `r` coordinates and only `d+1` are used.

#### Proof

Equations (5.2) give `X+alpha+Lambda=widehat H`, which is precisely the
left full fan `N`.  The right fan and its missing intersection are (5.4)
by (2.1) and (2.3a).  No other shared-window defect occurs by the direct
audit in Section 2. \(\square\)

The statements in this section concern the protected local state.  They do
not by themselves prove a one-position word-length recurrence through a
binary tree: naïvely splicing two physical `B+1` children retains both star
positions.  A parent construction must rebuild the local source with one
star, or explicitly consolidate one active aperture child with one
ordinary central child.  That consolidation is included as a hypothesis
in the next section.

### Proposition 5.4 (one-versus-two boundary obligations)

Let a global Hamilton owner path use all `W` owners.  It has `W-1`
internal transitions and hence one lower endpoint colour `E` not supplied
as an internal owner intersection.  If the aperture transition
`M_(d-1)M_d` remains internal, then its unrealized colour `J` is internal,
so

\[
                         J\ne E;
\tag{5.6}
\]

two exterior q1 obligations remain.  If that transition is the actual
opened edge, it may be chosen so that `J=E`; one exterior occurrence of
`J` then discharges both obligations.

#### Proof

An internal transition colour and the unique omitted transition colour of
a path are different labels in a one-copy q1 palette.  Opening precisely
the aperture transition removes `J` from the internal list, allowing it to
be the unique endpoint label. \(\square\)

The second case is a state condition, not a construction of the necessary
source rethread.

## 6. Conditional `B+1` bridge

Write `W` for the number of rank-`r` owners and `B=W+d`.  Assume a
same-parity Pascal construction supplies the following data.

1. Its child payloads are owner/declared-target exact and expose the two
   protected closing ports of Section 3 (or the longer anchored chain).
2. Their private-rooted selectors splice while retaining `p_*`.  The
   transition `M_(d-1)M_d` is the actual global opened edge, so its missing
   set intersection `J` is also the unique Hamilton-path boundary colour.
   The exterior child occurrence (3.3a), or another address-distinct
   protected cell, realizes `J` in the final source.
3. The pre-insertion depth-`d` scaffold contains the `d` rank-`(r+1)`
   values (1.7).  Outside them it has `W-d-1` selected rank-`r` owners and
   one controlled nonowner occurrence.
4. The post-insertion owner path contains (1.5), is otherwise owner-simple,
   and the displayed protected owner/q1 resources do not collide with the
   ambient completion.
5. A complete reference lower matching obeys the release ledger (4.1), and
   its transported edges, the exterior `J` occurrence, and all displayed
   source letters pass one common cap/guard state.
6. The parent source is rebuilt with exactly one active star `A_0=X` (or an
   explicit consolidation deletes the redundant child star).  Ordinary
   concatenation of two separately charged child words is not enough.

### Theorem 6.1 (conditional one-credit Pascal-pivot bridge)

Under hypotheses 1--6, inserting `X` produces a length-`B+1` source whose
depth-`d` row has exactly `W` selected rank-`r` owner occurrences and one
controlled nonowner occurrence.  Its selected owner skeleton is one path,
the declared q1 head palette and lower target bank are complete, the
persistent aperture and exterior discharge are both literal, and the lower
matching has a common source witness.

The same protected one-debt state can be passed through every later
same-parity splice by Section 5.  Under the one-star reconstruction in
hypothesis 6, the additive charge is one, not the recursion depth.

#### Proof

Insertion replaces the `d` rank-`(r+1)` cells (1.7) by the `d+1`
rank-`r` owners (1.5).  Hypothesis 3 therefore gives

\[
 (W-d-1)+(d+1)=W
\]

selected owners, while the controlled outside occurrence remains.  The
private splice theorem and hypothesis 2 give the path topology and identify
the native defect `J` with the one boundary colour.  Theorem 3.1 supplies
its exterior occurrence while retaining the literal anchor.  Theorem 4.1
transports the complete lower matching, and hypothesis 5 supplies one
simultaneous source/cap state.  Propositions 5.1--5.2 and hypothesis 6 keep
one active star rather than accumulating the two child stars. \(\square\)

This is a conditional `B+1`, hence `B+O(1)`, bridge.  It is not an
unconditional upper bound because hypotheses 1--6 include the correlated
global host which is still missing.

## 7. Exact scope

The composition closes the following rows under the hypotheses of Theorem
6.1.

1. **Local physical chronology:** the aperture, the promoted central value
   `N`, and the rank-`r` Johnson owner segment are one literal source block;
   the missing terminal colour is exactly `J` and is explicitly assigned
   to the exterior port occurrence (3.3a).
2. **Lower owner/target payload:** rectangle switches do not alter role
   payloads; the pivot promotes `N` to an exact-width head and realizes
   `Q_*` at its protected address.  Ordinary target coverage is preserved,
   not increased.
3. **Lower predecessor topology:** the private child rotors merge and open
   to one selected path at the surviving anchor.
4. **Old interval-deck transport:** every old OR witness at every width
   survives occurrencewise by monotonicity.
5. **Local lower compiler:** the release/transport matching (4.1) is exact
   and has one explicit common source witness; this is compiler neutrality,
   not a Hall-deficiency improvement.

The following rows remain separate.

1. **Global upper completeness.**  Monotone insertion preserves an already
   complete old OR deck, and the local upper-q1 colours are injective, but
   no theorem here constructs an upper-complete pre-insertion Pascal host
   or controls upper cross-sector collisions.
2. **Residence.**  Every moving-label run inside (1.5) meets a path endpoint,
   so the block has no self-contained short run.  Extending those clipped
   runs and preserving the Middle-Levels flip-gap bounds in the ambient
   chronology remain open.
3. **Global common cap/compiler.**  Theorem 4.1 closes the displayed local
   matching once all letters are admitted.  It does not prove that the
   complete ambient target matching, the Pascal port choices, and every
   exterior cell share one maximal common cap.
4. **Protected host and star consolidation.**  The decisive remaining lemma
   is to embed the displayed owner/q1/path bank into the full Pascal
   completion with hypotheses 1--6 and to rebuild two charged children with
   one parent star.  Arbitrary protected marginal extension is known to be
   false, so this is a correlated host theorem, not a counting corollary.

Thus the automatic opposite-fan repayment is refuted.  The exact surviving
statement is a one-debt regenerative state plus one exterior terminal
discharge.  Under the stated opening and consolidation hypotheses this is
a conditional `B+1` bridge; without them the debt is merely transported.
