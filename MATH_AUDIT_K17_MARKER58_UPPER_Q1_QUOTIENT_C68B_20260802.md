* endpoint multiplicity, including same-owner-orbit multiplicity two;

* all 1,144 rank-ten cap orbits covered;
* all 17 literal rotations of every selected and protected edge;
* every one of the 24,310 physical rank-eight facets exactly once;
* degree two at every one of the 24,310 physical rank-nine owners;
* all 19,448 physical rank-ten caps covered;

## 3. Two cubic rotations close connectivity and voltage

An exact bounded-neighbourhood enumerator checks degree-preserving incidence
rotations.  A length-three rotation replaces three selected residual options
while preserving their endpoint multiset, every protected row, and all cap
orbits.  From `c68b`, the first such move is

```text
remove primary variables  19 150 2935
add primary variables      2 178 2941
```

and changes the quotient topology to

```text
1388 vertices, voltage 14
  42 vertices, voltage  0
```

without losing a cap.  A second cubic move

```text
remove primary variables  1267 19509 19825
add primary variables      1269 19510 19816
```

joins those last two cycles.  The final independent replay returns

```text
PASS_CONNECTED_NONZERO_VOLTAGE
quotient components = 1
physical components = 1
quotient vertices/edges = 1430 / 1430
cycle voltage mod 17 = 4
physical edges = 24310
physical rank-ten caps covered = 19448
```

Because `gcd(4,17)=1`, the regular lift is one cycle.  A separate literal BFS
on all 24,310 physical owners also returns one component, so this conclusion
does not depend only on the voltage calculation.

Both moves touch only residual option rows.  All 232 protected quotient edges,
and hence all 986 developed marker paths, remain literal subpaths of the final
cycle.

This closes the finite owner/q1/rank-ten/connectivity/voltage host gate for the
first-58/open-3 marker bank.  It does **not** close joint source-state binding,
residence, ranks 11--17, or the lower compiler.
